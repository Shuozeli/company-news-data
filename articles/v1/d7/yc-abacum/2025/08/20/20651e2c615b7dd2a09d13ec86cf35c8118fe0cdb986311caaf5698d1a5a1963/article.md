# Redefining Financial Planning: Julio Martínez Leads Abacum’s Rise on the Global Stage

Julio Martínez outlines the vision behind the company’s global surge in redefining financial planning and analysis (FP&A) for mid‑market firms. Drawing on his finance and tech background—including completing the Stanford Executive Program in 2018—Julio explains that Abacum was built to fix the FP&A workflow they lived: draining hours spent on manual data cleanup, fragmented systems, stale insights, and broken models. Their platform delivers real‑time forecasting, headcount planning, and clarity in the face of economic turbulence, fueling smarter decisions across increasingly complex organizations while anchoring Abacum’s presence from New York to London and Barcelona
