# First Community Bank Celebrates Official Opening of Rock Hill Office

***April 6, 2023 (ROCK HILL, S.C.)*** – First Community Bank, headquartered in Lexington, South Carolina, is pleased to mark the official opening of its full-service banking office in Rock Hill at 1746 Ebenezer Road with a ceremonial ribbon cutting and celebration on March 30, 2023. This represents the bank’s first location in rapidly growing York County.


“As a community bank with an emphasis on relationship-style banking, we have the unique ability to form lasting partnerships built on our strong cultural values and we’re excited to share that with our customers,” said Regional Market President Catherine Faircloth. “Our team genuinely cares about each customer and their specific needs, and that sets us apart from other banks. From the moment you walk through our doors you’ll notice the difference, and we’re proud of that.”


The local team also includes Michael Clowney as Senior Vice President and Business Development Director and Anne Lambert as Vice President and Commercial Banker. Sally Anne Williamson will support the lending team as the Paralender and April Handley will support as a Teller.


"We are thrilled to welcome First Community Bank to our town, and we know that it shares our commitment to putting community first,” said Mayor John Gettys. “It's our people that make our town special, and we are proud to have a new partner that believes in investing in the growth and success of our residents. Together, we can help lift each other up and create a stronger, more vibrant community for all."


The bank also made a special donation to Pathways Community Center, a Rock Hill-based non-profit dedicated to effectively reducing the impact of poverty and homelessness in the community.


“As natives of Rock Hill, we are all excited to be a part of bringing First Community Bank into York County to serve the great people of this community,” said Senior Vice President and Business Development Director Michael Clowney.


First Community Bank, founded in 1995 and headquartered in Lexington, South Carolina, is a wholly owned subsidiary of First Community Corporation (NASDAQ: FCCO). First Community is a full-service commercial bank offering deposit and loan products and services, residential mortgage lending, and financial planning/investment advisory services for businesses and consumers. First Community serves customers in the Midlands, Upstate, York County, and Aiken, South Carolina, markets, as well as Augusta, Georgia.
