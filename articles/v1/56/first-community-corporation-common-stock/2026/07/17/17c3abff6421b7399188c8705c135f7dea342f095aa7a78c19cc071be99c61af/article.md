# First Community Bank Announces Leadership Transition in the CSRA

****


**October 21, 2024 (Lexington, S.C. and Augusta, GA.)** – First Community Bank announced today that J. Jordan Pierce has been named Regional Market President for the bank’s CSRA region. Mr. Pierce succeeds retiring Regional Market President Jeff Spears who led the CSRA region for First Community Bank since its acquisition of Savannah River Banking Company in 2014.


With more than 18 years of banking experience, including seven of those with First Community Bank, Pierce is well prepared to lead the bank’s continued growth in the CSRA market, which includes locations in Augusta and Evans, Georgia as well as Aiken, South Carolina. Pierce has the experience and knowledge of the Augusta community that will allow him to have an important impact on communities in the CSRA as he works with the bank’s local team to support business owners, professionals and entrepreneurs with the right financial solutions to help them be successful. Pierce earned a Bachelor of Business Administration from Augusta State University and throughout his career has been very active in the local community and is currently the Chair of Westminster Schools of Augusta, a member of the Rotary Club of Augusta, and an Elder of First Presbyterian Church of Augusta. His past community service includes Chair of the Columbia County Chamber of Commerce and the Columbia County Chamber Foundation, board member for the Augusta Partnership for Children, board member for the Augusta Symphony, and Campaign Chair and Chair of the United Way of the CSRA. Pierce is also a graduate of Leadership Augusta, Leadership Columbia County Executive Forum, and was a 2019 recipient of the Augusta Metro’s Top 10 in 10 award.


First Community Bank President and CEO, Ted Nissen, commented, “Jordan has been an integral part of our team in the CSRA since joining our bank in 2017 and we are excited about the contributions that he will continue to make as the leader of this important market for our bank. His passion for and dedication to the businesses and the communities in the CSRA make him the ideal person serve as our Regional Market President. I’d also like to thank Jeff for his many contributions to First Community Bank and the banking industry. He has made a significant impact during his career, especially in the CSRA and we wish him well in his retirement.”


About First Community Bank


First Community Bank is a full-service commercial bank offering deposit and loan products and services, residential mortgage lending and financial planning/investment advisory services for businesses and consumers. First Community serves customers in the Midlands, Aiken, Upstate and Piedmont regions of South Carolina as well as Augusta, Georgia. For more information, visit www.firstcommunitysc.com.
