# Didge at the 2025 Construction Robotics Summit

Didge was on display at the 2025 Construction Robotics Summit that took place in Brooklyn, NY on February 13th. The annual event is put on by Contech Alliance and New York Robotics Network to bring attention to the companies leading the way for innovation within construction robotics and technologies.


The Conference was a great success - it gave our team the opportunity to connect with many bright minds within the industry and share how Didge is a game changer with making construction safer, and more efficient.


Thank you to everyone that came to the event and spoke to our team. We are proud to be forging the future of construction alongside many other impressive companies. Special thank you to the organizers of the event. We look forward to returning next year and continuing to change the industry!
