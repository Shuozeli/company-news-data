# Didge at the Microsoft GC Safety Summit

“Huge win today for our team in the ATL metro as we conducted our first GC Safety Summit. EHS Professionals, Project Management, and Field Leadership from each of our General Contractors came together to discuss best practices, industry innovations, and program metrics to drive continuous improvement within our region.


Thank you to Cory Sherman, CSP CHST and Kyle Sherman with Safety Systems Management, LLC and to Jacob Ryals and Alex Rand with DIDGE AI for coming to highlight their products. Kudos to Safety Director Clay Whitman with Skanska for leading this effort and hosting this event, and to Turner Construction Company and Clark Construction Group for sending project leadership to collaborate.


At Microsoft, we believe that safety is not proprietary, so having our GC’s come together in a learning environment to continue to push the needle is how we get better and ensure our craftworkers are provided the safest working environment possible!“


Jacob Payne - Microsoft - Construction EHS Manager
