# Nextera Meets with Senator Elizabeth Warren during her visit to MassRobotics


May 25


Written By[Nextera Robotics](https://nexterarobotics.com/updates?author=6330c015577b973074d90178)


On Wednesday, May 24, 2023, Senator Elizabeth Warren paid a visit to the MassRobotics office in Boston, MA. During her visit the Nextera team had the opportunity to share how Nextera brings advanced technologies to business and government through solutions like Didge. Senator Warren was given the full tour of the Mass Robotics office and had the opportunity to meet with many other resident companies. MassRobotics is a fantastic organization that can bring a lot of value to companies starting their journey in robotics.
