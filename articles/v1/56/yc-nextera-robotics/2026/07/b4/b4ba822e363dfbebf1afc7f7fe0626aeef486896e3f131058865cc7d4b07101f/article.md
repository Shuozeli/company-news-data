# Nextera joins AMD at their booth at the 2024 Robotics Summit in Boston, MA

# Nextera joins AMD at their booth at the 2024 Robotics Summit in Boston, MA


May 1


Written By[Nextera Robotics](https://nexterarobotics.com/updates?author=6330c015577b973074d90178)


Nextera joined their longtime partner AMD at their booth at the 2024 Robotics Summit & Expo held at the Boston Convention and Exhibition Center in Boston, MA. Displayed at a booth was a Didge rover powered by AMD’s technology. During a scheduled presentation given by AMD to an audience of enthused robotics fans, Didge was a featured section to exemplify the technology of the future being used today.
