# Maxeon Focuses its Energy on U.S. Solar Market

**Author: George Guo, Maxeon CEO**


The solar energy market is in the midst of uncertainty, but Maxeon stands firm as a leading provider and innovator that is positioned to be your solar partner of choice for the long term. We want to assure you that Maxeon remains steadfastly dedicated to its long-term commitment of providing world-class solar panels and solar energy solutions to existing and potential new partners, dealers, and installers.


Maxeon has now strategically restructured its business portfolio and geographic market focus and is concentrating exclusively on the U.S. market, where its market presence and planned local manufacturing footprint create a strong platform to drive growth and success for the company and its expanding partner network.


With four decades of leadership, Maxeon has a proud legacy that originated as the innovation powerhouse for SunPower Corp. Becoming an independent entity in 2020, Maxeon has continued to lead the way as a top manufacturer of solar panels and solutions. Maxeon has since broadened its partner network, now focused on the U.S. residential, commercial, and utility-scale markets.


Maxeon's leadership history includes the ownership of more than 2,000 granted patents, enabling us to design the most innovative, reliable, and sustainably produced solar panels and energy solutions. The diversity and scope of Maxeon's technology set it apart, serving a vast array of solar customers.


In 2024, Maxeon embarked on an important recapitalization journey alongside our strategic investment partner, TCL. This relationship has uniquely positioned Maxeon to prosper, ensuring a robust and stable financial structure even in challenging times. TCL's backing of Maxeon signifies substantial support from a successful technology company, providing numerous advantages.


Working with TCL provides stability even in turbulent economic climates, allowing Maxeon to maintain its focus on innovation and delivery of top-quality solar solutions amidst market and policy uncertainty. This gives Maxeon a competitive advantage, both financially and technologically, reinforcing our leadership position in the solar industry.


At Maxeon, our customers are always our top priority. We remain committed to providing reliable, high-quality solar solutions that you've come to rely on. We value your trust in our solutions and eagerly anticipate powering your future with our advanced solar technologies.


With a growing network of reliable partners and distributors in the United States, and over a million customers powering their lives with our solar solutions, Maxeon remains the go-to partner for dealers and installers keen on promoting the shift to clean energy. We are enthusiastic about the future of solar and invite you to join us in Powering Positive Change.
