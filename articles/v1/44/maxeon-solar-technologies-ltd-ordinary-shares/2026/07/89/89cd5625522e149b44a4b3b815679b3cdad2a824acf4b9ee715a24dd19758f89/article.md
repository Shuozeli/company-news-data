Maxeon’s patented back contact cell design and module construction ensure that its solar panels outperform others in energy production over the long haul. Here’s how:


1.

2.


[Resistance to Hail](https://www.maxeon.com/maxeon-blog/how-maxeon-solar-panels-beat-the-growing-threat-of-hailstorms) **:** Maxeon panels are IEC-certified to withstand hailstones up to 45 mm in diameter thanks to a robust protective frame and tempered glass that outperform many competing panels.


3.


[Durability in Extreme Weather](https://www.maxeon.com/maxeon-blog/category/Extreme+Weather) : Maxeon’s patented cell design with thick cell connectors endure high heat, extreme cold, and temperature fluctuations without compromising performance.


4.


**Industry-Leading Longevity** : Thanks to the lowest degradation rate in the industry, Maxeon panels retain their power advantage for up to 40 years. This means more energy production from day one and sustained performance for decades to come.
