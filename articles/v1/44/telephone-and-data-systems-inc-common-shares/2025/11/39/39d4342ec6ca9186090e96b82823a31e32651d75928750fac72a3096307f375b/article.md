# TDS fully launches mobile phone product

TDS is excited to fully launch our mobile phone product, providing customers access to a reliable, nationwide 5G network at affordable prices. After months of progressively rolling out the new product offering, TDS Mobile is now available to all TDS customers (excluding those in California). The company is utilizing one of America’s most reliable 5G networks to deliver fast speeds and nationwide coverage — all at an affordable price for customers.


All[TDS Mobile plans](https://tdstelecom.com/shop/mobile.html) include nationwide 5G coverage, unlimited talk and text, a 30-day money back guarantee, and no contracts. Prices start at $13.95 per month by-the-gig and $29.95 per month for unlimited. Customers who sign up for the Unlimited Plan are also eligible to take advantage of the current Buy One Line, Get One 50% Off promotion.


“Since TDS was founded over 55 years ago, we have worked tirelessly to bring advanced technology to small and mid-sized communities across the country. We’re proud to add TDS Mobile to our portfolio of products and give our customers the nationwide 5G coverage they expect at a price they’ll love,” said TDS Chief Operating Officer Shane West.


TDS Mobile is offered exclusively to TDS broadband customers, both existing and prospective. Customers can keep their device and current phone number, making the switch to TDS Mobile simple and convenient.


The launch of this new mobile product coincides with our continued efforts to upgrade our internet network within our service footprint – including rural and underserved areas.


“As we continue to invest in our networks and expand our product offerings, TDS Mobile positions us to better serve our customers and support the growth of TDS communities,” added West. “We’re proud to deliver the reliable connectivity and flexibility our customers deserve.”


For more information and to sign up for TDS Mobile, visit[tdstelecom.com/mobile](https://tdstelecom.com/shop/mobile.html) .
