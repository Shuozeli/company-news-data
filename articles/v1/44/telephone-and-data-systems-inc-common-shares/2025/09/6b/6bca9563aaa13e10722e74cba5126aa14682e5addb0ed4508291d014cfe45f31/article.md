# TDS embarking on major upgrades in rural areas

TDS is working hard to bridge the digital divide and bring faster internet speeds to customers throughout our service footprint – including in rural areas and underserved locations.


Through the Enhanced Alternative Connect America Cost Model (E-ACAM) program and TDS private investment, TDS is doubling down on its commitment to improve rural broadband. E-ACAM is a multi-year, federal program that provides funding to deploy and support 100/20 Mbps or faster broadband service to some of the most rural geographies in our service areas.


Construction will continue through 2028 across 23 states. We recently launched a map on our website that allows you to enter your address to learn if upgrades are planned for your area.[Check out the map here](https://tdstelecom.com/support/networkupgrades.html) .


“TDS was founded on the premise of bringing reliable and modern communications services to underserved areas of rural and smalltown America. As we’ve grown into a national leader in fiber-optic construction, we remain committed to bridging the digital divide for our most hard-to-serve customers,” said Drew Petersen, TDS Senior Vice President of Corporate Affairs.


TDS has broken ground on E-ACAM projects[across the country](https://tdstelecom.com/about/news/categories/tds/TDSBreaksGrountOnLargeScaleHighSpeedInternetProjectInRuralDaneCountyWis.html) . Our construction projects will lay thousands of miles of fiber in 2025 alone, with more planned as we continue these network upgrades over the next few years.


In addition,[TDS Connect](https://tdstelecom.com/information/tds-connect.html) , our affordable high-speed service option, continues to be available to eligible customers.
