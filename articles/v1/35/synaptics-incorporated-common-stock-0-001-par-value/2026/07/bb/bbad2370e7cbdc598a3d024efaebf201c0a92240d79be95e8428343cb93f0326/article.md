## Product Family Overview


##


The DisplayLink DL-7xxx family is designed from the ground up for transport-agnostic connectivity, delivering a flexible platform for high-performance display and peripheral applications.


Supporting up to dual 8K at 60 Hz or quad 4K at 120 Hz, the DL-7xxx family includes 2.5 Gbps Ethernet, IoT connectivity, HDR10 support, dynamic splash screens, hostless graphics, and peripheral status reporting as standard. It is ideal for thin clients, docking stations, PCIe cards, and adapters.


Trusted by Dell, HP, StarTech, Plugable, Anker, Wavlink, OWC, i-Tec, MBeat, TobenONE, and many others, the DL-7xxx family enables partners to build scalable, future-ready products for evolving display and connectivity requirements.
