# An Update On Our Redding Hilltop Branch

By Plumas Bank


April 3, 2026


[Community banking](https://www.plumasbank.com/blog/category/community-banking)


Since opening in 2007, Plumas Bank’s Redding Hilltop branch has proudly served local businesses and community members with personalized, relationship driven banking. As we look ahead, we want to share an important update about this location—and, more importantly, reassure clients that our commitment to Redding remains strong.


With the Hilltop building’s lease expiring at the end of 2026, Plumas Bank has made the decision to transition services from the Hilltop branch to two nearby, full service Redding locations that opened last year. These modern branches—located on[Hartnell Avenue](https://www.plumasbank.com/locations/redding-california-hartnell) and in[Downtown Redding](https://www.plumasbank.com/locations/redding-california-downtown) —are within a short drive of Hilltop and offer expanded amenities designed to better serve our clients and the community.


##


## What This Means for Clients


- **Uninterrupted service** : Clients will continue receiving the same trusted banking services they rely on, without disruption.
- **The same familiar team** : All members of the Hilltop branch team will move to one of the nearby Redding locations, allowing clients to keep working with the bankers and lenders they know and trust.
- **Enhanced branch features** : Both nearby branches are full service and include drive up banking, ATMs, night drop, safe deposit boxes, and community meeting spaces.


## Our Continued Commitment to Redding


This transition reflects responsible stewardship and long term investment in the Redding market. By moving from a leased building into locations Plumas Bank owns, we’re able to invest more effectively in our facilities, our teams, and the services we provide. We are not reducing our footprint or services in the region—in fact, we’re continuing to grow, with recent additions to our commercial lending, credit administration, and treasury management teams.


## Timeline and Next Steps


The Redding Hilltop branch will remain open through **September 2026** , with additional details shared as plans are finalized. Clients will receive direct communication well in advance of the transition to ensure a smooth and seamless experience.
