# Plumas Bank Named “Best Bank” in North Lake Tahoe & Truckee for the 13th Consecutive Year

By Plumas Bank


July 6, 2026


[Community banking](https://www.plumasbank.com/blog/category/community-banking)


Many awards are decided by committee, a group of industry representatives contemplating in a conference room and deciding outcomes based on checklists and metrics.


But an altogether different kind of award is decided by something entirely more personal: community. These recognitions often carry unique meaning because they're decided by the people who experience the service firsthand.


For the 13th consecutive year, readers of the Sierra Sun have named Plumas Bank “Best Bank” in the annual Best of North Lake Tahoe & Truckee contest, celebrating the local businesses, organizations and people who help make the region thrive. The recognition spans all three of Plumas Bank's North Lake Tahoe branches — Truckee, Kings Beach and Tahoe City — where local teams have earned the trust of families and business owners year after year.


A streak like this reflects thousands of neighborly conversations and many years spent building robust relationships.


> "Receiving this community recognition means so much to both me and my team," said Sherina Kreul, vice president, branch manager in Tahoe City. "It’s a reflection of the trust and relationships we’ve built over the years, and it reinforces our commitment to showing up for our clients and our community every single day.”


## A Culture Committed to Community


While Plumas Bank shares the same values across every branch, no two communities are exactly alike.


Tahoe City is a walkable lakefront hub built on generations of close-knit relationships. Truckee's seasonal economy and inland location creates unique opportunities for businesses and residents. And Kings Beach draws both locals and year-round visitors to its convenient lakeside location slightly closer to the Nevada/California border. Each branch reflects the community it serves, and each team values its connection to the local culture.


Elizabeth Wainscoat, Truckee vice president, branch manager, sees banking as part of the fabric of the community itself. The same relationships that help neighbors navigate Truckee's unique rhythms also create stronger banking partnerships.


> “The businesses and residents of Truckee have a great respect for each other,” she said. “Truckee brings its own challenges with the seasonality of living here, and sharing how to navigate those challenges is something that makes us all stronger. Sharing our struggles and successes connects us in ways that may not happen in a larger community.
>
>
> “With that connectivity comes trust,” she continued. “Knowing each other is one thing, but trust is what makes good banking partners.”


A few miles away in Kings Beach, Assistant Vice President, Branch Manager Kevin Davies sees these same principles at work in a slightly different setting. Sometimes, he believes, building trust begins by creating a space where people feel comfortable enough to have real conversations.


“You can count on a warm and professional environment whenever you enter any Plumas Bank branch,” he said. “I have to say our branch offers a particularly cozy environment right on the shore of the lake. Whether it’s the view of the lake right across the street or a crackling fire in the winter months, it’s a setting that helps people feel comfortable, have real conversations, and build lasting relationships.”


## Trust, Personalized Service & Lasting Connections


Although each branch manager described the meaning of the award in a different way, their answers shared a common thread: All three talked about the value of in-depth conversations, understanding client goals and earning trust over time.


Kreul encapsulates the Plumas Bank difference by highlighting the bank’s foundational belief.


“At our core, relationships matter,” she said. “Because we know our clients personally, they become more than just clients — they become family. Through years of trust, understanding, and personalized service, we’ve built lasting connections that go beyond business. Our clients know they can count on us to be there when they need us most, providing guidance, support and peace of mind every step of the way.”


Wainscoat agrees, adding that relationships are strengthened by consistency and connection.


“Plumas Bank has the best clients, and we are happy to be able to serve them and earn their trust in meeting their banking needs,” she said. “Our personal connection to our clients is what sets us apart. We feel a strong sense of responsibility in the quality of service that we provide and strive to provide the follow through that our clients have come to expect.”


Davies believes the award reflects a commitment shared across every member of the team.


> “I think \[the award\] speaks to our commitment as a bank, a team, and as individuals to support our community both inside and outside the walls of our branches,” he said. “There is a genuine care taken by each of our team members to know and support our clients and the community in which we live and work.”


Thirteen consecutive years is an incredible milestone, and it’s one the Plumas Bank team is proud to celebrate. More importantly, it reflects the confidence clients have placed in the bank year after year.


Thank you to the readers of the Sierra Sun and to the communities of North Lake Tahoe, Truckee and Kings Beach for your continued trust.


“Winning ‘Best Of’ for 13 years is an incredible honor,” Kreul said. “It speaks to the consistency, dedication, and passion of our team. Each year motivates us to keep raising the bar, continuing to serve with integrity, and giving back to the community that supports us.”
