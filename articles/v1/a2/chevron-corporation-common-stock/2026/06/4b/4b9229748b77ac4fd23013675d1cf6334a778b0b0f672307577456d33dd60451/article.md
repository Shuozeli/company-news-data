# Making power moves

Chevron’s experience supplying energy to large industrial and commercial facilities informs how the project will be designed and operated.


“Chevron is applying decades of project execution and energy system experience to help customers power AI responsibly, securely and at scale,” said Chevron New Energies President Jeff Gustavson. “We bring our strengths to bear to help solve new energy challenges.”


As Chevron advances new power solutions for data centers, access to power remains a critical consideration for supporting its own operations. From electrifying its drilling fleet to powering compression infrastructure in the Permian, Chevron evaluates new power investments through an enterprise-wide lens—ensuring that emerging opportunities like data centers are advanced in a way that supports the reliability and availability of electricity needed across its existing operations.
