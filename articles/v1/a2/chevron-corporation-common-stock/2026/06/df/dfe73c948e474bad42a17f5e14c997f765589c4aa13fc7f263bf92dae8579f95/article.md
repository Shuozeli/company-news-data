# The energy that keeps America moving

A car powers up before dawn. A shipment leaves a warehouse on schedule. A long flight safely reaches its destination.


America’s freedom to move—on our roads and in the air—depends on a reliable energy infrastructure that Chevron has been helping to build for almost 150 years.


It’s an infrastructure that encompasses everything from finding oil beneath the ground to putting quality-tested fuel to use, as well as products and processes that have been improved over generations. The story of American energy is the story of countless people innovating, working hard and meeting challenges head on.
