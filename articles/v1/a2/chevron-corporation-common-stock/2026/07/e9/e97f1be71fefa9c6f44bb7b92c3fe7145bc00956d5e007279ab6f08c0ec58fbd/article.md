# How fuel additive science keeps pace with modern engines

Simpson-Green has spent 25 of her 39 years at Chevron working with fuel additives. She and her team were involved throughout the years-long next-generation reformulation effort. Their work began in the earliest stages, when they helped evaluate potential materials and how they might perform. Then they moved into testing and validation. The work required collaboration across multiple teams to create a fuel additive that delivers enhanced performance for modern engines without compromising performance for conventional ones.


The focus was always on how the final product would help drivers.


“There’s a shared mindset across Chevron that the performance of our products is validated through rigorous testing,” she said. “What we communicate about those products is based on repeated testing with statistically significant results.”
