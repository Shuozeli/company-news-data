# The Energy Tailgate: The technology innovations that are moving energy forward

Ryder Booth is the chief technology and engineering officer of Chevron Corporation, a position he has held since 2025. In this role, he is responsible for the development, scaling, project execution, and delivery of innovative technology solutions to support the current and future energy system. He also leads the company’s organizations in health, safety and environment, information technology, innovation, capital projects, facilities engineering, operations and turnarounds, asset retirement, reserves and storage, and supply chain management in support of Chevron’s global operations.
