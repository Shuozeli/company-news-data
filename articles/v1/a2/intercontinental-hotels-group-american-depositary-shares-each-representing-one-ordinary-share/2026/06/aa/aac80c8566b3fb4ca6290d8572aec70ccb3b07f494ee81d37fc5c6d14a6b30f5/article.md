**LONDON, 03 June 2026** – Each year, top travel editors and experts come together to hand pick hospitality’s best examples of excellence. Serving readers with a guiding light of where to go next, these hotly contested shortlists celebrate best-in-class demonstrations of how travel is evolving to meet the needs of today’s luxury guests.


Condé Nast Traveller’s inaugural[Triple Crown Awards](https://www.cntraveller.com/triple-crown) offers a new addition to the annual awards landscape, hailed as the publication’s *“most definitive collection of the best hotels in the world”* . This prestigious new accolade recognizes the collective recipients of all three of the publication’s most revered awards – *The Hot List, The Gold List* and *Readers’ Choice Awards* . Eight IHG hotels and resorts, spanning four luxury and lifestyle brands, have been recognized with Condé Nast Traveller extending the following high praise:

“Views of Asia's sparkliest city don't come any better.”

Spanning more than 590 properties across 70 countries, IHG’s Luxury & Lifestyle portfolio is intentionally differentiated, designed to meet evolving definitions of modern luxury across occasions – from ultra-bespoke, rarefied experiences to culturally connected, experience-led travel. Each brand, in its own unique way, provides a solution for every scenario today’s modern luxury traveller may navigate.


Other recent accolades further underscore IHG’s position as one of the world’s leading luxury and lifestyle players. IHG’s recent luxury additions[Six Senses London](https://www.ihgplc.com/en/news-and-media/news-releases/2026/a-new-dawn-six-senses-marks-its-london-debut-with-landmark-opening-at-the-whiteley) and[Kimpton Tsim Sha Tsui Hong Kong](https://www.kimptonhongkong.com/) were both praised within the *Condé Nast Traveler 2026 Hot List* for best new openings.


As the[Global Wellness Institute](https://globalwellnessinstitute.org/initiatives/wellness-tourism/) highlight wellness travel as one of the fastest growing segments with one billion wellness trips taken annually, further recognition for Six Senses demonstrates the brand’s leadership in this space. Condé Nast Traveler’s 2026 *Global Wellness & Spa Guide* recommends **Six Senses Vana** , a deep retreat at the foothills of the Himalayas, for the third consecutive year, whilst *VOGUE’s 2026 Global Spa Guide* included **Six Senses London, Six Senses Crans-Montana, Six Senses Ibiza** and **Six Senses** **Vana** within its shortlist of the[world’s 100 Best Spas](https://www.vogue.com/tag/misc/spa-guide) .


The spotlight now turns to the 39th annual Condé Nast Traveler Readers’ Choice Awards with more than 300 IHG properties shortlisted and gathering votes. The longest running recognition in the travel industry, Condé Nast Traveler’s Readers’ Choice Awards is voted for by global readers of the magazine worldwide. To vote, visit:[UK voting site](https://www.cntraveller.com/article/vote-readers-choice-awards) |[US voting site](https://www.cntraveler.com/story/vote-readers-choice-awards?page=5) .
