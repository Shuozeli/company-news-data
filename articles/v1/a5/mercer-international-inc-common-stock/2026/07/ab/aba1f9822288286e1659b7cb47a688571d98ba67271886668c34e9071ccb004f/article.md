# 20 years of Mercer Holz: Hagen Seidel on responsibility, trust, and sustainable wood procurement

### Thanks for two decades of vision and commitment


For Hagen Seidel, 20 years at Mercer Holz mean change, growth, and responsibility. For Mercer Holz, they mean an employee who, with his experience, calmness, and passion, has shaped our sustainable procurement like few others.


**Dear Hagen, thank you for your many years of service and your tremendous dedication. You have shaped our work and our team over many years—we would like to express our sincere gratitude for that.**
