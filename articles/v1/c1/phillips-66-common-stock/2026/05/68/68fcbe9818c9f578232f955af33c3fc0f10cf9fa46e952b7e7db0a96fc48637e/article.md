# Phillips 66 earns six safety recognitions

**Rodeo and Ponca City earn AFPM’s highest safety honor**


The American Fuel & Petrochemical Manufacturers (AFPM) recognized Rodeo Renewable Energy Complex and Ponca City Refinery with the Distinguished Safety Award–the organization’s highest honor–while recognizing four additional Phillips 66 sites across multiple award categories.


*Phillips 66 team members honored at the 2025 AFPM Annual Safety Awards.*


Congratulations to the Phillips 66 facilities and teams recognized across multiple award categories:


- **Ponca City Refinery** — Distinguished Safety Award and Innovation Award for Occupational Safety
- **Rodeo Renewable Energy Complex** — Distinguished Safety Award and Innovation Award Honorable Mention for Occupational Safety
- **Ferndale Refinery** — Elite Platinum Award
- **Lake Charles Refinery** — Elite Platinum Award
- **Billings Refinery** — Elite Gold Award
- **Los Angeles Refinery** — Elite Silver Award


AFPM’s Elite Silver Award recognizes facilities performing in the industry’s top 10%, while Elite Gold recipients rank in the top 5%. The Elite Platinum and Distinguished Safety awards represent the organization’s highest levels of safety performance and leadership.


*Ponca City Refinery team members accept the Distinguished Safety Award.*


**Why it matters**


These recognitions reflect the daily commitment employees and contractors make to protecting one another, operating responsibly and strengthening a culture where safety remains foundational to how work gets done.


Behind each recognition are employees and contractors whose focus on safe work, operational discipline and continuous improvement helps strengthen safety performance across the company.


> “Safe, reliable performance doesn’t happen by chance. It’s earned one job at a time and strengthened through the daily focus of employees across our sites and a shared commitment to protecting one another.”
>
>
> **Karen Albrecht**
> Phillips 66 VP, global HSE & compliance


*Rodeo Renewable Energy Complex receives the Distinguished Safety Award.*


These facilities exemplify the continued commitment employees and contractors across Phillips 66 bring to strengthening safety performance, supporting operational excellence and fostering a culture of safety throughout the company.


AFPM’s Annual Safety Awards are among the industry’s most recognized honors for occupational and process safety performance and leadership.
