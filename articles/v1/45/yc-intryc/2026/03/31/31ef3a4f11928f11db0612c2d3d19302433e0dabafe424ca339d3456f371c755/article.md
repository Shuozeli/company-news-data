# Intryc vs Calabrio: Dedicated QA vs Legacy WFM Bundle

March 13, 2026 | By Alex Marantelos


Calabrio is a workforce management platform where QA is an add-on module. Intryc is built from the ground up for AI-powered QA and evaluates 100% of interactions with no configuration overhead. For QA alone, Intryc is faster, cheaper, and more accurate.


## The Core Difference


Calabrio started as workforce scheduling software. QA was added later as one module in a larger suite. Intryc is purpose-built for QA—everything is designed around evaluating interactions and driving improvement.


## Coverage and Accuracy


Calabrio QA evaluates interactions based on sampling rules. Actual coverage tends to stay at 2-5% in practice.


Intryc evaluates 100% of your interactions using AI with a 90% accuracy guarantee. Blueground went from 3% coverage to 5.5% while adding 100% AI evaluation. SadaPay shifted to AI-powered evaluation—what used to take a week now takes a day.


## Training and Agent Development


Calabrio has learning management features, but they're separate from QA evaluation. A manager finds an issue, then manually creates training content or coaching tasks.


Intryc automatically generates training simulations from the exact interactions where agents failed. Deel's team got 40% more productive using AI-generated scenarios.

## Who Should Choose Intryc?


Choose Intryc if you need QA to work independently from your WFM system. Choose it if you want to start evaluating interactions this week, not next month. Choose it if your budget is tight but quality standards aren't.


## Frequently Asked Questions


### Does Intryc replace workforce management software?


No. Intryc is QA only. If you need scheduling, forecasting, or shift planning, you'll use Calabrio, Verint, or another WFM tool for that. Intryc handles QA independently.


### Can we use Intryc and Calabrio together?


Yes. Many teams keep Calabrio for WFM and add Intryc for QA coverage. Intryc pulls your ticket or call data and runs evaluation independently.


### How does Intryc achieve 100% interaction coverage while Calabrio stays at 2-5%?


Intryc uses AI to automatically evaluate every interaction—tickets, calls, chats—without requiring manual reviewer time. Calabrio's QA module relies on sampling rules where humans review interactions, which naturally caps coverage at 2-5% due to time constraints.


### Does Intryc require a Calabrio or WFM contract to work?


No. Intryc is completely independent and connects directly to your helpdesk or contact center platform. You can use Intryc without any WFM software. It integrates with Zendesk, Intercom, Freshdesk, Salesforce, and other platforms independently.


### What is the typical setup time for Intryc vs Calabrio QA?


Intryc can be fully operational in under 10 minutes—connect your helpdesk, set up your scorecard, and start evaluating. Calabrio's implementation typically takes 2-4 weeks and often requires professional services support to configure the QA module.


### Can Intryc evaluate AI agent and chatbot interactions, which Calabrio cannot?


Yes. Intryc is built to evaluate both human agent and AI agent/chatbot interactions using the same scorecard framework. Calabrio QA has limited support for AI agent evaluation and typically requires custom setup. As AI agents become more prevalent in support, this capability is critical.
