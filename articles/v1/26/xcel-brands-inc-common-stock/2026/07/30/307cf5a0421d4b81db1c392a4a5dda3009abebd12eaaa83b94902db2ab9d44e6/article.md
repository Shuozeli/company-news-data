# Xcel Brands partners with KBL Group for Coco Rocha line

NEW YORK - Xcel Brands, Inc. (NASDAQ:XELB) announced today a licensing agreement with KBL Group for its OFF/DUTY by Coco Rocha brand, according to a press release statement.


Under the agreement, KBL Group will serve as the production partner for OFF/DUTY by Coco Rocha’s ready-to-wear collections. The company will handle sourcing, product development, manufacturing, and supply chain management for the brand.


OFF/DUTY by Coco Rocha is a fashion and accessories brand created in collaboration with supermodel Coco Rocha. The brand offers wardrobe essentials designed for women, featuring pieces that transition from day to night.


"KBL Group brings exceptional expertise in product development and manufacturing, making them an ideal partner for OFF/DUTY by Coco Rocha," said Robert W. D’Loren, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of Xcel Brands.


David Guisinger, Chief Executive Officer of KBL Group, stated the partnership combines Xcel’s brand building approach with KBL’s product development and sourcing capabilities.


The partnership aims to support the brand’s expansion across multiple retail distribution channels.
