# Xcel Brands launches home collection with J.Queen New York

NEW YORK - Xcel Brands Inc. (NASDAQ:XELB) announced today a collaboration between its Tower Hill by Christie Brinkley brand and J.Queen New York to create a luxury bedding and home decor collection.


The collection will include premium bedding ensembles, decorative pillows, throws, and home accents, according to a press release statement. The products combine Tower Hill by Christie Brinkley’s lifestyle aesthetic with J.Queen New York’s textile manufacturing capabilities.


"This collaboration represents an exciting opportunity to expand the Tower Hill by Christie Brinkley brand into the home furnishings category with a best-in-class partner," said Robert D’Loren, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of Xcel Brands.


Christie Brinkley said the collection draws inspiration from her Bridgehampton home and items collected during her modeling career. "With this home collection, I wanted to bring our customer the same sense of warmth, and whimsy through unexpected pairings that are both effortlessly elegant and fun," she stated.


Julie Brady, Founder and Owner of J.Queen New York, said the partnership marks a new chapter for the company while maintaining its focus on quality and design.
