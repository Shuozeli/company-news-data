# Xcel Brands signs licensing deal for Christie Brinkley home decor line

[Xcel Brands](https://www.investing.com/equities/xcel-brands-inc)


(XELB) announced a licensing agreement between its Tower Hill by Christie Brinkley brand and J.Queen New York to produce a luxury bedding and home decor collection, according to a company statement.


The collection will include premium bedding ensembles, decorative pillows, throws, and home accents. The line draws on J.Queen New York’s textile manufacturing experience combined with the Tower Hill brand’s lifestyle aesthetic.


"This collaboration represents an exciting opportunity to expand the Tower Hill by Christie Brinkley brand into the home furnishings category with a best-in-class partner," said Robert D’Loren, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of Xcel Brands.


Julie Brady, Founder and Owner of J.Queen New York, said the partnership allows the company to continue growing while maintaining its focus on quality and design.


Xcel Brands is a New York-based media and consumer products company engaged in the design, licensing, and marketing of branded apparel, footwear, accessories, and home goods. The company also owns the Halston and C. Wonder brands, among others.
