# How Hybrid Intelligence Defeated Fraud in a Fast Growing P2P Marketplace

## The challenge


When growth outpaces platform safety


Scaling a global marketplace requires a defense that grows as fast as the user base. For a London-based peer-to-peer (P2P) platform, rapid expansion had opened the path to account takeovers and transaction fraud — complex threats that plague the industry.


To sustain growth and protect its community, the platform needed to move beyond basic moderation and implement a deeper, forensic level of screening. The goal was to spot bad actors in real-time without creating friction for legitimate buyers and sellers.


## The solution


A hybrid intelligence defense


To stabilize the platform and harden its defenses, we deployed a rapid-response framework that transformed the fraud department and instituted a proactive layer of security, including:


- **Agile resource deployment:**


Mobilizing a dedicated internal resource team with specific and solid past expertise in P2P transaction patterns and fraud
- **Performance-driven quality gates:**


Establishing rigorous toll gates from training to nesting phase, ensuring every analyst meets 100% accuracy before entering the live environment
- **Human-in-the-loop optimization:**


Pairing AI tools with the platform’s human experts to catch subtle fraud that automated systems usually miss
- **Strategic process consulting:**


Partnering with the client’s SMEs to share emerging trends and recommending improvements to the overall process
