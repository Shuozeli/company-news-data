# 3 Ways Mobile Deposit Will Change Your World | Southern Bank

**1. It saves time.**


Access to mobile banking on your smart phone or mobile device means fewer trips to the bank or ATM. You can check your balances, view transaction history, transfer funds, pay bills and more.


**2. It keeps you on-track financially.**


With mobile bill pay, you can schedule bill payments in advance so you’re not rushing last-minute to make a payment or facing a late fee after forgetting a bill. Not to mention saving on stamps and envelopes.


**3. It makes life easier.**


You don’t have to stop by the bank to cash a check any longer, leaving you free to focus on the things you want to do. With mobile deposit, you can deposit a check right from your smart phone or mobile device. It’s as easy as tap, snap, deposit.
