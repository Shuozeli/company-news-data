# Southern Bank Welcomes Steve Kamienski | Southern Bank

Southern Bank is pleased to welcome Steve Kamienski, J.D., as Director, Trust Administration for Southern Wealth Trust Services.


Based in Springfield, Missouri, Kamienski brings more than 25 years of experience in trust administration, fiduciary services, estate planning, and wealth management. After beginning a career in private practice focused on estate planning, Steve transitioned into banking and trust administration, where he held a series of leadership positions with both national trust companies and local wealth management organizations. Most recently, he served as Senior Trust Officer and Vice President at National Advisors Trust Company


“We are excited to welcome Steve to Southern Wealth Trust Services,” said Diane Wakefield. “We look forward to the expertise and leadership he brings to our clients and team.”


### About Southern Wealth Trust Services


Southern Wealth Trust Services provides personalized trust and fiduciary solutions designed to help individuals, families, and businesses protect, manage, and transfer wealth with confidence.
