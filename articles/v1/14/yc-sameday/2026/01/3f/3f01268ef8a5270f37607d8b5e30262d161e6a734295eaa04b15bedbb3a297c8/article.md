# How pest control companies can use AI to book more jobs

Running a pest control business means being available when customers need you most. Technicians are on job sites, treating homes, or handling emergencies. Phones ring while hands are busy with equipment and chemicals.


That's when calls get missed.


In pest control, a missed call often means a lost job. A homeowner who discovers termites in their foundation or hears rats in the attic needs help immediately. If they reach voicemail, they move on to the next number. Most don't call back.


AI-powered customer service fills that gap. Using Voice AI, it answers incoming calls, understands what the customer needs, and schedules jobs directly into your system. It works alongside your team to capture every opportunity.


## **Why traditional phone systems create problems**


Pest control is an urgent service business. When someone finds roaches in their kitchen or a wasp nest near their front door, they call for help right away.


Traditional customer service creates several issues:


**1. Limited hours:** Most pest control offices operate 9-to-5, but pest emergencies happen any time. Small home service businesses miss around 60% of incoming calls, with 85% of those callers never calling back. Customers who reach voicemail after hours often book with the first person who answers.


**2. High call volume during peak seasons:** Spring and summer bring more pest activity and more customer calls. Teams become overwhelmed quickly. Long hold times and missed calls can result in lost revenue during your busiest periods.


**3. Repetitive tasks:** Staff spend hours answering the same basic questions, scheduling appointments, and collecting customer information. This takes time away from actual pest control work.


**4. Inconsistent service:** Different team members handle calls differently. One person might forget to ask about the pest type. Another might not mention your service guarantees or current promotions.


These challenges hurt your bottom line.[Reducing call center costs](https://www.gosameday.com/post/how-can-home-services-companies-reduce-call-centre-costs) while improving service has become essential for growing pest control businesses.


## **How AI handles pest control calls**


An[AI-powered phone system](https://www.gosameday.com/) works as a virtual receptionist that never takes a break. When customers call, the AI answers immediately, understands their needs, and provides appropriate responses.


Here's what happens during a typical call:


The AI answers with your customized greeting. It asks targeted questions to understand the situation. What type of pest? Is this an emergency? What's the property size and location?


The system checks your technicians' availability and offers appointment slots. If the customer mentions termites, it knows this requires a different service than routine ant treatment and routes accordingly.


The technology integrates with your scheduling system. When a customer books an appointment, it updates your calendar instantly. The AI collects all necessary information upfront so your technicians arrive prepared.

## **Common AI applications in pest control**


Modern[voice AI platforms for pest control](https://www.gosameday.com/industries/pest-control) handle the specific needs of businesses:


- **Emergency triage:** The system identifies urgent situations like active wasp nests or sudden termite discoveries. It prioritizes them in your schedule and can alert technicians immediately for same-day service.
- **Seasonal management:** During slower winter months, AI promotes preventative treatments or off-season specials. When spring arrives, and ant calls increases, it handles the volume without missing calls.
- **Service routing:** Different pests need different expertise. AI routes bed bug calls to heat treatment specialists, termite inspections to certified inspectors, and wildlife removal to the appropriate team member.
- **Multi-location support:** For companies with multiple service areas, AI determines the customer's location and routes them to the nearest office or service team automatically.


## **How to implement AI for customer service?**


Start by identifying your biggest pain points. Are you missing after-hours calls? Does appointment scheduling take too much time? Do customers ask the same questions repeatedly? Focus on solving these specific challenges first.


Choose a system that integrates with your existing tools. Your AI solution should work with your scheduling software, CRM, and payment systems. This ensures smooth information flow without creating extra work.


Customize the AI to reflect your brand. The system should know your service area, pricing structure, common pest issues in your region, and your unique value propositions. This makes interactions feel natural and specific to your business.


Train your team on working alongside AI. Staff should understand how the system works, when it escalates calls to them, and how to access collected information. This collaboration delivers the best customer experience.
