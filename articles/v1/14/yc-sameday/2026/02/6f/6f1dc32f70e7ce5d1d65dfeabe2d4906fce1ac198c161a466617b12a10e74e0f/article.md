# How can home services companies reduce call centre costs?

For home services companies, phone calls equal bookings. But answering those calls is expensive. Staffing phones all day, covering peak hours, and handling after-hours calls quickly adds to operating costs.


Most incoming calls are routine: booking requests, schedule changes, and availability checks, yet they still require trained staff. As call volume grows, so does headcount, making call centers increasingly difficult to scale without compromising margins.


Reducing call center costs starts with handling calls more efficiently, using automation and AI phone systems to manage volume without adding staff.


## **Why do call center costs rise so quickly in home services?**


Home services businesses rely on phone calls more than most industries. Customers call to book urgent jobs, check availability, or get help during breakdowns. When calls go unanswered, customers rarely wait; they call the next provider.


Industry research shows that[up to 62% of callers](https://medium.com/@jtgrahamm/the-silent-profit-killer-why-62-of-your-business-calls-go-unanswered-and-what-its-costing-you-7619af72cce4) won’t call back after a missed call.


To avoid missed calls, companies add staff. However, staffing phones all day, covering evenings, and handling seasonal spikes can quickly drive up costs. According to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics,[customer service labor costs have risen steadily year over year,](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/office-and-administrative-support/customer-service-representatives.htm#:~:text=What%20Customer%20Service%20Representatives%20Do,Similar%20Occupations) driven by wages, training, and turnover.


For home services companies, growth often means higher call center costs before it means higher profit.


## **For how many calls do you actually need a human team member?**


Most inbound calls in plumbing, HVAC, and roofing follow repeat patterns. Booking requests, reschedules, service availability checks, and basic questions make up the majority of call volume.


Call center studies show that 50–70% of inbound calls are routine and predictable.


Yet trained agents still handle these calls manually. That’s where cost inefficiency starts.


An[AI phone answering service](https://www.gosameday.com/industries/hvac) can take over these repetitive interactions, answering instantly, gathering job details, and routing only complex cases to human agents. This reduces call load without reducing coverage.


This is especially important because[home services businesses can’t afford downtime](https://www.gosameday.com/post/ai-phone-answering-why-home-service-businesses-cant-afford-downtime) when missed calls directly translate into lost jobs.


## **What makes booking calls so expensive?**


Booking is one of the most time-consuming call types. Agents ask questions, check schedules, confirm details, and update systems, all while keeping callers engaged.


[Research from McKinsey](https://www.mckinsey.com/~/media/mckinsey/industries/technology%20media%20and%20telecommunications/high%20tech/our%20insights/beyond%20the%20hype%20capturing%20the%20potential%20of%20ai%20and%20gen%20ai%20in%20tmt/beyond-the-hype-capturing-the-potential-of-ai-and-gen-ai-in-tmt.pdf) shows that automation can reduce call handling time by 30–50% for structured interactions like scheduling.


An AI booking system handles this flow automatically. It checks availability, books jobs, manages reschedules, and confirms appointments without agent involvement. That directly lowers cost per call.


This is exactly how[AI booking systems work for home services](https://www.gosameday.com/post/how-ai-booking-systems-work-for-home-services-the-complete-guide-to-call-automation) when integrated with existing calendars and dispatch tools.


### **The cost of after-hours calls**


After-hours coverage is expensive and hard to manage. Paying night staff or outsourcing answering services adds recurring cost, yet missed after-hours calls often include emergency jobs with high value.


[AI phone agents](https://www.gosameday.com/) answer calls 24/7 without shift costs. They capture emergency requests, book next-day jobs, and prevent leads from going to voicemail, without adding headcount.

## **How can companies lower costs without shrinking their team?**


Reducing call center costs can be done without letting go of staff. The key is removing repetitive tasks from agents’ daily work. When AI handles routine calls like appointment bookings, schedule changes, and basic service questions:


- Agents can focus on complex customer needs and high-value conversations.
- Fewer hires are required during peak seasons, reducing recruitment and training costs.
- Training time drops because staff spend less time learning to handle repetitive calls.
- Burnout decreases, which matters:[according to Gallup, turnover caused by burnout](https://www.gallup.com/workplace/247391/fixable-problem-costs-businesses-trillion.aspx) can cost businesses up to twice an employee’s salary.


Using AI to manage routine calls helps teams stay lean and effective, keeping service quality high without adding staff or stress.

Home services companies that implement[AI phone answering and booking systems](https://www.gosameday.com/) consistently see measurable benefits:

- **Better use of existing agents:** human staff focus on calls that actually require their judgment.


Instead of scaling call centers with headcount, businesses scale their operations with AI systems, improving efficiency and profitability simultaneously.
