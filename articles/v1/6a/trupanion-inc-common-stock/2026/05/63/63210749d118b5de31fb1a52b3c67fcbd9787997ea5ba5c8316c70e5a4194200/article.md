# World Cup Pets: More than 600 Pets Named after World Cup Soccer Players

# World Cup Pets: More than 600 Pets Named after World Cup Soccer Players


May 20, 2026


5 min read


From superstars like Lionel Messi and Cristiano Ronaldo to one-name icons like Pelé and Marta, World Cup soccer players have inspired hundreds of unique pet names.


Our team at[pet medical insurance provider Trupanion](https://www.trupanion.com/) found more than 620 pets who share a name with famous World Cup players.


## Pets Named after Famous Soccer / Futbol Players


More pets are named after David Beckham than any other past or present World Cup soccer player. Nearly 300 pets are named after the former English team captain whose ability to bend the ball was so famous it inspired movies.


Brazilian star Pelé has the second highest number of pets named after him followed by fellow Brazilian and Women’s World Cup star Marta.


Together, Lionel Messi and Cristiano Ronaldo have more than 70 pets named after them, with Neymar Jr., Mohamed Salah, Lothar Matthaus, Diego Maradona and Clint Dempsey rounding out the top 10.

## Pet Names Inspired by Soccer /Futbol Terms


World Cup players aren’t the only things that have inspired some soccer-related pet names.


More than 5,200 pets share a name with Canada’s World Cup mascot Maple the Moose. Nearly 300 pets are named after soccer positions. And while we didn’t find any pets named futbol, we did find six named Soccer and one named Offsides.
