# The Most Famous Movie Pets

# The Most Famous Movie Pets


August 20, 2025


5 min read


Look out Lassie. Sorry Sylvester. Toodles Toto. Marley and Milo are now the most famous movie pets.


Our team at Trupanion combed through our database of over 2 million pets and found that more pets are named after Marley and Milo than any other iconic movie pet.


Marley, from 2008's *Marley and Me* and Milo, from 1986's *Milo and Otis,* had some close competition from other famous pets though.


Milo narrowly beat out Benji ( *Benji* series) and Lady ( *Lady and the Tramp)* among dogs. While Oliver from 1988's *Oliver and Company* was right next to Milo.


Below is a look at the Top 10 most famous movie dogs and cats according to Trupanion members.
