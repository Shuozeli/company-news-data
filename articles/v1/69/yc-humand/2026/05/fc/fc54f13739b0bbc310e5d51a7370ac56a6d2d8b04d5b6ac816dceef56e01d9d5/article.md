# Work Smarter

At Humand, we believe AI is here to amplify the work of every person within the organization. That is why we are introducing new automation and analytics capabilities integrated directly into key modules across the platform: internal communications, talent acquisition, feedback, training, and document management. AI is arriving to streamline the most time-consuming, day-to-day HR processes.


### **Feed & Groups: Deeper Connections and Interactions**


Writing company-wide announcements takes time—from finding the right tone and summarizing updates to ensuring the message is crystal clear. To solve this, we are adding a new AI writing assistant that allows you to generate a first draft and adapt it instantly:


- Change the style of your message for different audiences. Whether you need it more professional, friendly, or corporate, Humand AI will adjust it.
- Summarize long texts for a more engaging read or expand on an idea by adding key details.
- Transform complex concepts into simple, straightforward text to ensure it is understood across the entire organization.


Our goal is to give you the peace of mind that comes with knowing every internal communication is clear, professional, and effective—without spending hours editing copy.


### **Learning: Course creation from a single Idea**


Structuring a training program from scratch requires time that HR teams don’t always have. Therefore, we are integrating AI into the module to help you avoid starting from a blank page and accelerate content development:


- Generate a complete curriculum proposal based on your topic, objective, and target audience.
- Design multiple-choice quizzes complete with answer rationales to reinforce knowledge retention.
- Adjust the tone, length, or focus of an individual lesson without affecting the rest of the course.


By bringing AI into the Learning module, our goal is to make developing internal training as simple as having a good idea.


### **Performance: Less prep Work, better feedback**


Closing a review cycle means processing hundreds of open-ended responses before you can deliver feedback. That is why, with a single click, AI will assist leaders by centralizing the employee’s context through features such as:


- Analyzing review forms and generating a baseline draft that is ready to review and edit.
- The summary appears directly on the manager’s dashboard, remaining fully editable before final delivery.


We want every leader to walk into feedback conversations with the exact context they need to make the discussion genuine and impactful.


### **Recruitment: More time for the right interviews**


Reviewing hundreds of resumes to find the right profiles consumes hours that recruiters cannot afford to lose. What will Humand AI do to simplify it?


- Cross-reference candidate information with job requirements.
- Automatically identify the profiles most aligned with the position under a “Featured” tag.
- Synthesize key candidate data for a quick read before the interview.
- Filter pre-qualified applications to advance them through stages or send multiple communications in a single step.


By delegating the initial screening to AI, recruiters gain the speed needed to respond to applicants faster and open up more space to truly connect with people during interviews.


### **Service Management: Streamlined approvals**


Submitting an internal request seems simple, but a single mistyped field can halt the entire process. Integrating AI into forms will offer key advantages:


- Read invoices, receipts, or certificates and fill out text fields automatically without manual data entry.
- Visual alerts that highlight which fields should be double-checked before submitting the request.
- Not every form needs AI. Administrators can configure this feature based on the specific needs of each department.


This ensures requests are submitted error-free, allowing managers and leaders to grant fast approvals.


### **Documents: The entire PDF workflow at Humand**


Distributing mass documentation across the organization is now as simple as uploading a file. The Documents module will incorporate key capabilities to optimize your time:


- Automatically separate a consolidated file into individual documents ready for delivery.
- Recognize each employee’s profile and match it with their corresponding file.


Handling your entire document workflow within Humand brings unparalleled order to your records while eliminating manual tasks.


### **Chat: Structured messages from voice notes**


Record. Review. Send. With this new feature, leaders will have key tools to optimize their communication directly within Chat:


- Transcribe and group audio into key topics using a clean, easy-to-read format.
- Expand, review, and fine-tune the draft before hitting send.
- Ensure that every idea reaches the team clearly, professionally, and in a structured manner.


This new feature ensures that every idea reaches the team clearly and professionally.


### **Real impact on daily operations**


Artificial Intelligence in the workplace has a practical goal: assisting with routine tasks so teams can maintain their focus on decision-making. These tools do not replace leadership judgment; instead, they act as technical support to optimize workflows.


Automating course creation, synthesizing voice notes, or bulk-processing files handles the mechanical, repetitive side of administration. By delegating these processes to the platform, teams can prioritize data analysis, employee touchpoints, and internal strategy.
