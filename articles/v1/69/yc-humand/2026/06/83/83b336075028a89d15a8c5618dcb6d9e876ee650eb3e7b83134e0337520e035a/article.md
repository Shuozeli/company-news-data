# Learning: Autonomous status management and precision in certification

The **Learning** module introduces new capabilities for status management and document retrieval, enabling agile administrative control tailored to your organization’s operational reality. These tools allow you to update training records and obtain proof of compliance instantly.


### **Why is it important?**


Efficient learning management requires a platform that responds to exceptional scenarios and complex organizational structures. The ability to manually complete courses and filter certificate downloads reduces HR’s operational workload. This ensures that training records are always precise and that the documentation required for audits is available without delay.


### **Control and efficiency: What’s new?**


#### **Manual adjustment of training records**


Administrators now have the authority to mark a course as completed on behalf of an employee.


- **Manual course completion:** With this update, you can mark training as completed on behalf of any employee.
- **Specific status overrides:** Manual completion is now available for incomplete courses currently in *In Progress* , *Pending* , or *Pending Signature* status.
- **Automatic grading:** When manually closing a course, quiz-type lessons are automatically marked complete with the highest available grade.


#### **Targeted certificate downloads**


Bulk certificate downloading now includes a pre-filtering step. You can select one or multiple segments (such as locations or business units) to receive only the specific documentation you need.


- **Segmented certificate downloads:** Filter by specific community groups before executing a bulk download.
- **Custom destination email:** Simplify sharing with third parties by entering any email address to receive the generated file.


### **Learning in action**


How do these improvements apply to daily operations?


- *“A group of employees completed in-person technical training, and you need their progress to reflect as approved in the system.”* **→** Use the **Complete** option from the course report to instantly update their profiles without requiring them to go through the digital modules.
- *“The Legal department urgently requests compliance certificates for a specific branch ahead of an inspection.”* **→** Apply the segment filter when downloading the certificates and enter the department head’s email to send them the file directly.


### **Take your training management to the next level**


These new tools streamline the operational control of training programs, making day-to-day learning processes more organized and simpler to manage. These features are already available in your admin panel to provide greater predictability and autonomy to your organization.
