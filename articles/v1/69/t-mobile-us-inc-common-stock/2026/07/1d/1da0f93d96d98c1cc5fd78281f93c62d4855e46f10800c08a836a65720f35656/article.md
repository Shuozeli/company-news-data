# Inside the Network: How T‑Mobile Is Helping Keep People Connected Across Host Cities (Updated July 1, 2026)

# July 1, 2026


### **Spotlight: Supporting First Responders in Dallas/Fort Worth**


While fans gather across North Texas for tournament matches and related festivities, T‑Mobile teams are also working alongside[public safety agencies](https://www.t-mobile.com/news/community/how-t-mobile-shows-up-for-first-responders-across-america) to help keep communities safe throughout the event.


Across the Dallas/Fort Worth market, we have invested in network enhancements, expanded capacity and operational readiness efforts designed to support increased demand at stadiums, fan gathering locations, transportation hubs and other high-traffic areas. At fan events,[Dynamic CX](https://www.t-mobile.com/news/network/t-mobile-introduces-dynamic-cx-ai-powered-technology-designed-to-help-keep-customers-connected-during-summers-biggest-live-events) is helping the network adapt to changing demand as crowds gather.


At the same time, our teams are coordinating with emergency management organizations, venue operators and public safety partners to support communications readiness, operational coordination and situational awareness throughout tournament operations. These efforts include support for Emergency Operations Centers (EOCs), transportation hubs, major venues and public gathering locations across the region.


One example is the City of Irving, which is serving as the central communications desk for activated EOCs across North Texas, including state and federal partners. Our teams are supporting public safety operations through a combination of communications solutions and emergency response resources. That includes staging a[SatCOLT](https://www.t-mobile.com/news/disaster-definitions) (Satellite Cell on Light Truck) at the Irving EOC and providing first responders with new devices featuring push-to-talk capabilities and T-Priority.


“We’ve had a great experience working with T‑Mobile,” said John Chaney, Chief Infrastructure & Communications Officer for the City of Irving. “The addition of satellite connectivity on these devices is a game changer. Knowing we’ll be able to stay connected and communicate even if something impacts the terrestrial network provides tremendous peace of mind for me and my fellow first responders.”


[T-Priority](https://www.t-mobile.com/t-priority) helps provide eligible first responders with priority and preemption on the T‑Mobile network during periods of heavy congestion, helping ensure critical communications get through when network demand is at its highest. It also includes[T-Satellite](https://www.t-mobile.com/coverage/satellite-phone-service?msockid=0810e24aa91a6d702536f4b6a8c36c54) connectivity, providing an added layer of resilience by enabling communications via satellite if terrestrial cellular coverage is unavailable. Together, these capabilities help first responders stay connected and coordinate effectively across a wide range of emergency scenarios.


This reflects our broader commitment to supporting the communications needs of first responders, emergency managers and public safety agencies helping support tournament activities across host cities.

# June 24, 2026


Matches are now underway across host cities throughout the United States as fans from around the world gather for one of the summer’s biggest sporting events.


Helping customers stay connected during large events this summer is something T‑Mobile has been preparing for well in advance. Across all 11 U.S. host cities, we’ve invested in expanded 5G infrastructure, enhanced venue connectivity, increased network capacity and operational readiness efforts designed to support increased demand at stadiums, transportation hubs, fan gathering locations and other high-traffic areas.


Our teams are helping support network readiness,[public safety coordination](https://www.t-mobile.com/news/community/how-t-mobile-shows-up-for-first-responders-across-america) ,[emergency response](https://www.t-mobile.com/news/disaster-definitions) planning and ongoing operational monitoring designed to help keep communities, visitors and responders connected throughout the tournament.


Over the next few weeks, we’ll take you behind the scenes of these efforts across different host cities. In this installment, we’re spotlighting our home region in the Pacific Northwest, where teams have been supporting connectivity and preparing for match activity in Seattle, one of the tournament’s key host markets.


### **Spotlight: Seattle Match Days By the Numbers**


As Seattle welcomed fans for its first two tournament matches and related festivities across the city, our network was already hard at work helping keep customers connected.


In preparation for tournament activity, T‑Mobile invested in expanded 5G infrastructure, enhanced venue connectivity and additional capacity across key locations including Lumen Field, Seattle Center, fan gathering locations and Seattle-Tacoma International Airport. During Seattle’s first two match days, those investments helped support connectivity across the city as fans traveled, gathered and celebrated.


Across select fan events and mass transit locations,[Dynamic CX](https://www.t-mobile.com/news/network/t-mobile-introduces-dynamic-cx-ai-powered-technology-designed-to-help-keep-customers-connected-during-summers-biggest-live-events) — our new AI-powered capability designed to help the network adapt in near real time as crowds gather and move through event areas — and other network optimization technologies helped manage changing demand throughout both match days. Together, the sites supporting those locations served more than 630,000 unique devices while maintaining an accessibility rate of approximately 99%, meaning the vast majority of connection attempts were successful.


“The best network experiences are often invisible to customers,” said Ankur Kapoor, Chief Network Officer, T‑Mobile. “As fans gathered across Seattle to experience the excitement surrounding the tournament, T‑Mobile’s network was automatically adapting thousands of times to help keep people connected.”


Behind the scenes, our[Self-Organizing Network](https://www.t-mobile.com/news/network/t-mobile-advances-stronger-smarter-network-for-americans) (SON) automatically executed more than 65,000 automated optimization actions to help maintain performance and alleviate congestion as demand shifted throughout the two match days. With more than 85% of traffic driven by video streaming and downloads, those adjustments helped support fans as they streamed, shared and consumed content throughout the events.


Seattle will continue hosting tournament matches throughout the summer, and we’ll share additional insights from future match days around the country as the tournament continues.
