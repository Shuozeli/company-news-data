# PowerHouse July Tip of the Month: Beat the heat with fans


Using fans is one of the simplest ways to stay comfortable as temperatures rise. By keeping air moving, fans help you feel cooler even with the thermostat set a little higher. That can reduce how often your air conditioner needs to run without sacrificing comfort.


Ceiling fans and portable fans both offer easy ways to cool. In the summer, set ceiling fans to spin counterclockwise to push cool air down. Placing a box or tower fan near a window can help circulate fresh air through your home, especially during cooler parts of the day. Small adjustments like these can make a noticeable difference in how your home feels.


Fans work best paired with other smart habits. Close blinds during the hottest parts of the day and limit heat from cooking or electronics to help keep indoor temperatures more manageable. Together, these changes can ease the strain on your cooling system and help you use less energy over time.
