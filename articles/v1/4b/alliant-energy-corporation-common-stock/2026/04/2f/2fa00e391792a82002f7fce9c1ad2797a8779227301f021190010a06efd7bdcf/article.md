# Teach kids to be ultimate energy savers


[Alliant Energy Kids](https://www.alliantenergykids.com/) is our website just for children and educators. It’s full of fun activities, lessons and videos for kids to learn all about energy.


We have a new game called Energy Savers Quest. The player must make smart decisions at home or school to reduce energy use and become the ultimate energy saver.


We recommend the home scenario for third to fifth graders and the more challenging school scenario for sixth to eighth graders.


Go to[alliantenergykids.com/foreducators/3-5](https://www.alliantenergykids.com/foreducators/3-5) to play this game.
