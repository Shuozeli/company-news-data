# Play it safe all summer long


This summer, whether you stay home and roast marshmallows in the backyard or travel for some rest and relaxation, make sure you know how to be safe around natural gas and electricity.


Visit[811beforeyoudig.com](https://811beforeyoudig.com/) at least a few days in advance if you plan projects that involve digging, like planting a tree or building a fence. Professionals will mark your yard for buried gas and other utility lines. It’s a free service that prevents serious injury and property damage and it’s the law.


Keep an eye out for overhead power lines outdoors flying kites or drones, working with ladders or tall equipment, or setting sail from a dock. Be sure to keep yourself and any objects at least 10 feet away from all electrical wires.


Have a plan for summer storms. Sign up for[notifications in My Account](https://www.alliantenergy.com/customer-service/account-notifications) to stay up to date on any outages. Visit our[storm safety webpage](https://www.alliantenergy.com/safety/storm-safety) for more tips to help see you through severe weather safely.
