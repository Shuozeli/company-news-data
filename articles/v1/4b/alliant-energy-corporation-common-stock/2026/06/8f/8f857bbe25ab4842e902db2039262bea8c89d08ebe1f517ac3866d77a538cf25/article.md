# Easy ways to save energy this summer


Everyday household routines can have a bigger impact on summer energy use than you think. As temperatures rise, cooling equipment has to run longer to keep you comfortable. Indoor activities that generate heat can make the job even harder.


Here are a few tips to help manage energy use:


- Try to only run heat-producing appliances like your oven, dishwasher and dryer in the cooler evening or early morning hours.
- Limit how long refrigerator and oven doors stay open to reduce wasted energy.
- Seal air leaks and check weatherstripping around doors and windows in the summer keep conditioned air inside.


With a little planning, small actions can add up to meaningful energy savings.
