# Nokia welcomes UK government's 5G diversification announcement

**08 December 2021**


Nokia welcomes the UK government’s announcement relating to the deployment of open and interoperable networks. Nokia has long been a champion of open networks and we are the leading contributor to the O-RAN ALLIANCE’s technical work. Open RAN technology has the potential to enrich the mobile ecosystem with new solutions and business models. Nokia has a long history in the UK, that stretches back many decades. We play a vital role in the delivery of the UK’s Critical National Infrastructure and we are active in our support for the UK government’s ambitions to provide resilience and security to the UK’s telecoms infrastructure. We will continue to play a leading role in supporting and shaping its future and we offer our support whenever, and wherever, it is needed to help the UK government deliver on its strategy.
