## Local language


**Doch**
In German, *doch* can be used to counter a negated statement. For example, if someone asks you Sie sprechen kein Deutsch, oder?


(You don’t speak German, do you?), you could respond with Doch!


(I do!)


**Prost**
To toast with German speakers, you can say Prost!


It’s considered polite to do so, though generally only with alcoholic drinks.


**Pfand**
Recycling is taken very seriously in Germany. That’s why if you buy a bottle or can at a store, you pay a small Pfand


(deposit). You can return empty containers at a Pfandautomat


(reverse vending machine) in supermarkets. If you’re unable to return them yourself, it’s common to place them next to public trash bins instead of inside.
