## **3. Arizona's Climate & Your Home**


Before moving to Arizona, it’s essential to understand the unique climate and how it’ll impact you as a resident in terms of challenges and opportunities. From the heat, sunlight, and monsoons, think about how this may affect energy and landscaping.


### **Heat and sun**


Living in Arizona means you’ll experience a desert climate, so if scorching heat and intense sunlight aren’t your cup of tea, you may want to think twice about moving to Arizona. An abundance of heat and sunlight may also impact your home’s materials and surfaces, as well as energy consumption. You always want to be comfortable in your own home, which means ensuring that your home is well equipped to stay cool despite prolonged sun exposure.


### **Energy efficiency**


Living a lifestyle that’s more energy efficient will benefit you in the long run. By incorporating a few key strategies, you can create a highly energy-efficient home that keeps you cool and saves you money on utility bills. Here's how:


- **Harness the Power of the Sun:** Solar panels are a smart investment in Arizona's abundant sunshine. You'll slash your electric bills and enjoy the satisfaction of generating clean energy for your home.
- **Seal the Deal with Superior Insulation:** Look for Home builders like us, who prioritize air sealing and high-performance insulation materials. This keeps the cool air in and the scorching heat out.
- **Smarter Cooling with Smart Thermostats:** Smart thermostats are your secret weapon for efficient cooling. These programmable thermostats let you adjust temperatures based on your schedule and preferences. They can even learn your habits and automatically create a comfortable environment while minimizing energy use.
- **Landscape for Shade and Cooling:** Strategic landscaping goes a long way in Arizona. Plant shade trees near your south-facing walls to block the sun's harsh rays. Consider drought-resistant groundcovers and shrubs that reflect sunlight instead of absorbing it. This will create a cooler microclimate around your home and reduce your reliance on air conditioning.


By implementing these strategies, you'll create a more comfortable, energy-efficient home, save money on your utility bills, and reduce your environmental impact. Win-win-win!

### **Water conservation**


Conserving water is a must in a desert landscape! Here are a few ideas to get you started:


- **Low-flow fixtures** : Minimize water usage without compromising functionality!
- **Drought-resistant plants:** If you’re not good at remembering to water your plants, you’re in luck! Opt for drought-resistant plants to reduce excessive watering and promote water conservation.
- **Rainwater harvesting** : Collecting rainwater is a great sustainable practice. Just keep a couple of buckets in the corner of your backyard, and you can use your collection for watering plants, washing the car, outdoor cleaning, or filling up a bird bath.


### **Monsoon preparedness**


Water damage can pose a real threat to homes, and without being prepared, you may end up having to repair many parts of your home due to flooding, which can be costly.


- **Drainage systems:** Prevent water accumulation around your home during heavy rainfalls.
- **Flood mitigation strategies:** Elevate vulnerable areas using flood barriers to help mitigate the impact of flash floods. Looking into flood insurance may be a beneficial addition to taking care of your home.
- **Storm protection:** Implement storm-resistant features to safeguard your home from monsoon-related damage.
