# K. Hovnanian® Homes Celebrates NVBIA Recognition

On October 8, 2025, our[Mid-Atlantic Division](https://www.khov.com/new-construction-homes/virginia/mid-atlantic/) was honored at the Northern Virginia Building Industry Association’s (NVBIA) Great American Living Awards. While receiving recognition from industry peers is always encouraging, what truly matters to us is what these awards reveal about the hard work, dedication, and lessons learned along the way.


## **What the GALA Awards Mean to Us**


These awards aren’t just about trophies or titles. They’re judged by industry experts who understand the challenges and complexities of homebuilding. When they recognize our work, it’s a nod to the countless hours spent designing floorplans that fit real lifestyles, crafting welcoming exteriors, and paying attention to the little details that make a house feel like home.


As Chris McGrath, our Mid-Atlantic Division President, said, this recognition reflects the commitment and talent of our teams. But more importantly, it reminds us that building homes is about people — about you and your family’s future.


## **A Special Honor: Lifetime Achievement Award**


One of the most meaningful moments of the evening was when Gary Chandler, our Region Senior Vice President of Land Acquisitions & Entitlements, received the Lifetime Achievement Award and was inducted into the NVBIA Hall of Fame.


Gary’s expertise in identifying land opportunities and managing complex entitlement processes has been crucial in shaping thriving communities across[Maryland](https://www.khov.com/new-construction-homes/maryland/) ,[Virginia](https://www.khov.com/new-construction-homes/virginia/) , and[West Virginia](https://www.khov.com/new-construction-homes/west-virginia/) .
