# Academy Sports + Outdoors to Open Three New Stores in Q2

*Retailer continues to expand its national footprint while remaining invested in local communities*


KATY, Texas


, May 19, 2026


/PRNewswire/ --[Academy Sports + Outdoors](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4691357-1&h=2161848373&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.academy.com%2F%3Futm_medium%3Dpr%26utm_source%3DPressRelease%26utm_campaign%3DNewStore%26utm_content%3DFY25Q1%26utm_term%3DHomepage&a=Academy+Sports+%2B+Outdoors) ("Academy" or the "Company") ([Nasdaq:](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4691357-1&h=346328278&u=https%3A%2F%2Finvestors.academy.com%2F&a=Nasdaq%3A+ASO)ASO ), a leading full-line sporting goods and outdoor recreation retailer, is excited to announce it will open three new locations this quarter. The first store will open in[Altoona, Pa.](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4691357-1&h=3483828768&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.academy.com%2Fstorelocator%2Fpennsylvania%2Faltoona%2Fstore-0398&a=Altoona%2C+Pa.) ; which marks the third store in the state, followed by two new Tennessee locations in[North Knoxville, Tenn.](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4691357-1&h=367264690&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.academy.com%2Fstorelocator%2Ftennessee%2Fknoxville%2Fstore-0408&a=North+Knoxville%2C+Tenn.) and[Morristown, Tenn](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4691357-1&h=3075958942&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.academy.com%2Fstorelocator%2Ftennessee%2Fmorristown%2Fstore-0417&a=Morristown%2C+Tenn) . The openings build on momentum from[two new store openings in Q1](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4691357-1&h=1668821792&u=https%3A%2F%2Finvestors.academy.com%2Fnews-releases%2Fnews-release-details%2Facademy-sports-outdoors-grows-retail-footprint-two-new-locations&a=two+new+store+openings+in+Q1) , reinforcing Academy's ongoing commitment to strategic long-term growth. With each new location, Academy remains focused on serving local communities with a broad assortment of high-quality gear at everyday value.

**2026 Q2 New Store Opening Locations**


[Altoona, Pennsylvania](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4691357-1&h=2943635053&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.academy.com%2Fstorelocator%2Fpennsylvania%2Faltoona%2Fstore-0398&a=Altoona%2C+Pennsylvania)


[North Knoxville, Tennessee](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4691357-1&h=2623027588&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.academy.com%2Fstorelocator%2Ftennessee%2Fknoxville%2Fstore-0408&a=North+Knoxville%2C+Tennessee)


[Morristown, Tennessee](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4691357-1&h=4260793009&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.academy.com%2Fstorelocator%2Ftennessee%2Fmorristown%2Fstore-0417&a=Morristown%2C+Tennessee)


Academy plans to celebrate each new store by donating $15,000 to support more than 60 children through local non-profit organizations including Big Brothers Big Sisters of Blair County, Boys & Girls Clubs of the Tennessee Valley and Boys & Girls Club of Morristown. Each location will host a grand opening weekend featuring exclusive deals and giveaways, a ribbon cutting ceremony, and fun for the entire family. The Altoona grand opening weekend will be marked with a meet-and-greet experience featuring collegiate quarterback Rocco Becht. Fans of the football star can expect to get their gear autographed and snap a photo with Becht.


In pursuit of becoming the best sports and outdoors retailer in the country, Academy opened a total of[24 new stores across 16 states in 2025](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4691357-1&h=2301394961&u=https%3A%2F%2Finvestors.academy.com%2Fnews-releases%2Fnews-release-details%2Facademy-sports-outdoors-opens-five-new-stores-q4-taking-new&a=24+new+stores+across+16+states+in+2025) , including its first locations in Maryland and Pennsylvania. Academy's expansion efforts resulted in[16 new stores across 10 different states](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4691357-1&h=2134555288&u=https%3A%2F%2Finvestors.academy.com%2Fnews-releases%2Fnews-release-details%2Facademy-sports-outdoors-expands-footprint-five-new-stores%3Futm_medium%3Dpr%26utm_source%3DPressRelease%26utm_campaign%3DNewStore%26utm_content%3DFY25Q1%26utm_term%3DFY24NewStores&a=16+new+stores+across+10+different+states) in 2024.


Academy's store growth is supported by continued investments that enhance the customer experience, including the recent rollout of the new[myAcademy Rewards Mastercard® Credit Card, and enhanced myAcademy Rewards program](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4691357-1&h=2217767245&u=https%3A%2F%2Finvestors.academy.com%2Fnews-releases%2Fnews-release-details%2Facademy-sports-outdoors-launches-new-tiered-myacademyr-rewards&a=myAcademy+Rewards+Mastercard%C2%AE+Credit+Card%2C+and+enhanced+myAcademy+Rewards+program) , providing cardmembers and loyal customers with exclusive benefits and ways to save. Together, these initiatives reinforce Academy's commitment to delivering unbeatable value both in-store and beyond.


Every Academy store is a fun destination where families can find apparel, footwear, sports, hiking and camping equipment, hunting and fishing gear, outdoor cooking, and more from top national brands at an everyday value. Hunting and fishing licenses are also available to purchase in stores.

**About Academy Sports + Outdoors** Academy is a leading full-line sporting goods and outdoor recreation retailer in the United States. Originally founded in 1938 as a family business in Texas, Academy has grown to more than 300 stores across 21 states and counting. Academy's mission is to provide "Fun for All" and Academy fulfills this mission with a localized merchandising strategy and value proposition that strongly connects with a broad range of consumers. Academy's product assortment focuses on key categories of outdoor, apparel, sports & recreation and footwear through both leading national brands and a portfolio of private label brands.
