# Academy Sports + Outdoors Announces Participation in Upcoming Investor Conference

KATY, Texas


, April 2, 2026


/PRNewswire/ --[Academy Sports + Outdoors](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4655243-1&h=2480939277&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.academy.com%2F&a=Academy+Sports+%2B+Outdoors) ("Academy" or the "Company") ([Nasdaq:](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4655243-1&h=3189197889&u=https%3A%2F%2Finvestors.academy.com%2F&a=Nasdaq%3A+ASO)ASO ), a leading full-line sporting goods and outdoor recreation retailer, today announced its participation in the upcoming J.P. Morgan Retail Roundup Conference to be held in New York, New York from April 8, 2026 to April 9, 2026. Chief Executive Officer, Steve Lawrence, and Chief Financial Officer, Carl Ford, will participate in a fireside chat that will be webcast live on April 8, 2026, at 2:00 p.m. Eastern Time. The live webcast can be accessed through[this link](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4655243-1&h=733497805&u=https%3A%2F%2Fjpmorgan.metameetings.net%2Fevents%2Fretail26%2Fsessions%2F318619-academy-sports-outdoors-inc%2Fwebcast%2Fpublic&a=this+link) .


A live and replay webcast of the fireside chat will be made available for approximately 30 days on the Company's investor relations website at[https://investors.academy.com/](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4655243-1&h=1812905705&u=https%3A%2F%2Finvestors.academy.com%2F&a=https%3A%2F%2Finvestors.academy.com%2F) .


**About Academy Sports + Outdoors** Academy is a leading full-line sporting goods and outdoor recreation retailer in the United States. Originally founded in 1938 as a family business in Texas, Academy has grown to more than 300 stores across 21 states and counting. Academy's mission is to provide "Fun for All" and Academy fulfills this mission with a localized merchandising strategy and value proposition that strongly connects with a broad range of consumers. Academy's product assortment focuses on key categories of outdoor, apparel, sports & recreation and footwear through both leading national brands and a portfolio of private label brands. For more information, visit[www.academy.com](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4655243-1&h=1525130187&u=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.academy.com%2F&a=www.academy.com) .
