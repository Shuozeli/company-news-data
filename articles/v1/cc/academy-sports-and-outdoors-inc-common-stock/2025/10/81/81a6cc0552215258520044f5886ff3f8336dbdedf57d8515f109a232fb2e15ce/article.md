# Academy Sports + Outdoors Celebrates "Brooks Month" with Exclusive Launch of Hero Collection

*Academy continues growth of performance running assortment for athletes at all stages of training*


KATY, Texas


, Oct. 7, 2025


/PRNewswire/ -- Building upon its extensive lineup of performance running shoes from[Brooks](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4526863-1&h=2898486505&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.academy.com%2Fsearch%3FsearchTerm%3Dbrooks%26search_source%3Dtype_ahead&a=Brooks) , including trending styles for men and women,[Academy Sports + Outdoors](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4526863-1&h=849647144&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.academy.com%2F%3Futm_medium%3Dpr%26utm_source%3DPressRelease%26utm_campaign%3DNewStore%26utm_content%3DFY25Q1%26utm_term%3DHomepage&a=Academy+Sports+%2B+Outdoors) ("Academy" or the "Company") ([Nasdaq:](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4526863-1&h=2799381195&u=https%3A%2F%2Finvestors.academy.com%2F&a=Nasdaq%3A+ASO)ASO ), a leading full-line sporting goods and outdoor recreation retailer, is celebrating "Brooks Month" all October-long. Adding to the lineup of Brooks performance running shoes available in-store and online at Academy.com are the exclusive new Hero Collection as well as the all-silver Adrenaline GTS 25 performance sneaker.


The Hero Collection, which recognizes the influence of mentors in our communities, launches on October 10 and will offer a variety of colorways and price points in men's and women's styles:


- Two NEW colorways offered in the[Women's Ghost 17](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4526863-1&h=3994713927&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.academy.com%2Fc%2Fbrands%2Fbrooks&a=Women%27s+Ghost+17) performance shoe available for $149.99
- One NEW colorway offered in the[Men's Revel 8](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4526863-1&h=1536895690&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.academy.com%2Fc%2Fbrands%2Fbrooks&a=Men%27s+Revel+8) performance shoe available for $99.99


To bring this message to life, Academy and Brooks will donate a percentage of the proceeds to Marathon Kids, a nonprofit helping young people build healthy habits through movement and mentorship.


In addition,[myAcademy members](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4526863-1&h=2222250564&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.academy.com%2Fmyacademy-rewards&a=myAcademy+members) can enter for a chance to win a pair of the limited-edition Adrenaline GTS 25 Silver Edition shoes starting Monday, October 20*, with the shoes officially dropping in select Academy stores and online at[Academy.com](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4526863-1&h=893502970&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.academy.com%2F&a=Academy.com) on Saturday, November 1. The all-silver Adrenaline GTS performance sneaker is in celebration of 25 years of the Adrenaline GTS shoe and features a metallic finish.


"Performance running shoes aren't just for the track or on the road anymore. More runners are leaning into their shoes as a way to express their personal style, while also achieving top-tier results," said Academy Sports + Outdoors Executive Vice President and Chief Merchandising Officer Matt McCabe. "With the launch of Brooks' Hero Collection exclusively at Academy, we're excited to offer athletes a new way to get their miles in."
