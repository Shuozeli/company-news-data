# Academy Sports + Outdoors Opens Five New Stores in Q4, Taking New Store Total to 24 in Fiscal 2025

*With these locations, Academy strengthens presence in Arkansas, Indiana, and Texas*


KATY, Texas


, Nov. 18, 2025
