## What's new: 5% cash back on home, kitchen, and office


[Off to College shopping](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/amazon-prime-savings-back-to-school) just got more rewarding. Starting today through September 15, Prime for Young Adults members will earn 5% automatic cash back across three new categories to get move-in ready: home, kitchen, and office. Because dorm rooms don't furnish themselves, and neither do first apartments.


For the home, that means the products that turn a blank room into your room, like air purifiers, bedding, decor, and storage solutions from brands like Bissell, Dyson, Shark, and Levoit. For the kitchen, it's the coffee maker that gets you through 8 a.m. lectures and the air fryer to supplement a dining plan, with essentials from Ninja, Owala, Brita, Keurig, and Breville. For the office category, it's the tools behind the GPA: calculators, printers, notebooks, and desk accessories from Texas Instruments, HP, Amazon Basics, and Sharpie.


These categories join the existing lineup of beauty, apparel, electronics, PCs, and personal care, eligible for cash back every day, giving Prime for Young Adults members the broadest cash back selection since the program launched.


## How cash back rewards work


Prime for Young Adults members automatically earn 5% cash back on the final price. After an order arrives with Prime's fast, free delivery, members can head to the


[Amazon Prime webpage](https://www.amazon.com/amazonprime?primeCampaignId=studentWlpPrimeRedir) to sign in and check the cash back balance at the top of the page. A minimum balance of $5 is required to redeem. Once redeemed, cash back can be applied at checkout on most purchases, excluding gift cards, digital content, and Prime membership fees. Cash back cannot be applied retroactively and expires 180 days after it is earned.


Cash back rates and eligible categories vary throughout the year, with enhanced earning opportunities during major shopping events. Earnings are capped at $100 for one purchase and $500 in one year. Members can check cash back expiration date on the Amazon young adult page.
