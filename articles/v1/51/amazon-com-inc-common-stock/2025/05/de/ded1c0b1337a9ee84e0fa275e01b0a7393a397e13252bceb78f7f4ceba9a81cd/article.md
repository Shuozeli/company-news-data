# Claude Opus 5 now available on AWS

---


## Key takeaways


- Claude Opus 5 is Anthropic's most advanced Opus model for coding and knowledge work.


- The model powers agents that can run for hours with minimal oversight.


- Opus 5 is available with zero data retention on AWS.


---


On July 24,


[Amazon Web Services (AWS)](https://www.aboutamazon.com/amazon-aws-news) announced the availability of


[Anthropic's Claude Opus 5](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/introducing-claude-opus-5-on-aws-anthropics-most-capable-opus-model/) on


[Amazon Bedrock](https://aws.amazon.com/bedrock/) and


[Claude Platform on AWS](https://aws.amazon.com/claude-platform/) , the first Opus model in the fifth generation. According to Anthropic, Opus 5 matches top-tier intelligence benchmarks at Opus-tier pricing, with advances in agentic coding, knowledge work, visual understanding, and multi-step agents.


[Claude Fable 5 from Anthropic now available on AWS](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/aws/claude-fable-5-anthropic-available-amazon-bedrock)


Anthropic's 5th-generation AI model makes Mythos-level capabilities available to all customers and shows exceptional performance in coding, knowledge work, and vision—now available on Amazon Bedrock and Claude Platform on AWS.


## What's new in Claude Opus 5


- Agentic coding


—understands and navigates codebases like an experienced engineer, and writes production-quality code while pivoting and adapting its strategy as it works.


- Long-running tasks


—works dependently for hours, finding paths around obstacles, recovering from errors, and effectively reaching objectives.


- Knowledge work


—offers deeper reasoning on long documents and higher accuracy with measurably stronger retrieval and analysis than Claude Opus 4.8.


- Agent and workflow automation


—breaks complex jobs into sub-agents requiring less oversight.


## Getting started


You can get started with Claude Opus 5 in the


[Amazon Bedrock console](https://console.aws.amazon.com/bedrock/?trk=d8ec3b19-0f37-4f8c-8c12-189f913e205c&sc_channel=el) . Access is also available through


[Claude Platform on AWS](https://aws.amazon.com/claude-platform/) with unified AWS billing and authentication.

[Anthropic's Claude in Amazon Bedrock](https://aws.amazon.com/bedrock/claude/) product page for more detailed information on
