# Everything you need to know about Amazon Business and how it helps 11 million organizations save time and money

This post was originally published on April 13, 2023 and updated on July 21, 2026, to reflect Amazon Business's latest milestones.


---


## Key takeaways


- Amazon Business reached $60 billion in annualized gross sales in Q2 2026, now serving more than 11 million organizations worldwide.


- More than 1.8 million new organizations joined Amazon Business in the first half of 2026, with customers including hundreds of thousands of small businesses and 97 of the Fortune 100 companies.


- Amazon Business-specific discounts saved organizations more than $1 billion worldwide last year, while Prime Business members saved more than $880 million in shipping fees globally.


---


Amazon has spent more than 30 years building Amazon.com. It serves many customers with a vast selection of items at


[everyday low prices](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/amazon-orders-savings-discounts-shopping) with convenient delivery—and you may be one of them.


But business customers have different needs.


[Amazon Business now delivers fresh groceries to businesses in more than 2,300 cities](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/amazon-business-fresh-grocery-same-day-delivery)


Business customers can order fresh produce, dairy, baked goods, and frozen food alongside everyday essentials—all in one cart with Same-Day Delivery.


Amazon has long known that organizations were shopping for office supplies and other items in bulk on its store. To better serve them, the company launched Amazon Business in 2015.


“When organizations source with Amazon Business, they're not just shopping—they're streamlining their supply chain while saving time and money through powerful tools designed specifically for business needs,” said Shelley Salomon, worldwide vice president of Amazon Business. “As organizations look to do more with less, we're inventing new ways to help them save time and money—and reinvest in growing their business.”


Now in its 11th year of transforming business buying, Amazon Business has become one of the fastest-growing ventures in Amazon's history. In Q2 2026, Amazon Business surpassed $60 billion in annualized gross sales and now serves more than 11 million organizations worldwide, with more than 1.8 million new organizations joining in the first half of 2026 alone.


“I’ve been excited about Amazon Business since the day it launched,” said Doug Herrington, CEO of Amazon Worldwide Stores. “The Amazon Business team is committed to the long-term success of their business and its customers. Day in and day out, we dig into the specific purchasing needs of businesses and organizations and come up with innovative ways to deliver great prices, extensive selection, and consistent, reliable convenience. Businesses and organizations need different tools and features for their purchasing, and Amazon Business has built those tools. But we know that’s not enough, and we’ll keep iterating to make the customer experience even better.”


[What is Amazon Prime Business? Your questions answered](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/amazon-business-prime)


The membership fit for your business offers fast, free shipping and other awesome benefits.


Despite the progress and growth of Amazon Business, the energy inside the team makes it feel like a startup with a lot of room to expand further. Since launching in the U.S. in 2015, Amazon Business has grown to serve organizations across 11 countries—from small businesses to the world's largest companies, including 97 of the Fortune 100. Amazon Business tailors the best of Amazon—everyday low prices, vast selection, and a convenient shopping and delivery experience—along with offering a diverse supplier network, reliable and adaptable global logistics network, and intuitive AI-powered data analytics tools to meet the needs of organizations of all sizes.


Organizations including Citi, United Service Organizations (USO), Marriott International, and Carnival Corporation rely on Amazon Business to support their evolving needs.


“Amazon Business has made it easier for our teams to get what they need, when they need it,” said Scott Thompson, vice president, logistics, North America, at Carnival Corporation. “Procurement tasks that used to take hours—searching across vendors, routing approvals, reordering supplies—can now be done in minutes. It's a better experience for our team members, and we're looking forward to building on that with Amazon Business as we find new ways to improve how we work.”


Amazon Business also delivers value, selection, and convenience to organizations in the following ways:


- Amazon Business-specific discounts saved organizations more than $1 billion worldwide last year. Organizations can also save through Subscribe & Save for recurring purchases and private brands products like Amazon Basics that give businesses quality selection at low prices.


- Business customers now have access to hundreds of millions of products worldwide, 30% more items globally compared to last year, with access to specialized categories like industrial and scientific supplies, professional-grade equipment, health care and laboratory materials, education resources and technology, and strong selection growth in categories like repair tools, fresh groceries, and office furniture.


- Leveraging Amazon's fulfillment network to meet the unique delivery needs of business customers, Amazon Business made more than half a billion deliveries worldwide last year. In 13 states across the U.S., Amazon Business started rolling out dedicated, branded delivery trucks built for how businesses receive deliveries—from loading docks and office buildings to university campuses and mailrooms.


- Last year, Prime Business members saved more than $880 million globally in shipping fees, and this year, Prime Business members are also enjoying Amazon’s fastest delivery speeds yet.


- Amazon Business continues to expand the Prime Business program with benefits that support how organizations operate day to day. Amazon Quick was added to the growing list of Prime Business benefits, giving members discounted access to the personal AI assistant changing how people work. Amazon Quick joins the growing list of benefits Amazon Business recently introduced with CrowdStrike, Gusto, and QuickBooks, which help members manage cybersecurity, payroll, and accounting alongside their purchasing. Together, these expanded benefits can help small- and medium-sized businesses save nearly $1,100 per year.


- Amazon Business has introduced new AI-powered tools to help organizations discover savings and make smarter purchasing decisions.
