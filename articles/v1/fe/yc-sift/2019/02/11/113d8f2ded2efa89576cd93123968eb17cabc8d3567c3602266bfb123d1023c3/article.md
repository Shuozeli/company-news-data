# [Sift Named On Built In’s “Best Places to Work” Award List](https://engineering.sift.com/sift-named-built-ins-best-places-work-award-list/)


Sift


• February 26, 2019


Sift is excited to announce that we’ve been named one of Built In Seattle’s 2019 Best Places to Work! This win means a lot to us, since we care deeply about our team and our 2nd hometown.


When we opened our Seattle office just 2 years ago, we had big dreams of creating an excellent workspace and collaborative group. Our CEO and Co-Founder – Jason Tan – is a Washington native, and we’re proud to receive such an award in the PNW.


##### Join us!


Many thanks to our team for creating such an outstanding place to work. And we’re hiring! If you’re looking to join a great team, we’d love to[hear from you](https://sift.com/careers) !
