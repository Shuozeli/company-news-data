# BTQ Technologies 2024 Internship Program: Building the Future of Post-Quantum Security

BTQ Technologies Corp., a leading innovator in post-quantum infrastructure, invites passionate undergraduates and graduate students to join our internship program in 2024. Embark on a stimulating journey alongside our industry-leading experts as you delve into the cutting edge of post-quantum solutions, shaping the future of digital security.


**Who are we?**


BTQ was founded by a group of post-quantum cryptographers with an interest in addressing the urgent threat that a large-scale universal quantum computer poses to digital security. We're paving the way towards a more secure and efficient digital future through a range of post quantum products, from custom-built languages and compilers to cloud-accessible software solutions and dedicated hardware.


**What will you do?**


During your internship, you'll have the unique opportunity to:


-


Contribute to real-world research projects: Immerse yourself in exciting initiatives across various PQC domains, including:


-


Post-quantum cryptography signature scheme research: Explore novel designs for quantum-resistant digital signatures, the essential building blocks for secure authentication.


-


Zero-knowledge proof research: Design and analyze privacy-preserving protocols that demonstrate the validity of information without revealing any sensitive details.


-


Proof-of-work by quantum sampling: Investigate groundbreaking solutions for secure consensus mechanisms in blockchain technology using quantum mechanics.


-


PQScale (an aggregate signature API tool): Contribute to the development and enhancement of our flagship PQC toolkit, empowering diverse applications with scalable and efficient post-quantum signatures.


-


Gain diverse practical experience: Get hands-on with practical implementations of PQC algorithms and protocols, working with real-world data and utilizing cutting-edge hardware platforms.


-


Be part of a vibrant community: Immerse yourself in BTQ's collaborative and intellectually stimulating environment, interacting with leading experts, researchers, and engineers.


-


Shape the future of cryptography: Your contributions will directly impact BTQ's groundbreaking research and development, shaping the future of secure communications and transactions in the quantum age.


**Who should apply?**


We seek enthusiastic and talented individuals with:


-


A strong background in mathematics, computer science, or related fields.


-


Courses/experience in cryptography, distributed systems, and/or formal verification


-


Coding experience in one or more languages – Python/C/C++/Java/Go/Rust


-


Experience in advanced cryptography (e.g. MPC, zero-knowledge proofs, threshold signature)


-


Plus: published research papers in reputable cryptography journals and conferences


-


Preferable internship period: 6 -12 months


**How to apply:**


To join BTQ's Internship Program, please submit your application through email tocareers@btq.com :


-


CV or resume


-


Academic transcripts


-


Cover letter expressing your interest and relevant skills


-


Optional: Links to any research projects or code repositories or GitHub


Join us and be at the forefront of the quantum revolution!


BTQ Technologies is an international company, with research and development hubs in Vancouver, Canada, Taipei, Taiwan, and Sydney, Australia. We welcome applicants from around the world to apply for internships at any of our locations. Your chosen location will depend on the specific project you're matched with, offering you the chance to experience exciting new cultures and broaden your global network within the PQC community. So, whether you're drawn to the vibrant tech scene of Vancouver, the bustling energy of Taipei, or the sunny shores of Sydney, BTQ invites you to explore the world of post-quantum cryptography with us!


BTQ Technologies offers a competitive internship program with a stipend, travel allowance, and opportunities for continued collaboration and future employment. We believe in fostering the next generation of cryptographers and are committed to providing an enriching and empowering experience for our interns.


Don't miss this chance to shape the future of cybersecurity!Apply today and be a part of BTQ's journey towards a quantum-secure tomorrow.
