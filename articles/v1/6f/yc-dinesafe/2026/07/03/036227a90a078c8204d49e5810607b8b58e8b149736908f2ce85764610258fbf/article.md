# [Iwaspoisoned to Join NEMIS Academy Webinar on Using Real-Time Consumer Intelligence to Strengthen Food Safety](https://iwaspoisoned.com/news/iwaspoisoned-to-join-nemis-academy-webinar-on-using-real-time-consumer-intelligence-to-strengthen-food-safety)


Oct 20, 2025


IWaspoisoned looks forward to joining the upcoming webinar on Wednesday, November 26th, 2025, at 11 a.m. EST, hosted by NEMIS Academy. The talk titled "From Anecdotal to Actionable: Using Real-Time Consumer Intelligence to Strengthen Food Safety" will cover the topic of leveraging consumer-reported data as a real-time indicator of food safety risk.


NEMIS Academy is tailored for professionals involved in food quality control and quality management in food safety within the industry. It provides a range of webinars and workshops designed especially for the food industry community.


**Key Learning Outcomes from Webinar:**


- Learn how to translate consumer-reported data into a predictive early warning system for your organization.
- Identify the full spectrum of food safety risks detectable through crowdsourcing, including physical contaminants and undeclared allergens.
- Develop actionable metrics using consumer intelligence to justify food safety investments and benchmark performance for management.
- Master the framework for integrating real-time data streams into a modern, proactive food safety program.


We encourage all food industry professionals, food safety managers, manufacturers, retailers, and policymakers to join this insightful[webinar](https://register.gotowebinar.com/register/5136660390390221399) .
