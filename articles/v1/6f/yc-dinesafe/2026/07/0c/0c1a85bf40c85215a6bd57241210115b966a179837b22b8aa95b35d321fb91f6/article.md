# [Iwaspoisoned Participates in Predictably Good Webinar on Early Warning Signal & Predictive Analytics in Outbreak](https://iwaspoisoned.com/news/iwaspoisoned-participates-in-predictably-good-webinar)


May 7, 2026


Iwaspoisoned recently participated in a podcast episode of Predictably Good webinar a Creme Global series episode with Patrick Quade, on April 20th, 2026 and hosted by Cronan McNamara, where they explored the evolving role of data and technology in modern food safety systems.


During the discussion, Patrick Quade, Founder & CEO of Iwaspoisoned, shared how real-time consumer reporting and predictive analytics are reshaping the way foodborne illness outbreaks are identified and managed. Drawing from his journey of turning a personal experience into a global early-warning platform, he highlighted how crowdsourced data can complement traditional surveillance methods and significantly accelerate response times.


Creme Global founded with the vision of combining science with real-world application through innovative solutions, developed advanced tools and services that enable industry and regulators to make better data-based decisions.


**Key topics discussed included:**


- How real-time data can act as an early signal for food safety risks
- The role of consumer insight in strengthening public health response
- Why faster, more informed decision-making matters now more than ever


At Iwaspoisoned, we remain committed to advancing food safety through innovative technology, real-time reporting, and data transparency. By empowering consumers and supporting public health agencies with actionable insights, we continue to help build a safer, more responsive global food system.
