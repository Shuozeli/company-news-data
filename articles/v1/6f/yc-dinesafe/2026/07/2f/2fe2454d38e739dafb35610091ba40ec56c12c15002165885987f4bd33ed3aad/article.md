**Leveraging Global Crowdsourcing to Enhance Food Safety**
Iwaspoisoned.com natively supports 7 languages and has collected reports from over 170 countries, establishing itself as the premier platform for consumer food safety. This global reach enables individuals around the world to share their food safety experiences.


By crowdsourcing consumer food safety experiences, new insights emerge, enriching our understanding of the evolving food safety landscape. This approach captures real-time, personal narratives that offer direct insight into issues potentially overlooked by conventional data-gathering techniques, thereby strengthening food safety practices.


**Mapping the Global Reach of Consumer Reports Received** ****
The worldwide coverage of Iwaspoisoned.com illustrates how each report submitted by consumers—whether it concerns a dining establishment or a product bought from a grocery store—enhances the understanding of global food safety trends. It provides essential, timely observations on outbreaks, product concerns, evolving food safety patterns, and more.


**Partnership and Collaboration Network**
We are partnered with more than 400 public health agencies—learn about our collaborative efforts[here](https://iwaspoisoned.com/public-health-environmental-health-epidemiology-partnerships#scroll) . These partnerships, along with the global consumer reports we receive, an extensive regulatory database, and more, provide us and other players in the food industry with detailed, real-time overviews of food safety incidents and evolving trends. This all improves and elevates food safety management practices.


**Dedication to Food Safety and Awareness**
By sharing insights on food safety and the impact of crowdsourcing at numerous conferences, educational gatherings, and symposiums, we deepen our connections with consumers, public health entities, and their communities. Our engagement spans over 50 international events, where we've had the opportunity to inform and learn from public health departments, county health authorities, food safety experts, environmental health professionals, and others. Discover more about our previous and future activities[here](https://iwaspoisoned.com/events#scroll) . Our efforts reflect our continuous commitment to not only improving food safety trends, but keeping communities educated on such topics, worldwide.
