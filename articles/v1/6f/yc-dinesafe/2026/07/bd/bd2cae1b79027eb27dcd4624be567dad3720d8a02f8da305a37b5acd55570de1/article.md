# [Iwaspoisoned.com Presents at Northern New Jersey Public Health Association Event](https://iwaspoisoned.com/news/iwaspoisonedcom-presents-at-2024-northern-new-jersey-public-health-association-annual-event)


Jan 16, 2024


We were invited to present at the Northern New Jersey Public Health Association's (NNJPHA) meeting on January 25th, 2024.
From left to right: Daniel Dabrowski (Board President), Katherine Fabrizio (Treasurer), Patrick Quade (Dinesafe CEO), Kayla (Environmental Health Specialist)
This significant and recurring gathering in Northern New Jersey assembled a diverse range of public health experts, from Health Educators to Environmental Health Specialists, and Officers. Our presentation at this educational and interactive session served as a platform to share knowledge and discuss the role of consumer crowdsourcing in enhancing food safety.
The NJ Board President introducing Patrick Quade's presentation "Crowdsourcing and Food Safety"
The NNJPHA, established in 1948, plays an instrumental role in the region, striving to advance public health and facilitate access to services for health promotion and disease prevention. Their work is vital for the wellbeing of over 3.5 million residents across seven counties in Northern New Jersey. We are honored to have had the opportunity to present at their event this year event and look forward to strengthening our relationships with public health officials in New Jersey, as well as with public health professionals across the US!


Furthering our commitment to the public health community, we also offered a detailed business profile to the New Jersey Public Health Continuing Education. This initiative enables us to provide continuing education credits to attendees in the future, showcasing our dedication to the ongoing professional growth of New Jersey's public health professionals.


Inside the meeting room of NNJPHA's 1/25/2024 meeting
Daniel Dabrowski (Board President, Left) with Patrick Quade (Dinesafe CEO, Right)
