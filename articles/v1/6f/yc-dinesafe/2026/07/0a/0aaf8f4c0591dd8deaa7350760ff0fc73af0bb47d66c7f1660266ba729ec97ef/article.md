# [Iwaspoisoned Presents at Georgia Environmental Health Association's Annual Education Conference](https://iwaspoisoned.com/news/iwaspoisoned-speaks-at-georgia-environmental-health-association-conference-2024)


Aug 31, 2024


*Iwaspoisoned founder Patrick Quade (left) with Georgia Environmental Health Association's Vice President Austin Haney (right)* We recently presented at the Georgia Environmental Health Association's (GEHA) Annual Education Conference in Lake Lanier, GA, from August 27th to 29th, 2024. We spoke about our approach in leveraging consumer crowdsourcing for immediate insights into food safety trends. It was a pleasure engaging with the GEHA team, public health experts, and environmental health specialists at Lake Lanier. A heartfelt thanks to Austin Haney, Vice President of GEHA, for his remarkable leadership and support, ensuring our smooth participation and integration. If you'd like to learn more about the event, click[here](https://geha-online.wildapricot.org/event-5621391) .


GEHA, an influential non-profit dedicated to the advancement of environmental health professionals, serves a crucial role in ensuring the well-being of over 11 million residents. The organization’s efforts extend to sectors including government, academia, and private industry, emphasizing the importance of training, certification, and support to uphold public health and safety.
*Georgia Environmental Health Association's Annual Educational Conference Main Presentation Hall* Collaborating yet operating independently from the Georgia Departments of Public Health and Agriculture, GEHA initiates various projects aimed at enhancing community health and advocating for eco-conscious practices among Georgia’s businesses and industries.


The organization’s commitment to promoting public and environmental health is evident through its initiatives focused on education, involvement in the community, and the ongoing professional development of its members. GEHA prides itself on maintaining transparency and continuously updating its resources to reflect the dynamic nature of environmental health.
*Iwaspoisoned Founder Patrick Quade's Speaker Badge* The Annual Conference exemplifies GEHA’s dedication to fostering education, facilitating networking, and encouraging the sharing of innovative ideas, reinforcing the organization's commitment to safeguarding public health. This convergence of thought leaders and experts in environmental health, supported by institutions like the Association of Food and Drug Officials, aligns perfectly with our goals of contributing to public well-being.


We are so thankful to have been given the opportunity to share our insights and learn from fellow attendees at this gathering! We hope to attend again next year.
