# [Iwaspoisoned Joins the FDA Foods Coalition](https://iwaspoisoned.com/news/iwaspoisoned-joins-fda-foods-coalition)


Dec 21, 2023


A new collaboration—called the FDA Foods Coalition—involving consumer advocates, industry representatives, and public health organizations has been established with the goal of increasing focus on prevention and improving governance at the FDA. This coalition includes significant participants like the Consumer Brands Association and the International Dairy Foods Association. Recently, Iwaspoisoned.com has been invited to join this group.


Additional coalition invitees include prominent organizations from diverse food and drug industry sectors, such as:


- The[Consumer Brands Association](https://consumerbrandsassociation.org/) (CBA), with over 70 consumer packaged goods companies representing nearly 2,000 brands in food, beverages, household, and personal care products, contributes significantly to the economy by providing 2.3 million jobs, generating $151.0 billion in labor income, and contributing $361.3 billion to the US GDP.
- The *[Food Industry Association](https://www.fmi.org/)* (FMI) represents a substantial sector with an $800 billion industry footprint, including over 40,000 food stores and contributing to approximately $1 trillion in supermarket sales. It impacts over 100 million households and supports the interests of 6 million employees, actively promoting and advocating for its members and the broader food industry.
- The[National Grocers Association](https://www.nationalgrocers.org/) (NGA), representing independent, community-focused grocery retailers and operators, accounts for over $250 billion in sales and supports millions of jobs.
- The[International Dairy Foods Association](https://www.idfa.org/) (IDFA) plays a crucial role by representing the US dairy manufacturing and marketing industry. It supports over 3.2 million jobs, generates $49 billion in direct wages, and has an overall economic impact of $794 billion.
- The[Western Growers Association](https://www.wga.com/) (WGA) plays a vital role in the US produce market, providing over half of the country's fresh fruits, vegetables, and tree nuts, including almost 50% of organic produce, and generating billions of dollars in revenue.
