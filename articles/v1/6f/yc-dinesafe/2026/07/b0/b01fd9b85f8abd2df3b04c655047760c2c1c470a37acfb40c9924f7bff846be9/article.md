# [Iwaspoisoned presents at the 13th Annual Food Safety Conference of the Poultry Federation](https://iwaspoisoned.com/news/iwaspoisoned-presents-at-the-13th-annual-food-safety-conference-of-the-poultry-federation)


Feb 27, 2025


Iwaspoisoned is excited to join the 13th Annual Food Safety Conference hosted by the Poultry Federation, taking place from March 4-6, 2025, in Hot Springs, Arkansas. During this event, we’ll be presenting our unique approach to using consumer crowdsourcing for real-time food safety monitoring. This conference brings together industry leaders, food safety experts, and academics to engage in discussions and share valuable insights on the latest challenges in food safety, quality, and compliance.


The Poultry Federation (TPF) is a trade organization spanning multiple states, representing the poultry and egg industries in Arkansas, Missouri, and Oklahoma. TPF's mission is to advocate for and protect the industry's interests while serving as a unified voice in front of state governments. The 13th Annual Food Safety Conference, organized by TPF, will feature some of the top experts in the field. This expanding event attracts participants from across the nation, making it an essential gathering for professionals in the industry.


Our presentation, "Monitoring Social Media for Food Safety," will delve into how social media monitoring can help identify potential food safety risks. We look forward to sharing our insights on how businesses can utilize these tools to proactively address challenges. By incorporating social media monitoring into their food safety strategies, companies can stay ahead of emerging issues, safeguard consumers, and preserve trust in their brands.


We are honored to have been invited to contribute to this important conversation and are excited about the opportunity to collaborate with other industry leaders.
