# [IWaspoisoned Attends the New Food Webinar: Food Tech, Digital Transformation & AI](https://iwaspoisoned.com/news/iwaspoisoned-attends-the-new-food-webinar-food-tech-digital-transformation-ai)


Mar 20, 2025


IWaspoisoned recently had the opportunity to take part in New Food’s webinar, "Food Tech, Digital Transformation & AI," held on March 25, 2025, at 3 PM GMT. This event brought together leading experts in food safety and technology to discuss the evolving role of artificial intelligence, automation, and cybersecurity in the food industry.


New Food is a leading publication dedicated to delivering the latest insights, trends, and innovations in the global food and beverage industry. It plays a key role in driving conversations around food safety, sustainability, and technology through in-depth articles, expert commentary, and industry events like this webinar.


The webinar featured a panel led by food safety expert Dr. Darin Detwiler, LP.D., featured in Netflix's Emmy Award-winning documentary Poisoned: The Dirty Truth About Your Food. Our CEO/Founder spoke alongside industry leaders:


- Kristin Demoranville – Founder of AnzenSage, a cybersecurity expert dedicated to strengthening security risk resilience in the food sector.
- Tom Æ Hollands CSci HonFIFST BSc (Hons) – Innovation and Technical Director at Raynor Foods Ltd, an honorary fellow of the Institute of Food Science and Technology (IFST), and a recognized food scientist.


#### Topics of Discussion:


- AI’s Impact on Food Safety – How AI-driven solutions can help prevent outbreaks, ensure compliance, and build consumer trust.
- Automation & Robotics in Food Production – The role of advanced technologies in optimizing efficiency and reducing human error.
- Cybersecurity in the Food Industry – Protecting food systems from emerging digital threats in an increasingly connected world.
- Sustainability & Ethics – How AI can contribute to a more sustainable and responsible food industry.


At IWaspoisoned, we recognize the power of digital transformation in advancing food safety. Crowdsourced data, AI, and emerging technologies offer game-changing solutions for identifying and mitigating risks before they escalate. Participating in this discussion allowed us to exchange insights with fellow experts and stay at the forefront of food safety innovation.
