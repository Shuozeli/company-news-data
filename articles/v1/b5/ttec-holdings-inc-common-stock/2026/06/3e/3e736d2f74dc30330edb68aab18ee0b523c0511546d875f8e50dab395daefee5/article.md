# TTEC Digital awarded Verint Public Sector Partner of the Year

Left to right: Tiffany Hess (Verint), Stephen Parowski (TTEC Digital), Jeff Marshall (TTEC Digital), Mathew Taylor (TTEC), Brian Kegel (TTEC Digital), Joel Wolverton (Verint), and Ken Archer (Verint) on stage as TTEC Digital receives the 2026 Public Sector Partner of the Year Award.


TTEC Digital is honored to announce that we have been awarded the Public Sector Partner of the Year Award at Engage 2026 in Las Vegas. This award recognizes our commitment and impact in the public sector, demonstrating both execution excellence and strong alignment to the citizen experience.


To learn more about the Verint partners recognized across North and Latin American during Engage 2026, read the full press release at[https://www.verint.com/press-room.](https://www.verint.com/press-room/2026-press-releases/north-and-latin-american-partners-honored-at-engage-2026-for-driving-ai-business-outcomes/)
