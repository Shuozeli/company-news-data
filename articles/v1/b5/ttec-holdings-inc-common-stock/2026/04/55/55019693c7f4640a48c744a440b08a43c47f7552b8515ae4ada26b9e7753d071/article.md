# Where AI wins and where it still falls apart | TTEC Digital

*From CMSWire*


The traditional metrics of customer service are being replaced by a new standard: emotional calibration. In a recent CMSWire article,[Cliff Martin, Executive Director of CX Transformation at TTEC Digital](https://www.ttecdigital.com/people/cliff-martin) , shares his insights on how AI is fundamentally shifting the definition of "good" service.


Martin highlights that the true value of AI lies not just in its efficiency, but in its ability to adapt to a customer's specific emotional context in real time.


Key takeaways from the article:


- **Beyond the script** : Agentic bots are now capable of displaying situational empathy, moving away from predefined workflows toward interactions that adjust based on customer intent.
- **The consistency advantage** : AI can often outperform humans in structured, high stress tasks, such as healthcare scheduling or travel cancellations, by providing unlimited patience and bias-free accuracy.
- **Eliminating friction** : Martin addresses the critical "Who are you again?" moment, explaining how AI can act as a seamless "slot filler" for authentication and data gathering, ensuring human agents are empowered with the context they need to provide high-touch support.


As AI takes on the heavy lifting of data discovery, the role of the human agent is evolving to focus on complex, affective empathy.
