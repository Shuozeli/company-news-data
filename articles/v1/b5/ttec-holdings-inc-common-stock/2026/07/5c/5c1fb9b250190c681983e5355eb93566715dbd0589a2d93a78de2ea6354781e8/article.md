# NiCE recognizes TTEC Digital in inaugural AI Specialization Program

NiCE today announced that TTEC Digital has been named an **inaugural AI Specialization partner** under the NiCE 360 Partner Program. As one of only five partners globally to receive this designation, TTEC Digital is uniquely qualified to deliver measurable, high-impact AI outcomes for enterprises.


According to NiCE, the AI Specialization Program establishes the industry's most rigorous standard for AI delivery. Modeled on industry-recognized frameworks, the program gives enterprise buyers a trusted, independently verified benchmark to identify partners proven to deploy enterprise AI at scale.


**Quick facts about TTEC Digital & NiCE AI Specialization:**


- **Exclusivity** : One of only five partners selected globally.
- **The standard** : Independently verified, framework-based benchmark for enterprise AI delivery at scale.
- **Dual status** : TTEC Digital holds both AI Specialization and Platinum Partner status with NiCE.


"The enterprise market is flooded with AI hype, but technology alone doesn't solve business challenges," said Chris Brown, President of TTEC Digital. "True transformation requires connecting advanced tools with a company's broader operational and technology ecosystem. Being recognized as both an inaugural NiCE AI Specialization partner and a Platinum Partner reinforces TTEC Digital’s ability to deliver the deep consulting and end-to-end integration required to make AI work at scale and drive meaningful outcomes."
