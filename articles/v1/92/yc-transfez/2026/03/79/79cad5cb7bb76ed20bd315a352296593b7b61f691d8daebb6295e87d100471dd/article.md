# Top 8 Indian Banks for Foreigners and Expats

[Business](https://blog.transfez.com/en/business/)


Living in India is a vibrant and wonderful experience, but for many expatriates, navigating the local financial landscape can be a challenge. While India has a massive banking sector, not every institution is equipped to handle the specific documentation and service needs of **foreigners and non-residents** .


To help you manage your **payroll, savings, and daily transactions** without the headache of high fees or complex red tape, we have curated a list of the **top 8 Indian banks for foreigners** .

##


**1. State Bank of India (SBI)**


As the largest **government-owned bank** in the country, **State Bank of India** is often the first choice for expats. With over **24,000 branches** and a massive network of ATMs, you will never be far from help.


-


**Why choose SBI?** It offers highly competitive **administration fees** , dedicated NRI (Non-Resident Indian) services that often overlap with expat needs, and robust **credit card options** for foreign residents.


##


**2. Union Bank of India**


Following its merger with Andhra Bank and Corporation Bank, **Union Bank of India** has become a powerhouse with **9,500 branches** .


-


**Why choose Union Bank?** It is well-regarded for its **strong financial position** and excellent customer relationship support. Many expats appreciate the personalized service when setting up their first local account.

**3. Central Bank of India**


The **Central Bank of India** is a reliable choice that offers a wide array of plans for **savings and deposit accounts** .


-


**Why choose Central Bank?** With over **2,000 branches** , they focus on making cash withdrawals easy and affordable. Their **business loan services** are also a great perk if you are moving to India for entrepreneurial reasons.


##


**4. Punjab National Bank (PNB)**


Formed by the merger of Oriental Bank of Commerce and United Bank of India, **PNB** is one of the pillars of Indian banking.


-


**Why choose PNB?** It boasts over **10,000 branches** and an extensive ATM network. Their **customer service center** is known for being responsive, helping foreigners navigate the often-complex local regulations.


##


**5. Canara Bank**


**Canara Bank** is a household name in India, known for its high-quality digital features and reliable service.


-


**Why choose Canara Bank?** With **10,000 branches** , it is one of the most accessible banks in the country. Many expatriates choose Canara Bank to manage their **local payroll** due to its efficient processing and modern banking app.


##


**6. Bank of Baroda**


If you prioritize **fast response times** , the **Bank of Baroda** is an excellent alternative.


-


**Why choose Bank of Baroda?** With **8,000 branches** , they have built a reputation for excellent customer service. If you encounter an issue with your account or a transaction, their support team is known for resolving matters quickly and professionally.


##


**7. Bank of India**


**Bank of India** is a highly reputable institution that is a favorite among foreign residents for its transparency.


-


**Why choose Bank of India?** It provides a wide range of **customizable financial services** . Their widespread ATM presence ensures you can **withdraw cash anytime** without worrying about finding a compatible machine.


##


**8. Indian Bank**


Having recently merged with Allahabad Bank, **Indian Bank** now operates over **6,000 branches** .


-


**Why choose Indian Bank?** It is a trusted choice for both locals and foreigners. Their focus on **automated teller machines (ATMs)** across rural and urban areas makes it easy to perform financial transactions no matter where your Indian adventure takes you.


Choosing the right bank is the first step toward a stress-free life in India. By selecting one of these **top 8 Indian banks** , you can ensure your **savings, payroll, and transfers** are handled securely. Having a local account not only helps you avoid expensive **currency conversion rates** but also gives you access to a world of digital financial services designed to make your stay easier.
