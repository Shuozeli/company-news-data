# Built In Honors Cirrus Logic in 2025 Best Places to Work Awards

**Austin, TX, January 7, 2025** —[Cirrus Logic](https://www.cirrus.com/) (Nasdaq: CRUS), a leader in mixed-signal processing, was honored today as one of Built In’s 2025 Best Places to Work. Cirrus Logic earned two accolades as one of the 100 Best Places to Work in Austin in 2025 and 100 Best Large Places to Work in Austin in 2025, for companies with more than 1,000 employees.


“Our company is proud to once again be recognized as a Best Place to Work through the Built In Awards—a great start to the new year,” said Denise Grodé, Chief Human Resources Officer at Cirrus Logic. “This recognition reflects our ongoing commitment to bringing in top talent and keeping them engaged with meaningful work in a workplace where they can build their careers and truly thrive. Our people are the heart of our innovation.”


Built In, a leading platform for recruiting tech talent, determines Best Places to Work award winners using an algorithm comparing company data about compensation and benefits. The criteria reflects the benefits candidates are searching for most frequently as well as workplace attributes like remote and flexible work opportunities, and other people-first cultural offerings.


“Being recognized as a Best Place to Work is a testament to these companies’ commitment to building a workplace where individuals and innovation thrive,” said Built In CEO and Founder, Maria Christopoulos Katris. “At Built In, we understand that great companies are powered by great teams, and this achievement showcases their dedication to fostering a culture of growth, inclusivity, and excellence. Congratulations on this well-deserved honor.”


Cirrus Logic’s continued recognition as a top employer reinforces its dedication to fostering a collaborative and rewarding workplace. With an impressive employee retention rate with more than half of their 1,700 team members employed for more than six years, and nearly a quarter remaining for a decade or more, and more than 3,900 issued and pending patents worldwide, the company has an essential mix of newcomers and experienced talent driving innovation. Cirrus Logic demonstrates its commitment to both technological excellence and employee satisfaction. Recent accolades, including recognition on Built In’s 2024 Best Places to Work in Austin and USA Today’s Top Workplaces USA list, as well as consistent honors from the Austin-American Statesman since 2010, highlight the company’s success in cultivating a team-focused atmosphere.
