# Everspin Technologies to Present at the Needham Growth Conference

CHANDLER, Ariz.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Jan. 5, 2026-- Everspin Technologies Inc., (NASDAQ: MRAM), the world’s leading developer and manufacturer of persistent Magnetoresistive Random Access Memory (MRAM) solutions, today announced that Sanjeev Aggarwal, President and Chief Executive Officer and Bill Cooper, Chief Financial Officer, will present at the 28th Annual Needham Growth Conference, on Tuesday, January 13th at 2:15 p.m. Eastern Time in a fireside chat format.


A live webcast, as well as a replay, of the presentation will be available on the company’s investor relations website at[https://www.everspin.com/](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.everspin.com%2F&esheet=54387213&newsitemid=20260105418068&lan=en-US&anchor=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.everspin.com%2F&index=1&md5=0b696fe92077ab57f5e714625866f26b) .


**About Everspin Technologies**


Everspin Technologies, Inc. is the world’s leading provider of Magnetoresistive RAM (MRAM). Everspin MRAM delivers the industry’s most robust, highest performance non-volatile memory for industrial, automotive, aerospace and other mission-critical applications where data persistence is essential. Headquartered in Chandler, Arizona, Everspin provides commercially available MRAM solutions to a large and diverse customer base.
