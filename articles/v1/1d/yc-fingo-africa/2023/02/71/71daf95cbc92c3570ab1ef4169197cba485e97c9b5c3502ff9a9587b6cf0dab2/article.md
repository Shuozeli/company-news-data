## What is Digital Banking


[Digital banking](https://www.temenos.com/news/2019/12/19/what-is-digital-banking/) is the digitization of banking services so that they are accessible purely online.


Unlike traditional banks which have branches, digital banks employ online platforms like apps and websites to facilitate branchless banking.


With online/digital banking apps you can access regular banking services remotely. These include:


- Making withdrawals and deposits
- Checking your bank balance
- Requesting bank statements
- Monitoring your financial records
