# Earn rewards by onboarding yourself

Are you new to Fingo? Great news! You can earn a reward of Ksh 100 by completing a few simple key actions.


To claim your reward, simply follow the steps below:


1. Sign up for a Fingo account.
2. Complete the onboarding steps by providing your personal information.
3. Deposit at least KES 100 into your Fingo account.
4. Pay a utility bill, buy airtime or pay for entertainment using your Fingo account.
5. Use the Pay Bill or Buy Goods option on Fingo.
6. Send money to a Fingo Friend.
7. Send money to M-PESA.


Once you have completed all of these steps, you will receive your reward of KES 100. If you are unable to complete these steps immediately, don't worry, you can still claim your reward by waiting for 60 days.


It's that simple! Don't miss out on this amazing opportunity to earn some extra cash. Sign up for Fingo today and start enjoying the benefits of online payments.
