# How Can I deposit into my Fingo Account through M-PESA Paybill

We have several deposit methods. The easiest and fastest method is to deposit directly from the Fingo App by clicking the deposit button and following the instructions.


Should you want to deposit via paybill then please follow the below steps:


The Fingo paybill number is 490742:


1: Access your M-PESA menu


2. Choose Lipa na M-PESA Option


3. Select the Paybill option


4. Enter Fingo Paybill Number: 490742


5. Enter Your Fingo Account Number as the account number


6. Enter the amount you wish to deposit into your Fingo account


7. Enter Your M-PESA PIN to authenticate the transaction


8. Review and Confirm before finalizing the transaction then press OK to complete the process


NB: Note that one can only deposit from their own phone number to their Fingo account for now.
