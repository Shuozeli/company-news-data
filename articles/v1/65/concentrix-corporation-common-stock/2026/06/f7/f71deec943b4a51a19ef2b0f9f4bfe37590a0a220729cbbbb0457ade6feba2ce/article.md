# Concentrix Earns Comparably Recognition in Q2 2026 for Leadership and Career Growth Excellence

**NEWARK, Calif., (June 16, 2026) —** Concentrix (NASDAQ: CNXC), a global technology and services leader, announced today it has earned four Comparably awards in Q2 2026. Determined by game-changer feedback, the recognition reflects what matters most in today’s workplace: strong leadership, meaningful career opportunities, and a culture where people are equipped with the right tools and encouraged to grow.


**This recognition includes:**


- Best Company for Career Growth, reflecting the opportunities game-changers have to build new skills, take on greater responsibility, and advance their careers.
- Best Company for Leadership, recognizing a strong executive team that provides clear direction, supports development, and helps teams navigate change with confidence.
- Best Teams: HR, highlighting the critical work of our people teams in building a grounded, people-first culture that attracts and retains top-tier problem solvers.
- Best Teams: Marketing, recognizing a team that helps bring the Concentrix story to life through creativity, collaboration, and a deep understanding of what matters to clients and game-changers.


Together, these awards reinforce a reality Concentrix sees every day: technology alone doesn’t create outcomes. As the Intelligent Transformation Partner for both its people and clients, Concentrix helps organizations orchestrate technology, people, and AI to solve complex business challenges. The same focus on growth, leadership, and collaboration that drives results for clients is reflected in the experience of its game-changers.


Concentrix continues to invest in its people, providing opportunities to grow, develop new skills, and take on meaningful challenges as the future of work continues to evolve.
