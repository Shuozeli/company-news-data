# Concentrix Launches iX360 in Manila to Advance Intelligent Experiences

**Quezon City, Philippines** (May 11, 2026) –[Concentrix Corporation](https://www.concentrix.com/news/) (NASDAQ:[CNXC](https://ir.concentrix.com/) ), a global technology and services leader, recently announced the launch of iX360, a new immersive experience center designed to help clients and stakeholders apply intelligent experience technology to real business challenges. The launch coincides with the opening of Concentrix’ newest operations site in Manila, reinforcing the Philippines’ growing role as a hub for innovation and digital transformation.


iX360 is a hands-on environment where clients can see technology in action and better understand how it supports real customer and operational needs. Visitors can interact directly with solutions powered by analytics, automation, and artificial intelligence, gaining a clearer view of how intelligent experiences are designed, deployed, and scaled.


The experience center is built to connect strategy with execution. By making innovation practical and tangible, iX360 helps organizations understand how intelligent technology can improve performance, drive growth, and deliver meaningful business outcomes, while working alongside people and deep domain expertise.


“iX360 represents how we are bringing intelligent experience to life, where strategy, technology, and human ingenuity come together to create real business impact,” said Amit Jagga, EVP and Chief Business Officer of Concentrix Philippines. “As AI and automation continue to reshape industries, our focus is on ensuring that it works hand in hand with our people, enhancing productivity, unlocking creativity, and enabling better outcomes for our clients and people.”


The new Manila site brings together modern workspaces, advanced technology, and skilled talent to support clients around the world. Together with iX360, it reflects Concentrix’ continued investment in strengthening the Philippines as a center for innovation, digital capability, and integrated business solutions.


The launch of iX360 is part of the company’s broader global approach to helping clients move from interest in AI to real results. Through interactive experience centers like iX360, Concentrix enables clients to better understand how intelligent experiences deliver value at scale.


To learn more about how Concentrix helps enterprises move beyond AI pilots and put intelligent experiences into production, visit[www.concentrix.com](https://www.concentrix.com/about/news/concentrix-launches-ix360-in-manila-to-advance-intelligent-experiences/www.concentrix.com) .
