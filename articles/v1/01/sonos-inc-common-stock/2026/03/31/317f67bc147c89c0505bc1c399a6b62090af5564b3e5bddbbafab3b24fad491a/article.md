** March 13, 2026


UPnP SSDP messages are multicast, which in a large Wi-Fi network can be excessive. Disabling will reduced unwanted multicast traffic, however needs to be enabled for legacy Windows/MacOS Sonos Apps and 3rd party Apps that still require UPnP/SSDP. If just using just S2 App on phone/tablet and/or play.sonos.com, can be disabled.


[https://support.sonos.com/en-gb/article/adjust-connection-security-settings](https://support.sonos.com/en-gb/article/adjust-connection-security-settings)


> **UPnP** : This pertains to integrations that utilize the unsupported UPnP protocol. Note that turning this setting off will also prevent the Sonos app for macOS and Windows from controlling your system.
