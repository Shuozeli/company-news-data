# FLAVOR WITH A MISSION: PHORM ENERGY’S CHERRY SLUSH FLAVOR RETURNS TO SUPPORT FIRST RESPONDERS NATIONWIDE

**St. Louis, MO – May 4, 2026** **–** Phorm Energy and Folds of Honor are continuing to deliver on their commitment to honor and support those who serve our country and communities. Today, Phorm Energy, the game-changing energy drink brand produced by Anheuser-Busch with the backing of 1st Phorm and Dana White, announced the return of Honor. Educate. Unite. – a limited-edition Cherry Slush flavor that represents fueling for a greater purpose. This zero-sugar offering, featuring a red, white and blue can design, supports enhanced hydration through an electrolyte blend and provides an energy boost from natural caffeine from green tea.


Phorm Energy’s partnership with Folds of Honor, a nonprofit organization that provides families of fallen or disabled U.S. service members and first responders with academic scholarships, grows stronger in year two. The return of Cherry Slush reaffirms the brand’s commitment to giving back with each can sold unlocking a donation towards academic scholarships.


**Thomas Russell, General Manager, Phorm Energy,** said: “At Phorm Energy, recognizing those who do the work and honoring those who serve is at the core of who we are, and our partnership with Folds of Honor is a cornerstone of that belief. With the can’s return, we're proving that together we can fuel a greater purpose. From directly supporting funding of academic scholarships for firefighter families, to supporting the families of U.S. service members and first responders with every can of Honor. Educate. Unite. that we sell, we are committed to making a real difference and invite our community to join us."


This year, in addition to the contributions from every can sold, Phorm Energy will award a $10,000 academic scholarship to Folds of Honor for the families of fallen or disabled firefighters. With this contribution, Phorm Energy supports the mission of Folds of Honor by honoring their sacrifice, educating their legacy, and uniting communities around a greater purpose.


**Bob Fishbeck, Senior Vice President, Folds of Honor,** said: “Our mission is to turn sacrifice into hope by providing life-changing academic scholarships to the families of fallen or disabled U.S. service members and first responders. The commitment Phorm Energy demonstrates through the Honor. Educate. Unite. cans helps turn our mission into real-world support for those who deserve it most. Every can of Cherry Slush purchased helps us deliver on our promise to the families of our nation's heroes, ensuring their legacy is one of opportunity and education."


Today, in honor of International Firefighters’ Day, Phorm Energy will be coordinating product deliveries of the limited-edition Cherry Slush flavor to over 3,000 firehouses nationwide to support the frontline heroes who #DoTheWork. Providing hydration, energy, and mental focus, Phorm Energy Cherry Slush is now available nationwide for a limited-time as a 16-ounce single serve offering.


Phorm Energy, a top 15 brand in total energy1, has been rapidly growing since its[national launch](https://www.anheuser-busch.com/newsroom/Unlock_Your_Full_Potential_With_Phorm_Energy) in June 2025 becoming the #1 Fastest-Growing Innovation Brand Family2. [Phorm Energy](https://www.phormenergy.com/) is available in five permanent bold flavors including Screamin’ Freedom, Grape Smash, Orange Fury, Blue Blitz, and the most recent addition[Silver Lightning](https://www.anheuser-busch.com/newsroom/Phorm-Energy-Silver-Lightning) .
