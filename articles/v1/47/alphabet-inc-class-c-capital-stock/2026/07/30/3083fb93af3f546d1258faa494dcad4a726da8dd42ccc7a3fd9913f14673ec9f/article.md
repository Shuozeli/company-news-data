# It's not too late to get Pixel Care+ for your Pixel 9 or 10.


Kersi Shroff


Senior Product Manager, Pixel Care+


Jul 14, 2026


---


Existing Pixel 9 and 10 series owners have a limited-time opportunity to add device protection with[Pixel Care+](https://store.google.com/magazine/pixel_care?hl=en-US) . Enroll between now and August 2 through the[Google Store online](https://store.google.com/magazine/pixel_care) or[My Pixel app](http://g.co/mypixel/home) to get $0 screen and battery repairs, unlimited accidental damage protection and optional loss and theft coverage.


Eligible phones include the Pixel 9, 9 Pro, 9 Pro XL, 9a, 10, 10 Pro, 10 Pro XL and 10a. To qualify for open enrollment, you must be a U.S. customer with a phone that's in good working condition — fully functioning and free from cracks, mechanical defects or liquid damage.[Restrictions apply.](http://support.google.com/store?p=pixelcare_openenrollment_us)
