## Why Do You Need an RB2B Alternative?


There are three main reasons why users look for alternatives to RB2B. 👇


### Reason 1: Limited Visitor Tracking (US only)


RB2B only tracks website visitors from the US. ​​Its pixel only fires for US IP addresses and identifies traffic from profiles of US geolocations.


Although this frees the platform data of any GDPR regulations, websites with traffic coming in primarily from locations outside the US won’t be able to use the tool effectively.


### Reason 2: Credit-Based Pricing


RB2B offers two core plans: Free and Pro. Both work on credit-based usage. The free plan limits you to 200 credits per month, while the paid plan offers the same credits but allows you to purchase additional ones.


**Here’s the catch:** each lead discovery costs 1 credit, so 200 credits will fall short.


Another issue with this system is that RB2B users have observed a rare ‘false positive’ instance where the credit is being used up, but the data the software gives isn’t accurate. This can lead to quick exhaustion of your credits.


[Source](https://www.g2.com/products/rb2b/reviews/rb2b-review-10313508)


### Reason 3: Expensive


Of course, RB2B comes with a free forever plan. But, the jump from free to paid tier is significant for many users.


Also, when you opt for the Pro plan, the amount of credits will remain the same (200 credits). Purchasing additional credits starts at $119 per 200 credits and can significantly go up as usage increases.


[Source](https://www.g2.com/products/rb2b/reviews/rb2b-review-9760432)
