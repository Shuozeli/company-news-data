# 9 Classy Funeral Outfit Ideas

Picking the right outfit can feel like a tough choice when attending a funeral. What exactly is acceptable attire at a funeral? Does that change during the winter months or summer months? What if you don’t own a black suit? The good news is – there are tons of variations for what makes a “classy” funeral outfit. This article provides some suggestions on what to wear to a funeral for a respectable and classy look.


## Classy funeral outfits for men


Classy funeral outfits for men typically feature dark, neutral colors like black, charcoal, or navy and include pieces such as suits, blazers, and suit jackets. Suits should strive to be well-tailored or fitting and clean and should ideally be on the simpler side. You can choose to round out your funeral outfit with accessories, but it’s a good idea to keep the accessories understated if you’re concerned about appearing too flashy. Whether you’re[attending a funeral in the summer](https://everloved.com/articles/funeral-customs/what-to-wear-to-a-funeral-in-the-summer/) or winter, following are some classy funeral outfits you can feel confident and respectful in at the event.


### 1. Black suit with accessories


This outfit is a great example of how to accessorize a simple suit with classy and sophisticated pieces. Pair a dark suit with brown loafers and a flashier tie clip for a classy update to the classic funeral suit. A suit in a dark color such as navy or black would be the optimal choice for this elevated look.


[Source: Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/photo/photo-of-a-man-wearing-a-black-three-piece-suit-2474308/)


### 2. Tan suit with black loafers


Wearing a tan suit to a funeral might not be your first go-to pick, but this is an excellent choice for any one who is looking for an alternative to a simple black suit. This is a good choice if you need to wear colors other than black but still want something classy. You can accessorize the suit with black loafers and a nice watch as a final touch.


[Source: Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/photo/elegant-man-wearing-suit-and-necktie-19747985/)


### 3. Double breasted suit with a pocket square


This option is another choice for someone wanting to do something a bit different than the classic black suit. A dark navy or dark colored suitcoat paired with a brighter tie and undershirt end up in a classy combo. You can accessorize this look with a matching pocket square for an extra bit of class.


[Source: Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/photo/model-in-an-elegant-double-breasted-suit-18851488/)


### 4. Casual funeral outfit


This look is a bit more casual with a nice suit jacket paired with a t-shirt or clean white undershirt. For bottoms, choose dark colored slacks or dark colored jeans for a[more casual look](https://everloved.com/articles/funeral-customs/what-to-wear-to-a-casual-funeral/) . You can pair this look with a nice pair of loafers or boots and a simple wristwatch for a classy look.


[Source: Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/photo/man-in-a-business-casual-outfit-sitting-on-steps-20097460/)


### 5. Nice undershirt and lighter pants


Wearing a black undershirt paired with lighter grey pants offers fewer layers and lighter colors which are perfect for a summer funeral. This look is tied together with a few brighter accessories – a tan belt and watch both add a spot of brightness to the outfit that helps offset the darkness of the top.


[Source: Pxhere](https://pxhere.com/en/photo/1594083)


### Classy female outfits for a funeral


A classy funeral outfit for a woman typically includes dark, neutral colors and well-fitting, well-tailored pieces such as dresses, pantsuits, skirts and jackets. It’s common to lean towards conservative, modest pieces of clothing and to avoid bolder patterns or bright colors. Following are some options you can consider when choosing a funeral outfit – some are a bit flashier than others, but all can come together in a classy and elegant look.


### 1. Pantsuit with necklaces


One of the popular alternatives to classy funeral dresses is a pantsuit. This is a more flashy take on a funeral outfit, but still has a classy look to it. Pair a dark navy or black pantsuit with a lighter colored undershirt with a few statement pieces for a classy pantsuit to wear during the summer.


[Source: Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/photo/smiling-woman-in-suit-15666873/)


### 2. Polkadot dress


If you’re looking for a more casual alternative to elegant and classy funeral dresses, consider a skater dress! This funeral outfit is a perfect choice for any season, but especially summer. Choose a black or navy polka dot skater dress and pair the dress with a tan heel or flat for a classy funeral outfit.


[Source: Pxhere](https://pxhere.com/en/photo/1533261)


### 3. Simple black dress


Another summery choice is a classy black dress for funeral that ends mid-thigh paired with a simple wristwatch. Forego other accessories such as jewelry for a more simple but elegant look.


[Source: Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/photo/woman-in-black-long-sleeve-dress-standing-on-road-12268525/)


### 4. Black turtleneck and skirt


For those in a colder climate or who are[attending a funeral in the winter](https://everloved.com/articles/funeral-customs/what-to-wear-to-a-funeral-in-the-winter/) months – this outfit keeps you warm and looking classy. Pair your black turtleneck and skirt with black gloves and a gold accented belt for a classy and elegant option.


[Source: Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/photo/woman-reflecting-at-dusseldorf-christmas-market-29922241/)
