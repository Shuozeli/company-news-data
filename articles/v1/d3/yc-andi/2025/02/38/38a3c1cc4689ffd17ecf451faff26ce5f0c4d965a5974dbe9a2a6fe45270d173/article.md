# Performance Updates: Scaling Andi for Growing Demand

The rapid adoption of Andi has led to unprecedented demand for our AI search assistant. While we’re thrilled that so many users trust Andi for accurate, ad-free search, this growth created some performance challenges. Here’s how we’re addressing them:


## What We’ve Improved


- **Faster Response Times:** Deployed new optimized AI models that deliver answers more quickly while maintaining Andi’s high accuracy standards
- **Enhanced Error Handling:** Implemented smarter error reporting and recovery, ensuring users understand what’s happening when issues occur
- **Scientific & Mathematical Processing:** Improved handling of complex calculations, weather queries, and unit conversions
- **Infrastructure Scaling:** Added server capacity to handle the growing user base
- **Search Result Quality:** Enhanced our source verification and citation system for even more reliable answers


## What This Means for Users


- More consistent response times
- Clearer feedback when issues occur
- Better handling of technical and scientific queries
- Improved reliability for research and content generation


## What’s Next


While these updates significantly improve performance, we’re continuing to scale our infrastructure to meet demand. Our commitment remains unchanged: providing accurate, ad-free search that respects user privacy.


We appreciate your patience during peak usage periods as we expand our capacity. Your trust drives us to maintain the highest standards of search quality while scaling our service.


Let us know if you encounter any issues - your feedback helps us improve!
