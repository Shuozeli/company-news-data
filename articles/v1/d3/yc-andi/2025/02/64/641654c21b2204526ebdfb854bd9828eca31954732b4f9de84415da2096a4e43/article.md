# Generate Text on Andi

Do you constantly struggle to plan your vacations with GenAI? Either the cafes you find online don’t exist, or the budgets don’t match. Well, Andi can help you plan the perfect, personalized travel itinerary. Whether you are looking for the best rooftop pools, cheap eats, working cafes or hidden gems - Andi’s got you covered.


Let’s learn how you can use Andi to help plan your trip in 2 simple steps.


## Step 1 - Prompting Andi!


Andi is really good at finding information and generating results based on your requirements. The more information you provide, the better and more personalized your itinerary will be.


Let’s use a sample prompt -


> “Generate a 7 day itinerary for a trip to Bali from SF for a digital nomad. I want to work 4 hours on Monday, Wednesday and Friday from a nice co-working cafe. Please include hidden gems in my itinerary.”


As you can see, I’ve asked Andi to generate an itinerary including various and specific key points. Let’s see how Andi responds.


## Step 2 - Generate your personalized itinerary


Once you click on generate text, Andi will take a few moments to look through the search results and generate an itinerary that fits all of your needs!


Now, let’s review Andi’s itinerary and see how it did.


When you look closer at the write up Andi’s done, you can see it’s personalized and includes additional information such as working on alternate days with desired cafes, information about hidden gems, places, etc.


Making your travel plans with Andi is fun and can save you time, just like your smart friend who has traveled everywhere.


Andi works like humans, combining language models with reasoning and common sense. This means it can think before it answers and doesn’t make things up like other chatbots.
