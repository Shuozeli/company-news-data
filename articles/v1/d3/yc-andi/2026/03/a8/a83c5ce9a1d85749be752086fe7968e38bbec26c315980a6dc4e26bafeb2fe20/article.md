# Andi Named Best Free AI Search Engine by PCMag

**SAN FRANCISCO — March 27, 2026** — PCMag has named Andi the “Best Free AI Search Engine” in its updated[Best AI Search Engines](https://www.pcmag.com/picks/the-best-ai-search-engines) roundup, published March 24, 2026. This is the third consecutive year the publication has recognized Andi.


In the review, PCMag wrote that Andi “has a friendly interface that makes it easy to check sources, filter responses, summarize results, view related links, and more.” The publication also noted that Andi is completely free, with no credits, subscriptions, or upsells.


“Three years of recognition from PCMag means a lot to our team,” said Angela Hoover, CEO and co-founder of Andi AI. “We built Andi so search would work for the person searching, not for advertisers. Hearing that described as ‘friendly’ is the best compliment we could get.”
