# 2026 Industry Pulse: Providers

## Providers: Industry Pulse


Healthcare is the leading factor behind job growth in the U.S., but the industry is still contending with a staffing shortage. Faced with ongoing workforce constraints, regulatory pressures, shifting patient demand, and emerging technologies, provider organizations are exploring new ways to deliver care—often with less reliance on MDs.


### In this report, we examine the latest intelligence on the provider market and explore four workforce trends at the heart of the change:


- Contingent staffing is becoming structural strategy
- Advanced practice providers (APPs) are expanding primary care access
- Behavioral health delivery models are transitioning
- Technology is reducing reliance on in-office labor
