# Align sales and marketing in healthcare

Sales and marketing teams share the same goal—driving growth—but they often rely on different definitions of success and disconnected data to get there.


In healthcare, those differences can create blind spots. Without a shared understanding of the landscape, teams can interpret the same markets differently, leading to missed sales opportunities and disconnected campaigns.


Read the **healthcare leader’s guide to getting teams in lockstep with a single source of truth** to learn how shared healthcare intelligence can help commercial teams align strategies, make confident go-to-market decisions, and drive better results.


Inside the guide, you’ll see why sales and marketing teams often interpret the same signals through a different lens, and learn how to build a shared data strategy that creates lasting alignment across your organization.
