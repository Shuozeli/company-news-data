# 10 Reasons to Use Dry Thawing in Cell Therapy Processes

During cell therapy process development and manufacturing, you need unwavering trust in the technologies that you use. Barkey offers multiple solutions that deliver trust in the form of precise, gentle, yet robust dry thawing for your vials, bags, and bottles.


Below are 10 great reasons to incorporate a Barkey dry thawing system.


## 10 Reasons to Use Dry Thawing


- Rapid thawing is as critical as controlled rate freezing for successful cryopreservation
- Controlled reproducible thawing helps to minimize batch-to-batch variability
- Our thawing devices are designed for cleanroom use with low particle generation risk
- Dry thawing eliminates contamination risks of an open water bath and validation challenges of a bead bath, thus supporting cGMP compliance
- Paddles gently agitate bags to promote thorough thawing
- Compatibility with a wide range of container volumes and types
- Low maintenance and no consumables helps to keep you on task and on time
- Three thawing devices to accommodate your project needs: Barkey plasmatherm, Barkey varitherm, and Barkey vialguard pro
- Used by top biopharma companies for CGT manufacturing, including commercial CGT products
- High reliability and low maintenance mean one less thing to worry about
