# COSMIC v104 and Actionability v20 now available

We are excited to announce the availability of a new release of the[Catalogue Of Somatic Mutations In Cancer (COSMIC) Knowledgebase](https://digitalinsights.qiagen.com/products-overview/cosmic/) , the world’s largest, expert-curated resource for understanding the impact of somatic mutations in cancer.


The latest COSMIC v104 and Actionability v20 release adds millions of new genomic variants and non-coding mutations, plus hundreds of thousands of coding mutations, thousands of whole genomes, drugs, treatment combinations and clinical trials. This update marks a major expansion of the knowledgebase’s content, enabling users to fully contextualize variants by mutation frequency, tissue distribution and clinical actionability.


### Key updates of COSMIC v104 and Actionability v20


- Core curation focused on gastric and oesophageal cancers


- Over 6.8 million new genomic variants and 4.3 million new non-coding mutations, with fully curated new genes, tumor types and their mutational and clinical profiles, alongside updated driver likelihoods for coding mutations in the Cancer Mutation Census (GRCh37)


- Expanded Cancer Gene Census with 5 newly curated genes their associated Hallmarks, highlighting the functional roles of Tier 1 genes in tumor development and progression


- Mutational Signatures v3.6 adds 4 novel SBS signatures and includes updates to 2 existing mutational signatures


- New Actionability data, with 484 fully curated genes, 7571 treatment combinations and 8493 clinical trials with results.


Together, COSMIC v104 and Actionability v20 delivers focused, expert-curated data to accelerate research, discovery and clinical diagnostics in the complex landscape of cancer.


Learn more about the latest release[here](https://digitalinsights.qiagen.com/cosmic-release/) .
