# Accurate biomedical knowledge: Why it's paramount, yet elusive

## Feeling like your AI models just don't cut it anymore?


If you’re working in pharma or biotech, you're no stranger to AI. You’re probably familiar with the struggle to improve popular models by adding more (and more) training data. It makes sense – surely if you give your AI more data, you’ll be able to solve any problem. It’s just a matter of quantity.


But there’s a small problem.


## The limits of AI-derived biomedical data


Most biomedical data are unstructured and contain errors. To answer your specific questions, you’d have to remove the errors and structure the data. Although this might sound simple, this is still beyond current natural language processing (NLP) approaches and generative AI models.


For example, if a number or label has been entered incorrectly, you need to directly contact the authors to learn the correct value. This can’t be inferred from context. AI might not even recognize that the error exists, especially if it matches existing patterns in the data. AI models need “high-quality” underlying data to deliver insights you can trust – accurate, comprehensive, up-to-date and standardized.


To complicate matters, scientific knowledge evolves daily, and the amount of biomedical data is constantly growing. With all the groundbreaking discoveries yet to be made, you need to constantly update your foundational data, otherwise your picture of biology will go stale.


## Data analysis without knowledge = statistically significant nonsense


For data to become knowledge, it must be honed, fine-tuned and polished by a person. Otherwise, the data are never truly trustworthy.


As leaders in augmented scientific data collection, we’re excited by new AI curation opportunities and continue to take advantage of beneficial advancements.
