# New Release: HGMD Professional 2026.1 is here

The[Human Gene Mutation Database (HGMD) Professional](https://digitalinsights.qiagen.com/products-overview/clinical-insights-portfolio/human-gene-mutation-database/) 2026.1 is now available, adding more than 4,700 new mutation entries to the world’s largest collection of expert-curated data on germline mutations in nuclear genes underlying or associated with human inherited disease.


HGMD Professional now contains **575,068 mutations** .


More than **534,000 mutations** have GRCh38/hg38 chromosomal coordinates and HGVS descriptions to enable faster, more accurate variant matching.


## HGMD Professional now integrated in Franklin by QIAGEN


Did you hear? HGMD Professional is now integrated directly into Franklin by QIAGEN, enabling seamless access to expert-curated data directly within the interpretation workflow. No more platform switching—just instant access to classifications, mutation summaries, phenotype associations and supporting literature.


Explore the latest release and learn more about Franklin + HGMD[here](https://digitalinsights.qiagen.com/hgmd-release/) .
