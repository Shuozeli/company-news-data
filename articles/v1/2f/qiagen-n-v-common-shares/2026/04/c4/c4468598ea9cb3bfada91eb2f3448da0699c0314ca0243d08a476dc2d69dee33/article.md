# The IPA Spring Release is now available 🌱

## Advance your research with the latest content and features


The Spring 2026 release of QIAGEN Ingenuity Pathway Analysis (IPA) brings comparison analysis to IPA Interpret along with a fresh content drop.


### Compare up to 50 analyses with the IPA Interpret bubble map


If you’ve used Comparison Analysis in IPA Desktop, you may be familiar with this feature – but in IPA Interpret, we’ve expanded its scope and enhanced its graphics. Now, you can compare upstream regulators, canonical pathways, diseases and more across your analyses with IPA Interpret’s new comparison bubble map (Figure 1).


**Figure 1. The new IPA Interpret Comparison Analysis bubble map.** This interactive bubble map displays all tested entities (regulators, pathways, diseases and more) across your chosen analyses. The display can be customized to show specific parameters or results.


In addition to supporting up to 50 analyses, this interactive bubble map displays all entity types in a single matrix (unlike IPA Desktop, which supports up to 20 analyses and displays only one entity per heat map). In Figure 1, the bubbles’ sizes and colors correspond to calculated statistical significance and predicted activity, respectively, but you can change which parameters determine these characteristics. You can also change the order of the analyses by dragging their columns, and you can further filter and sort the results to highlight specific findings.


The interactivity doesn’t stop there. If you wish to find out more information about a bubble, you can simply hover your cursor on it to see the scores for the entity that it represents. You can also click on a bubble to display additional information: the full network or pathway that was scored, the entity summary and the dataset molecules used in the prediction (Figure 2).


**Figure 2. See how the entity was scored.** Clicking on a bubble will display more details on the scored entity, including the full network or pathway, its summary and the dataset molecules used in the prediction.


Similar to other areas in IPA Interpret, this Comparison Analysis includes an AI summary to fast-track insights from your comparison data, powered by an instance of ChatGPT 5.1. Please note that our LLMs are privately hosted by QIAGEN and do not retain or train on the query data.


### Fresh content updates


This content release brings more than 190K new findings, for a new total of over 14.9M. This includes 7 Ingenuity signaling pathways, which are hand-curated by our in-house team of curation experts:


- Cereblon and Thalidomide Signaling Pathway
- Germ Cell Differentiation Signaling Pathway
- Maternal to Zygotic Transition Signaling Pathway
- Ovarian Folliculogenesis Signaling Pathway
- Pulmonary Hypertension Signaling Pathway
- RNA m6A Methylation Signaling Pathway
- S-Adenosylmethionine Regulatory Signaling Pathway


This brings our total number of[available canonical pathways](https://qiagen.my.salesforce-sites.com/KnowledgeBase/KnowledgeNavigatorPage?id=kA41i000000L5q0CAC&categoryName=IPA) to 1408, of which 531 are Ingenuity signaling pathways.


Finally, over 16K new datasets from OmicSoft were added in this release, including single-cell data from the collaboration between Tahoe Therapeutics and[Parse Biosciences, now a QIAGEN company](https://corporate.qiagen.com/English/newsroom/press-releases/press-release-details/2025/QIAGEN-to-acquire-Parse-Biosciences-expanding-its-Sample-technologies-portfolio-into-highly-scalable-single-cell-solutions/default.aspx) .
