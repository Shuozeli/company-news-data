# May New Arrivals: The Best Niche Perfume Brands to Try Now

Earlier this month,[we brought you cozy gourmand and Mediterranean-inspired scents](https://www.scentbird.com/blog/niche-perfumes-gourmand-scents/) . For May’s second wave of arrivals, we bring you niche perfume brands that explore the darker, smoother side of fragrance: think spiced woods, saffron, amber, and pink pepper.


## [HERMETICA Redmoon](https://www.scentbird.com/perfume/hermetica-redmoon-92890585003737)


Redmoon plays with contrast in the most mysterious way. It opens with pink pepper, adding a sparkling brightness against warm saffron and amber, while vetiver and cedar create a smooth, woody depth underneath.


## [HERMETICA Sandalsun](https://www.scentbird.com/perfume/hermetica-sandalsun-153768791530064)


Warm and cozy, this fragrance brings out the sweeter side of woody notes, with sandalwood, hazelnut, vanilla, myrrh, and cacao. Its subtle gourmand touch lifts the woodiness of the scent, making it creamy and quietly addictive.


## [HERMETICA Darkoud](https://www.scentbird.com/perfume/hermetica-darkoud-201599499133219)


This perfume takes oud in a softer, more wearable direction while still maintaining its signature depth. Rose brightens the scent with a velvety floral glow, while saffron adds a touch of spice. Overall, it’s a modern oud fragrance with undeniable presence.


## [M. MICALLEF Notorious XO](https://parfumsmicallef.com/en/products/notorious-xo?srsltid=AfmBOoowBll0thbtfT1Z7MyuM9uiXVj5TB9NSdm4A8rxQiYyywPsyARs)


Inspired by the finest cognacs, Notorious XO feels decadent from the very first spritz. With notes of cognac, tonka bean, cinnamon, sandalwood, and praline, it feels deep and luxurious as it melts into the skin.


## [MARLEY FRAGRANCE Simmer Down](https://www.scentbird.com/perfume/marley-fragrance-simmer-down-58208440926566)


Simmer Down balances juicy fruits with warm woods for a fragrance that feels bright and confident. Bergamot and star apple keep it fresh, balanced out by pear blossom and cinnamon leaf. As it dries down, you’ll notice comforting notes of amberwood and sandalwood.


## [MARLEY FRAGRANCE Lights Down Low](https://www.scentbird.com/perfume/marley-fragrance-lights-down-low-276421235634035)


Lights Down Low is a sultry fragrance that reminds you of unforgettable moments by the beach, opening with sea salt and mandarin. Soon, rose hip and geranium add romance, while clove leaf, amber, and sandalwood give it a grounding, warm depth.


## [MARLEY FRAGRANCE Mellow Mood](https://www.scentbird.com/perfume/marley-fragrance-mellow-mood-14945824070764)


Crisp bergamot and lemon give this fragrance an uplifting brightness, which is deepened by its warmer notes of cognac and cedarwood. Balanced with earthy patchouli and aromatic basil, the fragrance feels effortlessly wearable.
