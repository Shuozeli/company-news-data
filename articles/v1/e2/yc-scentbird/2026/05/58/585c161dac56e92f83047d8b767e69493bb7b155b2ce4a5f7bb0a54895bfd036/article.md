# 10 Niche Perfumes To Try This May 2026: From Gourmand Scents to Tropical Favorites

From[cozy gourmand scents](https://www.scentbird.com/blog/cozy-perfumes-for-staying-in/) to sun-drenched Mediterranean escapes, this month’s drops are niche perfumes that are all about personality. This curated selection highlights modern blends that balance indulgence with wearability, designed to linger long after you’ve left the room.


## [DIME Stay Awhile](https://www.scentbird.com/perfume/dime-stay-awhile-129268714493290)


Both warm and modern, Stay Awhile is a delicious gourmand perfume that sounds just as good as your favorite dessert. It blends toasted pistachio, candied pear, vanilla bean, white chocolate, jasmine, tonka bean, and cashmere musk, creating a scent that is made for slow days at home.


## [BCBG Elixir De Cassis](https://www.scentbird.com/perfume/bcbg-elixir-de-cassis-183855368996758)


A lush burst of blackcurrant and strawberry puree opens this fragrance with a juicy, almost intoxicating sweetness. As it develops,[floral softness blends](https://www.scentbird.com/blog/house-of-bo-ave-maria-red-wine/) into a deeper, more smoldering mix of wild lily, tonka bean, and smoked caramel. The result is both playful and seductive, leaving a warm, lingering trail.


## [MANCERA Intense French Riviera](https://www.scentbird.com/perfume/mancera-paris-intense-french-rivera-69907305540971)


Reminiscent of a Mediterranean escape, Intense French Riviera is bright and irresistible, with notes of citrus fruits, virginian cedarwood, Cambodian oud, sea salt, and ambergris. It’s fresh yet grounded–perfect for a boost of confidence during the warmer months.


## [MIND GAMES Check Please](https://mindgamesfragrance.fr/products/check-please)


Check Please opens with crisp green apple and boozy apple cognac for an immediate spark of intrigue. Nutty undertones of hazelnut and nougat soften the composition, while plum and cinnamon add warmth. Overall, the scent feels elegant yet playful at the same time.


## [MONTALE PARIS Intense Starry Nights](https://www.scentbird.com/perfume/montale-intense-starry-nights-275856406975222)


This fragrance opens with a bright bergamot before unfolding into lush Egyptian rose and jasmine. Deep patchouli grounds the composition, giving it a mysterious depth. Finally, amber and musk create a sensual, lingering trail that you won’t be able to get enough of.


## [MONTALE PARIS Be My Plum](https://www.scentbird.com/perfume/montale-be-my-plum-56485630780805)


Juicy plum, mixed with sparkling pink pepper, takes center stage. Right after, white florals soften the heart while coconut and vanilla add a creamy warmth to the fragrance. Oud and musk linger on the skin, creating a sensual finish.


## [THE HARMONIST Yin Transformation Parfum](https://www.scentbird.com/perfume/the-harmonist-yin-transformation-1805)


Airy yet complex, Yin Transformation by[The Harmonist](https://www.scentbird.com/blog/the-harmonists-moon-glory-vs-sun-force/) is the type of fragrance that explores contrast. Almond milk and iris create a smooth, almost ethereal opening, while calypso orchid adds a unique brightness. Sandalwood and white musk anchor the fragrance in a calm, meditative finish.


## [THE HARMONIST Hypnotizing Fire Parfum](https://www.scentbird.com/perfume/the-harmonist-hypnotizing-fire-1804)


Hypnotizing Fire wraps the skin in a warm blend of creamy vanilla and benzoin. Bulgarian rose and patchouli add depth and sophistication, while pepper tree brings a spice that makes its dry down unforgettable. True to its name, the perfume wears like a slow-burning glow.


## [VACATION After Sun](https://www.vacation.inc/products/after-sun-by-vacation)


After Sun captures the feeling of cooling off after a long day in the sun. Aloe and green tea bring a clean, soothing freshness, linen and soft aquatic notes add an effortless touch, while a hint of gin adds a playful, unexpected twist.


## [VACATION Vacation](https://www.vacation.inc/products/vacation-by-vacation)


Pure summer in a bottle, this scent is bright and[instantly transportive](https://www.scentbird.com/blog/april-2026-fragrance-drops-transport-you/) . Coconut milk and pineapple create a creamy tropical opening, softened by banana and airy pool water notes. It dries down into something nostalgic, like endless vacation days.
