# Granny-Chic Perfumes: the Nostalgic Trend We Can’t Get Enough Of

Photo Tim Walker


It’s official— **granny-chic perfumes** are trending, and I think I know why. The crazier the world gets, the more we want our fragrance to feel comforting and nostalgic—like a hug from the kind of grandmother who always smelled amazing and kept hard candies in her handbag.


## What Are Granny-Chic Fragrances?


There are two answers, actually. First, the actual, original formula **vintage perfumes** that our grandmas wore (think Chanel No. 5 and Elizabeth Taylor White Diamonds). And then there’s the **modern-day fragrance trend** , ‘granny chic’. Parma violets, rose, anything powdery and soft— the notes and scents you associate with kind ladies with greying hair, Grandmillennials, or Gen X girlies who go hard on the vintage.


## OG (Original Granny) Perfumes That Defined the Trend


Spend five minutes on any fragrance forum and you’ll find people breathlessly recounting the scents that remind them of their grandmothers. And there are some heavy hitters in the list, for sure.


[Elizabeth Taylor White Diamonds](https://elizabethtaylor.com/white-diamonds-and-elizabeths-two-great-loves/) , the perfume that epitomized Hollywood glamor for generations of women who grew up captivated by Taylor’s films (and succession of high-profile marriages). Chanel No. 5 is a[controversial addition](https://www.allure.com/story/classic-fragrance-grandma-perfume) to the list, but as I grew up smelling it on the older ladies in my family on their way out for the evening, it hits hard for me.


[Shalimar](https://www.fragrantica.com/perfume/Guerlain/Shalimar-Eau-de-Parfum-53.html) ,[Jicky](https://www.fragrantica.com/perfume/Guerlain/Jicky-103.html) , even[Charlie](https://www.fragrantica.com/perfume/Revlon/Charlie-3141.html) —scents from our childhoods that wafted around our grandmothers, their friends and the other ladies who cared for us. Always, always a special place in our hearts (just maybe not our perfume collections).


## Modern Granny-Chic Perfumes to Try Now


Here’s where it gets nostalgic in a different way. Notes that evoke grandmothers, used in scents that are emphatically modern (and emphatically trending). Here are the six we can’t get enough of.


### [MERIT Retrospect](https://www.meritbeauty.com/products/retrospect) : the ethereal granny-chic scent


In the words of one[Scentbird reviewer](https://www.scentbird.com/perfume/merit-retrospect-66115596198079) , ‘Love this unique vintage smelling scent! Unlike anything I’ve ever experienced before. To my nose it smells like iris lipstick, and dusty pear.. in the most ethereal way. Nice powdery quality with just a touch of sweetness, touch of musk…I want to read an old poetry book or go to the library and wear pinup curls and smoke clove cigarettes…timeless old school beauty vibes.’


A glamorous granny-chic fragrance that invites us to pick up poetry? No wonder it’s Retrospect’s best-selling scent.


Notes: ambrette seeds, vanilla, moss, jasmine, orris, rose, pear


Find Merit Retrospect on Scentbird[here](https://www.scentbird.com/perfume/merit-retrospect-66115596198079?l) .


### [LE LABO Violette 30](https://www.fragrantica.com/perfume/Le-Labo/Violette-30-117822.html) : for modern violet lovers


Parma violets are grandmother-coded for a reason—that powdery, slightly sweet, slightly soapy scent that’s nostalgic in the most comforting way. The white violet note at the center of Violette 30 is fresher and greener than the sweet shop version, and Le Labo wraps it in white tea, cedarwood and guaiacwood so the whole thing feels less ‘rummaging in nan’s vanity’ and more ‘wearing silk in a botanical garden’. It’s the granny-chic perfume for people who were convinced they wouldn’t like granny-chic perfumes.


Notes: white violet, white tea, cedarwood, guaiacwood


### [LIBERTY Maze](https://www.fragrantica.com/perfume/Liberty-LBTY/Liberty-Maze-91993.html) : citrus grandma-core at its best


With Maze, Liberty went straight for the most British thing imaginable: afternoon tea. It opens with bergamot and Earl Grey, then layers in neroli, jasmine, honeysuckle and hawthorn—basically, every flower you’d find in a country garden if your grandmother kept one.


It’s citrus-led but the tea note is what makes it a grandmacore fragrance: it smells like a tin of biscuits and a chintz armchair and somebody offering you a third cup whether you want one or not. Lighter than most picks on this list, which makes it the easy entry point if powdery florals aren’t your thing (yet).


Notes: bergamot, Earl Grey tea, neroli, jasmine, honeysuckle, hawthorn


Find Liberty Maze at Scentbird here.


### [MAISON MARGIELA REPLICA Up at Dawn](https://www.fragrantica.com/perfume/Maison-Martin-Margiela/Up-At-Dawn-120743.html) : the rose with an edge


The whole Replica concept is built on bottling memories, which makes it a granny-chic shoo-in before you even smell it. Up at Dawn is the first Replica scent inspired by somewhere in the UK (specifically, the rose gardens at Windsor at first light) and it earns its rose. The opening is watery and gentle, the kind of dewy floral that could easily veer into dated, but pink pepper and a moss accord pull it somewhere spicier and more grounded.


As one Fragrantica reviewer said, it ‘pulled me into a very specific scene: that moment in Pride and Prejudice when Mr. Darcy emerges through the fog to find Elizabeth. The composition leans into a muted, grounded rose, not so much floral, but more atmospheric. It’s earthy, humid, very tactile, like wet soil and fabric absorbing morning mist.’ A rose for people who thought they were done with rose.


Notes: pink pepper, soil accord, elemi oil, rose heart absolute, powdery orris, ambrette absolute Guatemala, musks, patchouli-mossy woodiness, cashmeran


### [JULIETTE HAS A GUN Lipstick Fever](https://www.fragrantica.com/perfume/Juliette-Has-A-Gun/Lipstick-Fever-60868.html) : for vintage glamour


If granny-chic has a sensory peak, it’s the smell of a vintage lipstick. A waxy, slightly powdery, scent that drifts up when you uncap a tube your grandmother kept in her handbag for thirty years. Lipstick Fever is exactly that. Iris, violet and a soft vanilla base do the heavy lifting, and the result is dressing-table-in-a-1950s-film glamorous without tipping into costume. The kind of perfume you wear with a slick of red lipstick, then let the scent do the rest.


Notes: raspberry, violet absolute, iris absolute, patchouli essence, vanilla absolute


### [NARCISO RODRIGUEZ Narciso Poudrée](https://www.fragrantica.com/perfume/Narciso-Rodriguez/Narciso-Poudree-36679.html) : the powdery one


Powder is granny-chic at its finest, and Poudrée does it better than anyone. Rose and white jasmine up top, a powdery musk heart, vetiver and cedar at the base. One Fragrantica reviewer described it as smelling ‘like I took my mother’s vintage Gloria Vanderbilt body powder and ground it up with sweet tarts candy’, which is exactly where modern fragrance fans fall so hard for the granny-chic aesthetic. It’s glamorous, it’s vintage, it’s sensual and feels expensive. Grandmother adjacent, but rich, polished, and above all, feminine. Which brings us back to where we started—when the world goes crazy, reach for the scents that feel like a cashmere hug from your favorite grandmother.


Notes: white jasmine, Bulgarian rose, musk, vetiver, cedarwood

Who wouldn’t want to channel the femininity and strength-in-softness of our grandmothers’ generation? Reach for one of these granny-chic fragrances and reconnect with your original feminine icon (and her unforgettable hugs).


## **Granny-Chic Perfumes FAQs: Your Guide to These Powdery, Nostalgic Fragrances**


### Q1: What are granny-chic perfumes?


Granny-chic perfumes are fragrances inspired by vintage scents, featuring notes like rose, violet, iris, and powdery accords with a nostalgic feel.


### Q2: Are nostalgic perfumes the same as vintage perfumes?


Not exactly. Vintage perfumes are original classics, while nostalgic granny-chic perfumes can include modern fragrances inspired by those styles.


### Q3: What notes define granny-chic fragrances?


Common notes include rose, violet, iris, musk, aldehydes, and powdery floral accords.


### Q4: Why are retro granny-chic perfumes trending?


They offer comfort, nostalgia, and familiarity—qualities that resonate during uncertain or fast-paced times.


### Q5: Are powdery perfumes popular again?


Yes. Powdery perfumes are making a comeback as part of the granny-chic trend, blending vintage elegance with modern formulations.
