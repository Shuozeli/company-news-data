# Express Yourself With Death Becomes Her Perfume Collections | Scentbird

This Pride Month, Broadway and beauty take center stage as Scentbird’s Express Yourself platform partners with the Tony Award-winning musical *Death Becomes Her* . Based on the 1992 classic, the musical continues to dazzle its audience with its themes of identity and self-expression. In partnership with *Death Becomes Her* on Broadway,[Scentbird brings this cultural moment to life through fragrance](https://www.scentbird.com/collections-women) .


This June, Scentbird is spotlighting LGBTQIA+ owned fragrance brands through curated scent collections inspired by the show’s unforgettable leading women: Madeline Ashton, Helen Sharp, and Viola Van Horn. As part of the celebration, audiences attending the June 18th performance will have the opportunity to discover and sample fragrances from the featured LGBTQIA+-owned brands, helping them find scents that perfectly express who they are.


## About *Death Becomes Her* on Broadway


Based on the 1992 classic film, *Death Becomes Her* brings audiences into a dazzling world where beauty, ambition, friendship, and rivalry collide. The musical follows actress Madeline Ashton, writer Helen Sharp, and the enigmatic Viola Van Horn as they clash in their pursuit of power and youth. Packed with larger-than-life performances, razor-sharp wit, and spectacular costumes that are pure eye candy, it’s no wonder *Death Becomes Her* has become one of Broadway’s most talked-about hits, earning 10 Tony Award nominations in 2025.


*Death Becomes Her* puts the spotlight on three iconic women who inspire audiences to express themselves unapologetically. Madeline and Helen have Volia’s potion for eternal beauty, and now with Scentbird’s Express Yourself collection, you can have perfection too.
