# The 10 Best Perfumes for Women We’re Loving This Summer

Looking for the[best perfumes for women this summer](https://www.scentbird.com/blog/best-niche-perfume-for-designer-lovers/) ? You’re in the right place. This season’s new arrivals prove there’s never been a more exciting time to explore fragrance, with everything from creamy gourmands and luminous florals to woody compositions that linger long after sunset.


These are the scents you’ll be adding to your[Scentbird queue](http://scentbird.com/) and reaching for all season long.


##
**Our Picks for the Best Perfumes for Women This Summer**


We’re getting right into it. If you’re looking for your next fragrance obsession, we’re introducing you to some of the standout scents available on Scentbird this season.


### [FUGAZZI Pomegranoudh Extrait de Parfum](https://www.scentbird.com/perfume/fugazzi-pomegranoudh-25687936026391)


If you watch to catch the attention of the object of your affection this summer, Pomegranoudh is an alluring combination of sweetness and smokiness. It balances juicy sweetness with smoky depth, opening with pomegranate, bergamot, and red fruits before revealing rich oudh and patchouli.


### [GIVENCHY L’Interdit Eau de Parfum](https://www.scentbird.com/perfume/givenchy-l-interdit-175218335813985)


An homage to the original from 1957, this fragrance balances a radiant white floral bouquet of orange blossom, jasmine, and tuberose with darker notes of vetiver and patchouli. Timeless and elegant, it encourages you to embrace your individuality.


###
[JULIETTE HAS A GUN Banana Rush Eau de Parfum](https://www.scentbird.com/juliette-has-a-gun-perfumes)


Remember when we wrote all about[banana, matcha, and marshmallow perfumes](https://www.scentbird.com/blog/banana-matcha-marshmallow-perfume-gourmand-fragrances/) ? Banana Rush proves that gourmand fragrances can be playful and sophisticated at the same time. Ripe banana and maple syrup meet creamy coconut, frangipani, vanilla, and sandalwood for a warm, sensual finish that never feels overly sweet.


### [L’ORCHESTRE Piano Santal](https://www.scentbird.com/perfume/l-orchestre-parfum-piano-santal-122087854488199)


Dreamy and skin-like, Piano Santal wraps white sandalwood, warm milk, and soft musks into an intimate fragrance. It’s a quiet scent with an addictive quality that reveals itself gradually throughout the day.


### [L’ORCHESTRE Bouquet Encore](https://www.scentbird.com/perfume/l-orchestre-parfum-bouquet-encore-233619882742839)


Inspired by the energy of a late-night dance floor, Bouquet Encore pairs jasmine and tuberose with vibrant timut pepper and vanilla. The result? A floral fragrance that feels electric and impossible to forget–perfect for summer nights out.


### [M.MICALLEF Notorious XO](https://www.scentbird.com/perfume/m.micallef-notorious-xo-123735844377034)


Inspired by the craftsmanship of exceptional cognac, Notorious XO blends cognac, cinnamon, tonka bean, sandalwood, and praline into something rich, charismatic even. It was, after all, inspired by the craftsmanship of creating the finest cognac.


###
[CONFESSIONS OF A REBEL Rough Rider](https://www.scentbird.com/perfume/confessions-of-a-rebel-rough-rider-50826184980246)


Tension is Rough Rider’s middle name. It combines bergamot leaf, rugged lavender, vanilla tobacco, ripped denim accord, and raw amber to create a textured fragrance that is rebellious and earthy, yes, but also unexpectedly refined.


### [MATIERE PREMIERE Crystal Saffron](https://www.scentbird.com/perfume/matiere-premiere-crystal-saffron-66821414902493)


Saffron takes on a brighter, more contemporary character in Crystal Saffron. Enhanced by airy musks, ambroxan, and incense, the fragrance feels radiant and textured, with an addictive quality that keeps you coming back for more.


### [MATIERE PREMIERE French Flower](https://www.scentbird.com/perfume/matiere-premiere-french-flower-47934701287179)


French Flower captures the intoxicating beauty of tuberose at night. Ginger, green pear, and tea leaf bring freshness to the floral heart, resulting in a fragrance that feels luminous and absolutely captivating.


###
[MATIERE PREMIERE Vanilla Powder](https://www.scentbird.com/perfume/matiere-premiere-vanilla-powder-183215564631081)


Vanilla Powder reimagines vanilla through a contemporary lens. With notes such as vanilla absolute from Madagascar, coconut powder, palo santo oil from Ecuador, and musk, the perfume is creamy and comforting, close to the skin musks we all love.
