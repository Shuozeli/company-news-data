# Peachy Keen: The Best Designer Peach Perfumes For 2026

There’s nothing quite like biting into a perfectly ripe peach, from breaking through the velvet-soft skin with its soft, delicate fuzz to the succulent orange flesh below. But as life sadly doesn’t allow us to loll around savouring fresh peaches all day long, we’ll settle for dousing ourselves in designer peach fragrance instead.


And lucky for us, there’s an abundance of[gorgeous peach perfumes](https://www.marieclaire.com/beauty/fragrance/best-peach-perfumes/) to choose from, because these velvety-soft stone fruits are absolutely[having a moment](https://www.thezoereport.com/beauty/peach-fragrance) right now. Sweet, juicy, and soft, peach is a perfect fit for the[summer fruit trend](https://www.byrdie.com/peach-perfume-trend-11930190) , part of the collective gourmand obsession that shows zero signs of slowing down. And Google Trends show worldwide searches for “best peach perfume” up 60% in the past month. Is[2026 the year of the peach](https://www.allure.com/story/peach-perfumes-trend-summer-2026) ?


It may well be, but peach fragrances have been with us for over a hundred years, since the release of Guerlain’s[Mitsouko](https://www.guerlain.com/us/en-us/p/les-legendaires-mitsouko---eau-de-parfum-P024104.html) in 1919. A[grand parfum](https://theblacknarcissus.com/2012/11/04/the-empress-of-moss-mitsouko-by-guerlain-1919/) beloved by[Jean Harlow](https://riskdelightblog.wordpress.com/2017/04/16/on-guerlains-mitsouko-and-the-magic-of-knowing-nothing/) , the fragrance was[reportedly](https://boisdejasmin.com/2017/07/what-does-the-word-mitsouko-in-guerlain-perfume-mean.html) inspired by French author Claude Farrère’s novel ‘La Bataille’, a historical love-and-spy story set in the Russian-Japanese war of 1905. Mitsouko is married to Japanese Fleet Admiral Marquis Yorisaka… but she also falls in love with a British officer called Fergan. Will love or loyalty win? Fetch the smelling salts as we swoon! We won’t give away the ending but we can say Mitsouko is every bit as dramatic and exquisitely rendered as this turn-of-the-century love triangle. Possibly the most notorious fruity chypre ever released, it combines its peachy heart with a redolent base of vetiver, labdanum and oakmoss.


Some peachy perfume highlights from the next century: in 1944, Rochas released the cult perfume[Femme](https://www.rochas.com/en/perfumes/femme-rochas) , reviving interest in the peach note; 1984 saw the release of Chanel’s[Coco](https://www.chanel.com/gb/fragrance/p/113420/coco-eau-de-parfum-spray/?gad_source=1&gad_campaignid=19871072605&gclid=Cj0KCQjwo_PRBhDNARIsAEcVALUdAZEJOLJomAzeyfYYrYjXW1rA6nCDxkUGmOaiFmvqMwftjmvxbLAaAqCuEALw_wcB) with a no-holds-barred double peach note (in the top and at the heart), and then in 1992, there was[Angel](https://www.mugler.co.uk/fragrance/women/angel/angel-eau-de-parfum/M010101003.html) . The[iconic](https://lucaturin.substack.com/p/angel-stellar) Mugler perfume, pioneer of the modern gourmand craze, which featured peach and apricot in its sprawling, maximalist heart.


Back to the peach-toned present day, and designer fragrance houses are serving up these peaches in thoroughly modern ways that honestly, we can’t get enough of. Tom Ford’s immaculate Bitter Peach, released in 2020, is at the top of the Fragrantica peach hit list (and one of my personal favorites), with more recent releases from Gucci (Guilty Pour Femme) and Dior (Peachy Glow) close behind. Like peachy desserts, there’s a designer peach perfume for everyone, and often for less than you’d expect (especially with a[Scentbird fragrance subscription](https://www.scentbird.com/) ). Let’s sink our teeth in.


## The Best Designer Peach Perfumes For 2026


### [Tom Ford Bitter Peach](https://www.tomfordbeauty.co.uk/product/bitter-peach-eau-de-parfum) : Boozy Peach


Voluptuous. Opens with realistic, juicy, not super sweet peach—would describe it as realistic but sophisticated, rather than candy sweet. Then it quickly softens into something very textured and voluptuous, I’d assume these are the boozy notes at the heart. All I know is when I had my wrist glued to my nose smelling it over and over, I just kept thinking… mmm, voluptuous. (Probably not the technical term, but suffice to say I loved it).


### Gucci Guilty Pour Femme: Spicy Floral Peach


Confident. The peach that went to work and got the promotion. Where the others lead with fruit, Guilty opens on a pink pepper kick, with the peach arriving soft and warm just behind it, more blushing glow than juicy bite. It’s the floral one, really: peach folded into geranium and that signature Gucci lilac, sweet but with a spicy, grown-up edge that stops it ever tipping into dessert. Think peach worn with a sharp blazer rather than a sundress. Warm, a touch sultry, and built for someone who wants their fruit with a bit of attitude.


Try Gucci Guilty Pour Femme on Scentbird[here](https://www.scentbird.com/perfume/gucci-guilty-pour-femme-1429) .


### Dolce&Gabanna The One: Sensual Peach


Sensual. Opens with a burst of juicy, ripe fruit, peach (and its good friend lychee) coming through bright and clear, and giving this scent a great deal of its fruity, juicy sweetness. Definitely leans sweet, then softens into a very sophisticated white floral heart and a soft, milky, peaches-and-cream base. Sensual, sweet, floral and creamy, for romantic peach lovers everywhere.


Try Dolce&Gabanna The One on Scentbird[here](https://www.scentbird.com/perfume/dolcegabbana-the-one-981522) .


### Burberry My Burberry: Soft Dewy Peach


Our first Kurkdjian peach, this one is the softest of them all. It’s pure country-garden-in-the-rain peach, blended to perfection with quince (the most English of fruits?) and rose, while sweet pea and freesia make the dewy outdoorsy reverie all the more real. This one is all about the soft peachy glow (and that Burberry elegant English country garden aesthetic).


Get Burberry My Burberry on Scentbird[here](https://www.scentbird.com/perfume/burberry-my-burberry-176?) .


### [Dior Addict Peachy Glow](https://www.dior.com/en_gb/beauty/products/dior-addict-peachy-glow-Y0000181.html) : Playful Gummy Peach


This peachy glow, on the other hand, is the fun one. Where the first Kurkdjian peach is all subtlety, this is unapologetically sweet and candy-like (maybe the “glow” refers to peach collagen gummies, because that’s exactly the bouncy, juicy-candy character it’s got). Our second Kurkdjian peach, and a completely different mood to the first: vibrant, uplifting, and built for anyone who doesn’t want a “realistic” peach but a full, jammy, peach-candy boost. The peach-perfume equivalent of an energy drink when you’re on your way out for the evening. Let’s go!


Five delicious peaches, five completely different moods—whether you want yours boozy, creamy, dewy, candied or wrapped in spice, there’s a scent here that’s peach-perfect for you.
