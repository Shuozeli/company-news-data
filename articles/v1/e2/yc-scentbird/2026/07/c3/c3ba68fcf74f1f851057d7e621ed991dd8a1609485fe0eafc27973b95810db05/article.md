# Confessions of a Rebel Fragrances: One More Confession Before You Go

I’ve announced it[here](https://www.scentbird.com/blog/two-new-scents-from-confessions-of-a-rebel/) , and[here](https://www.scentbird.com/blog/two-new-confessions-of-a-rebel-scents-have-dropped/) , and[here](https://www.scentbird.com/blog/new-confessions-of-a-rebel-scents/) , and[here](https://www.scentbird.com/blog/confessions-of-a-rebels-new-scents/) …oh wait,[here](https://www.scentbird.com/blog/new-confessions-of-a-rebel/) also, and let’s not forget right[here](https://www.scentbird.com/blog/new-confessions-of-a-rebel-is-here/) too. And now one more time for those in the back: **Confessions of a Rebel** has two new scents!


Every single time this brand drops a duo, they reinvent the rules. What’s truly wild is how completely distinct each of these creations is from the last: no repeats, and most definitely no safe plays. Yet, despite moving in totally different olfactive directions every single time, every single drop is an absolute masterpiece.


## [CONFESSIONS OF A REBEL Shore Thing](https://www.scentbird.com/perfume/confessions-of-a-rebel-shore-thing-255877321184236)


We’ll start with Shore Thing because this summer the tide goes out and the sun goes down. But you? Still the hottest thing around.


Shore Thing opens with a golden pineapple skinny-dipping in coconut water, a drench of extra-pure indulgence. Then come flirty salt-crusted petals and sun-kissed tanned skin, radiating the slow-burn energy of a day well spent. Finally, coastal woods roll in like a tide all low and smooth, stretching the moment long past golden hour. Mist it on and know that in a sea of seasonal flings, babe, you’re the only shore thing. Go ahead, wear it under the sun, under the stars, and *definitely* under the sheets.


## [CONFESSIONS OF A REBEL Rough Rider](https://www.scentbird.com/perfume/confessions-of-a-rebel-rough-rider-50826184980246)


The next new thing on the virtual shelves of this brand is Rough Rider.


We went really in depth here why we believe this is the best men’s summer cologne[over here](https://www.scentbird.com/blog/confessions-of-a-rebel-just-launched-the-best-summer-cologne-for-men/) . But if you’d rather stay on this page and finish reading why you should let this cologne live rent-free on your skin all summer long and beyond, get comfy, because here we come.


This woody spicy blend never asks for direction; it demands total submission. The encounter begins with a bold, earthy bergamot leaf that sparks the opening like a sudden touch in the dark, sharp and heavy with anticipation. Then, a ripped denim accord cuts in, deliciously frayed at the edges, stripping away all inhibitions. The tension thickens as sweet, intoxicating vanilla tobacco veers unexpectedly into the terrain of rugged, seductive lavender, building a rich, throbbing aromatic friction that keeps you hanging on the edge. Finally, the base surrenders to a heavy dose of raw amber, grounding the experience in a golden, primal glow that clings tightly to hot skin long after the dust settles and the screaming stops.


This scent is rough around the edges in all the right ways. Wear it once, and don’t be surprised when it starts getting invited back on your skin time and time again.
