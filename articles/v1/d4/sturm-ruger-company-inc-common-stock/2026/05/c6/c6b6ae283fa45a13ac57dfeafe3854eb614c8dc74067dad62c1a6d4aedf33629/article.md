# Ruger Introduces ReadyDot® Micro Reflex Sight System for LCP® MAX Pistol

# Ruger Introduces ReadyDot® Micro Reflex Sight System for LCP® MAX Pistol


May 27, 2026


Sturm, Ruger & Company, Inc. (NYSE: RGR) is pleased to introduce the Ruger® ReadyDot® for the LCP® MAX pistol. The Ruger ReadyDot is designed to be an accessible, easy-to-use micro reflex sight system. The profile and shape were designed to provide visual guidance for fast target acquisition. A fiber illuminated reticle has been specifically designed for battery-less operation at typical concealed carry pistol ranges. This creates a red dot system that is easier to acquire and stay on target, all without worrying about batteries. The use of a reflex sight like the Ruger ReadyDot allows the shooter to keep both eyes open and focus on the target, rather than the front sight.


The included holster fully covers the trigger guard on the LCP MAX with the ReadyDot installed. **Please Note:** This ReadyDot micro reflex sight is NOT compatible with LCP MAX pistols equipped with a loaded chamber indicator.


For more information or to order your Ruger ReadyDot visit[ShopRuger.com](https://www.shopruger.com/) or your local independent retailer of Ruger firearms.
