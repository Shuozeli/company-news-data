# TikTok's New AI Chatbot: Tako

Have you ever scrolled through TikTok, stumbled on a video, and wondered if what you’re seeing is actually true? TikTok’s new AI,[Tako](https://www.tiktok.com/discover/tiktoktako?lang=en) , is here to change that. It’s ready to answer your questions, offer personalized recommendations, and even help you learn new skills, all without ever leaving the app.


Users can click the icon directly from their For You Page. The icon appears above the profile picture, which is the top-most button on the right side of the screen. This makes it easy to spot and very accessible for everyone. Once clicked, a chat window opens up within seconds. It allows users to type their own prompts or select any of the suggested ones. This integration ensures that the scrolling experience remains uninterrupted.


Released just recently, Tako has transformed the lives of millions of users. Specifically, content creators can determine exactly which videos are trending and how to incorporate their own ideas to achieve high engagement. Previously, people had to research and spend many hours trying to figure out what trends to take part in. With a simple question, Tako is here to answer your questions in just a few seconds.
