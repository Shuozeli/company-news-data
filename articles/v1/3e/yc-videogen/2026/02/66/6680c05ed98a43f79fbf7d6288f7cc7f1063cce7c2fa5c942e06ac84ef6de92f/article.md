## How we evaluated and researched the best AI video editors


We evaluated platforms across eight weighted categories to reflect typical marketing and product led workflows. We also included KPIs for each category because feature checklists alone don’t show how a tool performs in practice. KPIs make the comparison measurable and repeatable: they capture time saved (or added), iteration friction, and the operational impact on a team’s ability to ship consistent video output.


- Automation depth (20%): script to publish coverage, quality of TTS, subtitles, and B roll suggestions. KPI: time to first publish and re edit time.
- Editing control (15%): timeline precision, multi track handling, and export control. KPI: revision cycles per video.
- Localization and accessibility (15%): translation quality, caption accuracy, speaker labels. KPI: localization turnaround time.
- Collaboration and governance (15%): roles, approvals, brand kits. KPI: compliance errors and approval time.
- Content quality (15%): visual coherence, audio clarity, template strength. KPI: audience retention on first 30 seconds.
- Speed and scalability (10%): render speed, batch operations, queue reliability. KPI: videos shipped per month.
- Integrations and asset libraries (5%): stock, storage, and publishing. KPI: time spent sourcing assets.
- Cost value (5%): features per dollar and seat efficiency. KPI: cost per shipped minute.
