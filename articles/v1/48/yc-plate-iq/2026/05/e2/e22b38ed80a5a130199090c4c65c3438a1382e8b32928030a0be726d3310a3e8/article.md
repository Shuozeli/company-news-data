## What healthcare finance teams are up against


Our research reveals the gaps holding healthcare AP teams back, and what it takes to close them.


#### Invoice volume outpaces manual processes


44% of healthcare organizations process 5,000 or more invoices each month and ⅔ say it takes 5 or more days to process a single invoice.


#### Fragmented automation is failing


81% of healthcare organizations use a partial blend of automation and manual processes and only 4% have fully automated AP end to end.


#### Invoice fraud and overpayment are on the rise


42% of healthcare organizations experienced invoice fraud or overpayment in the last year.
