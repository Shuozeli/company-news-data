# The State of AP Maturity 2026: Grocery Edition


More than 200 finance leaders shared what grocery finance teams are getting right, the challenges they face, and why unified AP automation is the future.


## What grocery finance teams are up against


Our research reveals the gaps holding grocery AP teams back, and what it takes to close them.


#### Invoice volume outpaces manual processes


70% of grocery organizations process 5,000 or more invoices each month, and 43% say it takes 5 or more days to process a single invoice.


#### Fragmented automation is failing


93% of grocery organizations use a partial blend of automation and manual processes, and only 2% have fully automated AP end-to-end.


#### Invoice fraud and overpayment are on the rise


55% of grocery organizations experienced invoice fraud or overpayment in the last year, significantly higher than the 41% average across all industries.
