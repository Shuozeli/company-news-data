# Lab Grown Diamonds Price

## FAQ


### Are lab grown diamonds expensive?


Lab grown diamond prices vary widely based on the 4 Cs. A smaller stone with modest grades may cost only a few hundred dollars, while a large, high-quality lab grown diamond can reach tens of thousands. They are priced lower than natural diamonds of comparable quality, but the right price for you depends on the size and specifications you're looking for.


### Why are lab diamonds cheaper?


The price difference between lab grown and natural diamonds comes down to production. Natural diamonds are mined from the earth in limited jewelry-quality quantities, and the mining process is resource-intensive. Larger and higher-quality natural diamonds are particularly rare, which drives their prices up. Lab grown diamonds are created using advanced technologies in controlled settings, allowing for more scalable production. That lower cost of production is reflected in lower retail prices, with no difference in the diamond's physical, chemical, or optical properties.
