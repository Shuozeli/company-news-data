## How to Choose the Best Gemstone for Your Engagement Ring


When it comes to choosing the perfect stone for an engagement ring, diamonds have long been the traditional choice. However, more couples are now exploring other gemstones that offer unique colors,[meanings](https://www.brilliantearth.com/news/gemstone-meanings-the-surprising-symbolism-of-your-jewels/) , and, often, more affordable price points. The perfect engagement ring stone should balance beauty with durability to ensure it stands up to everyday wear for decades to come. When selecting the perfect gemstone for your engagement ring, consider these key factors:


**Durability** : The Mohs hardness scale ranks minerals from 1-10, with diamonds at the top (10). For daily wear, choose gemstones rated 7 or higher to ensure longevity. Active lifestyles particularly benefit from harder stones like diamonds, moissanite, or sapphires.


**Personal Style** : Consider whether you prefer the traditional brilliance of colorless gemstones or the character of colored options. Your ring should reflect your unique personality and relationship, whether that means a classic look or something distinctively yours.


**Budget** : Prioritize what matters most to you — exceptional color, size, clarity, or origin. Lab-created gems offer identical properties to natural ones at lower prices. Remember to include setting costs in your budget, as protective settings for softer gemstones may add to the overall price.


**Setting Compatibility** : Your setting should showcase and protect your chosen gemstone.[Bezel settings](https://www.brilliantearth.com/engagement-rings/bezel/) shield softer gems, while prong settings maximize light in harder ones. Metal choice affects appearance too — white metals enhance cool-toned gemstones, while yellow gold complements warmer types. Lower-profile settings reduce catching risk for active wearers.


## The Top 8 Gemstones for Engagement Rings


### 1) Diamond


Diamonds are the hardest known gemstone, scoring a perfect 10 on the Mohs hardness scale. This unmatched hardness makes diamonds exceptionally resistant to scratches, chips, and other forms of damage, ensuring they retain their brilliance over a lifetime. Beyond their durability, diamonds are renowned for their sparkle and ability to reflect light in a mesmerizing array of[colors](https://www.brilliantearth.com/diamond/colored/) . These attributes make diamonds the classic choice for engagement rings, symbolizing enduring love and commitment.

### 2) Moissanite


Moissanite is another top contender for engagement rings, with a hardness of 9.25 on the Mohs scale. Though slightly softer than diamonds, moissanite is still incredibly durable and resistant to scratches. It is also known for its exceptional brilliance and fire.

### 3) Sapphire


Sapphires, with a hardness of 9 on the Mohs scale, are well-suited for engagement rings. They are available in a wide range of[colors](https://www.brilliantearth.com/gemstones/buying-guide/sapphire/color/) , though the most popular is the classic deep[blue](https://www.brilliantearth.com/gemstones/sapphire/blue/) . Sapphires are highly durable and resistant to scratches, making them ideal for daily wear. Their rich hues and strong durability have made sapphires a beloved choice for centuries. Perhaps the most famous example is Princess Diana's sapphire engagement ring, which has become an iconic piece of royal jewelry.

Rubies are known for their vibrant[red color](https://www.brilliantearth.com/gemstones/ruby/red/) , which ranges from deep, rich crimson to lighter pinkish-red shades. ### 5) Alexandrite


Alexandrite is a unique and rare gemstone with a hardness of 8.5 on the Mohs scale. It is prized for its remarkable color-changing properties, shifting from green in daylight to red or purplish red under incandescent light. This chameleon-like nature adds an element of surprise and mystery to the gem. While slightly softer than diamonds, sapphires, and rubies, alexandrite is still durable enough for daily wear in an engagement ring.

While aquamarine is slightly softer than some of the other gemstones on this list, it is still hard enough to be suitable for daily wear, provided it is treated with care. Aquamarine’s calming hue and durability make it a favored choice for those who prefer a cooler color palette.

## What Gemstones Shouldn’t Be Used for Engagement Rings?


While the gems listed above are well-suited for daily wear, certain gemstones are not recommended for engagement rings due to their lower hardness and greater susceptibility to damage. Gemstones such as opals and[pearls](https://www.brilliantearth.com/gemstones/buying-guide/pearl/) , though stunning, are more easily scratched or chipped. Opals, for example, have a hardness of around 5.5 to 6.5 on the Mohs scale, making them more fragile. Pearls, even softer, rank between 2.5 to 4 on the Mohs scale, meaning they can be easily scratched or damaged. While these options are beautiful and a good fit for other types of[jewelry](https://www.brilliantearth.com/jewelry/shop-all/) , they are not ideal for engagement rings that are meant to be worn daily. Ultimately, the choice of gemstone is a personal one, but for those seeking durability, it is advisable to select gemstones higher on the Mohs scale.


By considering the durability and hardness of these gemstones, you can make an informed decision when choosing the perfect gem for your[engagement ring](https://www.brilliantearth.com/engagement-rings/) , ensuring that it remains as beautiful as the day you first wore it.


## FAQ


### Which gemstone is the most durable for everyday wear?


Diamonds rank highest on the Mohs scale at 10, making them the most scratch-resistant option. Moissanite (9.25), sapphires and rubies (both 9) follow closely behind, all offering excellent durability for daily wear. Any gem rating 7 or above will generally stand up well to the rigors of everyday life.


### Are colored gemstones more affordable than diamonds?


Many colored gemstones offer significant value compared to diamonds of similar size. Sapphires, morganite, and aquamarine can provide larger carat weights at lower price points. However, exceptional quality colored gemstones like fine rubies or alexandrite can actually command higher prices than diamonds, so it depends on the specific type and quality.


### Will a gemstone engagement ring look timeless over the years?


Classic gemstones like sapphires, emeralds, and rubies have adorned engagement rings for centuries and remain timeless choices. When set in traditional settings like[solitaires](https://www.brilliantearth.com/engagement-rings/solitaire/) or[three-stone designs](https://www.brilliantearth.com/engagement-rings/three-stone/) , colored gemstones create enduring beauty that transcends trends.


### How do I know if a colored gemstone is high quality?


While diamonds are graded on the[4 C’s](https://www.brilliantearth.com/diamond/buying-guide/) ([cut](https://www.brilliantearth.com/diamond/buying-guide/cut/) ,[color](https://www.brilliantearth.com/diamond/buying-guide/color/) ,[clarity](https://www.brilliantearth.com/diamond/buying-guide/clarity/) ,[carat](https://www.brilliantearth.com/diamond/buying-guide/carat/) ), colored gemstones prioritize different factors. For most colored gems, color saturation and evenness are paramount. Look for gems with vivid, consistent color, good transparency (except for opaque options like opal), and minimal visible[inclusions](https://www.brilliantearth.com/diamond/buying-guide/clarity/inclusions/) . Working with a reputable jeweler who specializes in colored gemstones is essential.


### What gemstone colors look best with different skin tones?


For warm skin tones (yellow/golden undertones), gems with warm colors like morganite, ruby, and emerald tend to be flattering. Cool skin tones (pink/blue undertones) often complement blue sapphires, aquamarine, and alexandrite beautifully. Neutral skin tones look balanced with nearly any gemstone color. However, these are just recommendations — ultimately, the most important factor is which gemstone you personally find most beautiful against your skin. Learn more in[our guide](https://www.brilliantearth.com/jewelry/buying-guide/what-color-jewelry-should-i-wear/) .


### How can I ensure my gemstone is ethically sourced?


Ask your jeweler about the[origin of their gemstones](https://www.brilliantearth.com/about/mission/transparency/) . Reputable dealers should be transparent about sourcing. Look for certifications from organizations like the Responsible Jewellery Council or gems from countries with strong labor and environmental standards. Lab-created gemstones offer a guaranteed ethical alternative for many gemstone types.
