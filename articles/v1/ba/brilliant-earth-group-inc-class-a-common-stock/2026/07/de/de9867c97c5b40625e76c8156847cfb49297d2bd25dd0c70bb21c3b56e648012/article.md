## 1. Start by Understanding What Your Partner Wants


Surprises are romantic, but an engagement ring might feel like too significant of a purchase to leave entirely to chance. Getting at least a general sense of your partner's style preferences can help ensure the ring is one they'll love.


Start by gauging how much thought your partner has already given to rings. Some people have[Pinterest boards](https://www.pinterest.com/brilliantearth/) ready to go, while others need help discovering what they like. Try casual conversations to figure out preferences. Not sure what to ask? Here are some conversation starters:


- What's most important to you in an engagement ring?
- Are there any styles or features that are must-haves for you?
- Is there anything you definitely don't want in a ring?


If you're trying to be more subtle, pay attention when rings come up in conversation or point out rings you come across while scrolling through social media together, then ask casual questions about their thoughts. This tactic feels natural and low-pressure. Friends and family members might also offer insights, especially if they've had recent conversations about rings or weddings.


For the most direct approach, shop together and take the guesswork out entirely. The[proposal can still be a surprise](https://www.brilliantearth.com/news/how-to-find-out-her-ring-size-without-her-knowing/) even if the ring isn't. What matters most is choosing a ring that reflects their lifestyle and preferences, after all, it's a piece they'll wear every single day.


## 2. Establish a Budget


Before diving into design details, determine how much you're comfortable spending on a ring. While some traditional guidelines suggest spending[three months' salary](https://www.brilliantearth.com/engagement-rings/buying-guide/engagement-ring-salary-rule/) , there's no set rule for[how much an engagement ring should cost](https://www.brilliantearth.com/engagement-rings/buying-guide/budget/) . Your budget should reflect your partner's preferences and your financial circumstances.


Assess your finances first and determine what you can spend without compromising other financial goals. An engagement ring is just one piece of a much bigger financial picture that includes wedding costs, a honeymoon, and potentially saving for a home or other life milestones. Factor in your monthly take-home pay and any upcoming expenses. Whether spending $2,000 or $20,000, the best approach is to set a realistic range based on your income. Financing options are usually available to make the purchase more manageable, but understand the full financial commitment before moving forward.


Keep in mind that the cost of the ring depends on many factors: center stone type and quality, metal choice, and setting style. When making those design decisions, determine what ring qualities matter most to you and your partner. The best approach is to find a diamond or gemstone engagement ring that combines size, quality, style, and affordability.


## 3. Decide on a Center Stone


The center stone typically serves as the ring's focal point and often represents the largest portion of the budget. Here are a few factors to consider when choosing a center stone:


- **Diamond or gemstone:**[Diamonds](https://www.brilliantearth.com/diamond/shop-all/) offer classic brilliance and durability, while[gemstones](https://www.brilliantearth.com/gemstones/shop-all/) like[sapphires](https://www.brilliantearth.com/gemstones/sapphire/) ,[rubies](https://www.brilliantearth.com/gemstones/ruby/) , and[emeralds](https://www.brilliantearth.com/gemstones/emerald/) provide vibrant colors and unique personality. Consider durability if the ring will be worn daily, as some gemstones are softer than diamonds.
- [Quality factors](https://www.brilliantearth.com/diamond/buying-guide/) **:** Each stone type has specific quality factors that affect appearance and price. Research the grading criteria for your chosen stone to understand where you can prioritize or compromise to maximize value within your budget.
- [Natural or lab grown](https://www.brilliantearth.com/news/lab-grown-vs-natural-diamond/) **:** Both options offer beauty and durability but differ in[origin](https://www.brilliantearth.com/news/how-are-lab-grown-diamonds-made/) and[price](https://www.brilliantearth.com/news/lab-grown-diamonds-price/) . Natural stones are mined from the Earth, while lab grown stones replicate this process in controlled laboratory settings with identical results. Lab grown typically costs less, making higher-quality or larger stones more accessible.
- [Shape](https://www.brilliantearth.com/diamond/buying-guide/shape/) **:** The stone's outline affects both price and appearance.[Round shapes](https://www.brilliantearth.com/diamond/round/) maximize brilliance but can cost more due to high demand and precision cutting.[Elongated shapes](https://www.brilliantearth.com/diamond/buying-guide/shape/elongated-diamond-shape/) create a lengthening effect that[flatters the hand](https://www.brilliantearth.com/news/rings-to-suit-your-hand/) and can appear larger per[carat](https://www.brilliantearth.com/diamond/buying-guide/carat/) , offering more visual impact for your budget. Choose a shape that aligns with budget, aesthetic preferences, and complements the wearer's style.


## 4. Choose a Setting


An[engagement ring setting](https://www.brilliantearth.com/news/engagement-ring-styles/) includes all the ring’s design elements apart from the center stone, such as the prongs, gallery, band, and any accent stones. The setting not only securely holds the center stone but also defines the ring’s overall style. Here are key elements to consider when choosing an engagement ring setting:


- **Style preference** : Settings range from minimalist and understated to ornate and glamorous. Think about whether your partner gravitates toward simple, clean designs or prefers more decorative details.
- **Metal type:** Each metal option offers distinct weight, cost, durability, and appearance characteristics that complement different styles and maintenance preferences. The most popular engagement ring metals include[platinum](https://www.brilliantearth.com/engagement-rings/platinum/) ,[white gold](https://www.brilliantearth.com/engagement-rings/white-gold/) ,[yellow gold](https://www.brilliantearth.com/engagement-rings/yellow-gold/) , and[rose gold](https://www.brilliantearth.com/engagement-rings/rose-gold/) .
- **Lifestyle compatibility** : It’s important to match the setting to regular day-to-day activities. Some settings offer more protection for the stone than others. Those who work with their hands or lead active lifestyles may want lower-profile settings that won't catch on clothing or equipment.
- [Wedding band pairing](https://www.brilliantearth.com/news/how-to-match-a-wedding-band-to-your-engagement-ring/) : Consider how your partner wants their engagement ring to sit alongside a wedding band. Some settings make it easier to achieve a flush fit, while others may require custom wedding bands or leave a gap between the two rings.


After determining the ideal setting, the final aspect is figuring out the correct ring size. If you have any concerns about sizing or resizing,[contact one of our jewelry specialists](https://www.brilliantearth.com/contact/) for guidance.

## Tips for Buying an Engagement Ring


Buying an engagement ring is a meaningful decision, and thoughtful preparation can make the process smoother. Here are our top tips and common mistakes to avoid:


- **Don't get caught up in trends:** What's popular now may feel dated in a few years. Choose a ring with timeless appeal that reflects your partner's personal style rather than chasing current trends.
- **Schedule an appointment:**[Visit our jewelers by appointment](https://www.brilliantearth.com/stores/) rather than walking in. This ensures dedicated time with a knowledgeable consultant who can guide the decision-making process, answer questions, and provide personalized recommendations without feeling rushed.
- **Plan your timeline:** The perfect ring takes time, from design conception to crafting to shipping. Start shopping at least two months before your[planned proposal](https://www.brilliantearth.com/engagement-rings/buying-guide/proposal/) date, especially during busy seasons.

## What to Know About Engagement Rings Before You Buy


Buying an engagement ring comes with excitement, anticipation, and sometimes anxiety about budget, options, and getting it right. But the bottom line is that this process is about making a thoughtful choice for your partner. Here are a few final things to keep in mind before buying an engagement ring:


- **There's no universal "right way":** Every couple's process looks different. What matters is finding a ring that reflects your partner's personality and fits comfortably into their life. It's okay to involve your partner in the process if that feels right for your relationship.
- **Take your time and ask questions:** Feeling overwhelmed is normal, and you don't need to learn everything at once. Each decision makes the next one easier. Ask "basic" questions without hesitation, expert guidance exists for a reason.
- **It's okay to change your mind:** Early preferences can change, and priorities may shift once you see real options. That's normal. Consider your initial preferences as a starting point, not a final decision.
- **Trust your research and instincts:** The right ring doesn't have to be the biggest or most expensive, it just needs to feel right for the person who'll wear it. Some uncertainty is natural with big purchases, but if the fundamentals align with what you know about your partner, you're making a solid choice.


Finding the right engagement ring is about balancing meaningful details with practical considerations. These aren't boring factors. They're what makes a ring loved and worn every day. Keep these points in mind as you shop, and you'll be well-equipped to find the perfect ring for your partner.

## FAQ


### What is a good carat size for an engagement ring?


There's no universally "good" carat size for an engagement ring, it depends on personal preference, budget, and lifestyle. In the U.S., the average center stone ranges from 1.0 to 1.5 carats, but many people choose smaller or larger stones based on what looks proportional on their hand and fits their budget. Quality matters more than size.


### How long does it take to make an engagement ring?


The timeline to make an engagement ring varies by design complexity and jeweler.[Ready-made rings](https://www.brilliantearth.com/engagement-rings/ready-to-ship/) can be purchased immediately (with possible sizing time of 1-2 weeks), while more personalized engagement rings typically take 4-6 weeks from design to delivery. During busy seasons, expect longer lead times.


### How should an engagement ring fit?


An engagement ring should fit snugly enough that it won't slip off easily but loose enough to slide over the knuckle without too much resistance. It should be comfortable to wear all day and shouldn't spin excessively on the finger. Keep in mind that fingers swell and shrink with temperature changes and time of day.


### Can any ring be an engagement ring?


Yes, any ring can be an engagement ring. While traditional engagement rings feature diamonds in classic settings, any ring can serve as an engagement ring if it's meaningful to the couple. Some people choose vintage rings, family heirlooms,[alternative gemstones](https://www.brilliantearth.com/news/7-stunning-diamond-alternatives/) , or entirely[custom designs](https://www.brilliantearth.com/engagement-rings/custom/) . What matters most is that the ring represents the relationship and suits the wearer's style.


### What qualities should you look for in an engagement ring?


The qualities you should look for in an engagement ring are durability suitable for daily wear, quality craftsmanship with secure stone settings, a style that matches the wearer's taste and lifestyle, and proper certification for any diamonds or precious gemstones. The engagement ring should be comfortable, proportional to the hand, and fit within budget without compromising on essential quality factors.
