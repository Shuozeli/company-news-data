## Engagement Ring Styles vs. Settings


Choosing the perfect engagement ring involves understanding two important elements: style and setting. While many people use style and setting interchangeably, these terms represent distinct features that influence the ring’s appearance and function.


- **Engagement Ring Style:** An engagement ring style refers to the overall aesthetic and design direction of the ring.
- **Engagement Ring Setting:** An engagement ring setting refers to the specific technique used to mount and secure the center stone in the ring.


Think of style as the personality of the ring and setting as its architecture. A style conveys mood while a setting provides the physical framework that secures the stone and supports the design. Together, they create the complete engagement ring.

## Types of Engagement Ring Settings


While style describes the overall aesthetic, the engagement ring setting refers to the specific technique used to mount and secure the center stone. Think of the setting as the[ring's architecture](https://www.brilliantearth.com/news/anatomy-of-a-ring/) , which provides the physical framework that holds the gemstone and supports the design. Different engagement ring settings offer varying levels of security, visual impact, and practicality.


### Solitaire Engagement Ring Settings


The solitaire setting is the most iconic of all engagement ring types. This design type features a single center stone held by prongs (typically four or six) and focuses all attention on the center stone without competing details. The simplicity of this classic ring type can make the center stone appear larger and more brilliant.


Solitaire settings work with any gemstone shape and suit most personal styles due to their adaptability. They’re practical, timeless, and can offer excellent value since the setting doesn’t feature additional accent stones.

### Three Stone Engagement Ring Settings


Three stone settings feature a center stone flanked by two smaller gemstones on either side. The design carries symbolic meaning, representing the past, present, and future of a relationship, though many people simply love the setting type for its balanced aesthetic.


The engagement ring’s side stones can match the center stone or differ in color and shape to provide contrast. This setting type offers more visual impact than a solitaire while remaining streamlined and timeless.

### Halo Engagement Ring Settings


Halo settings surround the engagement ring’s center stone with a ring of smaller gemstones. This design type makes the center stone appear larger, adds significant shine, and increases the ring’s overall presence on the hand. Halo settings work particularly well for[maximizing the impact of smaller center stones](https://www.brilliantearth.com/diamond/buying-guide/how-to-make-diamond-look-bigger/) .


The accent stones within halo settings can be arranged in various patterns from simple circles to elaborate flower-inspired designs. They can feature traditional diamonds or colored gemstones for added personalization and a pop of color.

### Hidden Halo Engagement Ring Settings


Hidden halo settings feature a ring of small diamonds positioned beneath the center stone, visible primarily from the side angles rather than from the top-down view. This detail adds brilliance to the engagement ring without overwhelming or distracting from the ring’s center stone.


The hidden halo creates a surprise of extra diamonds, adding value and light performance to the ring while maintaining a clean, classic appearance from above. This modern twist on the traditional halo setting is perfect for those who appreciate subtle sophistication and thoughtful design elements.

### Bezel Engagement Ring Settings


Bezel settings feature a metal border that completely or partially encircles the engagement ring's center stone. This sleek design type can be crafted with a full or partial metal rim, allowing for personalized variations in how the metal frames the center stone.


This engagement ring setting offers maximum security since the gemstone is secured firmly in place by a metal border. The protective nature of this setting makes bezels excellent for active lifestyles or hands-on professions. Bezel settings also work beautifully for contemporary or minimalist aesthetics due to the modern and sleek appearance they create.

### Toi et Moi Engagement Ring Settings


Toi et moi settings feature two gemstones of similar size positioned side by side. The gemstones might touch or sit slightly separated, often at an angle. The gemstones can match or contrast, perhaps a diamond paired with a[birthstone](https://www.brilliantearth.com/jewelry/buying-guide/birthstones/) for an added touch of color and personalization.


The name of this design type is French for "you and me," representing two people coming together. Toi et moi settings have experienced a resurgence in popularity due to their distinct appearance, symbolic meaning, and substantial presence on the hand.

### East West Engagement Ring Settings


East west settings position the center stone horizontally across the finger rather than the traditional vertical orientation, creating an immediate, distinct visual impression. This unconventional approach works particularly well with[elongated center stone shapes](https://www.brilliantearth.com/diamond/buying-guide/shape/elongated-diamond-shape/) like[oval](https://www.brilliantearth.com/diamond/oval/) ,[emerald](https://www.brilliantearth.com/diamond/emerald-cut/) , or[marquise cuts](https://www.brilliantearth.com/diamond/marquise/) .


The horizontal stone pairs beautifully with a[simple band](https://www.brilliantearth.com/wedding-rings/womens/simple/) that emphasizes its unique position or can be enhanced with side stones that follow the same alignment. This setting offers a modern twist on classic gemstone shapes. It's an eye-catching choice that feels both unique and sophisticated.

### Cathedral Engagement Ring Settings


[Cathedral settings](https://www.brilliantearth.com/news/types-of-cathedral-ring-settings/) draw inspiration from Gothic architecture, featuring elegant metal arches that rise from the band to cradle and elevate the center stone. These arches often showcase intricate metalwork or accent stones, adding architectural detail to the setting’s design.


The elevated structure allows light to enter the center stone from below, enhancing its brilliance while creating an elegant profile that leaves space for a wedding band to sit flush underneath.

### Pavé Engagement Ring Settings


Pavé (pronounced pah-VAY) settings feature small diamonds set closely together along the band, creating the appearance of continuous sparkle and light reflection. From the French word for "paved," these rings use a specialized technique where tiny prongs or beads barely wrap over each melee diamond, securing them while exposing maximum surface area for light interaction and brilliance.


This design adds significant sparkle to the setting without overwhelming the center stone. Pavé can cover the entire band or accent specific areas of the engagement ring setting, offering flexibility in the overall design.


[Shop Pavé Engagement Rings](https://www.brilliantearth.com/engagement-rings/pave/)


### Split Shank Engagement Ring Settings


Split shank settings feature a band that divides into two or more separate strands as it approaches the center of the ring, creating negative space that frames the center stone. This architectural design type can incorporate simple polished metals for a clean, modern look or feature pavé diamonds along the divided sections for additional sparkle and dimension.


The split shank design visually enhances the appearance of both the center stone and setting, making the overall ring appear larger and more intricate. This design adds dimension and drama to the ring’s profile while prominently showcasing the center stone within the setting.

## How to Choose an Engagement Ring Style and Setting Type


Choosing an engagement ring style and setting type becomes easier with a few guiding questions. Consider these factors to narrow down your options:


- **Personal style:** Think about your everyday jewelry choices and overall aesthetic. Do you typically wear delicate or bold pieces? What colors appear most often in your wardrobe? If you're drawn to vintage fashion, you might love Art Deco details, while a minimalist aesthetic often pairs well with clean, simple designs.
- **Lifestyle needs:** Will your ring need to withstand an active job or hands-on hobbies? If so,[low-profile settings like bezels protect the center stone better than high-set prongs](https://www.brilliantearth.com/news/high-set-vs-low-set-ring/) , and simpler designs are less likely to snag or require frequent maintenance.
- **Shine preference:** How much brilliance do you want beyond the center stone? Halo and pavé settings maximize sparkle, while solitaires keep focus solely on the center stone.
- [Budget priorities](https://www.brilliantearth.com/news/how-much-to-spend-on-engagement-ring/) **:** What's your balance between center stone size and setting detail? Settings with accent stones cost more, but certain design types can make smaller center stones appear larger.
- [Wedding band compatibility](https://www.brilliantearth.com/news/how-to-match-a-wedding-band-to-your-engagement-ring/) **:** Will you want your wedding band to sit flush against your engagement ring? Some settings accommodate straight bands easily, while others require curved or custom bands.


Comparing engagement rings side by side helps you clarify your preferences. When possible, try on various rings in person, as they can look and feel quite different when worn versus in photos. An experienced jeweler can show you how different settings affect your center stone's appearance and explain trade-offs between your options.

## Engagement Ring Styles & Setting Types FAQ


### What is an engagement ring setting?


An engagement ring setting is the structure that holds the center stone in place. It includes the prongs, bezels, or other elements that secure the gemstone to the band, as well as any design features surrounding the center stone.


### What are the different styles of engagement rings?


Engagement ring styles range from vintage and antique-inspired to modern and minimalist. Other popular style categories include nature-inspired, unique, and classic. Each engagement ring style reflects different aesthetic preferences and can be combined with various setting types.


### What is the most popular engagement ring style?


Classic solitaire rings remain the most popular choice for engagement rings. Their timeless simplicity appeals across generations, and they showcase the center stone beautifully.


### What does setting price mean for engagement rings?


Setting price refers to the cost of the metal framework and craftsmanship, separate from the center stone's cost. Settings with intricate details, additional accent stones, or complex construction techniques typically cost more than simple solitaire settings.


### What is the most classic engagement ring style?


The solitaire engagement ring with a[round brilliant diamond](https://www.brilliantearth.com/diamond/round/) is considered the most classic style. This design has remained popular for over a century due to its elegant simplicity and focus on the center stone's beauty.


### How much does an engagement ring setting cost?


Engagement ring settings without the center stone included typically range from a few hundred dollars to a few thousands or more, depending on metal type, design complexity, and whether accent stones are included.
