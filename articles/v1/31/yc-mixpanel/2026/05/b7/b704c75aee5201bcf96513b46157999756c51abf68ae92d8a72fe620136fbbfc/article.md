# MXP London Startup Hackathon & Masterclass: Build Prototypes and Master Product Strategy in One Day (May 19)

🇬🇧


If you're based in London, this one's for you!


MXP London: Startup Hackathon & Masterclass


📅


Tuesday, 19 May |


📍


Mixpanel London HQ, The Dock @ TIDE Bankside |


🆓


Free to attend We're hosting a one-day, product experience in partnership with


ProductX


and


Replit,


and there are still a handful of spots left. This isn't your typical hackathon. You'll go from strategy to working prototype in a single day, all built around one question:


how do you make better product decisions when AI has changed how fast everyone can build?


Here's how the day breaks down


👇


🧠


Morning masterclass with ProductX:


a live session on Product Strategy in the AI Era. Why traditional prioritization frameworks are breaking down, and what a sharper decision-making framework actually looks like in practice.


💡


Hackathon sessions:


work in small teams to define a problem, design a solution, and build a working prototype. The challenge prompt is revealed on the day, so no idea or team needed beforehand!


🔍


Expert product analysis:


after presentations, a panel of experts will breakdown each team's solution, connecting the product choices back to real business outcomes.


The stakes


🏆


-


Winning team is announced during the


MXP London main conference keynote on 21 May


and takes home a cash prize


-


Every participant gets a


free month of Replit Core


Spots are limited to keep it tight and collaborative. If you're a founder, PM, or growth lead in London, come spend a Tuesday with us.
