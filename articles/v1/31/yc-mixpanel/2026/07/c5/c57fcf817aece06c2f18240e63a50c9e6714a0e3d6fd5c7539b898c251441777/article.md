📣


Mixpanel is hosting a live session on July 30 — and it's one worth showing up for.

Context Engine


— grounds AI in your actual business logic, not generic assumptions


-


Mixpanel Agent


— your always-on analyst for root cause, KPI monitoring, and dashboards


-


MCP


— brings product intelligence into Slack, Claude, Cursor, and wherever your team works


-


Mixpanel Headless


— embeds that intelligence into your own tools and workflows


Live demos. Live Q&A. If you've been curious about what "AI-native analytics" actually looks like in practice, this is the session.
