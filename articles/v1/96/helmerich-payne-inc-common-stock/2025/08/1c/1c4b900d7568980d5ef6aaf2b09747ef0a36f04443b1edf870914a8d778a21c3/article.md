# Chay Chinsethagid Named Executive of the Year by Executive Women International


Chay Chinsethagid, senior vice president of global IT and FlexRig engineering, was honored with the 2025 Executive of the Year Award from the Tulsa Chapter of Executive Women International (EWI) on Aug. 14.


Chinsethagid’s recognition stems from his exceptional approach to leadership and his commitment to empowering others within the organization. Most notably, he has consistently supported his Executive Assistant, Trish Richey, in taking on significant leadership roles within EWI, including serving on the chapter board and as chapter president.


**Professional Excellence and Community Impact**


The award recognizes Chinsethagid’s distinctive leadership style that combines sharp decision-making with deep empathy. Colleagues describe him as a leader who remains grounded in data while balancing logic with courage, approaching even the most challenging decisions with transparency and integrity. His leadership philosophy extends beyond simply guiding a team to truly inspiring one.


As a servant leader, Chinsethagid is known for putting his team first and building a culture of trust and collaboration. His approach focuses on lifting others up and helping them succeed, earning him deep respect from colleagues who praise his positive energy and unwavering dedication.


Chinsethagid’s selection for this award reflects his professional accomplishments, his commitment to fostering leadership development and creating opportunities for others to excel within the EWI community.


**About Executive Women International**


EWI is a distinguished professional women’s organization with chapters spanning the United States and Canada. The organization brings together key individuals from diverse businesses to promote member firms, enhance personal and professional development, and encourage community involvement. The Tulsa Chapter, established in 1956, stands as EWI’s largest chapter with 72 member firms comprised of executives and representatives.


**H&P’s Long-Standing Partnership**


H&P has been an active and involved member firm of EWI’s Tulsa Chapter since 1984. The company’s current leadership team includes executives John Lindsay, Kevin Vann, Cara Hair, Chay Chinsethagid and Mike Lennox, along with representatives Kari Crowder, Heather Steven, Trish Richey and Amanda Richardson. The firm also maintains connections through retired and sustaining representatives Jamie Harris and Roberta Montgomery.


This marks a milestone in the company’s relationship with EWI, as H&P previously shared the Executive of the Year honor in 1996 when Walter H. Helmerich III and Hans Helmerich were jointly recognized for their contributions.
