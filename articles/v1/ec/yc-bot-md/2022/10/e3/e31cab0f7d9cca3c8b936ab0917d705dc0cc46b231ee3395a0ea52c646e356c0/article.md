# Bot MD participates in the launch of Health - Women in Tech Community at IDMS (Jakarta)

# Bot MD participates in the launch of
Health - Women in Tech Community at IDMS (Jakarta)


JAKARTA, INDONESIA.
19 October 2022


Dorothea Koh, CEO and Co-Founder of Bot MD will participate in the launch of Health - Women in Tech community at the International Digital Medic Summit 2022 held in Jakarta, Indonesia, where she will also introduce the Bot MD Care A.I. platform that is integrated with WhatsApp to automate patient monitoring, engagement and education at scale in Indonesia.


With the Bot MD Care platform, clinical teams in hospitals and clinics no longer need to call patients and can focus on patient care. In Indonesia, Bot MD Care has been used by leading hospitals and clinics to automate their patient workflows and engagement. Over 5,000 patients have been managed on the Bot MD Care platform to track their vital signs and symptoms across a range of clinical conditions including chronic diseases such as hypertension, diabetes and kidney diseases.


### "As a female founder, I am delighted to be part of the launch of the Health - Women in Tech community at the IDMS 2022 summit. I truly believe that women can make a huge contribution and impact to developing and innovating new technologies for the healthcare setting. We are also excited to showcase how our Bot MD Care A.I. powered WhatsApp platform has helped Indonesian hospitals and clinics scale better quality care and education to more patients in Indonesia."


Ms Dorothea Koh


CEO and Co-Founder,
Bot MD


Bot MD Care helps hospitals and clinics to automate chronic patient medication reminders, follow-up visits and education on recommended lifestyle and activity adjustments.


After patients are enrolled on the Bot MD Care platform, they will automatically receive instant messages in WhatsApp, reminding them to submit their vital signs such as blood pressure and blood glucose levels and return to the clinic for medication refill as well as regular medical test and reviews. When abnormal levels are recorded, the platform will automatically trigger an alert to the clinical team so they only need to attend to the patients who need help. The platform also charts the trends in patients' vital sign submissions including blood pressure, blood glucose.


Hospitals and clinics in Indonesia that are using Bot MD Care include Siloam Hospitals, ASHA IVF, Education Hospital Syiah Kuala University, Kortex and Granostic. The Bot MD Care platform was among the winning innovations of the World Health Organization's Western Pacific Innovation Challenge 2022.
