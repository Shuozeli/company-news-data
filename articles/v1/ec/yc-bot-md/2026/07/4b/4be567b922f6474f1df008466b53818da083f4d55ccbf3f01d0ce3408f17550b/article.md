# ASHA IVF partners with Bot MD to launch the first A.I. powered WhatsApp IVF patient engagement platform in Indonesia

### About Bot MD


Bot MD is a Y-Combinator backed startup with the mission of Empowering Doctors around the world. The company’s proprietary A.I. chat engine is able to integrate with hospital information systems to provide instant answers to doctor and patient queries. Bot MD can also power popular messaging platforms such as WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger, Viber and LINE to help doctors easily automate patient workflows. Since 2018, Bot MD has been used by over 18,000 clinicians from leading hospitals and clinics around the world including the National University Health System, the National University Cancer Institute, Singapore, Alexandra Hospital, Khoo Teck Puat Hospital, Changi General Hospital, Parkway Radiology, the National Kidney Transplant Institute and Siloam Hospitals. For more information, visit www.botmd.com


### About ASHA IVF Indonesia


ASHA IVF Indonesia is one-stop service center for fertility. We provide services supported by the latest technology in the world in the field of fertility with wholehearted service from all staff. The technologies we offer include IMSI, time-lapse incubator, PGTA, endometrial receptivity analysis and other supporting technologies that will support the success of the pregnancy program. As our clinical standards continue to improve, we also understand our patients' needs for an enjoyable and humane experience. Each couple who undergoes a pregnancy program at ASHA IVF Indonesia will be accompanied by a nurse who will be with the patient from the beginning of the program to the end. ASHA IVF has arranged for its nurse to be a friend of the patient during the pregnancy program.
