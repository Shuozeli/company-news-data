# MGIC Investment Corporation Announces an Increase to Quarterly Dividend

MILWAUKEE


, July 24, 2025


/PRNewswire/ -- MGIC Investment Corporation (NYSE:MTG ) announced that its Board of Directors declared a quarterly cash dividend of $0.15


per share, an increase of 15% from the last quarterly dividend paid of $0.13


per share. This quarter's dividend will be payable on August 21, 2025


, to shareholders of record as of August 7, 2025


.
