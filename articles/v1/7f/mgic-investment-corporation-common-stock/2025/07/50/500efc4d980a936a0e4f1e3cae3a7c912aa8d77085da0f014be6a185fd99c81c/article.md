# MGIC Investment Corporation Schedules 2nd Quarter 2025 Earnings Call

MILWAUKEE


, July 7, 2025

. A conference call/webcast has been scheduled for 9:30 a.m. Eastern Time on Thursday


, July 31, 2025, to discuss the Company's results for the quarter ended June 30, 2025
