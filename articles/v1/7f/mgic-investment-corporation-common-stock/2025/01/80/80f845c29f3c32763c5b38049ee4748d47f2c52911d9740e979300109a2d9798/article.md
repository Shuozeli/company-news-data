# MGIC Investment Corporation Schedules 4th Quarter 2024 Earnings Call

MILWAUKEE


, Jan. 6, 2025


/PRNewswire/ -- MGIC Investment Corporation (NYSE:MTG ) has announced plans to release its fourth quarter 2024 financial results after the market closes on Monday, February 3


, 2025. A conference call/webcast has been scheduled for 10:00 a.m. Eastern Time on Tuesday


, February 4, 2025, to discuss the Company's results for the quarter ended December 31, 2024


.

It is recommended that you join the call at least 10 minutes before the conference call begins. The call is also being webcast and can be accessed via the Company's Investor website found at[http://mtg.mgic.com](https://c212.net/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4334614-1&h=2336063901&u=http%3A%2F%2Fmtg.mgic.com%2F&a=http%3A%2F%2Fmtg.mgic.com) under Newsroom.
