# MGIC Investment Corporation Announces Quarterly Dividend of $0.15 Per Share

MILWAUKEE


, Oct. 23, 2025


/PRNewswire/ -- MGIC Investment Corporation (NYSE:MTG ) announced that its Board of Directors declared a quarterly cash dividend of $0.15 per share, payable on November 20, 2025, to shareholders of record as of November 6, 2025.
