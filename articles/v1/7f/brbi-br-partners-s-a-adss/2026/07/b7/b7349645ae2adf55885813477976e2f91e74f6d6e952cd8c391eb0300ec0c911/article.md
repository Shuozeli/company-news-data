# BR Partners maintains a positive outlook with improvements in the pipeline and a focus on execution

Itaú BBA reiterated its "outperform" recommendation for BR Partners (BVMF:BRBI11) units, with a target price of R$ 22 for 2026, assessing the company as a resilient investment thesis in the capital markets, supported by revenue diversification and consistent execution.


According to the bank, even with the mergers and acquisitions (M&A) market still weak, BR Partners maintains profitability above 20% thanks to the performance of the debt market (DCM), restructurings and special situations, in addition to the prudent use of the balance sheet to generate recurring revenue.


"Even in a volatile DCM environment, the prudent and opportunistic use of the balance sheet has allowed for recurring revenue, demonstrating an execution capacity that few peers can match," the analysts stated.


According to WarrenAI, an AI financial assistant that uses real-time data from Investing.com to answer investors' market questions:


“BRBI11 stock is trading at R$18.92, with a cumulative increase of 48.6% in 12 months and an impressive dividend yield of 8.9% — but analyst consensus already sees some of this optimism priced in, projecting only R$22.40 as the average target price.”


At 1:15 PM Brasília time, BR Partners units were trading up 1.78%, at R$ 18.90. However, fair-price estimates from InvestingPro, a premium data and analytics platform with AI-trained tools, point to a possible 21.7% drop in the investment bank's shares, based on 3 valuation models.


The Itaú BBA report highlights signs of recovery in the M&A pipeline for 2026, thanks to more attractive valuations, lower interest rates, and still liquid debt markets, which could unlock pent-up volumes and increase investment banking revenues by approximately 25%.


The bank points out that DCM remains a structural pillar of results, with fixed income issuances at record levels and volumes of R$ 11.2 billion for BR Partners in the last 12 months, supported by strong performance in infrastructure and corporate liability management.


The Wealth area has been gaining traction as a source of recurring revenue, with net inflows of R$1.2 billion in the last 12 months, WuM/AuM of R$6 billion and revenues of R$11 million, with expectations of acceleration as liquidity events in M&A recover.


The bank raised its estimates for 2026, projecting 17% profit growth, 22% ROE and operational efficiency gains, with expenses growing below revenues. The units trade at approximately 9 times the estimated 2026 earnings.
