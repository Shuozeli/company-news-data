# BR Partners reports profit of R$ 45.2 million in the second quarter and will list ADRs on Nasdaq

BR Partners reported a net profit of R$ 45.2 million in the second quarter, representing a 4.8% increase compared to the previous quarter and a 13.0% decrease compared to the same period in 2024. Revenue rose 9.3% in the quarter and fell 1.9% year-on-year to R$ 139.3 million.


The investment banking and capital markets area, which handles mergers and acquisitions (M&A) and debt issuances (DCM), generated revenues of R$ 73.9 million, a quarterly decrease of 5.9% and an annual decrease of 17.3%.


In "treasury sales & structuring" (an area encompassing treasury solutions for clients), revenue was R$ 23.9 million, a quarterly increase of 38.9% and an annual increase of 26.8%.


Asset management yielded an additional R$3.8 million, a quarterly increase of 13.7% and an annual increase of 12.8%. Assets under management reached R$5.455 billion, a decrease of 1.4% in the quarter and an increase of 47.1% in one year. The bank's initial goal was to reach R$10 billion by the end of 2025, which was later revised to between R$7 billion and R$8 billion.


Vinícius Carmona, director of investor relations, emphasizes that achieving this new wealth management target will be challenging. "We are beginning to enter a phase where the growth rate becomes more difficult; now we have to take clients who are already very well-structured in other banks."


In any case, the second quarter ended up being less bad than BR Partners feared. Carmona explains that the market for investment banking activity is experiencing its worst moment in five years, but that the capital markets segment surprised positively.


"Activity remained quite strong, especially for our business, which is more structured debt, corporate finance. We felt a reduction in the number of transactions, but still with excellent opportunities, with deals with better margins."


According to him, this dynamic should not change much for the second half of the year. "In large issuances, there was even a 'rush' in the market, with clients closing deals before a possible taxation of incentivized instruments next year. But that's not really the game we play. We do smaller, more structured operations. We do a lot of CRI (Real Estate Receivables Certificates), for example, and we have seen the real estate market doing well, especially in high-end developments."


From April to June, BR Partners' adjusted return on equity (ROAE) was 22.6%, compared to 21.6% in the first quarter and 24.9% in the second quarter of 2024. “If, in a bad moment in the market cycle, we managed to deliver a 22% ROE in the first half of the year, that makes us satisfied. If things start to improve, we have a very strong pipeline that can take off. We have the arsenal ready; what's lacking is the opportunity to use it,” says Carmona.


**Nasdaq Listing**
Last month, BR Partners announced the creation of an ADR program to list its receipts on Nasdaq. Among the objectives are to increase the liquidity of the stock, facilitate access for international investors, and reduce the discount in its valuation compared to American rivals, for example.


According to the Investor Relations director, the bank already has all the necessary approvals from the SEC (the American equivalent of the CVM) and Nasdaq, and the debut of its shares on the American market should occur in mid-September. “We are a small company, so we have a two-year transition period to comply with all the regulations. Therefore, the biggest cost comes after that. We know it's a bold path, but we want to have a more prominent role in the market.”


He points out that being listed on the American market will give BR Partners more visibility, and the bank is interested in eventually doing business with foreign investment banks. “We've already mentioned that we are interested in growing in certain verticals, but that it can be difficult to do so alone. In ECM \[equity issuances\], if we have a partnership with a player that already has the structure in place, a brokerage firm, it becomes much easier.”
