# BR Partners is expected to increase the number of Brazilian companies listed on the US stock exchange

The movement of Brazilian companies towards the US stock market continues at a strong pace. This Friday, July 18th, the investment bank BR Partners announced that it has approved the start of the issuance of American Depositary Receipts (ADRs), which is the issuance of shares listed in Brazil on the US market. According to NeoFeed, the American listing should occur in September and the expectation is to triple the liquidity of the company's shares in Brazil. The company held its IPO in June 2021, when it raised R$ 400 million.


The average daily trading volume on the B3 (Brazilian Stock Exchange) is around R$ 4 million. In 2025, the shares ranged between R$ 11.75 and R$ 17.05. This Friday, the 18th, it reached R$ 16.85. The company is valued at R$ 1.76 billion. The move to the American market is also due to the fact that there are investors specializing in financials and small caps in the United States and Europe who are showing interest in Brazilian assets. Even with the move to the United States, the company will remain listed on the B3 (Brazilian stock exchange).


The market reacted well to the company's announced decision. At the start of trading on the São Paulo stock exchange, around 10:30 am, the company's shares were up 1.33%, trading at R$ 16.77. Year-to-date, the shares have appreciated by 29%.


A survey conducted at the time by the consulting firm Elos Ayta showed that Brazilian companies traded an average of US$ 2.19 billion per day on US stock exchanges. And there is a trend for this volume to grow in the coming months. The pioneer of this movement was the fintech company Nubank, which opted for a dual listing, entering the American market in 2021. Shortly after, it delisted from the B3 (Brazilian stock exchange), offering only BDR (Brazilian Depositary Receipts) issuances through the B3.


Recently, the Brazilian company JBS, a global leader in animal protein production, went public on the US stock exchange to complete its second listing. In the first few days, its daily trading volume in the United States reached 51% higher than on the Brazilian stock exchange.


In any case, some ADRs (American Depositary Receipts) of Brazilian companies on the American stock exchange have been experiencing a decline since Trump's announcement on July 9th that he would tax Brazil starting August 1st. The aircraft manufacturer Embraer, one of the companies that will be most impacted if the tax determined by Trump is effectively applied, saw its ADRs fall 9% in trading after the White House announcement. This Friday, the company's shares on the NYSE are down 1.47%.
