# BR Partners reports profit of R$ 44.5 million in the 4th quarter, a year-on-year increase of 5.7%

BR Partners reported a net profit of R$ 44.5 million in the fourth quarter, representing a 5.5% increase compared to the previous quarter and a 5.7% increase compared to the same period in 2024. Revenue fell 1.5% in the quarter and 8.7% year-on-year to R$ 131.3 million. Return on average equity (ROAE) was 22.4%.


The investment banking and capital markets area, responsible for mergers and acquisitions (M&A) and debt issuances (DCM), generated revenues of R$ 84.4 million in the fourth quarter, a quarterly increase of 25.7% and stability in the annual variation. In "treasury sales & structuring" (an area encompassing treasury solutions for clients), revenue was R$ 16.8 million, a quarterly decrease of 41.4% and an annual decrease of 45.4%. Asset management yielded an additional R$ 4.5 million, a quarterly increase of 11.2% and an annual increase of 32.0%. Assets under management reached R$ 5.926 billion, down 0.2% in the quarter and 14.7% year-on-year. Revenue from client services (for clients) was R$ 16.8 million, a quarterly decrease of 41.4% and an annual decrease of 45.4%.


Vinícius Carmona, director of investor relations, says that the pipeline of mergers and acquisitions (M&A) operations, which had already been showing strength in recent months, has begun to materialize. "We went almost a year without major operations, then the pipeline improved, but conversion was still poor. Now conversion has started to move and we expect this to continue."


**Elections and World Cup**


Danilo Catarucci, partner responsible for the capital markets area, says that the strong flow of foreign investment into Brazil and even the resumption of IPOs are positive signs for fixed income. The year started strong, according to him, even with the anticipation that occurred at the end of 2025 with many companies trying to avoid new taxes. "I think we will have a good first semester, with many companies avoiding the volatility of the elections and the World Cup in the second half of the year."


In the wealth management area, BR Partners initially estimated that it would reach R$ 10 billion in assets under management by the end of 2025, but later revised that target. Now, they want to surpass that volume by the end of 2026.
