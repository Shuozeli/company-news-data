# BR Partners (BRBI11): Efficiency is a positive highlight in results in line with expectations in Q2 2025

On Thursday (7), after the market closed, BR Partners (BRBI11) released its second quarter 2025 (2Q25) figures, which came in line with expectations.
Highlights include profit before tax, which exceeded the average market projection by 8%, growing 18% sequentially and +10% annually. The bottom line, of R$ 45 million (+5% q/q and -13% y/y), had a more modest expansion due to higher corporate taxes. Still, ROE had a sequential expansion of 1 point, to 22.6%. The quarter marks a recovery in capital market issuances compared to the first months of the year, penalized by risk aversion.


**See BR Partners' results by vertical**


Revenue totaled R$ 139 million, with sequential growth of 9%, but 2% below the same period of the previous year.
Highlights include Treasury Sales & Structuring (+39% q/q and +27% y/y), benefiting from demand for hedging instruments for debt issuances.
Capital Remuneration (+33% q/q and +24% y/y) also contributed to the expansion, driven by the highest CDI rate of the period, as did the Wealth Management vertical (+14% q/q and +13% y/y), benefiting from R$ 1.7 billion in fundraising. These areas offset the more timid performance of Investment Banking & Capital Markets (-6% q/q and -17% y/y), despite weak M&A activity, even though capital market issuances recovered sequentially.


**Efficiency is a positive highlight**
Efficiency was the positive highlight of the result, with total expenses growing below revenue: +3% sequentially and -10% annually. The margin gain came from strong reductions in personnel (-11% y/y) and administrative (-40%), which we see as positive and symbolic of the company's cost discipline.
As a result, profit before taxes showed strong growth, to R$ 64 million (+18% q/q and +10% y/y). However, the revenue mix, more oriented towards areas that use balance sheets (Treasury and Capital Remuneration), led to a loss of tax efficiency.


These areas are taxed at a higher income tax rate; therefore, the bottom line expanded by 5% sequentially, to R$ 45 million, still 13% below 2Q24. Nevertheless, ROE expanded by 1 p.p. sequentially, to 22.6%, a very healthy level.


**BRBI11: Neutral Result, but with Positive Surprises**


Overall, we consider the result neutral, with the positive surprise in expenses offset by lower tax efficiency. The company announced the launch of its American Depositary Receipts (ADRs) program on Nasdaq, which allows foreign investors to buy the company's stock directly from abroad.


We view this move favorably, as it could lead to an expansion of BRBI11's trading multiple, currently at 7.0x (P/E 2026). We maintain our buy recommendation for the stock.
