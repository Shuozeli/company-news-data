# After attending Nasdaq, BR Partners will have a schedule of presentations in Europe

BR Partners will hold a series of meetings with investors in Europe in early 2026. With shares listed on Nasdaq two months ago, in addition to B3, where it has had shares since 2021, the investment bank wants to increase the base of foreign investors holding the company's shares.
In recent months, BR Partners has already held meetings, known in the market as non-deal roadshows, in cities in the United States, such as Salt Lake City, and in Canada, such as Montreal, said the Director of Investor Relations and Institutional Affairs, Vinicius Carmona. All of this will help prepare the ground with foreign investors for when the bank wants to make a new share offering. According to Carmona, this offering should not happen now or next year.


The strategy of attracting more investors to buy BR Partners shares has caused the base of this public in the bank's shares to go from zero to 18%. The biggest step was launching American Depositary Receipts (ADRs) on Nasdaq in early September. “In two months, the visibility has been impressive,” said Carmona. He recounts that, during this period, the trading volume of the stock has increased, both in the United States and on the B3 (Brazilian stock exchange). Furthermore, the Nasdaq listing itself is a seal that opens doors with investors in other countries, he said at an event Nasdaq held in São Paulo this Wednesday, the 5th, to attract Brazilian companies, as previously reported by this column.


**More chances of offerings in Brazil in 2026**


For Brazil, Carmona sees the chance of more stock offerings in 2026, but only from already listed companies, the so-called follow-ons. “If there is a minimum of fiscal responsibility with falling interest rates, there could be a good window for stocks.” According to the bank, the Selic rate could fall between 2.5 and 3 points next year, to around 12%. Falling interest rates here and in the US should help attract more foreign investors to Brazil, the executive emphasizes.
