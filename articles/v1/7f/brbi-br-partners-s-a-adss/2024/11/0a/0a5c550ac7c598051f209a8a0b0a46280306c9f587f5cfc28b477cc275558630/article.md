# BR Partners announces extraordinary dividend

# BR Partners announces extraordinary dividend


BR Partners


Original article:[Access link](https://www.brpartners.com.br/en-US/home.html)


NOVEMBER 27, 2024


2 minutes of reading


Christmas came early at BR Partners.


The investment bank led by Ricardo Lacerda has just announced an extraordinary dividend of R$81.9 million - equivalent to R$0.78 per unit traded on the stock exchange, which closed today at R$14.75.


This distribution adds to another R$122.8 million that the company had already announced this year and raises the dividend yield for the year to 13.6% - higher than today's Selic rate.


Since its IPO, the company has paid R$488 million in dividends, more than it raised in the offering (R$400 million) and almost a third of its current market cap.


BR Partners shares will trade ex-dividend on December 5, and payment will take place on the 27th of the same month.


BR Partners decided to make the extraordinary distribution because it had excess capital after completing two Financial Bills (Letras Financeiras) issuances that extended and diversified its liabilities.


The company raised R$160 million with a subordinated Financial Bill in July, which increased its Basel ratio by 3.4 percentage points. At the end of September, it raised another R$213 million, raising its Basel ratio by an additional 4.6 percentage points.


Today the Basel ratio stands at 25.2%, but should fall about 90-100 basis points after the extraordinary distribution, according to one analyst's calculations.


The extraordinary dividend will also increase the company's ROE by between 50 and 100 basis points.


BR Partners' move also appears to be in anticipation of potential dividend taxation, a topic that has been increasingly discussed.


Other companies have been making similar moves. Itaú has not yet announced an extraordinary dividend, but CEO Milton Maluhy said after third-quarter results that the bank will "certainly" pay extraordinary dividends this year.


XP also increased shareholder remuneration in the third quarter, distributing R$2 billion in dividends and R$1 billion in share buybacks.


BR Partners is valued at R$1.6 billion on B3 and trades at 7 times estimated earnings for 2025.
