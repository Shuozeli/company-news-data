# BRBI11 issues Financial Letters of R$124.2 million

# BRBI11 issues Financial Letters of R$124.2 million


BR Partners


Original article:[Access link](https://www.brpartners.com.br/en-US/home.html)


APRIL 03, 2025


1 minute of reading


BRBI BR Partners (BRBI11) reported that BR Partners Banco de Investimento, a wholly-owned subsidiary of the company, issued Subordinated Perpetual Financial Notes totaling R$124.2 million in private negotiations with institutional investors.


The Financial Notes are perpetual with repurchase options every 5 years from the date of issuance, subject to prior authorization from the Central Bank of Brazil.
