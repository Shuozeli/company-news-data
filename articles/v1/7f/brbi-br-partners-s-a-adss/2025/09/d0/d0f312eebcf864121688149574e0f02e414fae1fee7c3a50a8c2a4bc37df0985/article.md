# BR Partners Debuts on Nasdaq Focusing on Global Funds

# BR Partners Debuts on Nasdaq Focusing on Global Funds


BR Partners


Original article:[Access link](https://forbes.com.br/forbes-money/2025/09/br-partners-estreia-na-nasdaq-de-olho-em-fundos-globais/)


SEPTEMBER 18, 2025


4 minutes of reading


By ringing the closing bell on Tuesday (17) at Nasdaq, BR Partners CEO Ricardo Lacerda symbolically marked the listing of the investment bank's shares on the second largest stock exchange in the American market, and also one of the largest in the world.


His intention is that the ringing will be heard around the world and attract global investors interested in investing in Brazilian companies without needing to operate directly in the local market. With this, BR Partners intends to expand the company's exposure, advertised as the largest investment bank in Latin America, to a more diversified shareholder base.


According to a statement from the bank, the listing was carried out through the American Depositary Receipts (ADRs) program, the certificates traded in the United States that represent shares of foreign companies.


The objective is to expand the shareholder base and thus strengthen its attractiveness to foreign funds. Now, BR Partners has a dual listing, as it has also been on the B3 since June 2021, when it held its IPO and raised R$ 400 million.


Over the past 15 years, BR Partners has consolidated an independent and highly profitable business model, even in the face of challenging economic scenarios. Being on Nasdaq is another milestone in this trajectory, which reinforces our position among the leading financial institutions in Latin America,” says Vinicius Carmona, the bank's Investor Relations director, in a statement issued by his press office.


With BR Partners' entry into Nasdaq, BR Partners will also be regulated by the SEC, the US equivalent of the CVM.
Another objective announced by the bank when issuing its shares listed in Brazil on the US market and gaining greater international visibility is to seek better recognition among its peers already listed there and a fairer valuation of its shares, especially in a scenario where it will have other independent banks.


Background and projections


Upon debuting on the B3 (Brazilian Stock Exchange) on June 21, 2021, the shares were priced at R$ 16, closing at R$ 16.55 (up 3.44%) on its first trading day. According to B3 data available at 11:15 am this Thursday, the units reached R$ 17.98, a decrease of 0.39%. Data for the second quarter of 2025 indicates that BR Partners achieved total revenue of R$ 139.3 million, a figure that represents a 9.3% increase compared to the previous quarter. However, compared to the same period in 2024, it registered a decrease of 1.9%, impacted by lower activity in mergers and acquisitions, one of the pillars of the bank's operations.


Highlights included performance in treasury sales & structuring, which advanced 38.9% in the quarter, and capital remuneration, benefited by the higher CDI rate and the effect of securities carrying.


One of the segments in which the bank showed expansion was wealth management, with a 13.7% increase in the period, with net inflows of R$ 1.7 billion.


Net profit totaled R$ 45.2 million in the quarter, a growth of 4.9% over the previous period. Return on average equity (ROAE) reached 22.6%, with a Basel Index of 21.4%.
In a conference call with XP analysts in August, shortly after the release of first-half results this year, Lacerda and Carmona stated that the bank has found strength especially in the fixed income market.


According to the text, with a strategy of independence in origination, without compromising its own balance sheet, the institution has consolidated itself as a benchmark in refinancing operations, ensuring resilience even in an environment of high Selic rates. The issuance schedule remains robust, and the focus on niches with greater complexity and additional guarantees, aimed at institutional investors and family offices, ensures healthier margins and recurring revenue.


The executives also addressed the progress in structured products, such as FIDCs and CRIs. For the next steps, the priority is to expand participation in investment banking, DCM, treasury, and wealth management.
