## Why Choosing a Clover Preferred Provider Matters


Choosing the right doctor is one of the most important decisions you can make for your health. You want a provider who not only cares about you but also has the best tools to help you stay well as you age. That's where Clover’s Preferred Providers come in. These doctors use Clover Assistant, which helps them provide better care for Clover members.


## What is the Clover Assistant?


Think of Clover Assistant like a smart helper for your doctor. It helps your doctor get a complete view of your health. It pulls all your available medical information, like past lab results, prescriptions, and hospital visits, into one place right when you’re in the office. This lets your doctor easily see a fuller picture of your health, no matter where you get care. They can receive important alerts and reminders, so you get the best, most personal care possible.


## How Clover Assistant Helps You


When your doctor uses Clover Assistant, you can benefit in some really big ways. The results speak for themselves. Studies show how Clover Assistant can help providers enhance their patients’ wellness, especially for those with chronic conditions. Doctors using Clover Assistant have accomplished some amazing things for Clover members, including:


### Getting Ahead of Health Problems


Clover Assistant helps doctors find and treat problems early. This means better health management.


**Diabetes Care:** Doctors start treating patients with diabetes on average three years earlier.¹ This can help lead to better health, less reliance on insulin, and lower incidence of hypoglycemia later on.


**Kidney Disease Detection:** Doctors have found kidney disease one and a half years earlier.²


**Less Time in the Hospital:** Clover Assistant helps providers keep patients out of the hospital. This means less time at a facility and more time at home.³


**Fewer Hospital Stays for Heart Condition:** Patients with congestive heart failure have 18% fewer hospital stays and 25% fewer readmissions.³


**Fewer Hospital Returns for Lung Condition:** Patients with COPD have 15% fewer all-cause inpatient hospital stays and 18% fewer 30-day readmissions.⁴


**More Preventative Care:** Clover Assistant helps doctors make sure you get important checkups. This means better preventative care.


**More Patient Screenings:** Patients are getting 11% more colon cancer screenings and almost 5% more breast cancer screenings. ⁵
With this kind of support, your doctor can spend less time looking for information and more time talking with you. That means a better visit for you and a more personalized health plan.
