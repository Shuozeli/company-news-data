## Off-Lease Inventory


An ”off-lease” car is simply a vehicle that was previously leased. Lease agreements — which typically range from two to four years — often have mileage restrictions and require regular documented maintenance, making off-lease vehicles a worry-free (and often frontline-ready) option for stocking your lot.


On OPENLANE, you’ll find thousands of new exclusive off-lease vehicles options available daily, sourced from OEM, captive finance and rental companies.


### Why Dealers Win with OPENLANE Off-Lease Inventory


- **Exclusive Access** . Get fast, easy and early access to high-quality inventory including 70% of North America’s off-lease vehicles, directly from the source.
- **Confidence in Condition** . Every dealer trade vehicle on OPENLANE undergoes a detailed inspection by an experienced inspector to generate a condition report for each vehicle. This includes a test drive, On-Board Diagnostics II (OBD2) scan translated to plain language by our exclusive Code Boost IQ, and high-quality 360° photos enhanced by Visual Boost AI™.
- **Peace of Mind** . Buy with confidence thanks to our As Described Guarantee available for purchase on most vehicles.
- **Competitive Pricing** . Save money with the most competitive buy fees* in the industry, allowing you to invest more in your business.


## Dealer Trades

## Rental and Fleet Vehicles


OPENLANE also partners with rental and fleet companies to offer a unique inventory channel for dealers seeking reliable, well-maintained vehicles. These vehicles come from reputable sources and are typically held to high maintenance standards, meaning their condition is above average for their age and mileage.


Fleet vehicles often have highway miles, which typically generate less wear-and-tear than city miles. They also provide dealers with a wide range of makes, models and trim levels. By incorporating rental and fleet vehicles into your inventory strategy, you can grow your lot, attract a broader customer base, and boost your dealership’s appeal.


### Why Dealers Win with OPENLANE’s Rental and Fleet Vehicles
