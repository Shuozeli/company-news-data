# How to Plan a Road Trip in India: Complete Guide

Everyone wants to know how to plan a road trip the moment the weather turns pleasant and the group chat starts buzzing with getaway ideas. Search interest around the topic has jumped sharply this week, and so has interest in the apps that keep the drive organised.


A good trip plan covers more than a destination pin on a map. It settles the route, the budget, the paperwork and the backup options before day one arrives, so nothing gets decided in a parking lot at six in the morning.


# **How to Plan a Road Trip Route Without Overestimating Your Time**


Anyone figuring out how to plan a road trip for the first time tends to pick the destination first and the days second, which is backwards. Fix the number of days you actually have, then work backwards to see how far that realistically takes you.


Indian highways look fast on Google Maps and feel much slower once you hit a market town, a toll queue, or a stretch of NH44 doing repair work. A comfortable driving day for most families sits around 300 to 350 kilometres, not the 500 the map suggests.


A few things worth locking down early:


- **Origin and destination:** Decide the anchor points first, then let intermediate stops fill in around them.
- **Buffer days:** Add one extra day for every three days of driving. Weather, festivals and closed ghats do not check your itinerary first.
- **Night driving:** Avoid it on hill routes and unfamiliar state highways. Visibility, stray cattle and poor road markings make the risk not worth it.


## **Setting a Road Trip Budget Before You Get Attached to a Destination**


Fuel is usually the smallest line item people overestimate and the biggest one they actually underestimate once tolls, parking and the inevitable extra night get added in. A rough Indian road trip budget for a group of four in a sedan usually breaks down like this:

Book stays with free cancellation until closer to the date. Weather and road conditions change fast enough in hill states that a rigid, non refundable booking made three weeks out often ends up wasted money.


## **Best Road Trip Apps to Keep the Whole Trip Organised**


This is where the apps come in, since half of how to plan a road trip well is picking the right digital toolkit before you leave rather than figuring it out at a fuel station with two bars of signal.


- **Google Maps:** Download the offline map for your entire route the night before. Signal drops fast once you leave the highway in hill states.
- **A FASTag balance app:** Low balance at a toll booth is still the most common reason for a queue jam on Indian highways. Check it before departure, not at the barrier.
- **A weather app with hourly forecasts:** Especially for hill and coastal routes, where a clear morning can turn into a landslide warning by afternoon.
- **Splitwise or a similar expense tracker:** Useful for groups splitting fuel, tolls and food, since manual tallying after day two of a trip rarely stays accurate.
- **The zingbus app:** Genuinely useful if any leg of the trip gets swapped for a bus, whether that is one tiring overnight stretch or the whole outward journey, since it handles live tracking and boarding point details in one place.

## **Sorting the Vehicle, Documents and FASTag Before You Leave**


A pre trip vehicle check takes twenty minutes and saves an entire afternoon lost to a roadside garage. Tyres, brakes, coolant and wiper fluid are the four things worth a physical look, not just a dashboard glance.


Documents to carry, physically and as a phone photo:


- **Driving licence and RC:** Both originals, since traffic police in several states still ask for the physical copy.
- **Insurance and PUC certificate:** Valid and not about to expire mid trip.
- **FASTag:** Linked and topped up, since cash lanes on national highways are becoming rarer by the year.


## **When Taking a Bus Beats Driving the Whole Stretch Yourself**


For a lot of Indian families, how to plan a road trip has quietly turned into how to plan a road trip with fewer hours behind the wheel. A full day of driving through Punjab’s flat, monotonous stretches or a hill climb after dark is exhausting in a way a map never shows.


Swapping one leg for an overnight bus, say the Delhi to Manali route, means arriving rested instead of arriving cramped from a car seat. It also removes the single biggest road trip risk entirely, which is driver fatigue on a route nobody in the car has driven before.

## **Small Habits That Keep a Road Trip From Falling Apart Midway**


- **Share the driving:** Two drivers minimum for anything over five hours. Fatigue creeps in well before anyone admits to feeling it.
- **Carry cash in small denominations:** Small dhabas and local parking attendants in smaller towns still do not accept cards or UPI reliably.
- **Keep a physical printout of key bookings:** Phone batteries and patchy signal have ruined more check ins than anyone likes to admit.
- **Pack a basic first aid kit:** Motion sickness tablets, band aids and ORS sachets cover most minor issues that come up on Indian highways.

## **Frequently Asked Questions**


### **What Is the First Step in How to Plan a Road Trip?**


The first step in how to plan a road trip is fixing your route and number of days before you look at anything else. Everything after that, budget, stops and packing, follows from that one decision.


### **How Many Kilometres Should You Drive in a Day on an Indian Road Trip?**


A comfortable daily driving distance on Indian roads is around 300 to 350 kilometres. Hill routes and monsoon conditions can cut this closer to 200 kilometres, so build the itinerary around actual road type, not straight line distance.


### **What Are the Best Road Trip Apps to Use in India?**


The best road trip apps for India cover navigation, tolls and weather together. Google Maps with offline download, a FASTag balance checker, and an hourly weather app form a solid core stack for most highway and hill routes.


### **How Much Should a Weekend Road Trip Budget Be for Two People?**


A weekend road trip budget for two people usually runs between 6,000 and 12,000 rupees, depending on the distance and stay category. Fuel and stay together typically account for over half of that total.


### **What Documents Do You Need Before a Road Trip in India?**


Documents needed before a road trip include a valid driving licence, the vehicle RC, current insurance and a valid PUC certificate. Carrying a phone photo of each alongside the originals avoids delays at checkpoints.


### **Is FASTag Mandatory for a Road Trip in India?**


FASTag is effectively mandatory on Indian national highways, since cash lanes have shrunk significantly at most toll plazas. A low or empty FASTag balance is one of the most common causes of toll queue delays.


### **What Is the Safest Time of Day to Drive on Highways in India?**


The safest driving window is generally early morning to late afternoon. Visibility drops sharply after dusk, and stray animals, unlit vehicles and poor road markings make night driving on unfamiliar highways genuinely risky.


### **How Do You Plan a Road Trip During the Monsoon?**


Planning a monsoon road trip means checking rainfall forecasts a day ahead, avoiding landslide prone hill stretches, and building in extra buffer time. Routes through the Western Ghats and lower Himalayas need the most caution during peak monsoon months.


### **Is It Safe for a Woman to Go on a Solo Road Trip in India?**


Solo road trips for women in India are increasingly common and can be done safely with preparation. Sticking to daylight driving, sharing live location with family, and choosing well reviewed stays are the basics that matter most.


### **What Should You Pack for a Two Day Road Trip?**


A two day road trip packing list should cover a basic first aid kit, phone chargers, cash in small denominations, one extra layer of clothing, and printed copies of key bookings in case signal drops.


### **When Does Taking a Bus Make More Sense Than a Self Drive Road Trip?**


Taking a bus makes more sense on long, tiring corridors like Delhi to Manali, where driver fatigue and hill roads add real risk. It also suits solo travellers who would otherwise be doing the entire drive alone.


### **How Many People Can Book a Bus Rental for a Group Road Trip?**


A group road trip bus rental can typically accommodate anywhere from twenty to fifty passengers depending on the coach size booked, making it a practical option for college trips, weddings and larger family groups.


### **What Is the Best Way to Split Road Trip Expenses With Friends?**


The best way to split road trip expenses is through a shared expense tracking app rather than manual tallying. Logging every payment as it happens avoids the awkward, error prone math that usually happens at the end of a trip.
