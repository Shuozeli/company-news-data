# Places to Visit in India in August 2026: Festivals and Dates

Anyone researching places to visit in India in august 2026 needs the festival calendar first, since five major celebrations fall this month. Independence Day and Hariyali Teej share August 15, Nag Panchami falls on August 17, Onam peaks on August 26, and Raksha Bandhan closes the month on August 28.


August also sits deep in the monsoon, so travel plans need to factor in rainfall alongside festival dates. This guide lists the exact date, region and best destination for each celebration, along with booking and weather notes.

## **August 15: Independence Day and Hariyali Teej, Best Places to Visit**


Independence Day 2026 falls on a Saturday. Hariyali Teej, a monsoon festival honouring Goddess Parvati and Lord Shiva, lands on the exact same date this year, since Teej follows the lunar Shravan month rather than a fixed calendar day.


- **Delhi:** Red Fort hosts the Prime Minister’s flag hoisting and national address, with cultural programs held across the city through the day.
- **Jaipur:** The best city for Hariyali Teej, with a dedicated Teej Mata procession, decorated swings, mehndi markets and green attire traditions.
- **Amritsar:** The Golden Temple offers a quieter, monsoon-green alternative for travellers who prefer a spiritual stop over the long weekend.


Since August 15 falls on a Saturday, it creates a natural long weekend from Delhi. This[Delhi to Manali bus trip guide](https://www.zingbus.com/blog/delhi-to-manali-bus-trip-planning-guide) covers timing, fares and boarding points for travellers using the weekend for a short hill escape.


## **August 17: Nag Panchami, Best Places to Visit**


Nag Panchami 2026 falls on Monday, August 17, observed through prayers to snake deities during the monsoon month of Shravan.


- **Ujjain:** Mahakaleshwar, one of India’s twelve Jyotirlingas, sees some of the heaviest Nag Panchami crowds in the country. This[places to visit in Ujjain guide](https://www.zingbus.com/blog/places-to-visit-in-ujjain-complete-guide) covers temple timings and nearby ghats.
- **Varanasi and Haridwar:** Both river towns hold special Shiva temple rituals on this date, alongside their regular Ganga aarti schedule.


## **August 26: Onam, Best Places to Visit**


Thiruvonam, the main day of Onam, falls on Wednesday, August 26, 2026, marking the peak of Kerala’s ten day harvest festival that begins around August 16 with Atham.


- **Kochi and Thrissur:** Host major Onam celebrations with Pookalam flower carpets, Onasadya feasts and cultural programs.
- **Alleppey:** Famous for snake boat races on the backwaters, usually timed close to the Onam period.

## **August 28: Raksha Bandhan, Best Places to Visit**


Raksha Bandhan 2026 falls on Friday, August 28, the full moon day of Shravan. Unlike Onam or Teej, it is observed pan India rather than tied to one region, which makes it better suited to short trips near home rather than long distance travel.

## **Booking and Weather Notes for August Travel**


- **Monsoon risk:** Hill routes to Manali, Mussoorie and Uttarakhand can face temporary landslide closures in August, so road status is worth checking a day before departure.
- **Book early:** Both the Independence Day weekend and Raksha Bandhan push travel demand up sharply. Booking through the zingbus app at least a week ahead helps secure preferred departure times.
- **Onam accommodation:** Kochi and Thrissur hotels fill up quickly around Thiruvonam week, so Kerala stays should be booked well in advance.
- **Pack for rain:** A light raincoat and waterproof footwear are more useful than an umbrella on hill and coastal routes in August.

## **Frequently Asked Questions**


### **What Festivals Fall in August 2026 in India?**


Five major festivals fall in August 2026: Independence Day and Hariyali Teej on August 15, Nag Panchami on August 17, Onam’s main day Thiruvonam on August 26, and Raksha Bandhan on August 28.


### **What Is the Date of Raksha Bandhan in 2026?**


Raksha Bandhan in 2026 falls on Friday, August 28. The festival is observed on the full moon day, Purnima, of the Hindu month of Shravan.


### **What Is the Date of Independence Day in 2026?**


Independence Day in 2026 falls on Saturday, August 15. It is a fixed national holiday celebrated every year on the same calendar date.


### **When Is Onam Celebrated in 2026?**


Onam celebrations begin around August 16, 2026 with Atham, and the main day, Thiruvonam, falls on Wednesday, August 26, 2026.


### **What Is the Date of Nag Panchami in 2026?**


Nag Panchami in 2026 falls on Monday, August 17. The date follows the lunar Hindu calendar and occurs during the monsoon month of Shravan.


### **What Is the Date of Hariyali Teej in 2026?**


Hariyali Teej in 2026 falls on Saturday, August 15, the same date as Independence Day this year. It follows the Shukla Paksha Tritiya of the Shravan month.


### **Which Places to Visit in India in August Are Best for Onam?**


Among places to visit in India in august for Onam, Kochi, Thrissur and Alleppey stand out, offering Pookalam displays, Onasadya feasts and the famous Kerala snake boat races.


### **Is August a Good Month to Travel in India?**


August works well for monsoon destinations like Kerala’s backwaters and North Indian temple towns, though hill routes carry landslide risk and require extra planning.


### **Does Independence Day 2026 Fall on a Long Weekend?**


Independence Day 2026 falls on a Saturday, which does not automatically create an extended weekend but still allows a short two day trip for most travellers.


### **Where Is Nag Panchami Celebrated Most Widely in India?**


Nag Panchami is celebrated widely across India, with Ujjain’s Mahakaleshwar temple and river towns like Varanasi and Haridwar seeing the largest gatherings.


### **What Should You Pack for Travel in India in August?**


Travel in India in August calls for a light raincoat, waterproof footwear, quick dry clothing and a dry bag for electronics, since most of the country is in active monsoon.


### **Which Festival in August 2026 Attracts the Most Travellers?**


Onam typically attracts the highest number of travellers in August, since Kerala sees a dedicated tourism season built around the ten day festival and its Thiruvonam finale.
