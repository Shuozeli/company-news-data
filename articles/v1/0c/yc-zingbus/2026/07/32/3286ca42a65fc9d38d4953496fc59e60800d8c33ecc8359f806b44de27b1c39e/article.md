# MG Road Bangalore Shopping Guide: What to Buy, Where to Eat, Where to Go After Dark


MG Road, originally called South Parade during British colonial rule, was renamed Mahatma Gandhi Road on 26 February 1948. It runs east to west through the heart of Bangalore’s Central Business District, connecting Trinity Circle on one end to Anil Kumble Circle on the other. The road itself is lined with retail stores, office buildings, banks, and restaurants on both sides, but what makes the area significant for visitors is the network of streets that branch off it.


Brigade Road splits south from MG Road near the midpoint and runs down to Residency Road. Church Street runs parallel on the north side, roughly 750 metres long, packed with cafes, bookstores, pubs, and restaurants. Lavelle Road and Vittal Mallya Road connect on the south side, hosting Bangalore’s luxury hotel and fine dining cluster. Commercial Street sits about 1.5 km northeast, accessible on foot or by a short auto ride. Together, these streets form the single densest zone for shopping, eating, and nightlife in the city. The area holds Cubbon Park on its northern edge and Parade Ground as a colonial era landmark.


The Purple Line of Namma Metro runs along this stretch with MG Road Metro Station and Trinity Metro Station providing direct access. That metro connectivity, combined with the concentration of activities within a 1.5 km radius, makes this the most walkable entertainment district in Bangalore.


## Shopping on MG Road: Malls, Showrooms and Street Finds


The mg road bangalore shopping guide starts with understanding the three tiers of retail the area offers. You have large air-conditioned malls carrying international and national brands, standalone showrooms selling Karnataka specific products, and street vendors offering budget accessories and clothing at negotiable prices. Each tier serves a different shopper and a different budget.


### 1MG Lido Mall


Sitting directly on MG Road at Trinity Circle, 1MG Lido Mall is the most accessible mall in the CBD. The brand mix leans mid-range to premium: H&M for basics, Fab India for Indian ethnic wear, Marks and Spencer for workwear, MAC and Estee Lauder for cosmetics, Puma and Nike for sportswear, and Aldo for footwear. The Bombay Store inside the mall carries curated Indian home decor, textiles, and gift items. Foodhall on the upper floor operates as a gourmet grocery store with imported cheeses, artisan breads, and specialty ingredients. A cinema screen, play zones, and restaurants round out the offering.


**Best for:** Mid-range branded shopping, cosmetics, casual dining, catching a movie after shopping


### UB City Mall


Located on Vittal Mallya Road, about a 10 minute walk south from MG Road, UB City is widely recognised as India’s first luxury mall. The brand roster includes Louis Vuitton, Burberry, Emporio Armani, Diesel, Paul Smith, and L’Occitane. The open air food court at UB City hosts some of Bangalore’s more talked about restaurants: Farzi Cafe for Indian fusion, Shiros for Japanese, Caperberry for modern European, and Cafe Mangii for wood fired pizzas. Sublime Galleria, an art gallery on the upper level, regularly rotates exhibitions. Skyye Lounge on the rooftop offers panoramic views of the city skyline.


**Best for:** Luxury brand shopping, fine dining, art browsing, rooftop drinks


### Garuda Mall and Brigade Road


Garuda Mall sits at the Brigade Road and Residency Road junction, roughly 500 metres from MG Road. The mall carries Zara, Calvin Klein, and a range of Indian and international brands alongside a multiplex, food court, gaming zone, and spa. Brigade Road itself is one of Bangalore’s most established retail streets. International sportswear brands like Asics, Adidas, Reebok, Skechers, and Under Armour operate standalone stores along this stretch. Indian menswear brands like Louis Philippe, Van Heusen, Arrow, and Rare Rabbit sit alongside them. For visitors interested in budget shopping, Brigade Road also has several outlets selling export surplus clothing at lower price points.


**Best for:** Mix of international and Indian brands, sportswear, multiplex entertainment, street browsing


### Cauvery Emporium and Karnataka Handicrafts


Cauvery Handicrafts Emporium is the government run store for Karnataka state handicrafts, located directly on MG Road within a 5 minute walk from MG Road Metro Station (Exit A). The emporium stocks Mysore silk sarees, sandalwood carvings, rosewood artefacts, Channapatna wooden toys, Bidriware (silver inlaid metalwork from Bidar), and Karnataka coffee. Prices are fixed, and the quality is government certified, which removes the bargaining uncertainty that comes with buying these items from private vendors. Operating hours run from 10:00 AM to 7:30 PM.


For silk saree shopping beyond Cauvery, Deepam Silks and Prasiddhi on MG Road carry dedicated Kanjivaram, Mysore silk, and Banarasi collections. If you are looking for a broader street shopping experience,[Commercial Street](https://www.zingbus.com/blog/commercial-street-bangalore-shopping-guide-timings/) is about 1.5 km from MG Road Metro Station and offers one of the most extensive budget shopping stretches in South India.


**Best for:** Authentic Karnataka handicrafts, silk sarees, fixed price shopping, gifts and souvenirs


### Street Shopping and Budget Finds


MG Road and Brigade Road both have sections of street vendors selling oxidised jewellery, hair accessories, phone cases, sunglasses, printed scarves, and budget clothing. Prices start as low as Rs 50 for accessories and Rs 200 for clothing items. Quality is mixed, so inspect stitching and material before paying. Bargaining is expected at street stalls. The area around Brigade Road near Garuda Mall has the densest cluster of street vendors, particularly active after 5:00 PM on weekdays and throughout the day on weekends.


**Note:** Prices are approximate and may change based on season, demand, and availability.


## Where to Eat Around MG Road Bangalore


The food scene across the MG Road district covers everything from Rs 70 filter coffee and dosas to Rs 3,000 per head fine dining. The restaurants cluster differently by street: MG Road itself has iconic standalone eateries, Church Street is the cafe and casual dining strip, and Brigade Road and Lavelle Road lean toward pubs that double as restaurants.


### Restaurants That Have Been Here for Decades


**Koshy’s Parade Cafe** has operated on MG Road since 1940 and remains one of Bangalore’s most recognised names. The menu runs Anglo-Indian: mutton cutlets, egg burji, grilled fish, and filter coffee. A meal for two costs approximately Rs 600 to Rs 900. The crowd mixes retired regulars, college students, journalists, and tourists, and the wood panelled interior has not changed much in decades.


**Indian Coffee House** on Church Street is another institution. The single page menu offers dosas, cutlets, omelettes, and coffee, all under Rs 100 per person. Waiters in white uniforms and turbans serve food at communal wooden tables. The experience belongs to a different Bangalore entirely, and it sits in the middle of the city’s most modern cafe strip.


**Ebony** on Barton Centre, MG Road, serves North Indian and international cuisine across two floors with a terrace offering city views. The butter chicken here draws specific mention from repeat visitors.


### The Church Street Cafe Strip


Church Street runs roughly 750 metres parallel to MG Road and has become Bangalore’s primary cafe and casual dining zone. The concentration is high enough that you can walk the full length in 15 minutes and pass over a dozen distinct eating options.


**Truffles** is one of the most popular names on Church Street, known for American-style burgers, thick shakes, and generous portions. Expect a wait during weekend lunch hours. A meal for two costs around Rs 700 to Rs 1,000.


**Cafe Azzure** sits on the fourth floor of a building on Church Street with a rooftop terrace overlooking the metro line. Continental and Italian dishes dominate the menu. Costs sit around Rs 800 for two.


**The Permit Room** serves South Indian food with a cocktail bar twist: filter coffee cocktails, rasam shots, and modern plating of classic Karnataka dishes. A meal with drinks for two costs approximately Rs 1,500 to Rs 2,000.


Mainland China on Church Street handles Chinese fine dining. Paragon covers Kerala-style biryani and seafood. Matteo Coffea works for coffee, desserts, and a quiet sit-down. The sheer variety within a 10 minute walking radius is what makes the mg road bangalore shopping guide incomplete without a food section.


### Budget Eats Under Rs 500 for Two


Anand Sweets near Brigade Road serves chaat, sev puri, and South Indian snacks under Rs 200 for two. Street food carts around MG Road Metro Station sell vada pav, pani puri, and fresh juice for Rs 30 to Rs 80. If you are shopping on Brigade Road and need a quick fill before the evening, several budget Karnataka restaurants along the side lanes serve rice meals and thali plates for Rs 150 to Rs 250 per person.


## Nightlife Around MG Road: Pubs, Bars and Breweries


Bangalore’s pub culture started on MG Road and Brigade Road in the 1980s, and the area still holds the highest concentration of bars, pubs, lounges, and breweries in the city. The district is compact enough that bar-hopping on foot between 3 or 4 spots in a single evening is practical.


### The Original Pubs


**Pecos** on Brigade Road opened in the 1980s and is widely considered one of the first pubs in Bangalore. The interiors lean retro: dim lighting, classic rock on the speakers, beer pitchers, and simple bar food. A round for two costs around Rs 500 to Rs 800. Pecos does not try to be trendy, and that is precisely the point.


**Chin Lung** on MG Road is another OG name. The menu is short, but the garlic chicken is a local favourite. The crowd is mixed, the prices are moderate, and the bar serves straightforward drinks without craft cocktail pretensions. For anyone writing or reading an mg road bangalore shopping guide, knowing these two names is baseline.


**The Pub** in the basement of The Paul hotel on Brigade Road has pool tables, dart boards, and a pub food menu. It has operated for decades and draws a consistent after-work crowd.


### Craft Breweries and Lounges


**Arbor Brewing Company** on Magrath Road (connecting to MG Road) is one of India’s earliest craft breweries. The Bangalore Bliss IPA and the Beachshack IPA are their best known brews. A pint costs between Rs 350 and Rs 500. The space also serves solid American-style food.


**Biere Club** operates as a beer garden near Lavelle Road and brews its own range on site. The outdoor seating makes it a strong pick for groups.


**Hard Rock Cafe** on St. Mark’s Road hosts weekly live bands and DJ nights. The combination of branded merchandise, live music, and a global menu makes it a reliable option for visitors who want a familiar format with local energy.


### Rooftop and Premium Spots


**Skyye Lounge** at UB City sits on the top floor with 360 degree views of Bangalore. Drinks are premium priced (Rs 600 to Rs 1,200 per cocktail), and the crowd skews towards a dressed up, late evening demographic. Clear winter evenings offer the best visibility.


**The Tao Terraces** at 1MG Lido Mall serves Pan-Asian cuisine with a terrace bar setup. Weekend nights tend to transition into a light party atmosphere after dinner service.


Most pubs and bars in this district operate until 11:30 PM to 12:30 AM, depending on the establishment’s licence. Some lounges with late night licences stay open until 1:00 AM. Cover charges apply at a few spots during weekend nights, typically Rs 500 to Rs 1,500 per person, often redeemable against food and drinks.


## How to Reach MG Road Bangalore


MG Road is one of the most connected locations in Bangalore across metro, bus, and cab networks.


### By Metro


MG Road Metro Station on the Purple Line is the most direct route. The station sits between Trinity and Cubbon Park stations. Exit A leads to MG Road Boulevard and Cauvery Emporium. Exit B leads to Trinity Circle and the Brigade Road junction. Both exits connect easily to Church Street. Metro trains run daily from 5:00 AM to 11:00 PM, with Sunday service starting at 8:00 AM. The Purple Line connects MG Road to Whitefield, Indiranagar, Majestic, and locations across the east-west corridor.


Trinity Metro Station, one stop east from MG Road Station, provides an alternative entry point closer to 1MG Lido Mall and the eastern end of the road.


### By Bus


BMTC buses stop directly on MG Road. Multiple city bus routes from Majestic, Shivajinagar, Koramangala, and other parts of Bangalore terminate at or pass through MG Road. For travellers arriving from other cities, Majestic Bus Stand (Kempegowda Bus Station) is approximately 4 km from MG Road, reachable by metro in about 10 minutes on the Purple Line.


### From Bangalore Airport and Railway Station


Kempegowda International Airport is roughly 35 to 40 km from MG Road. Cab or app-based rides take 60 to 90 minutes depending on traffic. Vayu Vajra airport buses operated by BMTC also connect the airport to the city centre.


Bangalore City Railway Station (also called Krantivira Sangolli Rayanna or KSR Station) is about 4 km from MG Road. The station connects to Majestic Metro Station, from where MG Road is 3 stops east on the Purple Line.

## What to Know Before Spending a Day on MG Road


A few patterns catch out first-time visitors and even some regulars. Knowing them ahead saves time, money, and a couple of avoidable frustrations.


### Park and Explore


MG Road, Brigade Road, Church Street, and the connecting lanes are all within a 1 to 1.5 km radius. Taking a cab between spots inside this zone usually costs more in waiting time (traffic) than in fare. The most efficient approach is to arrive by metro at MG Road or Trinity station and cover the rest on foot.


### Avoid Weekend Crowds


If you prefer a calmer shopping experience, go before 5:00 PM on a weekday. The street vendors set up their best displays in the evening, though, so the trade-off is real.


### Walk to UB City


Visitors sometimes treat UB City as a different destination because the address says Vittal Mallya Road. It connects to MG Road through a short, well-lit walk. Combine it with dinner or rooftop drinks at Skyye after shopping on MG Road.


### Carry a Light Layer


Bangalore’s weather stays pleasant most of the year, hovering between 18 and 30 degrees Celsius. But stepping from a warm afternoon into a heavily air-conditioned mall can feel sharp, especially during monsoon months when humidity is already high.


### Pay with UPI


Malls, restaurants, cafes, and most pubs accept Google Pay, PhonePe, and card payments. Street vendors sometimes prefer cash, so keep Rs 500 to Rs 1,000 in small notes if you plan to buy from the Brigade Road pavement stalls.


### Book Pub Tables Early


Popular spots like Hard Rock Cafe, Arbor, and The Permit Room fill up by 8:00 PM on Fridays and Saturdays. A reservation (most accept calls or online bookings) removes the 30 to 45 minute wait that walk-in groups face.

### What Are the Best Malls on MG Road for Shopping?


The best malls on MG Road for shopping are 1MG Lido Mall for mid-range to premium brands, UB City on Vittal Mallya Road for luxury labels, and Garuda Mall near Brigade Road for a mix of national and international brands. Each mall serves a different budget and shopping style.


### Is There Street Shopping on MG Road?


Yes, there is street shopping on MG Road and especially on Brigade Road. Street vendors sell budget jewellery, accessories, scarves, sunglasses, and clothing. Prices start from Rs 50 and bargaining is expected. The stalls become most active after 5:00 PM.


### What Is Cauvery Emporium and Where Is It Located?


Cauvery Emporium is a government run handicrafts store on MG Road, about 5 minutes on foot from MG Road Metro Station (Exit A). You can reach MG Road Bangalore by taking the Purple Line of Namma Metro to MG Road Metro Station or Trinity Metro Station. Trains run daily from 5:00 AM to 11:00 PM, starting at 8:00 AM on Sundays. Exit A connects to MG Road Boulevard, and Exit B leads to Brigade Road junction.


### What Are the Best Pubs Near MG Road?


The best pubs near MG Road include Pecos and The Pub on Brigade Road for classic pub vibes, Arbor Brewing Company on Magrath Road for craft beer, Hard Rock Cafe on St. Mark’s Road for live music, and Skyye Lounge at UB City for premium rooftop drinks. Most pubs operate from 11:30 PM to 12:30 AM.

MG Road is generally safe to visit at night. The area remains active with pub and restaurant foot traffic until midnight, and the metro operates until 11:00 PM. MG Road is approximately 35 to 40 km from Kempegowda International Airport. A cab ride takes 60 to 90 minutes depending on traffic. BMTC Vayu Vajra airport buses also connect the airport to the city centre with stops near MG Road.


### What Should You Buy on MG Road Bangalore?


Things worth buying on MG Road Bangalore include Mysore silk sarees and Karnataka handicrafts from Cauvery Emporium, international fashion brands from 1MG Lido Mall and Garuda Mall, luxury items from UB City, and budget jewellery and accessories from Brigade Road street vendors. The mg road bangalore shopping guide covers options from Rs 50 street finds to premium luxury purchases.


### Is There Parking Available Near MG Road?


MG Road Metro Station does not have dedicated parking. The nearest paid parking is at Brigade Road, about 300 metres from the station. 1MG Lido Mall and Garuda Mall offer paid basement parking for visitors. Most visitors arrive by metro, auto, or cab given the central location and congested traffic.


### What Is the Best Time to Visit MG Road Bangalore?


The best time to visit MG Road Bangalore is between October and February, when temperatures are most pleasant for walking between outdoor attractions. For daily timing, late afternoon (4:00 PM to 5:00 PM) lets you catch the tail end of shopping, move into dinner, and transition to nightlife without switching locations.


### Does MG Road Have a Weekly Off Day?


MG Road does not have a universal weekly off day. Malls, showrooms, restaurants, and pubs operate seven days a week. Individual stores may occasionally close on specific days, but the area as a whole remains active throughout the week, with weekends being the busiest period.
