## Day Trips That Cost Less Than a Restaurant Dinner


Three destinations within 80 km of Hyderabad can be covered in a single day and brought back under Rs 1,000 per person. These are the weekend getaways from Hyderabad budget travellers use when they want a break without packing a bag.


### Pochampally: Ikat Weaving Village Under Rs 800


Pochampally sits 50 km east of Hyderabad and is the birthplace of Pochampally Ikat, a UNESCO recognised textile tradition. The village has active weaving centres where you can watch the double ikat dyeing and loom process from start to finish. This is not a museum; it is a working production village where every house has at least one loom running. A walk through the lanes takes about 2 to 3 hours. Buying a dupatta or fabric directly from a weaver starts at Rs 300 and goes up based on thread count and pattern complexity.


- **Getting there:** TSRTC buses from MGBS Hyderabad run hourly and cost Rs 60 to Rs 80 one way. A shared cab split four ways costs roughly Rs 100 per person each way.
- **Budget breakdown:** Transport Rs 120 to Rs 200, food Rs 150 to Rs 300, optional fabric purchase Rs 300 and up. Total without purchase: Rs 400 to Rs 800.


### Bhongir Fort: The 180 Foot Rock Climb Under Rs 700


Bhongir Fort sits on a single volcanic rock 50 km from Hyderabad on the Warangal highway. The 10th century Chalukyan fortress is visible from the road and draws trekkers on most weekends. The climb involves rocky scrambles and a final section with chain railings. The view from the top covers open Deccan plains in every direction. Allow 2 to 3 hours for the full climb and descent. For a deeper look at this and other fort treks nearby, the[places to visit near Hyderabad under 100 km guide](https://www.zingbus.com/blog/places-to-visit-near-hyderabad-under-100-km/) covers the full range.


- **Getting there:** TSRTC buses to Bhongir cost Rs 40 to Rs 60 from MGBS. Trains from Secunderabad reach Bhongir in under an hour for Rs 20 to Rs 40.
- **Budget breakdown:** Transport Rs 80 to Rs 150, food Rs 150 to Rs 300, entry free. Total: Rs 300 to Rs 700.


### Ananthagiri Hills: Forest Air Under Rs 2,000


Ananthagiri Hills is the closest hill station to Hyderabad at roughly 80 km. The altitude is modest (around 600 to 700 metres), but the dense tropical forest creates a microclimate that feels noticeably cooler than the city. Small waterfalls, forest trails, and Kotepally Reservoir make up the activities. The forest is part of a protected zone and hosts leopards, deer, and birds. July to October is the best window for waterfall flow. The[hill stations near Hyderabad guide](https://www.zingbus.com/blog/hill-stations-near-hyderabad-weekend-hill-escapes/) covers Ananthagiri and other altitude escapes in detail.


- **Getting there:** TSRTC buses from MGBS to Vikarabad (55 km) cost Rs 50 to Rs 80. From Vikarabad, shared autos or hired jeeps cover the remaining 25 km. A group cab from Hyderabad costs Rs 250 to Rs 400 per person split four ways.
- **Where to stay (optional):** Tourism department cottages inside the forest start at Rs 500 to Rs 1,000. This can be a day trip (leave at 6 AM, return by 8 PM) or an overnight stay.
- **Budget breakdown:** Transport Rs 150 to Rs 400, stay Rs 0 to Rs 1,000, food Rs 200 to Rs 400, activities free. Total: Rs 500 to Rs 2,000.


## Overnight Trips Under Rs 3,000


The 150 to 210 km band around Hyderabad holds four destinations that reward an overnight stay. Each offers a full day of exploration that a day trip cannot do justice to. These are the weekend getaways from Hyderabad budget planners book most frequently because the overnight format gives a proper reset without pushing costs past the midpoint of the Rs 5,000 ceiling.


### Warangal: Kakatiya Heritage Under Rs 2,500


Warangal is 150 km northeast of Hyderabad and was the capital of the Kakatiya dynasty. The Thousand Pillar Temple (Rudreshwara Temple), a UNESCO site, is the headline attraction. The Warangal Fort ruins, Bhadrakali Temple on a lakeside hilltop, and the Kakatiya Rock Garden round out a full day. Two meals of Telangana-style biryani and traditional thali cost around Rs 300 to Rs 500 for the day.


- **Getting there:** TSRTC buses from MGBS take 3 to 4 hours and cost Rs 150 to Rs 250. Trains from Secunderabad take about 2.5 hours and cost Rs 100 to Rs 200.
- **Where to stay:** Budget hotels near Warangal Fort start at Rs 500 to Rs 800 per night. Haritha Hotel (Telangana Tourism) offers government-rate rooms near the main attractions.
- **Budget breakdown:** Transport Rs 250 to Rs 500, stay Rs 500 to Rs 800, food Rs 300 to Rs 500, activities Rs 50 to Rs 150. Total: Rs 1,500 to Rs 2,500.


### Nagarjunasagar: Dam, Island Museum and Buddhist History Under Rs 3,000


Nagarjunasagar is 160 km southeast of Hyderabad and centres on one of the largest masonry dams in the world, built across the Krishna River. The main draw for history-oriented travellers is the Nagarjunakonda Island Museum, accessible only by a motorboat from the dam. The museum houses 2nd to 3rd century Buddhist artefacts excavated before the reservoir submerged the original valley. Ethipothala Falls, a 70 foot cascade about 11 km from the dam, adds a nature stop to the itinerary. For more destinations in this distance band, the[places to visit near Hyderabad under 300 km guide](https://www.zingbus.com/blog/places-to-visit-near-hyderabad-under-300-km/) covers the full range.


- **Getting there:** TSRTC buses from MGBS take 3 to 4 hours and cost Rs 150 to Rs 250.
- **Where to stay:** Haritha Hotel at the dam site and a few private lodges offer rooms starting at Rs 500 to Rs 1,000 per night.
- **Budget breakdown:** Transport Rs 300 to Rs 500, stay Rs 500 to Rs 1,000, food Rs 300 to Rs 600, boat ride and entry Rs 100 to Rs 200. Total: Rs 1,500 to Rs 3,000.


### Bidar: Deccan Sultanate Forts Under Rs 2,800


Bidar is 140 km northwest of Hyderabad in Karnataka and holds one of the most intact medieval fort complexes in the Deccan. Bidar Fort, built in the 15th century, covers an area larger than many of the more famous Rajasthani forts, with triple moats, extensive ramparts, and underground chambers. The Bahmani Tombs on the outskirts are architecturally significant but rarely crowded. Bidar is also known for Bidriware, a traditional silver inlay metalwork craft originating from the town. A small Bidriware piece makes a practical souvenir and costs Rs 200 to Rs 800.


- **Getting there:** TSRTC and KSRTC buses from MGBS take 3 to 3.5 hours and cost Rs 150 to Rs 300.
- **Where to stay:** Budget hotels and guesthouses in Bidar town start at Rs 500 to Rs 800 per night.
- **Budget breakdown:** Transport Rs 250 to Rs 500, stay Rs 500 to Rs 800, food Rs 300 to Rs 500, Bidriware purchase optional. Total: Rs 1,500 to Rs 2,800.


### Srisailam: Forest Drive, Jyotirlinga and River Under Rs 3,500


Srisailam is 210 km south of Hyderabad and combines three things that rarely overlap in a single destination: a Jyotirlinga temple (Mallikarjuna Swamy), a tiger reserve (Nagarjunasagar-Srisailam), and a dramatic Krishna River gorge. The 60 km forest road from the check post to the temple town passes through Nallamala Hills, and peacock, deer, and langur sightings are common on the drive. The Pathala Ganga descent to the river, the dam boat ride, and the 5 AM aarti at the temple are the core experiences.


- **Getting there:** TSRTC buses from MGBS run throughout the day, cost Rs 300 to Rs 500, and take 5 to 7 hours depending on the route and stops. Private AC buses cost up to Rs 750.
- **Where to stay:** Devasthanam guest houses near the temple start at Rs 300 to Rs 500 per night. Private lodges run Rs 600 to Rs 1,000.
- **Budget breakdown:** Transport Rs 500 to Rs 800, stay Rs 400 to Rs 1,000, food Rs 300 to Rs 600, Pathala Ganga boat and entry Rs 100 to Rs 200. Total: Rs 1,500 to Rs 3,500.


## The Full Weekend Under Rs 5,000


One destination in the Hyderabad radius requires a proper two-day commitment because the distance and the sheer scale of what it offers demand both Saturday and Sunday.


### Gandikota: India’s Own Grand Canyon Under Rs 4,500


Gandikota is approximately 380 km from Hyderabad in the Kadapa district of Andhra Pradesh. The Pennar River has carved a gorge through the red sandstone here that earned it the informal name “Grand Canyon of India.” A 13th century fort, one of the oldest mosques in South India (Jamia Masjid inside the fort), and a riverside camping zone complete the experience. This is the farthest destination on this list and the one that justifies every rupee within the weekend getaway from Hyderabad budget ceiling. For longer trip planning, the[places to visit near Hyderabad under 500 km guide](https://www.zingbus.com/blog/places-to-visit-near-hyderabad-under-500-km/) covers Gandikota alongside other multi-day destinations.


- **Getting there:** APSRTC buses run to Jammalamadugu (nearest town, about 15 km from Gandikota). The bus journey costs Rs 300 to Rs 500 and takes 7 to 8 hours. An overnight bus (departing Hyderabad late evening) saves a night of accommodation. From Jammalamadugu, shared autos to Gandikota cost Rs 30 to Rs 50.
- **Where to stay:** APTDC Haritha resort at Gandikota has basic cottages starting at Rs 600 to Rs 1,200. Riverside tent camping is available through local operators for Rs 800 to Rs 1,500 per person (includes tent, dinner, and breakfast).
- **What to do:** Walk the gorge viewpoint at sunrise. Explore the fort complex, including the Ranganatha Temple, Madhavaraya Temple, and Jamia Masjid. Walk down to the Pennar River for a swim if conditions allow. The gorge viewpoint at golden hour is the single most dramatic natural view within 500 km of Hyderabad.
- **Budget breakdown:** Transport Rs 500 to Rs 800, stay Rs 600 to Rs 1,200, food Rs 400 to Rs 700, activities Rs 100 to Rs 200. Total: Rs 2,500 to Rs 4,500.

## What Separates a Rs 2,000 Weekend from a Rs 6,000 One


The budget math around Hyderabad weekend trips is forgiving, but a few decisions tilt the balance sharply in one direction or the other. These patterns apply across every destination on this list.


**TSRTC is your best friend for cost control.** Telangana State Road Transport Corporation buses reach every destination on this list for under Rs 500 one way. Private AC buses cost more but still stay within budget. The moment a private cab enters the equation, the Rs 5,000 ceiling becomes difficult to hold for anything beyond 100 km.


**Devasthanam and government guesthouses cut stay costs in half.** Srisailam, Nagarjunasagar, and Warangal all have temple trust and Telangana Tourism (Haritha) accommodation that undercuts private hotels by 30 to 50 percent. The rooms are basic but clean, and the locations are typically closer to the main attractions.


**Eat thali, not biryani.** A proper South Indian meal at a local restaurant near any of these destinations costs Rs 80 to Rs 150 and fills you for 5 hours. The same money at a tourist-facing restaurant buys one plate of biryani that may or may not be the quality you expected. Budget travellers who eat where locals eat save Rs 300 to Rs 500 per day on food alone.


**Book the overnight bus for Gandikota and Srisailam.** Both destinations are 5 or more hours from Hyderabad. Taking an overnight bus (departing 10 PM to 12 AM) means you arrive by early morning, save one night of accommodation, and start exploring from sunrise. The return bus on Sunday evening gets you back to Hyderabad by late night. This is how weekend getaways from Hyderabad budget travellers keep the longer trips under Rs 4,000.


**Travel in a group of two or four.** Every cost on this list is per person. Two people sharing a room halve the per-head accommodation cost. Four people in a shared cab bring transport to bus-level pricing with door-to-door convenience. The most common configuration that holds Rs 5,000 comfortably is a pair of friends sharing one room and splitting one autorickshaw.

## Frequently Asked Questions


### What Are the Cheapest Weekend Trips from Hyderabad?


The cheapest weekend trips from Hyderabad are Pochampally (Rs 400 to Rs 800 as a day trip), Bhongir Fort (Rs 300 to Rs 700 for a half-day trek), and Ananthagiri Hills (Rs 600 to Rs 2,000 depending on whether you stay overnight or return the same day).


### Can You Visit Srisailam from Hyderabad Under Rs 5,000?


Yes, Srisailam can be visited from Hyderabad under Rs 5,000 per person. TSRTC buses cost Rs 300 to Rs 500 one way. Devasthanam guest houses near the temple start at Rs 300 to Rs 500 per night. The total trip, including food, boat ride, and transport, fits within Rs 1,500 to Rs 3,500 per person.


### What Is the Best Hill Station Near Hyderabad on a Budget?


The best budget hill station near Hyderabad is Ananthagiri Hills at approximately 80 km. The forest setting provides a cool break from the city, and the total day trip cost sits between Rs 600 and Rs 1,500 per person. For overnight stays, government cottages inside the forest start at Rs 500.


### How Do You Reach Gandikota from Hyderabad by Bus?


You can reach Gandikota from Hyderabad by taking an APSRTC bus to Jammalamadugu, the nearest town approximately 15 km from Gandikota. The bus journey takes 7 to 8 hours and costs Rs 300 to Rs 500. From Jammalamadugu, shared autos cover the final stretch for Rs 30 to Rs 50 per person.


### Is Warangal Worth an Overnight Trip from Hyderabad?


Yes, Warangal is worth an overnight trip from Hyderabad because the Thousand Pillar Temple (UNESCO site), Warangal Fort, Bhadrakali Temple, and the town’s Kakatiya heritage take a full day to cover properly. An overnight stay at a budget hotel (Rs 500 to Rs 800) allows you to start the temple circuit early the next morning when crowds are thinnest.


### What Is the Best Time for Weekend Getaways from Hyderabad Budget Trips?


The best time for weekend getaways from Hyderabad budget trips is October to February, when temperatures across Telangana and northern Andhra Pradesh are comfortable for outdoor exploration. July to September works well for Ananthagiri Hills and Srisailam, where the monsoon brings waterfalls and lush forest cover. Avoid April to June for all destinations due to extreme Deccan heat.


### What Is Pochampally Famous For?


Pochampally is famous for Pochampally Ikat, a UNESCO recognised textile tradition involving a double ikat dyeing and weaving technique. The village, located 50 km east of Hyderabad, has active weaving centres open to visitors where the entire production process can be observed. Buying fabric directly from weavers starts at Rs 300 for dupattas.


### Can You Visit Bidar from Hyderabad in a Day?


Bidar can be visited from Hyderabad in a day since it is only 140 km away (about 3 to 3.5 hours by bus). An early morning departure allows 5 to 6 hours of exploration at Bidar Fort and the Bahmani Tombs before catching an evening return bus. However, an overnight stay gives more time for the fort’s extensive underground chambers and the Bidriware market.


### Are TSRTC Buses Reliable for Weekend Trips from Hyderabad?


TSRTC (Telangana State Road Transport Corporation) buses are reliable for weekend trips from Hyderabad to destinations like Warangal, Srisailam, Nagarjunasagar, Bidar, and Ananthagiri Hills. Frequency is high on popular routes, fares are the lowest available, and buses depart from MGBS (Mahatma Gandhi Bus Station) in central Hyderabad.


### What Should You Pack for a Budget Weekend from Hyderabad?


For a budget weekend from Hyderabad, carry a reusable water bottle, sunscreen, comfortable walking shoes for fort treks, a light rain jacket (October to December), a torch for early morning temple visits, UPI-enabled phone, and Rs 500 to Rs 1,000 in cash for autos, temple donations, and small vendors in rural areas.


### Is Gandikota Gorge Really Comparable to the Grand Canyon?


Gandikota gorge earned the informal comparison to the Grand Canyon because of the red sandstone cliff walls and the river running through the base. The scale is significantly smaller than the actual Grand Canyon, but the view from the fort at sunrise is genuinely dramatic and unlike anything else within 500 km of Hyderabad. The comparison sets expectations high; the destination meets them if you go for the landscape and history, not for American-scale geology.


### Can You Camp at Gandikota?


Yes, camping is available at Gandikota near the gorge viewpoint. Local operators offer tent camping packages for Rs 800 to Rs 1,500 per person, typically including a tent, dinner, and breakfast. The APTDC Haritha resort also has cottages for those who prefer solid walls. Camping at the gorge is the most atmospheric overnight option.


### Do These Destinations Have UPI Payment Options?


UPI payments are accepted at most hotels, restaurants, and larger shops across Warangal, Srisailam, Bidar, and Nagarjunasagar. Smaller destinations like Pochampally, Bhongir, and Gandikota have patchier digital payment coverage. Carrying Rs 500 to Rs 1,000 in cash covers gaps at autorickshaws, village vendors, and temple donation counters.


### What Is the Nagarjunakonda Island Museum?


The Nagarjunakonda Island Museum is an archaeological museum located on an island in the Nagarjunasagar reservoir. It houses 2nd and 3rd century Buddhist artefacts excavated from the Nagarjunakonda valley before it was submerged by the dam. Access is only by motorboat from the dam, and the round trip including museum time takes approximately 2 to 3 hours.


### How Far in Advance Should You Book Accommodation for These Trips?


For most destinations on this list, booking 3 to 5 days ahead is sufficient. Srisailam requires earlier booking (1 to 2 weeks) during Shivratri and major pilgrimage seasons. Gandikota camping fills up on long weekends, so book at least a week ahead for Friday to Sunday trips during October to February.
