# Best Couple Getaways Near Delhi Within 300 km (2026 Guide)

A Friday evening bus from Delhi. A Saturday morning that begins in a heritage fort, beside a lake, or on a ridge overlooking a valley. A Sunday return that leaves enough of the weekend intact to not feel like the trip was squeezed between obligations. That is the format most couple getaways near delhi actually follow, and the 300 km radius holds enough range that you never need to repeat a destination across a year of weekends.


This guide covers ten destinations within 300 km of Delhi, grouped by what the trip feels like rather than where it sits on a map. Heritage and history trips for couples who want palace dinners and old stone walls. Hill stations for couples who want cool air and scenic walks. Adventure trips for couples who would rather raft together than dine together. And slow, spiritual escapes for couples who just want two quiet mornings with nobody asking them anything.


# **Couple Getaways Near Delhi: 10 Destinations Within 300 km for Every Kind of Weekend**


Three destinations within 300 km offer the kind of setting where the backdrop itself creates the evening. These couple getaways near delhi work best for anniversaries, long-overdue reconnections, and the kind of trip where you dress up for dinner even though there is nowhere formal to be.


### **Neemrana: The Fort Where You Sleep Inside History**


Neemrana Fort Palace sits 122 km from Delhi on NH48 toward Jaipur. The 15th century fort has been converted into a heritage hotel with terraced gardens, a step-well swimming pool, courtyards with Rajasthani arch framing, and a zip-line across the valley. Rooms are built into the fort’s original chambers, and the rooftop dinner overlooking the Aravalli foothills at night is the core couple experience. This is a stay-focused getaway: the fort is the destination, not a stop on the way to something else.


- **How to reach:** Drive takes about 2 to 2.5 hours from Delhi via NH48. Buses from Kashmere Gate ISBT to Neemrana run hourly (Rs 100 to Rs 200). Jaipur-bound zingbus services also pass through the Neemrana corridor.
- **Stay cost:** Heritage rooms at Neemrana Fort start at approximately Rs 5,000 to Rs 8,000 per night for a couple. Budget stays in Neemrana town start at Rs 1,500.
- **Best for:** Anniversary trips, photography, rooftop dinners, couples who prefer a single luxurious stay over sightseeing


### **Agra: The Taj at Sunrise, Then Everything Else**


Agra is 210 km from Delhi and holds one of the most universally recognised symbols of love in human history. The Taj Mahal at sunrise (gates open at 6:00 AM) with mist still sitting on the Yamuna is a different monument from the one you see at noon with 500 other visitors. Mehtab Bagh on the opposite bank gives a full Taj Mahal view at sunset without the entry ticket. Agra Fort and Fatehpur Sikri round out the heritage circuit. The[Delhi to Jaipur by bus guide](https://www.zingbus.com/blog/delhi-to-jaipur-by-bus-complete-travel-guide-2026/) covers the NH48 corridor that passes close to the Agra spur for travellers planning a multi-stop trip.


- **How to reach:** Delhi to Agra buses take 3 to 4 hours via the Yamuna Expressway. Fares start at Rs 200 to Rs 500.
- **Stay cost:** Heritage hotels like The Oberoi Amarvilas offer Taj-view rooms (premium pricing). Budget couples can stay at mid-range hotels from Rs 1,500 to Rs 3,000 per night with no view but a 10-minute walk to the monument.
- **Best for:** First trips together, photography, sunrise and sunset pairing, Mughal history walks


### **Jaipur: Forts by Day, Rooftops by Night**


Jaipur is 280 km from Delhi and delivers the most activity-dense couple weekend in this entire radius. Amber Fort in the morning, Nahargarh Fort for sunset (the city view from the top is one of the best in Rajasthan), Johari Bazaar for jewellery shopping, and a Rajasthani thali dinner on a heritage haveli rooftop. The city has an increasingly strong cafe and rooftop bar culture, and hostels with private rooms offer affordable couple-friendly accommodation without the formality of a hotel.


- **How to reach:** Delhi to Jaipur overnight buses take 5 to 6 hours. zingbus operates on this route with fares starting from approximately Rs 270.
- **Stay cost:** Heritage havelis offer rooms from Rs 2,000 to Rs 5,000 per night. Budget hotels near the old city start at Rs 1,000. Hostel private rooms start at Rs 800.
- **Best for:** Couples who want a packed weekend with forts, food, shopping, and nightlife in a single city


## **Hill Stations: Cool Air and Long Walks**


The Himalayan foothills begin about 250 km north of Delhi, and three hill stations in that zone serve as some of the most reliable couple getaways near delhi for weekend trips across every season except peak monsoon. The[places to visit near Delhi within 300 km guide](https://www.zingbus.com/blog/places-to-visit-near-delhi-within-300-km-2-day-trip/) covers these and other destinations in the same radius.


### **Mussoorie: The Queen of Hills for a Reason**


Mussoorie sits at 2,005 metres, approximately 280 km from Delhi. Mall Road for evening walks, Camel’s Back Road for quiet morning strolls, Kempty Falls for a half-day outing, and Landour for old-world bakeries and church spires make a proper two-day couple itinerary. The Doon Valley view from Gun Hill (accessible by cable car, Rs 75 per person each way) is a strong sunset pick.


**How to reach:** Delhi to Mussoorie (via Dehradun) overnight buses take 6 to 7 hours. Fares range from Rs 500 to Rs 1,200.


**Stay cost:** Mid-range hotels with valley views cost Rs 2,000 to Rs 4,000 per night. Budget stays near Mall Road start at Rs 1,000 to Rs 1,500.


**Best for:** Classic hill station romance, Mall Road evening walks, cafe-hopping in Landour, monsoon mist (July to September adds drama but also rain)


### **Kasauli: The Weekend That Asks for Nothing**


Kasauli is 290 km from Delhi and sits at about 1,800 metres in the Shivalik range of Himachal Pradesh. It is a cantonment town, which means it is quiet by regulation and design. The appeal for couples is the absence of itinerary pressure. There is a Christ Church, a sunset point, a nature walk to Monkey Point, and an evening stroll through the bazaar. After that, the rest of the time is yours, and that is the point.


- **How to reach:** Delhi to Kasauli overnight buses (via Chandigarh) take 5 to 7 hours. Fares start at Rs 400 to Rs 800. Chandigarh is the nearest major stop, and Kasauli is 65 km further by local transport.
- **Stay cost:** Heritage guesthouses and boutique stays cost Rs 2,500 to Rs 5,000 per night. Budget rooms start at Rs 1,000.
- **Best for:** Couples who want two days of doing almost nothing in cool air with a view


### **Nainital: Lake Views and Boat Rides**


Nainital is approximately 300 km from Delhi and centres on Naini Lake, a kidney-shaped body of water surrounded by seven hills. A paddleboat on the lake at golden hour, the cable car to Snow View Point, the walk from Naina Devi Temple to Thandi Sadak (Cold Road), and tea at a lakeside cafe make a couple weekend that photographs well and feels unhurried. Tiffin Top and Dorothy’s Seat are longer walks for couples comfortable with moderate treks.


- **How to reach:** Delhi to Nainital overnight buses take 7 to 8 hours. Fares range from Rs 500 to Rs 1,200.
- **Stay cost:** Lakeside hotels cost Rs 2,000 to Rs 5,000. Budget stays on the Mall Road start at Rs 1,200.
- **Best for:** Lake romance, boat rides, cable car sunsets, couples who want Instagrammable backgrounds with minimal effort


## **Adventure and Outdoors: Couples Who Would Rather Move Together**


### **Rishikesh: Rafting, Cafes, and River Mornings**


Rishikesh at 230 km from Delhi is not just for solo travellers. The 16 km Shivpuri to Rishikesh rafting stretch is one of the most popular couple activities in North India (Rs 800 to Rs 1,500 per person for the combo package). Riverside camping at Shivpuri or Byasi, morning yoga on the ghat, and Cafe de Beatles for a sunset coffee round out a weekend that mixes adrenaline with calm. The[Valentine’s Day getaways from Delhi guide](https://www.zingbus.com/blog/valentines-day-getaways-from-delhi/) covers Rishikesh and other romantic picks in detail.


- **How to reach:** Delhi to Rishikesh overnight buses take 5 to 6 hours. zingbus operates on this corridor.
- **Stay cost:** Riverside camps (tent + meals + rafting) cost Rs 1,500 to Rs 3,000 per person. Budget guesthouses in Tapovan start at Rs 800.
- **Best for:** Active couples, rafting-and-camping combos, river-facing mornings with no alarm


### **Jim Corbett: Wildlife and Luxury Lodge Stays**


Jim Corbett National Park is approximately 260 km from Delhi and offers couples a jungle lodge stay combined with morning and evening safari drives. The Bijrani and Dhikala zones offer the strongest wildlife sighting probability. Even outside the park, the forest lodges along the Kosi River serve as couple-friendly retreats with bonfire dinners, forest walks, and genuine distance from phone signals.


- **How to reach:** Delhi to Ramnagar (Jim Corbett) buses take 5 to 6 hours. Fares range from Rs 350 to Rs 700.
- **Stay cost:** Mid-range jungle lodges cost Rs 3,000 to Rs 6,000 per couple per night (often includes meals and one safari). Budget stays start at Rs 1,500.
- **Best for:** Wildlife-loving couples, jungle lodge romance, bonfire evenings, digital detox weekends


## **Slow and Spiritual: Where the Quiet Is the Point**


### **Mathura and Vrindavan: Temple Mornings and Street Colour**


Mathura is 160 km from Delhi, and the twin town of Vrindavan is another 15 km further. For couples interested in spiritual travel, the temple circuit, the Holi celebrations (March), the ghats at dawn, and the peda sweet shops on the main road create a trip that is intimate without needing a private beach or a mountain view. The Banke Bihari Temple and the ISKCON Temple are the two most visited sites. Sunset at the Kesi Ghat on the Yamuna is the couple moment here.


- **How to reach:** Delhi to Mathura buses take 3 to 4 hours. Fares start at Rs 150 to Rs 300.
- **Stay cost:** Budget ashram stays from Rs 500. Mid-range hotels from Rs 1,200 to Rs 2,500.
- **Best for:** Spiritually-inclined couples, temple architecture, Holi season, street food explorations


### **Lansdowne: The Quietest Weekend from Delhi**


Lansdowne is 250 km from Delhi and sits at about 1,700 metres in the Garhwal hills. It is a cantonment town like Kasauli but quieter, with fewer tourists and virtually no commercial buzz. The Tip-N-Top viewpoint, Bhulla Tal lake, St. Mary’s Church, and the Garhwali Museum take about half a day. The rest of the trip is walking through silent oak and deodar forest roads with your partner. Lansdowne is the kind of couple getaway near delhi that works precisely because it offers nothing to do except be together in a place that is beautiful and calm.


- **How to reach:** Delhi to Lansdowne buses (via Kotdwar) take 5 to 6 hours. Fares range from Rs 300 to Rs 600.
- **Stay cost:** Garhwal Mandal Vikas Nigam (GMVN) guesthouse from Rs 800 to Rs 1,500. Private hotels from Rs 1,500 to Rs 3,000.
- **Best for:** Couples who find romance in silence, forest walks, fog, and the absence of a packed itinerary

**Book the Friday night bus.** An overnight bus from Delhi to Mussoorie, Rishikesh, Jaipur, or Nainital means you arrive Saturday morning and gain an entire extra day at the destination. zingbus operates overnight AC sleepers on most routes in this guide. **Pick the right stay over the right city.** A Rs 3,000 heritage room in Neemrana creates a stronger couple memory than a Rs 5,000 hotel in a city you spend most of the time outside of. For couple getaways near delhi, the accommodation is the experience as much as the destination.


**Avoid long weekends unless you book three weeks early.** Jaipur, Mussoorie, Nainital, and Rishikesh spike in both price and crowd density during 3-day weekends and festivals. A regular Saturday-Sunday trip during off-peak months gives the same destination at half the price and a fraction of the crowd.


**Plan one shared activity per day, not six.** A couple trip works when you have time to talk, eat slowly, and wander without a checklist. One fort visit, one sunset point, and one proper dinner is enough for a 24-hour stay. Two activities per day is the absolute maximum before the trip starts feeling like a tour.

### **Which Is the Most Romantic Destination Near Delhi for an Overnight Trip?**


The most romantic destination near Delhi for an overnight trip is Neemrana, where the 15th century fort-palace offers heritage rooms, rooftop dinners, step-well pools, and Aravalli views, all within a 2 to 2.5 hour drive. ### **Can Couples Do a Rishikesh Trip on a Budget?**


Yes, couples can do a Rishikesh trip on a budget of Rs 3,000 to Rs 5,000 per person for a weekend. Riverside camp packages (tent, meals, and rafting) cost Rs 1,500 to Rs 3,000 per person. Budget guesthouses in Tapovan start at Rs 800 per night. Bus fares from Delhi start at approximately Rs 400 to Rs 700 per person.


### **What Is the Best Time to Visit Mussoorie as a Couple?**


The best time to visit Mussoorie as a couple is March to June for clear views and pleasant weather, or September to November for post-monsoon greenery and fewer crowds. July and August bring heavy rain and fog, which some couples find atmospheric but others find restrictive for outdoor walks.


### **How Far Is Kasauli from Delhi and How Do You Reach by Bus?**


Kasauli is approximately 290 km from Delhi, reachable by overnight bus in 5 to 7 hours via Chandigarh. Buses from Delhi run to Chandigarh frequently, and local transport covers the remaining 65 km to Kasauli. Some direct buses to Kasauli via Dharampur are also available.


### **Is Jim Corbett Good for a Couple Weekend?**


Jim Corbett is excellent for a couple weekend that combines wildlife safari mornings, jungle lodge stays, Kosi River walks, and bonfire dinners. Safari zones like Bijrani and Jhirna are open from mid-October to mid-June. Mid-range jungle lodges cost Rs 3,000 to Rs 6,000 per couple per night, often inclusive of meals and one safari.


### **Can You Visit Agra and Jaipur in the Same Weekend?**


Visiting both Agra and Jaipur in a single weekend is possible but tight. Agra is 210 km from Delhi and Jaipur is 280 km. The two cities are about 240 km apart. A Friday night bus to Agra, Saturday morning Taj visit, Saturday afternoon bus to Jaipur, Sunday in Jaipur, and Sunday night return to Delhi makes it work, but it suits couples who enjoy packed itineraries more than relaxed ones.


### **What Is the Cheapest Couple Getaway Near Delhi?**


The cheapest couple getaway near Delhi is Mathura and Vrindavan at 160 km. Bus fares start at Rs 150, ashram stays cost Rs 500 per night, and meals at local eateries cost Rs 100 to Rs 200 per person. A full weekend for two can fit within Rs 3,000 to Rs 5,000 total.


### **Are There Couple-Friendly Hotels in Nainital on a Budget?**


Yes, Nainital has couple-friendly hotels starting at Rs 1,200 per night near the Mall Road area. Lakeside properties cost Rs 2,000 to Rs 5,000 per night. For couple getaways near delhi, overnight buses work better than driving when the distance exceeds 200 km. Driving to Mussoorie or Nainital means arriving tired and losing half of Saturday to the road. An overnight bus lets both partners sleep and arrive fresh. For closer destinations like Neemrana (122 km) or Mathura (160 km), driving gives more flexibility and privacy.


### **Is Lansdowne Good for Couples Who Want a Quiet Trip?**


Lansdowne is one of the best choices for couples who want a quiet trip near Delhi. The cantonment town at 250 km has minimal commercial activity, silent forest walks, fog-draped mornings, and no crowd pressure. It suits couples who define romance as uninterrupted time together in a beautiful setting rather than activities and itineraries.
