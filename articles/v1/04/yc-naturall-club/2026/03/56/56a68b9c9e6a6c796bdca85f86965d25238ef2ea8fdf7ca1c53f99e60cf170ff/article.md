# NaturAll Wants You To Make Your Voice Heard

NaturAll’s mission to uplift our community drives everything we do. From formulating non-toxic and junk-free hair products, to informing our supporters of political happenings and news, we are proud to serve and support our customers through it all.


As the presidential election date


**November 3, 2020** gets closer and closer, we believe it is important to do our part to ensure that you have the resources you need to have your voice heard and your political opinions accounted for in our government.


As a Black woman-owned business based in the predominantly Black city of Philadelphia, this upcoming election is incredibly important for everyone involved. We want our supporters to be well informed and prepared so that their votes arrive at ballot boxes


**as early as possible** and are counted accurately.


Vote.Org is a non-profit organization that specializes in collecting voter information nationwide, and making it easily available to anyone who needs it. Their website offers a simple and straightforward way to check your current voter registration status, register to vote, apply for mail in voting, and more.


Stay informed by texting JUSTICE to 29071 for weekly updates and resources leading up to election day. To find more information about polling locations and their operating hours visit


[Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/State_Poll_Opening_and_Closing_Times_(2020)) .org, and for COVID-19 specific information about voting, visit


[RockTheVote.org](https://www.rockthevote.org/how-to-vote/nationwide-voting-info/covid-19-voting-information/) .


If you are already registered and prepared to vote, be sure to spread the word to family and friends and get further involved if you so choose. We hope everyone can take advantage of this amazing resource and share it with any and everyone!
