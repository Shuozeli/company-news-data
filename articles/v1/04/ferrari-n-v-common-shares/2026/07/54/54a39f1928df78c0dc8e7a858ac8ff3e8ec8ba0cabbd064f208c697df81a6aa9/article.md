# Hungarian Grand Prix - Qualifying recap: Lewis second, Charles third

Budapest, 25 July - It’s time for qualifying for the Hungarian Grand Prix. Clear skies at the Hungaroring with air temperature at 26 degrees and the track at 48.


**Q1.** Both SF-26s are on Medium tyres: Lewis posts a 1’18”730; Charles a 1’18”878, which is then cancelled for exceeding track limits. On his second run he records a lap in 1’19”344 followed by a 1’18”894. They both make the cut.


**Q2.** The two Ferraris are now on Soft tyres. Charles laps in 1’18”936, Lewis in 1’17”931. Leclerc goes again on a new set of Softs, but Hamilton stays with the original set. The Monegasque gets down to 1’17”626 and Lewis also improves to 1’17”803.


**Q3.** Lewis and Charles each have two sets of new Softs available for the decisive phase. The Englishman laps in 1’17”219, his team-mate does a 1’17”445. As all the drivers tackle a second run, hardly anyone improves. Lando Norris does to grab pole, beating Lewis by just 8 thousandths of a second. Charles is third.
