# Weekend notebook: class victories in the Italian Gran Turismo Championship at Mugello. Podium finishes in Australia

Ferrari wrapped up a successful weekend of racing with several class victories and podium finishes in the Italian Gran Turismo Championship Sprint at Mugello, winning in both GT3 and GT Cup. At Darwin, the Maranello manufacturer also secured a pair of third places in round four of the GT World Challenge Australia.
By contrast, Emil Frey Racing’s two Ferraris finished well off the podium in the fifth round of the DTM at Oschersleben. In Japan, D’Station Racing was forced to retire from the Super Taikyu race.


**CIGT** . On home soil at the Mugello Circuit in Tuscany, Ferrari ended the weekend with class victories and valuable points-scoring finishes with an eye on the overall standings. The number 51 Spirit of Race Ferrari 296 GT3, driven by Francesco Braschi and Jef Machiels, continued its strong run in GT3 Pro-Am. A class win and second place – fourth and sixth overall – extended the crew’s lead in the rankings. The sister car, the number 50 of Rafael Duran and David Perel, also finished on the podium in both races, taking second and third in class.


In the Am class, the number 26 Easy Race Ferrari 296 GT3 of Leonardo Gorini and Mattia Marchiante finished third in Race-1, a result matched in Race-2 by the number 36 Target Racing Ferrari of China’s Han Huilin. It was a disappointing weekend for the number 21 AF Corse Ferrari of Gino Forgione and Michele Rugolo, and an even unluckier one for Fabrizio Fontana and Stefano Gai. The Spirit of Race pairing, who led the Am class in the number 69 Ferrari 296 GT3, were involved in an accident that left the drivers unharmed but kept the car out of both races.


Ferrari’s one-make series cars also enjoyed an outstanding weekend in the GT Cup races, securing two overall wins and several podium finishes. In the First Division Pro-Am class, the number 101 Best Lap Ferrari 296 Challenge of Vito Postiglione and Stefano Comandini won Race-1, leading an all-Prancing Horse podium ahead of the number 151 Spirit of Race entry of Lorenzo Megna and Diego Di Fabio in second and the number 119 Zanasi Racing Ferrari of Matteo Sartori and Mattia Simonini in third. The number 188 Spirit of Race Ferrari of Fedor Samorukov and Joshua Rowledge finished fourth, a result the crew repeated in Race-2.
Best Lap completed a clean sweep of the weekend as Lorenzo Pegoraro and Alex Frassineti took Race-2 in their Ferrari 296 Challenge, crossing the line just 146 thousandths of a second ahead of the number 112 MRNC12 Ferrari shared by Riccardo Paniccià and Filippo Croccolino.


The Maranello manufacturer also claimed two victories in the First Division Am class, courtesy of a double-win for the number 231 KRYPTON by DL RACING Ferrari, driven by Francesco Galli and Simone Iaquinta. In Race-1, they finished ahead of the number 250 Ferrari of David Gostner, who also took second in Race-2, and the number 203 Zanasi Racing Ferrari of Giacomo Ghermandi and Vicky Piria.


In the Second Division Am class, Lorenzo Bontempelli and “Linos” had an encouraging first outing of the season in the number 503 Easy Race Ferrari 488 Challenge.


The next round of the Sprint series will take place at the Autodromo Nazionale Monza from 9 to 11 October.


**DTM** . Emil Frey Racing had a difficult weekend at the demanding Motorsport Arena Oschersleben. The team’s two Ferrari 296 GT3 Evos finished on the fringes of the top ten in both races at the German circuit. Matteo Cairoli took tenth and eleventh in the Swiss team’s number 14 Ferrari 296 GT3 Evo, though collecting valuable points that leave him fifth in the overall standings with three rounds remaining.
Thierry Vermeulen, meanwhile, secured ninth and fifteenth in the number 69 Ferrari 296 GT3 Evo.


The next round of the German series takes place at the Nürburgring from 14 to 16 August.


**GT World Challenge Australia.** Hidden Valley Raceway in Darwin hosted two closely fought races in the fourth round of the Australian series, with Ferrari taking a pair of third places.
Race-1 began chaotically, with the number 26 ARGT Ferrari 296 GT3 of Jaxon Evans and Elliott Schutte knocked out of contention after contact at turn one. The spotlight fell instead on the number 181 OnlyFans Racing Ferrari of Renee Gracie and Will Davison. The pair mounted a fine comeback to climb to third and hold the position to the chequered flag, securing a second consecutive podium after their win at Ipswich. Josh Buchan and Cameron Campbell rounded off a strong day with fifth place in the number 23 Zagame Autosport Ferrari 296 GT3, having also taken pole position for Race-2.


The Zagame Autosport pairing were the stars of the weekend’s second race, out in front for long stretches before having to give up the lead. Buchan and Campbell finished third all the same, their best result of the season.
Jaxon Evans and Elliott Schutte finished fifth – and remain fifth in the championship standings – while Renee Gracie and Will Davison crossed the line eighth and lie seventh in the overall standings.


**Super Taikyu** . After setting the fastest time in qualifying to start from pole position, the number 777 D’Station Ferrari 296 GT3, driven by Satoshi Hoshino, Tomonobu Fujii, Yu Kanamaru and Tsubasa Kondo, had an unlucky race and was forced to retire after just 15 laps at Autopolis in the fifth round of the season.
The next round of the Japanese series takes place at Okayama from 24 to 25 October.
