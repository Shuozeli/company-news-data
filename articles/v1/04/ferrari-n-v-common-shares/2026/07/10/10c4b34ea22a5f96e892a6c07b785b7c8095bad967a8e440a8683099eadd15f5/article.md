# Ferrari Challenge Trofeo Pirelli Japan: 2027 calendar announced

Suzuka, 26 July 2026 – The 2027 Ferrari Challenge Trofeo Pirelli Japan calendar was presented this weekend at Suzuka, during the final round of the 2026 season. The new campaign will consist of six events, taking drivers and teams to some of the most iconic circuits in the rich history of Japanese motorsport.


The fifth season of the regional series, launched in 2023, will begin with an opening event whose venue and date are still to be confirmed, marking the start of another year in which Ferrari clients and dealers will take centre stage at some of Japan’s most renowned circuits.


In May, from 13 to 16, the championship will head to the historic Fuji Speedway for the second round. Located at the foot of the iconic Mount Fuji, one of the most depicted and photographed landmarks in the history of Japanese art, the circuit will host the series before the summer phase begins with the Okayama round, scheduled from 25 to 27 June, at one of the most technical and challenging tracks in the country.


The Ferrari Challenge Japan will visit Suzuka in July, from 16 to 18, for an event at the venue that represents a symbol of Japanese motorsport. In August, from 20 to 22, the series will move to Mobility Resort Motegi, a complete and demanding circuit that has often played a decisive role in the championship standings. The season finale will take place from 1 to 3 September at Autopolis International Racing Course, where the 2027 campaign will conclude and the titles across all classes will be awarded.


**Ferrari Challenge Trofeo Pirelli Japan 2027 Calendar**


- **TBA – Venue and dates to be confirmed**


- **13–16 May – Fuji Speedway**


- **25–27 June – Okayama International Circuit**


- **16–18 July – Suzuka Circuit**


- **20–22 August – Mobility Resort Motegi**


- **1–3 September – Autopolis International Racing Course**
