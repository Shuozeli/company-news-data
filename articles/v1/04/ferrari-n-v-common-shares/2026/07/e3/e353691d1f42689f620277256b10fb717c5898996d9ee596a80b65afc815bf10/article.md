# Gilbert Yates and Beighton take home championship wins

Brands Hatch, Sunday 26 July 2026 - Action resumed at Brands Hatch as the Ferrari Challenge Trofeo Pirelli UK championship returned to the circuit’s Grand Prix configuration. In the final day of racing at the legendary track, drivers pushed their Ferrari 296 Challenge cars to the limit as Ferrari fans watched on. The weekend also saw Gilbert Yates and Christopher Beighton crowned Trofeo Pirelli and Coppa Shell champions, respectively. Official Ferrari Driver, Phil Hanson, was Driver Steward for the event, and also presented the podium trophies.


**Qualification 2.** In Trofeo Pirelli, Gilbert Yates (Charles Hurst) claimed Pole Position with a time of 1:26.153. Behind him, just 0.1-sec separated Bradley Yates (Charles Hurst) in 2nd and Joseph Dean (Stratstone Colchester) in 3rd, with times of 1:27.327 and 1:27.410, respectively.


In Coppa Shell, Sean Ran (HR Owen) qualified in Pole Position, with a time of 1:27.817. There was just 0.2-sec between Christopher Beighton (Graypaul Nottingham) in 2nd and Andrea Seminara (HR Owen) in 3rd, who posted best laps of 1:28.131 and 1:28.303, respectively.


**Race 2. Trofeo Pirelli.** Pole Sitter, G. Yates made a flying start, with B.Yates hot on the heels of his father. Behind them in 3rd, Dean began to find his race rhythm and pulled away from 4th-placed Mike Dewhirst (Dick Lovett Swindon). As the race progressed, Dewhirst began closing in on Dean, before performing an excellent move on the Stratstone Colchester driver at Westfield Bend. Meanwhile, G. Yates continued to dominate the field. G. Yates was first to take the chequered flag, sealing the championship title with an outstanding ninth victory from ten races. The win also completed a Perfect Weekend for the Northern Irish driver, while his son crossed the line in 2nd. Behind them, Dean mounted pressure on Dewhirst before reclaiming 3rd with a decisive move at Dingle Dell on the final lap.


**Coppa Shell.** No sooner had the race started,Beighton overtook two-time Pole Sitter, Ran, on the exit of Druids on the first lap. Further back, a battle for 3rd was underway between 3rd-placed Seminara, Darren Howell (JCT600 Leeds) in 4th and William Bolster (Charles Hurst) in 5th. As the battle intensified, 5th-placed Bolster seized the opportunity to overtake Howell to claim 4th after battling. However, neither driver was able to catch up to Seminara to challenge for the last step of the podium. Up ahead, Beighton pushed on with his sights firmly set on the championship win. At the finish line, Beighton secured the Coppa Shell championship title, with Ran taking home 2nd place and Seminara completing the top three.


**Next Race.** The Ferrari Challenge UK series travels to Donington Park on 19-20 September. Spectators can watch the race in person or online at live.ferrari.com.
