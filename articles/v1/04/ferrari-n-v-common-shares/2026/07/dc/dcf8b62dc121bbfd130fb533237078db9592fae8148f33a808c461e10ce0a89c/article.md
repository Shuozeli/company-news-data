# Hungarian Grand Prix - Race recap: Charles fourth, Lewis fifth

Budapest, 26 July 2016 - It’s time for the Hungarian Grand Prix at the Hungaroring, the eleventh round of the season. It’s the last race before the summer break, with the action resuming at Zandvoort in the Netherlands on 23 August. The Ferraris start from second and fifth on the grid with Charles Leclerc and Lewis Hamilton respectively, both of them on Soft tyres.


**Start.** A slow getaway for Charles on the dirty side of the track, but Lewis gets off the line well. At the end of the opening lap, Hamilton and Leclerc are fourth and fifth.


**Lap 13.** Lewis pits for Hard tyres. He rejoins ninth and immediately gets ahead of Arvid Lindblad for eighth place.


**Lap 14.** Now Max Verstappen pits, coming out behind Lewis before overtaking him on the next lap.


**Lap 16.** Charles pits, rejoining eighth behind Liam Lawson.


**Lap 20.** Hamilton passes Isack Hadjar, who then pits to emerge fifth ahead of Leclerc.


**Lap 31.** A second stop for Lewis, who again takes on Hard tyres. He rejoins seventh behind Hadjar, passing him on the next lap.


**Lap 37.** Now Charles makes his second stop, also coming out seventh behind Hadjar, passing the Frenchman on the next lap.


**Lap 41.** Max Verstappen pits. Lewis is up to fourth, while Charles is still sixth.


**Lap 58.** Piastra stops with a problem. Both Ferraris pit to switch for Soft tyres, running fourth and fifth.


**Lap 66.** Lewis is given a five second penalty for speeding in the pit lane.


**Finish.** At the flag, Lando Norris wins from Max Verstappen, Kimi Antonelli, Charles and Lewis.
