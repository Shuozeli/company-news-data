# Gilbert Yates secures perfect first race, Ran triumphs

Brands Hatch, Saturday 25 July 2026 - The Ferrari Challenge Trofeo Pirelli UK championship returned this week for the fifth round at Brands Hatch. The 1.198-mile Indy Circuit layout is a driver favourite in the championship. Its iconic corners and undulating terrain, provides an unforgettable experience for the drivers in their Ferrari 296 Challenge cars. Official Ferrari Driver, Phil Hanson, was Driver Steward for the event, and also presented the podium trophies.


**Qualification 1** . In Trofeo Pirelli, 2024 and 2025 winner, Gilbert Yates (Charles Hurst), claimed Pole Position with a best lap of 0:45.777, with his son, Bradley Yates (Charles Hurst) trailing just 0.2-sec behind, with a time of 0:45.997. Joseph Dean (Stratstone Colchester) followed just 0.3-sec behind him to secure 3rd place on the grid, with a time of 0:46.394. In Coppa Shell, Sean Ran (HR Owen) took Pole Position with a best lap of 0:46.346 – merely 0.1-sec ahead of Christopher Beighton (Graypaul Nottingham), who posted a time of 0:46.471. William Bolster (Charles Hurst) qualified in 3rd, less than 0.2-sec behind Beighton, with a time of 0:46.666.


**Race 1. Trofeo Pirelli.** Pole Sitter G. Yates made a quick getaway and began to build a strong lead over 2nd-placed B. Yates. Dean also started excellently from 3rd, but couldn’t quite find the pace to catch up to the Yates duo. As the competition heated up, B.Yates mounted pressure on his father, but the race leader continued to extend his advantage. Further back, Dean defended against keen overtaking attempts from Mike Dewhirst (Dick Lovett Swindon). With the positions settled, G. Yates crossed the chequered flag 10-sec clear of his son in 2nd place. On such a short circuit, near the end of the race, the leaders had to contend with backmarker traffic, while Dean claimed the final podium position with a composed and consistent drive.


**Coppa Shell.** Ran took off from Pole Position to lead the pack, with Beighton close behind him in 2nd. Bolster followed in 3rd, tailed by Darren Howell (JCT600 Leeds), who claimed 4th after passing Andrea Seminara (HR Owen) on the first lap. With the order now set, Ran continued to extend his lead. Further back, Seminara and Ian Morris (Maranello Sales) fought closely but ultimately maintained their positions in 5th and 6th, respectively. As the chequered flag fell, it was Ran who took home the win after a dominating performance, with Beighton and Bolster completing the podium positions in 2nd and 3rd, respectively.


**Next Race.** The racing action resumes tomorrow at Brands Hatch, this time on its Grand Prix Circuit configuration. Qualifying 2 takes place at 11:55, with Race 2 commencing at 15:00. Spectators can watch the race in person or online at live.ferrari.com.
