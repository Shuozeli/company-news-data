# Yamatatsu Secures Championship as Tan Dominates Again in Coppa Shell

Suzuka, 26 July 2026 - The final race of the 2026 Ferrari Challenge Trofeo Pirelli Japan season, Round 5 Race 2, took place at Suzuka Circuit. Held under clear skies once again, the final race of the season produced another series of high-quality battles as drivers fought to conclude their campaigns on a high note. As in Race 1, the 25-car field was divided into two groups. Group A featured 16 cars across the Trofeo Pirelli, Trofeo Pirelli Am and Coppa Shell classes, while Group B consisted of 9 Coppa Shell Am entries. With ambient temperatures reaching 40°C and track temperatures climbing to 51°C, Race 2 began with the Group B competitors. Akihiro Tsuzuki (Auto Speciale) claimed his sixth victory of the season in Trofeo Pirelli, while Ernst Kirchmayr (Nicole Competizione) celebrated his maiden Ferrari Challenge Trofeo Pirelli Japan victory in Trofeo Pirelli Am. In Coppa Shell, newly crowned champion Yi Hang Tan (European Version) completed a dominant weekend with a second consecutive victory, while Kento Inami (Yanase Fiorano Motori) also secured a weekend sweep with back-to-back wins in Coppa Shell Am.


**Trofeo Pirelli.** In the Trofeo Pirelli class, championship contender Tsuzuki secured pole position with a stunning lap, becoming the first driver to break the two-minute barrier in the 296 Challenge at Suzuka. championship leader Yamatatsu (Cornes Osaka) lined up alongside him on the front row, just 0.2 seconds behind. The two drivers immediately pulled clear of the field, repeating the pace they had shown in Race 1. Tsuzuki gradually extended his advantage before an accident on the West Straight with seven minutes remaining brought out the red flag. The race was declared over, handing Tsuzuki his sixth victory of the season, while Yamatatsu secured the 2026 Trofeo Pirelli championship.


In the Trofeo Pirelli Am, Phil Kim (Nicole Competizione), who stepped up from Coppa Shell this season, claimed his first class pole position. However, a jump start resulted in a drive-through penalty. That promoted Austrian driver Ernst Kirchmayr, who had qualified second in class, into the lead, with Z. Falcon (Cornes Nagoya) chasing from fourth on the grid. A late-race incident brought out the red flag, allowing Kirchmayr to celebrate victory in only his second Ferrari Challenge Trofeo Pirelli Japan start.


**Coppa Shell.** In the Coppa Shell class, Pole position had already been enough for Yi Hang Tan to mathematically secure the Coppa Shell championship before the race even began. Tan then underlined his dominance by immediately pulling away from the field at a pace capable of leading even the higher-class Trofeo Pirelli Am cars. The race ended under red-flag conditions following a late accident, giving Tan a perfect pole-to-win finish to both the race and his championship-winning season. Starting fourth in class, Naoryu (Cornes Shiba) finished second to claim the final podium position of the season.


In the Coppa Shell Am class, Tak Shaw (Cornes Shiba) delivered a commanding qualifying performance to secure pole position among the nine-car Coppa Shell Am field and initially controlled the race. However, his pace dropped on lap seven, allowing Kento Inami to take the lead before BAMBOO MAEDA (Cornes Shiba) also moved past him three laps later. MAEDA then closed in on Inami before crashing at the final corner on lap 11, bringing out the Safety Car. The race finished under caution, with Inami completing a clean sweep of the Suzuka weekend with his second consecutive victory.


With the conclusion of the regional championships, the champions from each series around the world will now turn their attention to the Ferrari Finali Mondiali, the season-ending world finals. This year's event will take place at Circuit de Barcelona-Catalunya from 17 to 23 November.
