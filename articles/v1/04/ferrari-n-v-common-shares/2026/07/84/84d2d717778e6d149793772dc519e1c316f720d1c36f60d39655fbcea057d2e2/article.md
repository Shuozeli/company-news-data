# Ferrari Challenge Trofeo Pirelli UK Announces 2027 Calendar

Brands Hatch, 26 July 2026 – The 2027 Ferrari Challenge Trofeo Pirelli UK calendar was unveiled during the fifth round of the 2026 season at Brands Hatch. The ninth edition of the regional series will feature five rounds across the United Kingdom and the Netherlands, reaffirming a schedule that combines some of the most historic and prestigious circuits in British motorsport.


The season will get underway at Silverstone from 23 to 25 April, with the iconic British venue hosting the opening round of the 2027 campaign. The second event will take place at Zandvoort, in the Netherlands, from 28 to 30 May, continuing the tradition of Ferrari Challenge UK visiting a circuit beyond British shores, before returning to the UK for the third round at Snetterton on 26 and 27 June, a demanding and highly technical circuit that has long been regarded as one of the toughest challenges on the calendar.


From 23 to 25 July, the championship will move to Donington Park for a joint event with the Ferrari Challenge Trofeo Pirelli Europe, bringing together the two one-make series of the Prancing Horse on one of Britain's most historic circuits for one of the highlights of the season.


The campaign will conclude at Brands Hatch from 3 to 5 September, where the 2027 season will come to a close and the class titles will be decided.


**2027 Ferrari Challenge Trofeo Pirelli UK Calendar**


- **23–25 April** – Silverstone
- **28–30 May** – Zandvoort
- **26–27 June** – Snetterton
- **23–25 July** – Donington Park
- **3–5 September** – Brands Hatch
