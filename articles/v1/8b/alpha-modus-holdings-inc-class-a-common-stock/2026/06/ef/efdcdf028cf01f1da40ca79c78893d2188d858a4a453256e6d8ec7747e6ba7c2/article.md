# Alpha Cash Clears Two States in One Week: Kentucky and Pennsylvania Approved for Check Deposit

CORNELIUS, N.C., June 09, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) --[Alpha Modus Holdings, Inc.](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=g8HwpfOTI5XWJqAexS6FywPhcjVALF7j-hOYvn31Nk-H10h_jXCgWE1AC-SZFzbwm7WBCzeoai1AZuFxwGztxxoCKv9NrbuVm29oJT48KvY=) ("Alpha Modus") ([NASDAQ: AMOD](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=h3z-MgkWjB-GJsWBJpUvCqqeTL1xHBCLSAcSoDsExpu2YrnMiFOAQCd_Wk6nqo7jMDYcoAe8DW_mN8xrB1QKtuoPU_0o7QL4ltmTt41rkcibAuLTXXWsdBce4QP-hVEE) ) today announced regulatory clearance for[Alpha Cash](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=g8HwpfOTI5XWJqAexS6Fy4pIi9d5JgCDb3FAUV26izm18CJFtS_PsI7kdGaajjAUtk4AcblJUULEUdTKICbtvg==) to enable check deposit in both Kentucky and Pennsylvania, advancing the state-by-state rollout of the company’s mobile financial services platform. The back-to-back clearances mark a significant acceleration in the compliance buildout and expand Alpha Cash’s addressable footprint to consumers across two states who have historically had limited access to check cashing services.


Kentucky’s clearance was issued under the authorized delegate framework administered through the Nationwide Multistate Licensing System (NMLS), confirming that Alpha Modus Financial Services, LLC may conduct check deposits in the Commonwealth through its partner relationship with Barri Money Services, LLC. Pennsylvania’s clearance followed the same structure, with the PA Department of Banking and Securities confirming compliance.


“Getting check deposit right is harder than people realize,” said Chris Chumas, Chief Strategy Officer of Alpha Modus Holdings, Inc. “The licensing requirements, the banking partnerships, the compliance infrastructure across state lines: it’s a high bar to clear. Kentucky’s and Pennsylvania’s regulatory clearances confirm that we’ve built a model that works and can be replicated. For consumers in these states who’ve had limited options to deposit a check without a traditional bank account, this is a real change.”


[Alpha Cash](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=g8HwpfOTI5XWJqAexS6Fy4v_VEtQMBCdNga8umhQghWpaXA340iKL0Tr5VW3Z2JByvoYW0IgdTc9QDu5XF8mVA==) is currently live in the[Apple App Store](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=E4z2u7HHqC3Og9FXg0fThpHAPhgE23imuHELXb9dmeBvMBjxtG3m9rOQf4GAVFZa2vHyV2cjKAXqWHNMYk2RnGUE0DyENhWD6eFIUlyMYpS5sy7xnwRiRYh_q_JECNxQ) and[Google Play](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=PmtZAwHdmd_FhTdAzaOS56H4GcNLWefomLNjuTNpnJQXu0Um2j-4iPa4OvYTbJ7XrJQbRA4vm8ZB_ghPshD_ZCYJJCwOqUdgQ5FUOO5BPrBJygtXjpGEXcJwebv_Jvac_cr0sMF0INayl_9o4pH8MQ==) with functionalities including:


- In-person cash loading
- Free Peer to Peer transfers
- Reloadable prepaid debit card access
- Gift card purchases
- Ticket and event purchases
- Mobile Top-Up
- Check Deposit (state-by-state)


The company intends to continue filing for authorization in additional states, with each clearance expanding the addressable footprint for check deposit functionality.


Together,[Kentucky](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=g5MefkgqQACltZSetn0tjRtfeM_P3MwT9Pz8CYVPKLjQq0JxtcLrJEgFfie4iJt9cYR-Pt7hGWKTKrzWvDpeoI9xC01hVerihiI9zK73fKX3-WqBj6KIFFT36joI3my9) and[Pennsylvania](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=4fhZNAoO7FHX5SFviRfCprR_qdey4GNbfYgeme6aiscCYMT7EWx7N1fjeduLLL5uNxTwzwJKxXryxJng4SXDUg4BY-ga9HRkz-z7Rq51EALyduJbCa02BudqSh2nShSMSyHSg3semms8hNXeUb9myQ==) represent a combined market of more than 19 million people across approximately 7.2 million households. Applying[FDIC 2023](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=SmX2kIhXvbOvp9002bDgOLgnWXbt9AI6vwfg-qtZRNfqHPb-FqYrX6trFWxsIew0nCtBxLtQOO0gnPJopyIs_UtWwgzUcI0G8Bk742MqIZM=) national benchmarks, an estimated 302,000 households across both states are unbanked and an additional one million are underbanked, relying on nonbank services to meet core financial needs, a combined addressable market of approximately 1.3 million households for Alpha Cash. In[Kentucky, a poverty rate of 16.4 percent](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=g5MefkgqQACltZSetn0tjRRAoljtzQTp2csZT70npRxOTkGaGMvPQ0AnKxyhymbMh8TRvbr11bdjQwqlXy6e_fZMO5E8qedtCLEyOFm3X0Wq1O98n6LrtonhiwueBswE6zt-Gpn_Wt3DGPltpV0aAA==) compounds the need: access to affordable, branch-free check deposit is a practical necessity for hundreds of thousands of working families.[Pennsylvania’s 12 percent poverty rate](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=4fhZNAoO7FHX5SFviRfCpsIvTKZNwJIAcb0TSUV3Ol6uwFYoHD6N3vKJw-DgSMws4mNM-T8Ftk5JRLXKuD3tiHP9nYk3n_6muFIyje-b7bc5ZKA-Pp-TLNuNMCm0ZqrjgC0kxXF261JA6K54uEPCqQ==) tells a similar story, concentrated in Philadelphia, Erie, and the state’s rural corridors.
