# Alpha Cash Comes Home: North Carolina Approval Takes Seven-State Footprint Past 3 Million Unbanked Households

CORNELIUS, N.C., June 23, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) --[Alpha Modus Holdings, Inc.](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=hbLlDkDtoFe3rZjFGyxXDDGBOtlGhZ05vx4JsuNWQm1KdHozwZLlelJbPV2CCOTuow_A5sBBEV2uMHMBkBhpVABzhJckcdJurTn8S8OpTIU=) ([NASDAQ: AMOD](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=o1cK69D77upryeURAMdBMW4NaPPpSlNzegnHZ_DCRsEe97BmuWJ28LA_4T05Up5DUgG4OeUw4AdoqBj-ysSfju8csUrE4TH2PaDXtMM203J_o_JnIQbfi1r4ljI187Pi) ) announced today that Alpha Modus Financial Services, LLC has received a non-objection response to operate as an authorized delegate of Barri Money Services, LLC in the State of North Carolina under the North Carolina Money Transmitters Act, N.C.G.S. §§53-208.41 et seq. The clearance, issued through the North Carolina Commissioner of Banks, authorizes[Alpha Cash](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=hbLlDkDtoFe3rZjFGyxXDEUk_YWZuyWaEAWtSHuqYVDOVDh8wefYtQRvk-JjR8Dxt4JjtPiy9YNM9wDFtWmxQg==) check deposit and remittance services for North Carolina residents across both physical kiosk locations and the Alpha Cash mobile application.


North Carolina is home to[more than 4.1 million households](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=bKuv5qc6I3jK84-Er1owSP5Bw_kPgDjBbJMGTdPTiPeoNlTTOdziXXZGrIHwjbk6WJYovcyVtKPdk18q52jpWe6AngxRkA_AlE9EweKEGL_liBeW52Xk1dSOBwd3zemz) . Applying national rates from the[FDIC’s 2023](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=S6EtHqDI-5LucBM6MM6UtZrKLQlM_IXZKFFZDoit6ViP4fBgyOf3yxYkkErDWMYtF4rxrz36qNjOaZtRM3pIZYK4ic-Sc0_KkDDhrShyfg0R9njXaqK00OrbH-KZFVKaPfsDlsZlhYS2fLKv2c9jetvcbVJ2ptDp1TnQs4lkgDKzDSeoYwLbV4S0AyvgQqJSTs1dH_qCrNvatqvhzskbiw==) Household Survey, an estimated 770,000 North Carolina households are either unbanked or rely on nonbank financial services to meet core financial needs. For those residents, depositing a paycheck or accessing basic money transfer services has historically required a fee-based intermediary or a trip to a location with limited hours.


William Alessi, Chief Executive Officer of Alpha Modus, stated, “We built this company in North Carolina, so this approval carries a different kind of weight for our team. There are working families across this state paying fees every payday to access money they’ve already earned. Alpha Cash was designed for exactly that situation, and now we can serve those communities at home.”


Through the authorized delegate structure with Barri Money Services, LLC, Alpha Cash can now offer North Carolina residents check deposit and international money remittance services at physical kiosk locations and through the Alpha Cash mobile application[Alpha Cash](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=hbLlDkDtoFe3rZjFGyxXDLec0tCm8m5D715Ze9n_ncGfC8P1mEnLHKtotvfeqpZwd1_CPyIQ7sx2ozGYD3u0jQ==) is available for download on the[App Store](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=IzNcXxRLGFg0FZ_lPBR26vxvK9oYPlbMrQmRc-vAdtP9txNXV68zWiGwMzOPPL0UJK6RSFhyqdLhQua5AzYhfoKyMbhBr7pz21cNdcb2vne-7NShi7GxlR2AtXlsjDv0) and[Google Play Store](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=awUh0mZcW3T4sJUk_Dmq36CMx0udG8AvELWSXAydbUrSCn2JMqfMh6-YT8W7cPhxsTg0zpjgug6_XGuCj_aWWrWnGRrrm26VWAG1CfHXvm4DO8iyhkEcWRQdZGTkThkJMjz1to99HrVJddvvClatCw==) .


North Carolina’s non-objection response extends Alpha Cash’s regulatory footprint for check deposit to seven states in under two weeks. Combined with favorable regulatory feedback and non-objections in Kentucky, Pennsylvania, Michigan, New Mexico, Vermont, and Nebraska, as[previously announced](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=WvXYI7q0JXWlYEeh67cweUphBCPCXN0iPgqW7cwhuTOP5jD-fuRqfYHqMiy398ZJqLzOCXpBN13uPvhWK6vrc9TXhbUqvl0xs5goLNAIjQGVkTQuog8yO7MqJF5uiLi-K1lcDv9-q7Z4lefxfQhXcLdi-6Y1iSnJrCkAyZylxYDZ0J6BXJHSUB9XugffiNgTjusoxj91YVITDsnwVn2TwX8dL3brE22nVcS_ZxjMnN0bolNuylOrTq6I9rLRR_MWmmgITq5o8yKyqk_u_xNSui-34vUSgqlnb09FztpbnRw38fLl_u4IMUbBriufqu3DHQehns6u4w2tTKOaHYt0eQ==) , the platform is now accessible to more than 17 million households across seven states, including an estimated three million that are unbanked or underbanked. The Company intends to continue filing for authorization in additional states, with each determination widening the addressable footprint for Alpha Cash’s check deposit and broader financial services.
