# Alpha Modus and SurgePays Sign 25,000-Activation Pilot to Launch Alpha Cash Across SurgePays' Prepaid Wireless and Convenience-Store Distribution Network

CORNELIUS, N.C. and BARTLETT, Tenn., May 07, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) --[Alpha Modus Holdings, Inc.](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=9QPipqnn5iBFm-7WfuB6qAZu76byHcZzqckSwypWvhQX9uz7y3HkxoBaXPSlY-dOtNr2jj-nS0LmgjmDKLBK0kmJpVo74eaQ-MRY_AQhfnk=) (“Alpha Modus”) ([NASDAQ: AMOD](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=kJk1jAxlvnYG8us_v_7chFWEuiymN_7BL85Ga7SHYK4S_3zff_wS3u8HeUfpcJgtnIGPGhuu39drP0Bk8nd-SEzo9eCpOeUvC_wf1ArwzKZrhYeE36jW_5B3hRDBlYtt) ), through its subsidiary Alpha Modus Financial Services, LLC (“AMFS”), and[SurgePays, Inc.](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=HGAOy7DGhD6Vu3WA1zWvBF0MkcCL6xJ2RjakxaG-CCeWrMukBhT_Z_uj2AEGRG2qbdRcBp662BO8ClQKEo4c-w==) ([NASDAQ: SURG](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=kJk1jAxlvnYG8us_v_7chIYGGGlJ7Yz0g2Ry748ntegCAS22fUA22SuFDGmrfjQzjqze44VNgjYf3CLrgX2xvBozNaIhQpSJEWBcRmc-0a0kJY7qHeWwcgngJNVO-ZgU) ) ("SurgePays"), today announced the execution of a multi-year Commercial Integration and Distribution Agreement to deploy the[Alpha Cash](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=9QPipqnn5iBFm-7WfuB6qK_JYZtwTEn1cT3Av7UuRtOdReXiufXcrCbz2kZK2lX6I4KXx5Kwj80S_LPZ98itzg==) mobile wallet across SurgePays' prepaid wireless and convenience-store retail channels, formalizing a previously announced partnership.


The agreement enables the direct integration into SurgePays’ consumer-facing ecosystem, including[ProgramBenefits.com](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=A_Fday44bb_oljvN3BWvVaVMnnPpWjFyTQB_2uKYr5unT25zLfwxdUXKPb5n_C-hQCz4lJEfweL-sjqDvwdf7MyfJheDC6W3fyNEACcfFso=) and SurgePays' Prepaid Wireless and Convenience-Store Distribution Network, positioning Alpha Cash within platforms that collectively serve millions of underbanked and underserved Americans.


Under the terms of the agreement, SurgePays will promote Alpha Cash across:


- ProgramBenefits.com
- Point-of-sale marketing channels
- Digital intake and onboarding flows
- Wireless activation pathways, including Lifeline and related programs


The integration is designed to embed Alpha Cash directly into existing consumer acquisition and benefit enrollment journeys, reducing friction to adoption and enabling scaled wallet distribution through infrastructure already in place.


The agreement includes an initial deployment phase designed to validate performance across SurgePays’ network, with:


- Up to 25,000 devices enabled with Alpha Cash access
- A defined activation structure tied to funded wallet accounts
- A revenue-sharing model aligned to long-term consumer usage


Alpha Modus will fund a consumer activation incentive and compensate SurgePays on a per-activation basis, along with providing SurgePays a share of downstream wallet revenue generated from enrolled users for a defined period.


“This agreement should move Alpha Cash into live distribution channels that already reach the consumers we are targeting,” said William Alessi, Chief Executive Officer of Alpha Modus Holdings. “This is not a theoretical partnership - we will be embedding directly into the onboarding flows and platforms that serve millions of underbanked Americans. The structure should allow us to validate performance while building toward broader scale through an established national footprint.”


"Alpha Cash gives our prepaid wireless subscribers a mobile wallet built for how they already engage with their phones,” said Jeremy Gies, Executive Vice President of SurgePays. “The Pilot lets us evaluate enrollment economics at scale before we expand beyond the initial phase of the partnership. This agreement enables us to integrate that solution directly into our existing channels and evaluate adoption at scale.”


According to the FDIC’s[2023 National Survey](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=__gaDoOE09XGjwyFQyZIMCdiOdeE1Q3xVN8GFy1xvyDdVrmPowIIXMLyn3OaA9oAeG2D2TrJ-IcnTBazAccQor4HPzAjRmaE0NyFeFl5pRI-F3nMqslO9TAfpyr35kDKOmbYLpDZOPgS0QzOMbOMEoOBi5I7WpwJL5oogrylEo2WYCSAwi6LBN1ezAxuEKDcQf-21jHCjT0ttLuRwiiVmw==) , approximately 24.6 million U.S. households rely on alternative financial services for everyday transactions, representing tens of millions of consumers facing high fees for basic financial access. By integrating into SurgePays’ ecosystem, Alpha Modus is positioning Alpha Cash to directly address this market through a mobile-first platform embedded within existing consumer touchpoints.


U.S. consumers reliant on alternative financial services spend an estimated $347 billion annually in fees and interest, representing a significant and persistent economic burden ([FinHealth Spend Report](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=or9INWMrxcE8l06mD6gyLO8ZLGY8QG5vMBmZw9P4eKvsyh6DXGkCDdUkpSmITr8kE8mu0SYlhYledgudgEtyQGlCunI2Vsd9cc8TEqbyIyftX0Tn3HSnDDlSTPlANiPJvqnDh_S-qIIHTFVABljfAk9OPLQ8KGpB4Rq6H9LkhZM=) ). Through ProgramBenefits.com and SurgePays' Prepaid Wireless and Convenience-Store Distribution Network, SurgePays, Inc. operates within a distribution ecosystem that engages millions of these consumers through existing enrollment and service channels.


The agreement follows a previously announced Letter of Intent between the parties and represents one of the definitive agreements contemplated under that framework.

**About SurgePays, Inc.**


SurgePays, Inc. (NASDAQ: SURG) is a fintech and mobile virtual network operator (MVNO) that delivers prepaid wireless, financial products, and AI-driven decisioning tools to the approximately 138 million subprime consumers in the United States. Through its proprietary point-of-sale platform deployed across approximately 9,000 convenience stores and a growing Retail Media Network, SurgePays enables retailers to offer wireless activations, top-ups, and consumer financial services. The Company's subsidiaries include LinkUp Mobile, Torch Wireless, the HERO mobile virtual network enabler (MVNE) platform, and ProgramBenefits.com, an AI-powered enrollment and decisioning platform serving the subprime consumer market.
