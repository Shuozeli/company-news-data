# Announcing the release of Egis Platform Version 2.1.0

**Release Category: Minor**


Thermon continues to focus on advancing the efficiency of Heat Trace Asset Management by making continuous improvements to the Controls & Monitoring solutions portfolio. As a part of Thermon’s Digitization initiative and our commitment to providing great experience to our customers, we release new features/fixes on our products on a regular basis.


We are pleased to announce the release of v2.1.0 software of the Egis Platform (formerly called Genesis Network Software or Genesis Server).


**Features**


1. **Network Health** : Augmentation of Network Health feature for improved monitoring of Genesis Bridges and controllers in the network.
2. **Alarm Acknowledgement** : Extended alarm acknowledgement support to third-party controller alarms and added bulk acknowledgement capabilities.
3. **User Experience**


1. **Notes** : Ability to view notes on the ‘Alarms’ dashboard as well as the ability to print notes from both the ‘Alarms’ and ‘Circuit’ views.
2. **Setpoint Values** : Ability to view setpoint values for circuits with alarms on the ‘Alarms’ dashboard.
3. **Graphs** : Added the capability to display alarm event data in graph for improved visibility and analysis.


**Resolved Issues**


1. **Installation and Setup** : Modification to the setup procedure to eliminate some of the additional manual steps involved during a fresh site installation.
2. **LockOut TagOut (LOTO)** : Resolved issues affecting bulk lockout/tagout operations across multiple circuits.
3. **Device management** : Addressed issues related to moving or reassigning bridges, panels, controllers and Genesis Duo.
4. **Alarms** : Fixed issues related to acknowledge action, alarm visibility, and acknowledged state.
5. **Dashboard** : Resolved issues related to filtering and refresh operation.


**Recommended Action**


We strongly encourage all customers to upgrade to this latest release. Please coordinate with our **Field Services** or **Product Management** teams to facilitate your upgrade.
