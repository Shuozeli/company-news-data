# Thermon’s DLX™ Self-Regulating Heat Trace now Offers an Expanded Range to Optimally Protect your Commercial Piping Systems

Thermon is transferring the warmth needed to protect your commercial piping systems with the launch of our expanded range of DLX™ self-regulating electric heat trace. Now available in 100-120 Vac and 208-277 Vac options, 10 W/m and 20 W/m outputs, and with braid or foil and drain wire construction, DLX™ provides a safe, reliable, easy to install, and energy efficient solution in an optimized form factor.Thermon’s DLX™ Self-Regulating Heat Trace delivers:


- SAFETY


- The self-regulating core prevents DLX™ from overheating, even when overlapped.


- RELIABILITY


- DLX™ uses proven and proprietary compounding, extrusion, and cross-linking technology to extend service life.


- EASE OF INSTALLATION


- A cut-to-length, small profile, and low weight design make DLX™ the perfect solution for complex small piping layouts.
- Foil & Drain Wire construction offers our most flexible and easiest to install heat trace to date


- ENERGY EFFICIENCY


- DLX™ automatically adjusts heat output based upon the temperature, delivering optimal heating for cold and warm spots alike.


Thermon’s DLX™ brings the technology of our renowned FLX™ trace heaters to a reduced size and weight offering. It continues to provide the inherent safety, reliability, low cost of ownership, and ease of installation that Thermon is known for.


Select[DLX™](https://thermon.com/products/heat-trace/electrical-heat-tracing-cables/self-regulating-heating-cables-commercial/dlx/) today to keep your systems warm and flowing when the temperature drops.
