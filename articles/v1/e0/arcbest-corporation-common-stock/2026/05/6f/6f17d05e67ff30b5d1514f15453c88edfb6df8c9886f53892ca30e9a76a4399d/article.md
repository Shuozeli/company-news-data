# Finding an Expedite Service Provider

## Keep time-sensitive freight on track with the right partner


When you’re trying to meet a tight deadline, there’s no room for uncertainty. But with so many providers promising fast shipping options, how do you know which is best equipped to handle your freight?


This guide outlines what to look for when evaluating an expedited shipping provider, along with key questions to ask and common pitfalls to avoid.


## What expedited shipping really means


Expedited shipping isn’t just “faster than usual.” It’s a specialized transportation service designed for urgent, high-value or sensitive freight.


Expedite service typically falls into three broad categories:


### Ground expedite


-


Cargo vans and sprinters for small, time-sensitive loads


-


Straight trucks for faster loading and unloading, often with team drivers for nonstop transit


-


Team drivers to keep freight moving when hours-of-service limits would delay a solo driver


### Air solutions


-


Next Flight Out (NFO) for the fastest airport-to-airport transit via commercial flights


-


Air charter for a dedicated aircraft reserved exclusively for your shipment


-


On-board courier (OBC) for hand-carried delivery by a trained professional on a commercial flight


### Special handling services


-


Temperature-controlled transport to protect sensitive freight


-


Secure transport for high-value shipments


-


White-glove service for delicate items


[Explore reasons to consider expedited shipping](https://arcb.com/blog/reasons-to-choose-expedite-transportation) .


## 6 key factors to consider when choosing a provider


Look for a provider that demonstrates capability, experience and consistency across the following categories:


### 1. Speed, availability and network reach


A strong provider should offer:


-


24/7/365 access to capacity, not just during business hours


-


Rapid response times with pickup often available within hours


-


Coverage across the U.S., Canada, Mexico and international air options


Fast availability can make the difference between staying on schedule or shutting down operations.


### 2. Service options and flexibility


Every shipment is unique, so flexibility matters. Look for:


-


Multiple equipment types


-


Scalable solutions when priorities shift


-


Ability to pivot between ground and air when needed


The right partner won’t force your shipment to fit a single mode — they’ll match the mode to your situation. It’s also a good idea to consider[how an expedite provider can add value across your entire supply chain](https://arcb.com/blog/integrating-expedite-shipping-into-your-everyday-supply-chain) , not just when something goes wrong.


### 3. Visibility and communication


Transparency is essential during urgent or high-value moves. Your provider should offer features like real-time tracking, proactive status updates and a dedicated contact for rapid communication. You shouldn’t have to chase updates on a tight deadline.


### 4. Safety, security and compliance


Make sure the provider can show:


-


A strong safety record and validated carrier network


-


Proper certifications (like hazmat, TSA and international compliance)


-


Secure handling processes for sensitive freight


Reliable providers protect both your shipment and your reputation.


### 5. Reliability and performance history


Past performance is a strong indicator of future service. Evaluate factors like on-time delivery, industry expertise, customer testimonials and case studies. Experienced expedite carriers understand the pressure involved and know how to manage it.


### 6. Pricing transparency


Expedited shipping often involves premium rates, but they should be clear and predictable. That should look like:


-


Upfront pricing without hidden fees


-


Quotes that specify equipment type, estimated transit time and any accessorials


-


An explanation of how rates are calculated


Clarity prevents surprises in high-stakes situations.


## Questions to ask before booking


Begin your evaluation of any expedite service provider with these questions:


-


How quickly can you pick up?


-


What equipment or service type do you recommend for this load (and why)?


-


Can I track the shipment in real time?


-


Who is my point of contact during transit?


-


What is your on-time and damage record?


-


Do you have backup options if plans change?


The company should be able to answer those questions confidently and clearly.


## Common mistakes to avoid


Even seasoned shippers sometimes move too quickly with urgent freight. Be mindful of these missteps:


-


Choosing a provider solely based on the lowest rate


-


Not verifying safety, insurance or carrier credentials


-


Not confirming after-hours or weekend support


-


Skipping questions about communication or tracking


A small oversight during booking can create significant challenges in transit.
