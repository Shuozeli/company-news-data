# Reasons to Choose Expedite Transportation

## Need to meet urgent shipping needs?


Unexpected delays and tight deadlines are normal parts of managing a supply chain, but they don’t have to derail it. Expedited shipping helps businesses stay on track by prioritizing speed, reliability and control when it matters most.


From tighter control to faster transit times, ground expedite offers advantages that standard services can’t always match.


## Four reasons shippers turn to expedite shipping


### Speed and reliability for high-priority shipments


Ground expedite shipments are transported directly from pickup to delivery, without detours or delays to drop off other shipments. Whether it’s a just-in-time assembly line component or life-saving medical supplies, expedite helps you avoid costly downtime and keep operations running smoothly.


### Advanced logistics technology offers enhanced security


Speed matters, but security is just as important. And for industries like life sciences, aerospace and electronics, you need both. Ground expedite vehicles typically are equipped with advanced features like satellite tracking, door sensors and in-cab panic alarms.


These enhancements help provide confidence that your shipment stays secure throughout its journey.


### Flexible vehicle options for diverse types of freight


Expedite solutions are designed to handle a wide range of shipment sizes, from small parcels to full truckloads. With access to a diverse fleet — including cargo vans, sprinter vans, straight trucks and 53-foot trailers — providers like ArcBest can match your freight with the right vehicle, optimizing both speed and cost.


### Hassle-free cross-border capabilities


Providers with a strong North American network can simplify cross-border logistics. With customs expertise, a deep understanding of international regulations and experienced drivers, they help ensure your shipment passes through international checkpoints seamlessly, allowing you to stay on schedule.
