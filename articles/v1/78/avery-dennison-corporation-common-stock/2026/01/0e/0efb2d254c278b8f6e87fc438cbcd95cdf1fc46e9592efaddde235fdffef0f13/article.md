From life-changing wishes with Make-A-Wish® Nevada at SEMA to high-tech medical wraps that make the hospital visit a little less scary for the kids and cinematic custom designs, Q4 2025 was a masterclass in creativity. We’ve rounded up our favorite social media highlights from the final quarter of the year, showcasing the unbelievable talent of the Avery Dennison installer community.


Thank you to every shop that tagged us in 2025. Here is our list of faves, in no particular order.

In addition to wrapping vehicles in our booth, they were also able to have a massive meet-n-greet, get announced before the Hoonigan Burnyard show, and ride in a 2000 hp Australian burnout car with @madnats_mischife__hpr_h8ter and @burnoutwars! From a VIP photo shoot to that pure adrenaline rush, dreams came true for Jakob and Cameron. Massive thanks to Hoonigan for providing this unforgettable experience! Also, special thanks to the following for making this happen: @makeawishnevada @wraplegends and @360wraps Corona.
