## The top 10 Supreme Wrapping Film™ colors of 2025: timeless icons & modern classics

Every year, the automotive world sees trends shift. Flashy neons might have their moment, and color-shifting films certainly turn heads, but there is something powerful about our color classics. In 2025, the community has spoken loud and clear. The top-selling Supreme Wrapping Film™ (SWF) colors of the year aren't just about fleeting hype—they are about timeless elegance, aggressive sophistication, and unmatched versatility.


Dominated by deep blacks, greys, and crisp whites, these shades prove you don't need to shout to be heard. Whether it's the stealthy aggression of a matte finish or the liquid depth of a high-gloss metallic, these ten colors represent the pinnacle of style for installers and enthusiasts alike.


Let's dive into the countdown of the Top 10 Supreme Wrapping Film colors of 2025.


###
10. Satin Metallic Dark Grey - SW900-854-M


Our list starts with a shade that finds a sweet spot between understated and attention-grabbing. Satin Metallic Dark Grey combines a silky finish with subtle metallic flair, highlighting body lines without gloss’s full-on shine. It’s a top pick for those who want a modern, high-end look with a finish that’s easier to keep looking sharp. The satin surface hides fingerprints and smudges well, making this a favorite on daily drivers and premium models.


###
9. Matte Metallic Charcoal - SW900-845-M


At number nine, we have Matte Metallic Charcoal. This color takes matte to the next level. Fine metallic flakes give it dimension and a hint of shimmer, creating movement across the surface when the light hits. This wrap is a go-to for anyone who wants to add an edge without too much attention. The color works especially well on muscle cars and performance SUVs, offering a unique blend of subtlety and style that holds up to close inspection.


###
8. Gloss Dark Grey - SW900-865-O


Sometimes, simplicity wins. Gloss Dark Grey takes the eighth spot, proving that simple can still stand out. This cool, confident "battleship" shade updates any vehicle with a modern, industrial look. Installers love it for its ability to pair perfectly with black details, creating sharp two-tone builds or a sleek monochrome effect.


###
7. Gloss Rock Grey - SW900-821-O


Gloss Rock Grey has quickly become one of the most requested colors—especially among car owners looking for something different from traditional greys and silvers. This sleek, glossy pale light grey enhances a vehicle’s body lines and makes details pop with an understated look.


###
6. Gloss Metallic Silver - SW900-803-M


Silver is an automotive staple, but Gloss Metallic Silver brings a brightness that revitalizes the hue. Mimicking the depth of high-end metallic paint, sunlight brings out a brilliance that flat factory silvers can't touch. It is the easiest way to modernize a car’s appearance while keeping it classy.


###
5. Gloss Carmine Red - SW900-436-O


Breaking the monochrome dominance is the breathtaking Gloss Carmine Red. This shade isn’t overly bright or flashy; instead, it offers a richness and sophistication that brings to mind classic sports cars. When you wrap in Gloss Carmine Red, you aren't trying to be subtle—you're making a bold statement of performance and luxury.


###
4. Matte Black - SW900-180-O


Matte Black has earned its legend status for a reason. Its non-reflective finish instantly transforms even modest vehicles into menacing machines. Whether used for a full wrap or hood accents, it remains the ultimate choice for a custom, stealth aesthetic.


###
3. Gloss White - SW900-101-O


Achieving a flawless white wrap is an art form. Gloss White offers a crisp, clean finish that rivals factory paint. White is the ultimate blank canvas. It is incredibly popular for commercial fleets because of its clean look and readability for branding, but it is equally massive in the enthusiast market. A gloss white wrap with black accents is a timeless look that works on literally every vehicle type, from Teslas to trucks. It feels fresh, clean, and unmistakably premium.


###
2. Satin Black - SW900-197-O


Just missing the top spot is Satin Black. If Matte Black is the aggressive rebel, Satin Black is the sophisticated assassin. Sitting perfectly between matte and gloss, it offers a deep, dark finish with a subtle sheen. It shows off body lines while still maintaining that sinister, stealthy vibe. For many drivers in 2025, this is the definitive "new black"—modern, sleek, and undeniably cool.


###
1. Gloss Black - SW900-190-O


And here it is, the number one Supreme Wrapping Film color of 2025, Gloss Black. It might seem surprising that the top spot goes to a standard Gloss Black, but the reason is simple, versatility. It is the essential tool in a wrapper's arsenal, perfect for full color changes, chrome deletes, racing stripes, and creating that panoramic glass roof look. Gloss Black proves you don't need wild patterns to be the best, you just need to look flawless.
