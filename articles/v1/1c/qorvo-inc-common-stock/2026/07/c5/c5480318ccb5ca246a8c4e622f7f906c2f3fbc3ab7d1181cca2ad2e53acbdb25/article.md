# Qorvo Recognized with 2026 Handshake Early Talent Award

**GREENSBORO, NC – March 10, 2026 –** Qorvo® (Nasdaq: QRVO), a leading global provider of connectivity and power solutions, today announced it has been named a recipient of the 2026 Handshake Early Talent Award (ETA), recognizing Qorvo as a top employer for Gen Z talent.


The annual Handshake Early Talent Award honors organizations that demonstrate exceptional commitment to attracting, hiring and developing early career professionals. Qorvo was selected from nearly 1 million employers on Handshake’s platform, joining a distinguished group of companies recognized for investing in the next generation of talent.


“At Qorvo, innovation starts with people,” said Leanne Simpson, Qorvo Senior Manager of Global Talent Acquisition. “We are proud to create meaningful opportunities for students and early career professionals to contribute to real-world technologies that power mobile devices, automotive systems, infrastructure, aerospace and defense applications. This recognition from Handshake highlights our dedication to developing emerging talent and supporting high-performing early-career talent across our business.”


Handshake is the leading career platform for Gen Z, connecting millions of students, alumni, employers and career educators. Nearly 1 million companies across the U.S. use Handshake to recruit and develop early career talent across industries ranging from technology and manufacturing to healthcare and public service.


Through internships, university partnerships and early career development initiatives, Qorvo continues to invest in building a strong pipeline of future innovators and leaders.


[Handshake](https://joinhandshake.com/) is the career network for the AI economy. As the only three-sided job marketplace connecting 20 million knowledge workers, 1,500 educational institutions, and 1 million employers, Handshake powers career discovery, hiring, and upskilling—from first internships to full-time roles, freelance work to gig work, and beyond. The Handshake platform combines dynamic profiles, intelligent job matching, and interactive skill-building to connect talent with opportunity and help the workforce stay ahead in the AI economy.
