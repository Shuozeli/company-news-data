# Qorvo Wins Supplier Champion Award from Solid State Storage Technology Corporation

Download[full size](https://www.qorvo.com/-/media/images/qorvopublic/news-item/20260130-supplier-champion-award_1200x1200.jpg) image


**GREENSBORO, NC – March 3, 2026 –** Qorvo® (Nasdaq:QRVO), a leading global provider of connectivity and power solutions, announced today it has received the Supplier Champion Award for Power from Solid State Storage Technology Corporation (SSSTC). The award is presented annually to the supplier with the strongest overall performance in SSSTC’s quality assessment.


SSSTC evaluates suppliers on a quarterly basis, scoring quality performance in customer production, field quality, support for new product introduction projects, and commitment to continuous improvement. Across all four quarters, Qorvo consistently ranked ahead of competitors and achieved the highest overall score for calendar year 2025.


The award recognizes Qorvo’s performance supporting solid-state drive (SSD) platforms, where dependable power management and power loss protection are critical to system stability and data integrity. Qorvo’s integrated power solutions are widely used in high-performance SSD designs that demand consistent quality and reliability.


“We are proud to receive this recognition from SSSTC,” said Jeff Strang, GM of Qorvo Power Management. “This award reflects our team’s commitment to quality and our strong collaboration with customers developing advanced SSD platforms.”
