# From Sunset Blvd to Netflix Fame: The Story Behind The Oppenheim Group

Lights, camera, real estate. The Oppenheim Group went from a luxury brokerage to a cultural phenomenon thanks to Selling Sunset. But behind the drama, glitter, and high-stakes negotiation lies a real company moving serious real estate. Let's peek behind the scenes — and see what makes them tick.


## 🔑 Who Are The Oppenheim Group?


Founded (in its modern form) by Jason Oppenheim, the firm is based on the Sunset Strip in Los Angeles. (ogroup.com)


The company operates in L.A., Orange County, San Diego, and even Cabo San Lucas. (ogroup.com)


They've closed billions in luxury real estate deals — the firm claims more than $3.5 billion+ in total sales and hundreds of millions in active listings. (ogroup.com)


Their offices are as luxe as the listings: in recent years they've upgraded their flagship Sunset Blvd location into a more lavish, open-concept space. (ogroup.com)


## 🎬 Why the Netflix Spotlight?


We all know Selling Sunset is part business, part reality TV — and part spectacle. But the show reflects more than fictional drama; it's built on real deals.


Every property sold on the show is (allegedly) a real transaction by The Oppenheim Group. Jason Oppenheim has stated that the firm lists and sells the homes featured. (TIME)


The cast includes real estate-licensed agents (though occasional controversy surfaces about whether all cast members actively sell). (People.com)


The show gives the brand massive exposure — transforming The Oppenheim Group's name into a symbol of luxury real estate in pop culture.


## 🧩 What Sets Them Apart (beyond the drama)


Let's dig into their differentiators — the qualities that help them command attention (both on TV and in real life):


Feature Why It Matters
