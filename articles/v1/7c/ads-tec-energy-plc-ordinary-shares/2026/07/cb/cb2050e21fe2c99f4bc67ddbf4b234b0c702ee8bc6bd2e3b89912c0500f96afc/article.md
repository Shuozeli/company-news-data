# SmarterE x ADS-TEC Energy

ADS-TEC Energy presented its diverse storage-based platform solutions at two exhibition stands at Europe's largest forum for the energy industry, "The smarter E Europe" with four trade fairs (Power2Drive Europe, Intersolar Europe, ees Europe, and EM-Power Europe) in Munich. At Power2Drive Europe, the ultrafast charging systems ChargeBox and ChargePost were presented and at ees Europe, the industrial storage solutions PowerBooster and StorageRackSystem.
