# Strong partners for the expansion of charging infrastructure at filling stations

ADS-TEC Energy develops and produces battery-buffered, ultra-fast charging stations that can charge modern electric cars in minutes instead of hours, even on power-limited electricity grids. The Munich-based charging network operator JOLT acquires and operates the charging stations from ADS-TEC Energy.


JOLT has already started using the battery-buffered charging stations: They will be installed at the EG Group's ESSO service stations in Munich, Berlin, Hamburg, Frankfurt, Stuttgart, Dresden, Düsseldorf and Nuremberg, as well as at TAMOIL service stations in the Netherlands. JOLT is also working with ADS-TEC Energy to expand in the United States and plans to install and operate up to 5,000 ultra-fast charging stations over the next five years.


Maurice Neligan, CEO of JOLT Energy Group: “This partnership will enable JOLT to build its charging network in cities and urban centers even faster. For us, speed of deployment and flexibility to provide high quality, ultra-fast charging in large urban centers is critical. ADS-TEC Energy's smart charging and battery technology is best in class in terms of performance, low noise and charging time and is supported by a highly competent team of engineers and specialists. This is a great example of two companies complementing each other to capitalize on growth opportunities in an emerging and dynamic market.”


Thomas Speidel, CEO ADS-TEC Energy: “We look forward to working with JOLT to enable widespread adoption of e-mobility in Europe and the US through super-fast charging in almost any location. JOLT's management team has outstanding expertise, and the sales and operating concept is flexible and innovative. The fact that JOLT has been able to attract TAMOIL and one of the world's leading independent convenience retailers like EG Group speaks for itself.”


The exceptional performance is reflected in concrete evaluations of individual charging stations by customers. Both the charging speed per charging process and the energy throughput of significantly more than 140 charges per week (around 6 MWh energy throughput) exceed previous expectations of the ChargeBox. And without any noticeable drop in charging power for each individual charging process. This is the result of a usage study in the Stuttgart area, where a disproportionately high number of Porsche Taycans with a peak charging capacity of 270 kW are charging at a ChargeBox at a public filling station. This exemplary proof of concept, which can also be applied to other charging stations, is boosting customer inquiries.
