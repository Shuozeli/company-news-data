# Electric vehicles in our fleet: a complete success!

The ultra-fast charging solutions have an integrated battery. This means that electric vehicles can be recharged in a few minutes instead of hours, even on existing power-limited networks. A standard power connection is all that is needed. No new transformer is required, and there is no need for expensive network expansion. On top of that, you save on building cost subsidies and permanently high power prices. The battery is also used for other purposes. "We can use our cheap PV electricity for charging. On our company roof in Nürtingen alone, we have an annual yield of 200 MWh. There is no more sustainable and cheaper solution than to use your own electricity directly," explains Speidel.
