### CDP: The Gold Standard for Carbon Disclosure


CDP is an internationally recognized non-profit organization that operates a global disclosure system, allowing companies, cities, states, and regions to measure and manage their environmental impacts. CDP's annual environmental disclosure and scoring process is widely regarded as the gold standard for carbon disclosure. Thousands of companies and organizations worldwide participate in the CDP's disclosure platform, which assesses their climate change, water security, and deforestation-related risks and opportunities.


*Paul Robins, Head of Corporate Partnerships, CDP commented:*


*“The addition of Minimum into our network of accredited solutions providers will bring highly valued expertise to the thousands of companies using CDP to disclose their environmental data and support them to implement leading actions to manage their risks and reduce their impacts. We are confident that Minimum’s capabilities will be useful, and we are glad to accredit them as a valued, high-quality service provider.”*


### Why Minimum's Accreditation Matters

This recognition by CDP elevates Minimum's standing within the sustainability industry, making it a trusted and recommended choice for businesses looking to enhance their environmental reporting and sustainability efforts.
