# Can AI Assistants Help Prevent Executive Burnout?

## What is burnout?


Burnout is a form of long-term exhaustion caused by constantly feeling overwhelmed, typically by work or school. It goes beyond feeling stressed and instead occurs when one feels drained. Some symptoms include isolation, irritability, dreading work, and no time for non-work-related activities. Burnout is the result of overworking under high stress and pressure.
