# What would your voice assistant be like?

##### Have you wondered what your voice assistant would be like?


April AI provides a personalized voice assistant to help manage your inbox, calendar, and ultimately your schedule in order to help out during your workday. It’s tailored to your executive style, so each April assistant is slightly different and customized to your preference based on how you interact with it. Let’s take a look at what different personalities of April could be like.


##### No-nonsense Assistant


This voice assistant is all about efficiency. There’s no need for extra fluff and politeness; this AI personality quickly flags urgent emails and schedules meetings with quick accuracy. It can be nice to get items quickly checked off the to-do list and minimize the pleasantries. If you’re an executive who prioritizes every single second of the day, April will adapt to your style.


Example:


-


You: “Give me my top five emails right now”


-


You: “Confirm my next calendar event”


-


April: “Here’s a brief summary of the project’s next steps”


Motivational Assistant


Sometimes, we all need a voice of encouragement. When you’re in back-to-back meetings all day, it can feel very overwhelming and easy to get lost in the noise. April is here to be a support and cheer you on. It can motivate you with positive affirmations, remind you to take breaks, and emphasize every small win. If you’re a busy professional who appreciates more warmth in interacting with AI, this might be for you.


Example:


-


You: “How’s my inbox looking?”


-


April: “Good job on clearing ten emails this morning! Time for a small break?”


-


April: “You have a ton of meetings coming up this afternoon. Is there anything I can help with?”


##### Detail-oriented Assistant


While some people find organization through looking at the big picture and long-term goals, it’s easier for most to find structure in knowing what is happening every day. The small details matter, and April can help with people who thrive on knowing facts, details, statistics, and figures. It can help prepare metrics before meetings and have personalized reminders.


Example:


-


You: “What are the key numbers from last week’s report in the email?”


-


April: “Remember to mention the 12% revenue growth from the report in your meeting”


-


April: “Email from finance flagged, take a look”


##### Laid-back Assistant


Adding a fun personality can break up the monotony of a long day. This April blends work with light jokes to create variation in your typical day. Think of this April as helping add to the collaborative atmosphere of your team.


Example:


-


You: “Remind me not to forget my coffee for my meeting later today”


-


You: “Any surprise emails today?”


-


April: “Your inbox has five emails from your usual troublemakers”


*Personalization* isn’t just about being light-hearted. It’s important to have an AI and an assistant that truly supports you where you need it. Having a tool that adapts to your communication style, work preference, and personality makes incorporating AI more natural and efficient.
