# How Voice AI is Revolutionizing Accessibility

## Hands-free technology


Workplace accessibility remains a challenge for many who face physical, cognitive, or sensory barriers. Hands-free technology can provide a way to facilitate interaction within the workplace and help foster a more effective and inclusive environment. Voice AI assistants like April are able to create accessible workplaces and interactions where all leaders can thrive.


## Inclusive workplaces


Voice AI eliminates the need for traditional inputs like keyboards or touch screens, which can inhibit people with mobility or vision impairments. By using voice commands, one can manage emails, calendars, and even Slack without having to stare at a screen for long hours. In tech-heavy environments that dominate the industry, it’s unfortunate that there aren’t enough measures taken to include people who have different compatibility needs. Tools like April are able to help people hands-free and promote a culture where everyone has the tools to succeed.


## Managing time


Beyond formal accessibility needs, voice AI helps executives who are dealing with multiple responsibilities, including family and travel. Using hands-free voice commands allows for work to be done simultaneously without having to sacrifice time needed in other areas of life beyond work. Think of checking your inbox on a drive to pick up a family member. Even if you’re walking through the airport to catch a flight, you have an opportunity to utilize that time to check your calendar at the same time in order to make sure you relax before your vacation.


## Working style


Accessibility can even be thought of in terms of remote and hybrid work environments. Executives working from home have to deal with multiple devices, switching from documents and Zoom screens, and maintaining communication with those in person. Voice AI assistants are able to help executives by reducing the need to physically interact with devices, leading to less eye strain and helping them stay on top of balancing their workday, even from home.


## Bigger Picture


Voice AI tools are able to empower executives on an individual level and also help scale accessibility at an organizational level. When tools like April are implemented in daily processes, companies are able to standardize practices. Small changes like using voice-powered tools accumulate into a happier and healthier team. Each improvement adds up so that inclusion and productivity are key components of the modern workplace.
