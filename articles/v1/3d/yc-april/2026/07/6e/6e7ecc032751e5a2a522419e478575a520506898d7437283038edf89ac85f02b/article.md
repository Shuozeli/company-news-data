# Your Questions About April, Answered

April is transforming the workplace one user at a time. It’s easy to start using April, so let’s go over some of the basics and clarify any common questions one might have.


What exactly is April?


April is a voice-driven AI executive assistant designed to help busy executives manage their email, calendar, and meetings hands-free. April utilizes natural language processing and machine learning in order to summarize long email threads, prioritize urgent messages, schedule or reschedule meetings, and prepare meeting briefs. Best of all, all of this can happen through simple voice commands.


How does April help manage emails?


April helps users achieve inbox zero by triaging emails, summarizing conversations, and suggesting email replies based on users’ preferences and instructions. April adapts to your executive style so it’s even easier to communicate. You’d have more time to spend on the tasks that actually matter, over repetitive emails and notifications.


What platforms integrate with April?


April is already live with Google Workspace and is soon releasing Microsoft Outlook, and Microsoft 365.


Is April secure for working with confidential data?


April prioritizes privacy and security through using end-to-end encryption so that meeting data, communications, and calendar information remain protected. It’s tailored for executives, and that comes with understanding compliance and privacy needs.


Can April reschedule meetings by voice?


April is designed to schedule, reschedule, or cancel meetings with voice commands. April syncs with Google Calendar in order to minimize back-and-forth: think optimal meeting times and avoiding conflicts. April can also give pre-meeting summaries and follow-up reminders, giving you peace of mind during the workday.


Who should use April?


Anyone can use April, but it’s ideal for busy professionals, executives, and managers who deal with email traffic, busy calendars, and hectic workdays.


How do I get started with April?


Sign up for an April account online, connect your email and calendar platforms, and configure your voice preferences. It’s a quick set-up before users can start interacting with April and implementing it into their routine. April has a free trial period, so sign up today!
