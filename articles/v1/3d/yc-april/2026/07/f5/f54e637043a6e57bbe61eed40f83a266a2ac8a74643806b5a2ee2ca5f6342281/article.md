# Executive AI Assistants - Reviewed

AI assistants aren’t just a quick trend. They’re an everyday tool that helps executives, leaders, and employees try to maximize their productivity in an efficient way. There are many AI assistants, but what makes a great executive AI assistant in 2025?


Some factors that are important to consider when choosing an executive assistant are the following:


-


Voice-powered and hands-free workflow: It should be easy to use an assistant and ask for support when you need it throughout the day. What’s easier than simply speaking to get tasks done?


-


Protects privacy and ensures security: Handling data and sensitive information is not something to be taken lightly. It’s important to have an AI that you trust.


-


Actually efficient (reduces time spent managing emails, calendar, etc): It’s time to look past the buzzwords and use an assistant that genuinely gets the job done.


-


Delivers insights: It’s nice to have an assistant that goes beyond the bare minimum as well, and actually gives you perspective into how to improve and important takeaway summaries from each day.


Let’s look into a couple of AI assistants and see which is the best one to incorporate into your workday.

Why April stands out:


-


True voice-first experience: you can summarize, reply, and schedule just by talking


-


Custom summaries, reminders, and meeting prep according to your unique style


-

-


New user-friendly and empowers people to go hands-free and stress-free
