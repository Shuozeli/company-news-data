# Albemarle Publishes 2025 Sustainability Report

*June 9, 2026*


Albemarle published its 2025 Sustainability Report:[Resourceful, Responsible, Resilient](https://www.albemarle.com/sites/default/files/2026-06/albemarle-sustainability-report-2025-final.pdf) , highlighting the company’s sustainability achievements and progress toward its 2030 goals.


"At Albemarle, our values shape how we lead, how we operate and how we create value over the long term," said Albemarle Chairman and CEO Kent Masters. "This report reflects our commitment to disciplined execution, responsible operations and continuous improvement as we support our customers and communities while building a more resilient future."
