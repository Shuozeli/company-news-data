## FAQ


### What is the main limitation of Sybill?


English-only, no CRM automation, no real-time coaching. Strong for analyzing body language and facial expressions after the call. Weak for teams that need multilingual support, methodology scoring, and automatic CRM updates.


### Does Demodesk do emotional analysis like Sybill?


Demodesk analyzes sentiment and emotional tone. It does not claim non-verbal multimodal analysis (facial expressions, body language). Demodesk's advantage: when a sentiment shift is detected, the AI drafts an action response (re-engagement email, coaching note) rather than flagging it for human review.


### Can you run both Demodesk and Sybill together?


Yes, but redundant. Both record and transcribe. Demodesk covers the full CI stack plus execution; Sybill adds non-verbal analysis. If non-verbal insight is truly differentiating for your team, run Sybill for analysis and Demodesk for action. Most teams find Demodesk solo sufficient.


### How does Demodesk handle languages Sybill does not?


Demodesk supports 98 languages natively. Transcription, scoring, and CRM updates all work in any supported language. Sybill is English-only, which makes it unsuitable for any sales team with non-English native speakers.
