## What to evaluate


Five criteria separate platforms worth paying for from dashboards in disguise.


**Methodology scoring.** Out-of-the-box MEDDIC, BANT, SPICED, MEDDPICC, custom. Generic scoring underperforms.


**Coverage.** Every call scored, not 5 sampled. Manual review caps at 5–10% of calls per rep.


**CRM automation.** Two-way structured sync with Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive. Free-text export is not enough.


**Languages.** Global teams need 50+ supported languages with accent detection.


**Pricing transparency.** Published pricing or third-party benchmark. Quote-based opacity is a red flag.

## How to run a vendor trial


Three questions to ask every vendor.


1. Can the trial start within a week without a 6-month commitment?
2. Does the scoring work against our specific methodology, or is it generic?
3. What does the CRM update accuracy look like on real calls?


Vendors who stall on any of these are signaling their actual implementation timeline.
