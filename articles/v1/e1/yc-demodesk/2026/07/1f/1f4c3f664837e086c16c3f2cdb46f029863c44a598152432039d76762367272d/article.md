# How to share recordings with external customers in Demodesk

## What and why


Demodesk lets you share any call recording with people outside your company. Switch the recording's access from **locked** to **public** , copy the link, send it. External recipients can watch in their browser and download the video file — no Demodesk account required.


Sales handovers, customer demos, technical walkthroughs, and stakeholder alignment all happen after the call. One link replaces the screen-recording re-run, the emailed attachment, and the follow-up meeting.


## Who this is for


Account executives, customer success managers, and solutions engineers who need to send a recorded demo, walkthrough, or discovery call to a prospect, customer, or internal stakeholder outside the Demodesk workspace.


## Prerequisites


- A Coaching & AI seat (needed to record and manage sharing settings)
- A recorded meeting available in your Meeting Hub
- Consent from all participants to share the recording externally (check your regional consent rules before sharing outside the EU)
- A way to send the link — email, Slack, CRM, any channel works


## Steps


### 1. Open the recording


Go to **Meeting Hub** in the top navigation and select the meeting. The recording detail page shows the video player, transcript, AI summary, and the sharing panel on the right.


### 2. Switch access from locked to public


In the sharing panel, toggle access from **locked** (default) to **public** .


- **Locked** — only teammates with the right permissions can view it.
- **Public** — anyone with the link can watch and download, inside or outside your company.


The shareable link generates automatically once you toggle to public.


### 3. Copy the link


Click **Copy link** next to the public access toggle. Paste it into your follow-up email, Slack message, or CRM activity note. No password, no account creation on their end.


### 4. Tell your recipient they can download the file


External viewers can watch in the browser or save a local copy. To download, they open the **three-dot menu** (⋯) on the recording page and click **Download** . The MP4 saves to their device.


If you're sending to a customer who wants a copy for their own records or to share internally, mention this in your email.


### 5. Revoke access when you're done


Go back to the sharing panel and switch access to **locked** . The public link stops working immediately. Anyone who already downloaded the MP4 keeps their local copy — public sharing is not a security control. Only share recordings you're comfortable existing outside your workspace.


## Tips


- **Set a retention policy first.** Recording retention is configurable per workspace (12 hours up to 1 year). Bookmark recordings you plan to share externally so they aren't deleted before the customer watches them.
- **Redact before you share sensitive calls.** If a recording contains internal pricing discussions, competitive intel, or confidential customer data, trim or reshoot before making it public.
- **Use the AI summary in your email.** Paste the AI-generated summary above the link. Busy stakeholders get the gist in 30 seconds and skip to the parts they need.
- **Track who's viewed it.**
- **Default to locked.** Keep the workspace default as **locked** so recordings never leak accidentally. Flip to **public** deal-by-deal.
