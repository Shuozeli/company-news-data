# The Forrester Wave™

Forrester maintains hundreds of evaluations that cover thousands of unique vendors across markets — all based on an objective, data-driven analysis. Whether you’re navigating complex purchasing decisions or working to stay ahead in a competitive landscape, the Forrester Wave equips you with the knowledge and tools you need to make smarter, more strategic investments.


The Forrester Wave uses a transparent methodology to compare the most important players for our clients in a specific software, hardware, or services market. Each evaluation provides a simple visual overview that reflects Forrester’s clear call about the top providers in defined market sectors, based on a robust analysis of their current offerings and strategies. Each Forrester Wave analysis also includes an interactive experience that empowers clients to move from analysis to action, developing a custom shortlist informed by their unique priorities.


### Forrester Wave evaluations help you:


**Compare top options** . Get a clear snapshot of how the top providers rank against one another using our signature Forrester Wave graphic. Then, leverage the detailed analysis of vendors’ products and services to compare options based on our proven methodology.


**Shortlist providers faster** . Use interactive tools to filter and narrow the pool of vendors according to your unique needs so you can generate a short list, prioritize features, and quickly build consensus on your partner options.


**Make a confident decision** . Make a final selection with precision and confidence knowing your decision is grounded in transparent, objective criteria.
