# Forrester Market Insights

The Forrester Market Insights service provides a holistic perspective on buyers and the competitive landscape. It provides **objective advice** to see your company as your buyers see you and includes:


### Market
Insights


Stay ahead of changing industry and market dynamics and plan for the future with leading research and insights. You’ll get market forecasts, trends and predictions, technology and service provider landscapes, all Forrester Wave™ evaluations, and more.


### Buyer
Insights


Uncover opportunities to strengthen your roadmap based on what your buyers want and where your competitors are weak. You’ll have access to customer insights, customer obsession research, Forrester inquiry topic snapshots, and Forrester’s unique perspective for technology, CX, marketing, revenue, and product functions with 95+ buyer priorities.


### Key
Resources


Derisk decisions, move with confidence, and grow your business with objective advice and proven approaches. Use inquiry sessions, analyst relations research and frameworks, analyst-led webinars, events, and an exclusive Crafting Analyst Relations certification course.
