# Real-time payments (RTP): The instant revolution driving emerging markets

## **Quick summary**


- [Real-time payments](https://www.dlocal.com/payments/real-time-payments/) **are transforming money movement in emerging markets** , cutting transaction times from days or hours to just seconds—boosting trust, convenience, and financial inclusion.
- **Systems like Brazil’s Pix, India’s UPI, and Egypt’s InstaPay** are setting new benchmarks for instant payments.
- **Millions across Africa, Asia, and Latin America** are shifting from cash-heavy systems to faster, more efficient digital methods.
- **Bre-B in Colombia highlights** how real-time payment systems are evolving—delivering fast, secure, and transparent transfers tailored to local needs.
- **The road ahead relies on regulatory frameworks** that enable real-time payments to drive inclusion and digital growth in emerging markets.


This blog continues our series based on[dLocal’s Emerging Markets Payments Handbook 2025](https://www.dlocal.com/emerging-markets-payments-handbook-2025/?utm_source=hb_25&utm_medium=blog%20#1&utm_campaign=handbook) **.**


[Emerging markets are home to over 85% of the world’s population](https://www.dlocal.com/blog/markets-and-consumers/how-emerging-markets-are-becoming-the-new-engine-of-global-growth/) and account for a growing share of global economic activity. Yet, many still rely on legacy payment methods that are slow and fragmented. **Real-time payments (RTP)** are changing this landscape by enabling instant, account-to-account transfers that cut waiting times from days to seconds. This innovation is crucial in regions where cash remains dominant, and card penetration is limited.


By offering seamless, fast, and secure payment options, RTP supports financial inclusion, accelerates eCommerce adoption, and builds greater consumer confidence across **Africa,[Asia](https://www.dlocal.com/blog/markets-and-consumers/asias-payment-powerhouse-countless-ways-to-pay-one-digital-future/) , and Latin America.**


## **Regional leaders driving RTP innovation**


A number of regional real-time payment (RTP) systems have taken center stage, each designed to meet the unique demands of their local markets:


- [Brazil’s Pix](https://www.dlocal.com/payment-method/pix-payment/) has become more than just a payment method—it's a daily habit for over 175M users who send instant payments using phone numbers or QR codes. With the introduction of Pix Automático, scheduled payments like bills and subscriptions are now effortless, marking a significant leap in convenience in a market still adapting to digital finance.


- [India’s UPI](https://www.dlocal.com/payment-method/upi-payment/) **(Unified Payments Interface)** handles billions of real-time transactions every month. It powers more than half of India’s eCommerce payments and extends far beyond major metros, reaching millions of users in smaller cities and rural towns. Apps such as Google Pay and PhonePe thrive on UPI’s open system, transforming how India pays.


- [Egypt’s](https://www.dlocal.com/payment-processors-in-africa-and-middle-east/egypt-payment-methods-processors-ecommerce-market/) **InstaPay** is championing inclusion by enabling instant transfers between banks—even in less urbanized areas. With over 12 million users, it supports remittances and everyday commerce with simplicity and speed.


- [Bre-B in Colombia](https://www.dlocal.com/blog/markets-and-consumers/getting-ready-for-breb-colombias-new-real-time-payment-rail/) highlights how real-time payment systems are evolving—delivering fast, secure, and transparent transfers tailored to local needs.


Other emerging markets like[Mexico](https://www.dlocal.com/payment-processors-in-latin-america/mexico-payment-methods-processors-ecommerce-market-dlocal/) (SPEI),[Kenya](https://www.dlocal.com/payment-processors-in-africa-and-middle-east/kenya-payment-methods-processors-ecommerce-market/) (PesaLink),[South Africa](https://www.dlocal.com/payment-processors-in-africa-and-middle-east/south-africa-payment-methods-processors-ecommerce-market/) (PayShap),[Pakistan](https://www.dlocal.com/online-payment-processors-in-asia/pakistan-payment-methods-processors-ecommerce-market/) (Raast), and[Indonesia](https://www.dlocal.com/online-payment-processors-in-asia/indonesia-payment-methods-processors-ecommerce-market/) (BI-FAST) are rapidly building their RTP systems, all driving toward the same goal: making payments faster, easier, and more accessible.


## **Data Insight: The growing impact**

Source: PCMI


## **Driving digital growth and inclusion**


Real-time payments serve as a catalyst for digital economy acceleration in emerging markets by:


- **Bridging the gap to digital finance:** RTP connects directly to users’ bank accounts and mobile wallets, bypassing card networks and cash dependence.


- **Supporting mobile-first lifestyles:** With high smartphone penetration, consumers can transact securely anytime, anywhere.


- **Improving transparency and trust:** Instant confirmation reduces payment uncertainties and fraud risk.


- **Enabling businesses to scale:** Faster settlements improve cash flow for merchants and vendors, accelerating economic activity.

## **The road ahead**


The future of payments in emerging markets relies on **regulatory frameworks, fintech innovation, and widespread adoption** . Governments and regulators are fostering innovation by updating frameworks that promote safer, faster, and more accessible financial ecosystems.


Meanwhile, fintech companies are creating solutions tailored to local realities. Businesses that embrace real-time payments today will stand out in the digital economy, prepared to meet consumer expectations and unlock new opportunities for growth.


Beyond speed, real-time payments are reshaping consumer behavior, driving digital inclusion, and fueling economic development across Africa, Asia, and Latin America. As these regions advance, RTP will remain key to delivering **secure, seamless, and accessible financial experiences** .
