# Amkor Semiconductor Educator Bootcamp Builds Future Workforce

Building the semiconductor workforce starts earlier than hiring. It begins by giving educators a clear view of how the industry works and where students can fit into it. On June 3 and 4, Amkor Technology hosted its inaugural Semiconductor Educator Externship Bootcamp, bringing together educators, industry partners, and educational organizations to explore the semiconductor ecosystem from wafer fabrication to advanced packaging.


**Practical Experience for Classroom Impact**
The two-day bootcamp, developed in partnership with the SEMI Foundation, TechSmart Learning Fund, and West-MEC, provided educators with hands-on exposure to semiconductor manufacturing processes. Participants explored the complete semiconductor ecosystem and brought that learning to life through a cleanroom simulation, gaining practical experience they can translate directly to their classrooms.


The goal was straightforward and practical: help educators return to their classrooms with the context, tools, and technical understanding needed to make semiconductor careers more visible to students. As advanced packaging becomes more central to the industry, this type of workforce education becomes increasingly critical for preparing the next generation of talent.


**Partnerships Drive Immersive Learning Experience**
Amkor recognized multiple partners whose contributions made the immersive cleanroom experience possible. Advanced Technologies Consultants and ROOTS Education Co. provided leadership and facilitated the cleanroom simulation that gave educators hands-on experience with semiconductor manufacturing environments.


The program also benefited from the partnership of numerous individuals whose contributions made this first bootcamp possible. These collaborative efforts reflect Amkor’s commitment to working across the ecosystem to address workforce development challenges.


**Long-Term Impact on Career Pathways and Communities**
Programs like the Semiconductor Educator Externship Bootcamp create impact that reaches far beyond the two days of the event. The knowledge and experience educators gain will flow into future classrooms, shaping career pathways and influencing communities across the semiconductor industry.


By investing in educator development now, Amkor is helping build the foundation for a sustainable workforce that can meet the industry’s growing demands. This approach addresses workforce development at its source, ensuring students have access to accurate, up-to-date information about semiconductor career opportunities.


**Building Momentum for Future Workforce Initiatives**
The success of this inaugural bootcamp establishes a foundation for ongoing workforce development efforts. As advanced packaging continues becoming more central to semiconductor manufacturing, the need for educated educators who can effectively communicate career opportunities will grow.


Amkor’s commitment to workforce development extends beyond this initial program, with the company continuing to explore ways to make semiconductor careers more visible and accessible to students at all levels. For those interested in joining the semiconductor industry, Amkor offers career opportunities across multiple disciplines and locations.
