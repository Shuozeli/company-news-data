# Amkor Vietnam Wins Semiconductor Industry Award at SEMIExpo

Amkor Technology, Inc. is proud to announce that SungHun Kim, Senior Vice President and Country Manager of Amkor Technology Vietnam, has been recognized with the Vietnam Semiconductor Distinguished Industry Award 2025 at SEMIExpo Vietnam. This prestigious recognition underscores SungHun Kim’s exceptional leadership and Amkor Vietnam’s transformative contributions to Vietnam’s dynamic semiconductor sector.​


**Driving Innovation and Strategic Growth**
Amkor Vietnam’s commitment to driving the nation’s semiconductor industry forward is evident through ongoing strategic investments, advanced technology transfer, focused workforce development, and ecosystem collaboration. These initiatives reinforce Vietnam’s growing influence within the global semiconductor supply chain and further establish Amkor as a trusted innovation partner in advanced packaging and manufacturing.​


**Empowering the Semiconductor Leaders of Tomorrow**
During SEMIExpo Vietnam 2025, Amkor Technology Vietnam hosted industry delegates and STEM undergraduates for a special, behind-the-scenes tour of its advanced packaging and test facility in Bac Ninh. As part of the TECH Zoomers Bootcamp, attendees received hands-on exposure to Amkor’s state-of-the-art IC assembly and testing solutions, gaining practical insights into how Amkor’s expertise supports global market needs.


By bridging education and innovation, Amkor continues to invest in cultivating future industry leaders—demonstrating a steadfast commitment to advancing technology and building a resilient workforce for Vietnam’s semiconductor ecosystem.​


Congratulations to SungHun Kim and the entire Amkor Vietnam team for their achievements and continued leadership in powering the future of the semiconductor industry.
