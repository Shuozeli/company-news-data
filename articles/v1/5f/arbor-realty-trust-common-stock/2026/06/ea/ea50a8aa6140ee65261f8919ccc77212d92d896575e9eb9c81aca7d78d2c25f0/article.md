# Indianapolis Multifamily Market Snapshot — June 2026

The Indianapolis multifamily market outranked all other U.S. metropolitan areas in Arbor’s latest[Top Markets for Multifamily Investment Report](https://arbor.com/research/top-markets-for-large-multifamily-investment-archive/) . It has been supported by strong labor market conditions, tight occupancy levels, and a favorable affordability profile. Indiana’s capital has maintained a[steady labor market](https://www.indyecondev.com/) , with relatively low unemployment, and consistent wage gains. At a time when the national labor market is cooling, Indianapolis’ stability has become increasingly valuable. High occupancy rates have influenced local rent performance as well. As national rent growth moderated, Indianapolis outperformed, with annual rent growth exceeding the U.S. average for 30 consecutive months through early 2026. This trend demonstrates the sustained relative strength of the market, a significant distinction in the current phase of the cycle.
