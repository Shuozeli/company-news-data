# Gate US Joins BitGo's Go Network, Expanding Secure Access to Exchange Liquidity

## Key takeaways


-


Access Gate US liquidity without moving assets on exchange. Institutions can trade on Gate US while assets remain securely held in regulated custody at BitGo Bank & Trust.


-


A market structure built for institutions. Go Network Off-Exchange Settlement (OES) separates custody from trading, allowing institutions to access exchange liquidity while maintaining the protections of an independent regulated fiduciary.


-


Part of BitGo's Global Liquidity Layer. Gate US expands the venues available through Go Network OES, giving institutions more ways to access global digital asset markets through one connected infrastructure.


Institutional digital asset trading continues to evolve, but one challenge has remained consistent: accessing liquidity has often required institutions to move assets onto multiple exchanges, increasing operational complexity and risk.


Today, BitGo announced that Gate US is joining BitGo's Go Network Off-Exchange Settlement (OES), giving institutions another secure way to access U.S. digital asset liquidity while keeping assets safely held in regulated custody at BitGo Bank & Trust, National Association (BitGo Bank & Trust).


The integration expands Go Networks growing partner venues and reinforces our vision of building the infrastructure layer that connects institutions to global digital asset markets.


## Access exchange liquidity while assets remain in custody


Traditionally, institutions have transferred assets onto exchanges before they could trade, requiring firms to move capital between venues and manage multiple relationships.


Go Network OES takes a different approach. Instead of transferring assets onto Gate US, clients allocate balances held in custody at BitGo Bank & Trust for trading. Those balances are securely projected to Gate US and accepted for execution while the underlying assets remain in segregated, regulated custody throughout the trading lifecycle.


Once trades are executed, settlement is completed through BitGo Bank & Trust's Go Network infrastructure. The result is a more efficient trading experience that allows institutions to access exchange liquidity without compromising how they safeguard assets.


“We believe the future of institutional trading isn't about forcing clients into a single venue or workflow. It's about connecting them to every opportunity through a common infrastructure layer,” said Adam Sporn, Head of Prime Brokerage and Institutional Sales at BitGo. “Whether clients trade through BitGo Prime or directly with exchanges like Gate US, they should be able to access liquidity without sacrificing the protections that come from keeping assets in regulated qualified custody. Go Network is how we're making that possible.”


## A market structure designed for institutions


As institutional participation in digital assets continues to grow, firms increasingly expect market infrastructure that reflects the principles of traditional capital markets. One of the most important is the separation of custody from trading.


Go Network enables institutions to maintain custody with an independent regulated fiduciary while accessing liquidity across connected exchanges. Rather than placing assets with each trading venue, institutions can keep assets protected in qualified custody while securely participating in digital asset markets.


This approach helps reduce operational complexity, minimizes counterparty exposure, and gives institutions greater confidence as they scale trading operations.


“Institutional clients want the ability to trade with confidence, without compromising on custody or counterparty risk. Partnering with BitGo on Go Network OES lets Gate US offer our liquidity to institutions in a framework built around regulated, segregated custody—reinforcing the kind of trusted infrastructure this market needs to keep scaling. We're excited to deepen our relationship with BitGo and provide mutual clients with a secure and efficient path to our markets," Laura Liu, COO of Gate US said.


## Meeting traders where liquidity exists


Every institution has a different trading strategy. Some clients choose to execute through BitGo Prime, which provides a single point of access to aggregated global liquidity across exchanges, market makers, and OTC liquidity providers. Others maintain direct relationships with specific exchanges to access regional markets, specialized products, or venue-specific liquidity.


BitGo supports both. Go Network OES complements BitGo Prime by connecting external trading venues to the same regulated custody and settlement infrastructure. Whether clients trade through BitGo Prime or directly on Gate US, BitGo provides a consistent operating model built around security, operational efficiency, and regulated custody.


Our goal isn't to dictate where institutions trade. Our goal is to make sure they can trade wherever opportunities exist, without sacrificing the protections that come from keeping assets in qualified custody.


## Connecting Institutions to Global Liquidity


The addition of Gate US is another step toward BitGo's broader vision of building the Global Liquidity Layer for institutional digital asset markets. As Go Network continues to expand, institutions gain seamless access to a growing network of exchanges and liquidity providers through a consistent custody and settlement framework.


BitGo is building the infrastructure that connects institutions to liquidity wherever it exists. And as institutions continue to expand their digital asset operations, the value of that connectivity compounds, providing greater flexibility, stronger operational controls, and secure access to global markets through one trusted infrastructure provider.

## Frequently Asked Questions


### What is Go Network Off-Exchange Settlement (OES)?


Go Network OES allows institutions to trade on participating exchanges while keeping assets securely held in regulated custody at BitGo Bank & Trust or BitGo Singapore Pte. Ltd. (BitGo Singapore). Rather than transfer assets to exchange accounts, clients allocate assets held at BitGo’s regulated entities for trading, with settlement completed through Go Network in qualified custody.


### Do my assets leave BitGo custody when I trade on Gate US?


No. Assets remain securely held in segregated, regulated custody at BitGo Bank & Trust throughout the trading lifecycle. Available balances are projected to Gate US for execution, while the underlying assets remain in BitGo Bank & Trust custody until settlement.


### How does Go Network improve security?


Go Network separates custody from trading, allowing institutions to maintain assets with an independent regulated fiduciary instead of transferring them onto an exchange before every trade. This reduces operational complexity while helping mitigate counterparty risk.


### Can I still trade through BitGo Prime?


Yes. Go Network complements BitGo Prime. Clients can access aggregated global liquidity through BitGo Prime or continue trading directly with connected exchanges like Gate US while benefiting from the same regulated custody and settlement infrastructure.


### Why is BitGo connecting to exchanges if it offers BitGo Prime?


Institutions access liquidity in different ways. Some prefer a single point of access through BitGo Prime, while others maintain direct relationships with specific exchanges for regional markets or specialized products. BitGo supports both models, giving institutions the flexibility to trade how they choose while maintaining a consistent security and operating framework.


### What is the Global Liquidity Layer?


The Global Liquidity Layer is BitGo's vision for connecting institutions to digital asset markets through a unified infrastructure. By aggregating liquidity from various counterparties and venues across the globe, BitGo enables institutions to access global opportunities through one secure platform.


### Which exchanges and venues are supported by Go Network OES?


Go Network continues to expand with exchanges and trading venues around the world, giving institutions secure access to an increasingly global network of liquidity while maintaining regulated custody at BitGo Bank & Trust or BitGo Singapore. Current support includes: Crossover Markets CROSSx, Deribit via Copper ClearLoop, Finery Markets, Gate Global, HTX, INX, KuCoin, OKX US, and STS Digital with additional venues being added regularly.
