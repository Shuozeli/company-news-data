# BitGo Prime: Connecting Institutions to Global Digital Asset Markets

## Key Takeaways:


-


BitGo Prime's Global Liquidity Layer connects institutions to global digital asset markets, combining intelligent liquidity sourcing with the flexibility to trade through the workflows they already use.


-


BitGo Prime extends beyond trading, by integrating financing, collateral management, and settlement into one institutional experience.


-


Recent enhancements simplify every stage of the institutional workflow, from connecting to markets and executing trades to financing positions, managing collateral, and moving capital.


Institutional participation has transformed digital asset markets: liquidity has expanded, new exchanges have launched, market makers have become more sophisticated, and regional trading hubs have emerged across North America, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia Pacific.


For institutions this is good news. The problem has shifted from finding liquidity to operating efficiently across it. Maintaining relationships with numerous venues and counterparties, integrating multiple trading systems, and coordinating settlement workflows across platforms introduces operational complexity and increases risk, making it harder to deploy capital.


Since 2019, BitGo Prime has offered institutions a secure way to trade digital assets from regulated custody, and as markets have evolved, so has our platform. Over the past several months, we've expanded BitGo Prime's capabilities, advancing our vision of building the financial institution for digital assets. Keep reading to learn what's new.


## Connecting to Global Markets through BitGo Prime's Global Liquidity Layer


At the core of BitGo Prime is the Global Liquidity Layer, giving institutions a single connection to global digital asset markets. By intelligently sourcing liquidity across exchanges, market makers, Over-the-Counter (OTC) counterparties, and regional liquidity hubs, BitGo Prime helps institutions achieve competitive pricing, cost-efficient execution, and resilient access to liquidity without the operational burden of managing dozens of trading relationships.


Institutions don't all trade the same way. Some require fast, automated execution through APIs and electronic workflows. Others rely on experienced traders to source liquidity for large or complex transactions with minimal market impact. BitGo Prime supports both through a combination of low-touch electronic execution and[high-touch OTC trading](https://www.bitgo.com/resources/blog/bitgo-unveils-secure-all-in-one-otc-trading-desk/?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=prime&utm_campaign=july_globalliquiditylayer) , allowing institutions to access global liquidity through the workflows that best fit their strategy.


Rather than maintaining independent connections to exchanges, liquidity providers, and OTC counterparties, clients gain a single institutional point of access to BitGo Prime, simplifying connectivity while preserving flexibility.

## Secure Collateral


Capital only works if counterparties have confidence in the collateral behind it. BitGo Prime's[tri-party collateral management](https://www.bitgo.com/resources/blog/crypto-collateral-management-institutions/?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=prime&utm_campaign=july_globalliquiditylayer) solutions enable institutions to establish trading and financing relationships while keeping eligible collateral securely held under segregated custody with BitGo's regulated custodian entity BitGo Bank & Trust, National Association,[an OCC-chartered national trust bank](https://www.bitgo.com/resources/blog/bitgo-secures-occ-approval/?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=prime&utm_campaign=july_globalliquiditylayer) .


Rather than transferring assets between counterparties, BitGo acts as a neutral third party, administering collateral arrangements according to agreed terms between the pledgor and secured party. This reduces counterparty risk, improves capital efficiency, and allows institutions to establish financing and trading relationships with greater confidence.


## Efficient Capital Movement


Once positions are financed and collateral is in place, institutions need a fast and reliable way to move capital.


[Go Network](https://www.bitgo.com/products/settlement/?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=prime&utm_campaign=july_globalliquiditylayer) is BitGo's proprietary institutional settlement infrastructure, purpose-built for trading, financing, and collateral management workflows. It enables institutions to transfer and settle fiat and digital assets via[Delivery-vs-Payment (DvP)](https://www.bitgo.com/resources/blog/crypto-settlement-and-payment-infrastructure/?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=prime&utm_campaign=july_globalliquiditylayer) 24/7/365 across BitGo's regulated custody rails, helping reduce counterparty risk, improve capital efficiency, and eliminate manual settlement workflows.


Together, tri-party collateral management and Go Network create a connected post-trade infrastructure. Institutions can secure collateral, finance positions, and settle transactions through a single platform, keeping capital moving instead of waiting on fragmented operational processes.

## Frequently Asked Questions


### What is the Global Liquidity Layer?


The Global Liquidity Layer is the institutional trading infrastructure within BitGo Prime. It provides a single institutional connection to global digital asset markets by intelligently sourcing liquidity across exchanges, market makers, OTC counterparties, and regional liquidity providers.


### Is the Global Liquidity Layer a new trading platform?


The Global Liquidity Layer brings together the trading capabilities BitGo Prime has continued to expand over time, including global liquidity access, API connectivity, OEMS integrations, advanced order types, execution analytics, and reporting, under a unified trading infrastructure.


### Which execution workflows are supported?


Clients can trade electronically through REST, FIX, and WebSocket APIs, OEMS integrations, and BitGo's web trading platform, or work directly with BitGo's OTC trading desk for block trades, financing solutions, structured products, derivatives, and other high-touch execution needs.


### How does BitGo Prime improve execution quality?


BitGo Prime combines intelligent liquidity sourcing with Transaction Cost Analysis, enhanced post-trade reporting, and transparent execution tools to help institutions evaluate total execution outcomes rather than quoted spreads alone.


### What financing capabilities are available?


BitGo Prime offers portfolio-based digital asset financing, self-service borrowing and lending, and tri-party collateral management, enabling institutions to improve capital efficiency while maintaining eligible collateral in regulated custody.


### What is Go Network?


Go Network is BitGo's institutional settlement infrastructure. It enables clients to move fiat and digital assets within BitGo's regulated custody ecosystem while helping reduce counterparty risk, improve capital efficiency, and simplify post-trade settlement.


### Why bring trading, financing, and settlement together with access to regulated custody?


Institutions increasingly want infrastructure that operates as one integrated platform rather than a collection of standalone products. By bringing execution, financing, collateral management, settlement together, with access to regulated custody through BitGo Bank & Trust, N.A., BitGo Prime helps clients reduce operational complexity, improve capital efficiency, and manage more of the digital asset lifecycle through one trusted partner.


But integration should not come at the expense of market structure. BitGo has consistently advocated that regulated qualified custody should remain independent from trading activities, giving institutions the operational benefits of a unified platform while maintaining the protections that come from the separation of custody and execution. This approach helps reduce counterparty risk, strengthens client asset protections, and reflects the institutional market structure principles BitGo has championed for more than a decade.
