# BitGo Prime Expands Liquidity Network with the Addition of Virtu Financial

## Key Takeaways:


-


Virtu Financial joins BitGo Prime, LLC’s (“BitGo Prime”) global liquidity network, adding depth to digital asset pairs for institutional clients.


-


Client assets stay in custody with BitGo Bank & Trust, National Association (“BitGo”), or BitGo Europe GmbH throughout trading and settlement, so liquidity access does not require moving assets off of regulated custody.


-


The partnership combines BitGo’s regulated custody framework and trading infrastructure with Virtu's expertise in market making and electronic trading technology


Institutions searching for best pricing and execution rarely trade through one liquidity provider. They often need to source from multiple exchanges, liquidity providers, and counterparties, each with its own agreements, connectivity, and operational workflows. There is no single point of access which means institutions end up managing relationships instead of simply accessing liquidity.


BitGo Prime was built to remove that friction serving as a unified liquidity layer. Through a single onboarding and connection, clients gain access to aggregated liquidity from a growing network of leading counterparties, while trading directly from regulated custody without moving assets.


The addition of Virtu Financial further strengthens that network, bringing more liquidity, greater depth, and increased competition for orders routed through BitGo Prime.


## How the partnership works


BitGo Prime clients trading through the platform now have Virtu's pricing available alongside BitGo Prime's other liquidity partners. Clients do not need a separate relationship or connection with Virtu directly. The aggregation happens on BitGo Prime's platform, and clients trade against the combined pool.


Assets in custody do not move during this process, they remain with BitGo, an OCC-chartered digital asset trust bank, or with BitGo Europe GmbH, which holds a Markets in Crypto-Assets Regulation license from Germany's Federal Financial Supervisory Authority (BaFin). That structure lets clients access new liquidity sources without changing where their assets sit or how they are held.


## What Virtu brings to the network


Virtu is a publicly traded market maker operating across equities, exchange-traded funds, foreign exchange, futures, fixed income, and commodities, on hundreds of venues in more than 50 countries. Its entry into BitGo's network extends that same infrastructure and market making approach into digital assets.


“Clients continue to look for trusted partners that can provide deep liquidity, consistent execution, and operational reliability through all market conditions,” said Adam Sporn, Head of Prime Brokerage and U.S. Institutional Sales at BitGo. “Expanding our liquidity network with leading firms like Virtu is an important part of delivering a best-in-class trading experience for our clients.”


Scotte Moegling, Head of Business Development for Digital Assets at Virtu Financial, framed the move as a continuation of Virtu's broader digital asset strategy: "The addition of Virtu to BitGo's network reflects Virtu's continued commitment to expanding our presence across digital assets, allowing us to increase liquidity and provide institutional clients with the competitive pricing they have come to expect across other asset classes we participate in."


## Why this matters


Liquidity depth compounds. Each new provider added to BitGo Prime's network does not just add one more source of pricing, it adds another point of competition among providers already in the network, which can tighten spreads and improve fill quality across the board.


For institutions, that shows up as fewer venues to manage and a deeper network giving BitGo Prime more room to route orders intelligently, matching client flow with whichever provider is offering the best price at that moment, rather than relying on a single counterparty's quote.


Virtu's addition is a step in that direction: one more established name adding to a network that already includes multiple liquidity providers, all accessible through a single point of access and without leaving regulated custody.

## Frequently asked questions


### Do BitGo Prime clients need a separate relationship with Virtu to access its pricing?


No. Virtu's pricing is available automatically to BitGo Prime clients as part of the aggregated liquidity pool. Clients do not onboard with Virtu separately or manage a direct connection to it.


### Do assets need to leave custody to trade against Virtu's liquidity or the rest of the network?


No. Assets remain in custody with BitGo, or BitGo Europe GmbH throughout trading and settlement. Execution happens directly from custody rather than requiring funds to move to a separate trading venue first.


### What is liquidity aggregation?


Liquidity aggregation is the combining of price quotes from multiple liquidity providers into a single pool that clients can trade against, rather than sourcing prices from each provider one at a time.


### What is a market maker?


A market maker is a firm that continuously quotes both buy and sell prices for an asset, providing the liquidity that allows other participants to trade without waiting for a matching counterparty order.
