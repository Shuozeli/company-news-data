# BitGo Adds Day-One Support for Robinhood Chain Mainnet

## Key Takeaways:


-


BitGo is a day-one infrastructure partner for Robinhood Chain


-


Institutions can now securely manage Robinhood Chain assets through BitGo’s wallet and custody infrastructure


-


BitGo’s support enables institutions to participate in stock tokens, tokenized real world assets, and onchain financial markets from launch


BitGo now supports Robinhood Chain, providing institutions with immediate access to secure wallet and custody infrastructure for one of the most significant blockchain launches focused on bringing financial markets onchain.


## About Robinhood Chain


Robinhood Chain is an Ethereum Layer 2 built on the Arbitrum Nitro stack, designed for tokenized real-world assets, 24/7 trading, and seamless bridging between traditional and decentralized finance. Since its public testnet went live in February 2026, the network has processed over 199 million transactions (as of July 1, 2026). With Robinhood’s brand and credibility behind it, the chain represents a meaningful step toward mainstream adoption of onchain financial markets.


## What’s Available Today


BitGo’s Robinhood Chain integration, delivers the core building blocks for institutions:


-


Hot and cold wallet support built on BitGo’s proven multi-signature architecture, through BitGo, Inc. and BitGo’s[globally regulated entities](https://www.bitgo.com/entities/) , including[BitGo Bank & Trust, National Association](https://www.bitgo.com/resources/blog/bitgo-secures-occ-approval/?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=ecosystem&utm_campaign=rh_mainnet) , an OCC-chartered trust bank


-


ERC-20 support for assets launching on the network at mainnet


-


Native ETH gas management consistent with Robinhood Chain’s Arbitrum-based gas tank model, with enterprise fee address infrastructure ready for deployment


## Why Robinhood Chain Matters for Institutional Crypto


For BitGo clients, including exchanges, asset managers, and fintech platforms, Robinhood Chain represents one of the most significant new venues for tokenized asset workflows to emerge in recent years, and BitGo ensures institutions can participate from day one. As stock tokens, real-world assets, and local fiat move onchain, the institutions managing those assets need a custody provider that's already embedded in the networks where that activity is happening. This is part of BitGo’s broader commitment to bring the infrastructure layer for the chains that matter. From the earliest days of bitcoin custody to today’s multi-chain world, BitGo has consistently been dedicated to supporting networks that define the next phase of institutions crypto. BitGo's day-one support for Robinhood Chain is a direct expression of our commitment.


BitGo clients can begin building on Robinhood Chain today. Get started on the[BitGo platform](http://app.bitgo.com/) or[contact our team](https://calendly.com/d/crmk-p2t-k78?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=ecosystem&utm_campaign=rh_mainnet) to learn more.
