# Inside the Infrastructure Powering DTCC's Tokenized Securities Milestone

How BitGo and industry partners demonstrated the secure custody, movement, and settlement of tokenized securities in institutional capital markets.


## Key takeaways


-


**BitGo Bank & Trust, National Association provided the regulated infrastructure behind the movement of tokenized securities.** As the only OCC regulated, full-service qualified custodian integrated with the DTCC Tokenization Service, BitGo Bank & Trust enabled institutions to securely custody, move, and settle tokenized U.S. Treasuries and equities collateral within existing, regulated workflows.


-


**Institutional tokenization has moved beyond theory.** DTCC's Tokenization Service demonstrated that DTC-held securities can be represented onchain while preserving the protections, ownership rights, and market structure institutions expect.


-


**Infrastructure, not just tokenization, is what enables adoption.** Qualified custody, secure asset movement, trading, and settlement are essential to bringing tokenized assets into institutional markets.


-


**Collaboration is accelerating the next generation of market infrastructure.** BitGo, Marex, DriveWealth, Tradeweb, Talos, Temple Digital, and DTCC demonstrated how trusted infrastructure providers can work together to support real-world institutional workflows.


Tokenization has long promised to improve settlement efficiency, collateral mobility, and capital utilization across financial markets. This week, that promise moved another step toward reality.


As the only OCC regulated, full-service qualified custodian integrated with the DTCC Tokenization Service, BitGo Bank & Trust, National Association (BitGo Bank & Trust) helped demonstrate how tokenized securities can move securely through institutional markets. Working alongside[DTCC](https://www.dtcc.com/) ,[Marex](https://www.marex.com/) ,[DriveWealth](https://www.drivewealth.com/) ,[Talos](https://www.talos.com/) ,[Temple Digital Group](https://templedigitalgroup.com/) ,[Tradeweb](https://www.tradeweb.com/) , and other market participants, BitGo Bank & Trust supported the custody, delivery, and settlement of tokenized U.S. Treasury collateral used in live repo and reverse repo workflows.


The milestone demonstrates that tokenization is no longer simply about creating digital representations of traditional assets. It is about enabling those assets to move securely through the operational infrastructure institutions already rely on for custody, collateral management, trading, and settlement.


## What happened behind the scenes


The limited production event demonstrated an end-to-end institutional workflow for DTC-tokenized U.S. Treasuries and equities.


Rather than existing solely as digital assets on a blockchain, the tokenized Treasuries and equities moved through familiar institutional processes while maintaining the ownership rights, operational controls, and regulatory protections associated with traditional securities.


## At a high level, the workflow included:


-


**DTCC**[tokenized eligible DTC-held U.S. Treasuries](https://www.bitgo.com/resources/blog/real-world-asset-tokenization/?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=custody&utm_campaign=dtcctokenizedservice) and equities through its Tokenization Service.


-


**BitGo Bank & Trust, National Association** securely custodied the tokenized securities and supported their delivery and settlement throughout the transaction lifecycle.


-


**Marex** and **DriveWealth** facilitated repo and reverse repo transactions, enabling tokenized Treasuries and equities to be pledged as collateral in support of liquidity and capital efficiency.


-


**Tradeweb, Talos, and Temple Digital** connected counterparties through familiar institutional RFQ and execution workflows.


-


**BitGo Bank & Trust** supported the secure movement and settlement of tokenized collateral between participants while maintaining regulated qualified custody throughout the process.


Together, these participants demonstrated how tokenized assets can move across institutional markets without sacrificing the resiliency and operational rigor expected of traditional financial infrastructure.


## Why qualified custody infrastructure is foundational


While tokenization often receives the most attention, institutions cannot adopt[tokenized assets](https://www.bitgo.com/resources/blog/what-is-tokenization-of-assets/?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=custody&utm_campaign=dtcctokenizedservice) without trusted infrastructure.


Institutional markets require assets to move securely between counterparties, collateral must be pledged and released efficiently, ownership must remain verifiable, and settlement must occur within regulated operational frameworks.


Through BitGo Bank & Trust’s wallet infrastructure, tokenized U.S. Treasury and equities collateral was able to move throughout the transaction lifecycle while remaining under qualified custody. This gave participating institutions the ability to leverage tokenized assets without compromising security, controls or regulatory oversight.


By connecting qualified custody with collateral movement and settlement, BitGo Bank & Trust helped demonstrate how tokenized securities can integrate into existing institutional operations rather than requiring entirely new ones.

## Frequently Asked Questions


## What is the DTCC Tokenization Service?


The DTCC Tokenization Service enables eligible DTC-held securities to be represented as tokenized assets while preserving the ownership rights, protections, and operational controls associated with traditional securities. The service is designed to help financial institutions integrate tokenized assets into existing capital markets workflows, including collateral management, trading, and settlement.


## What are tokenized securities?


Tokenized securities are digital representations of traditional financial assets, such as U.S. Treasuries, equities, or other securities, recorded on blockchain infrastructure. Rather than creating entirely new assets, tokenization allows existing financial instruments to move more efficiently through modern digital infrastructure while maintaining established legal and regulatory frameworks.


## Why does tokenization matter for institutions?


Tokenization has the potential to improve how assets move through financial markets by enabling faster settlement, greater collateral mobility, improved capital efficiency, and increased operational automation.


For institutions, the value extends beyond digitizing assets. The real opportunity lies in integrating tokenized assets into existing custody, trading, financing, collateral management, and treasury workflows without disrupting established operational processes.


**What role did BitGo play in the DTCC Tokenization Service milestone?**


BitGo Bank & Trust is the only OCC-regulated, full-service qualified custodian integrated with the DTCC Tokenization Service.


During the production event, BitGo Bank & Trust provided the qualified custody wallet and settlement infrastructure supporting the movement of tokenized U.S. Treasury and equities collateral through institutional repo and reverse repo workflows. This enabled institutions to securely custody, transfer, and settle tokenized collateral within a regulated framework.


## Why is qualified custody important for tokenized securities?


Regulatory and operational frameworks governing institutional risk and compliance require that tokenized securities be securely custodied, transferred between counterparties, pledged as collateral, and settled in ways that satisfy those same standards.


Qualified custody provides the security, legal protections, and operational controls institutions expect when participating in digital asset markets.


## Is tokenization only about blockchain technology?


No. While blockchain provides the underlying technology, institutional adoption depends on the broader infrastructure surrounding the asset. Trading venues, custodians, collateral managers, settlement providers, and market infrastructure must all work together to enable tokenized assets to function within real-world financial markets.


## How is BitGo supporting the future of tokenized markets?


BitGo provides the digital asset infrastructure institutions need to participate in tokenized markets. Through qualified custody, secure wallet technology, institutional trading, financing, collateral management, and settlement solutions, BitGo helps institutions manage digital assets throughout the entire transaction lifecycle. As tokenized securities become more widely adopted, BitGo is continuing to build the infrastructure that connects traditional capital markets with onchain finance.
