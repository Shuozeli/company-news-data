# Prominence Health Named to Becker’s 2026 “ACOs to Know” List for Excellence in Value-Based Care


**Reno, NV (February 24, 2026)** – Prominence Health, a subsidiary of Universal Health Services (UHS), is proud to announce its recognition on the Becker’s Hospital Review 2026 “ACOs to Know” list, honoring accountable care organizations (ACOs) meeting and exceeding value-based care standards while advancing quality, affordability and patient-centered outcomes. Becker’s Hospital Review recognizes leading ACOs that demonstrate exceptional performance in cost savings, efficiency and care coordination – benefiting both patients and providers alike.


Prominence Health’s inclusion on the list underscores its impressive track record, including a milestone achievement of over[$100 million in savings for Medicare in 2024](https://uhs.com/news/universal-health-services-accountable-care-organizations-acos-drive-record-results-saving-medicare-100-million-in-2024/) – a record-setting performance that underscores the effectiveness of its population health strategies. Additionally,[Prominence Health Medicare Advantage plans earned a notable 4.5 out of 5-star CMS rating for the 2026 plan year](https://uhs.com/news/prominence-health-medicare-advantage-plans-earn-4-5-out-of-5-star-cms-rating/) , an achievement held by fewer than 10% of Medicare Advantage plans nationwide.


“We are honored to be recognized by Becker’s among the top ACOs nationally,” said Kamal Jemmoua, CEO for Prominence Health. “This distinction affirms our team’s focus on delivering high-value, patient-centered care while responsibly managing costs. Our innovative approaches continue to improve not only health outcomes but also the overall experience for the patients and communities we serve.”


[View the full Becker’s 2026 “ACOs to Know” list →](https://www.beckershospitalreview.com/hospital-management-administration/50-acos-to-know-2025/)


Prominence Health remains committed to pushing the boundaries of accountable care, leveraging data-driven insights and advanced care coordination to enhance quality and affordability for all its members.


### About Prominence Health


Prominence Health is a value-based care organization bridging the gap between affiliated health systems and independent providers, building trust and collaboration between the two. Prominence Health creates value for populations and providers to strengthen integrated partnership, advance market opportunities, and improve outcomes for our patients and members. Founded in 1993, Prominence Health started as a health maintenance organization (HMO) and was acquired by a subsidiary of Universal Health Services, Inc. (UHS) in 2014. Prominence Health serves members, physicians, and health systems across Medicare, Medicare Advantage, Accountable Care Organizations, and commercial payer partnerships.
