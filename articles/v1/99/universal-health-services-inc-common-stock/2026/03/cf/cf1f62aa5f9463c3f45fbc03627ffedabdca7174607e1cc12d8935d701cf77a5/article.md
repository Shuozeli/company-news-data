# Chief Women Magazine: Karen E. Johnson – A Leader Bridging Strategy and Care in Behavioral Health


karen-johnson_735-1


**March 2026 –** UHS Senior Vice President and Chief Clinical Officer for the Behavioral Health Division Karen E. Johnson has been featured in the 10 Most Influential Women in Healthcare, 2026 edition of *Chief Women Magazine* .


In this article, Karen discusses her 40-year devotion to the behavioral healthcare sector, driven by a commitment to positively impact every individual who enters UHS facilities.


An excerpt from the article: “For Karen, leadership is an extension of her core values: compassion, integrity and accountability. Her philosophy is deeply rooted in the belief that quality and safety must be the bedrock of every clinical operation. She finds great harmony in working for an organization like UHS, which has maintained the same mission for over 45 years. This mission mirrors her own commitment to providing care that patients recommend to their families and physicians prefer for their patients.”
