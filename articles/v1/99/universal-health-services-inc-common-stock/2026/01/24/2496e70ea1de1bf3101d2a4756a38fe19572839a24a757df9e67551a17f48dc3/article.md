# Marc D. Miller Named to the Top 10 CEOs Transforming Healthcare by The CEO Forum


CEO_Forum_image


**King of Prussia, PA (January 13, 2026)** – Universal Health Services (NYSE: UHS) today announced that President and CEO Marc D. Miller appears among the “Top 10 CEOs Transforming Healthcare in America” as published by The CEO Forum Group.


The CEO Forum selected Marc in the exclusive category of Operational Excellence – bestowed for continued positive outcomes in clinical results, technology innovations, occupancy and utilization, safety and patient experience, and consistent growth in financial performance.


Honorees were selected following interviews with CEO Forum Group leadership and a subsequent criteria-based review by the group’s editorial board. Candidates were evaluated on their ability to drive transformation, which The CEO Forum defines as a “CEO who creates new value that reinvigorates a company, reinvents an industry or reboots society.”
