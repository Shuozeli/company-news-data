# LEADERS Magazine: The UHS Difference – An Interview with Cheryl K. Ramagano

**Cheryl discusses her 40+ year career journey with UHS, the company’s culture and values, and the keys to UHS’ industry leadership.**


**January 2026 –** UHS Senior Vice President and Treasurer Cheryl K. Ramagano has been featured in the January-March 2026 edition of LEADERS Magazine. In this article, Cheryl discusses her 40+ year career journey with UHS, the company’s culture and values, and the keys to UHS’ industry leadership.


“The UHS difference is its people. There are many of us that have been with UHS for more than 25 years. This is a real distinction between UHS and most other companies. Given UHS’ continued growth, our employees have a variety of opportunities for professional growth and career advancement. Employees feel good about working for UHS and find value and purpose in contributing to the company’s mission and success.”
