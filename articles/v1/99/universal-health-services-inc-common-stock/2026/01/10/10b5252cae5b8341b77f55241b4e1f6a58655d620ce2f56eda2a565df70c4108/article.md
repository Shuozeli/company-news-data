# Forbes: High Tech, High Touch: How Telehealth Is Reshaping the Future of Care


**January 2026 –** In a recent Forbes article, UHS President and CEO Marc D. Miller shared how UHS is leveraging telehealth and other technologies to help improve efficiency, care coordination and patient experience.


“Recognizing that every patient’s needs are unique, UHS is advancing innovative technologies – including telehealth options – to deliver care that is more accessible, efficient and personalized,” said Marc D. Miller, President and CEO, UHS. “Integrating virtual care and remote monitoring supports earlier intervention, enhances real-time care management and helps sustain patient engagement…while reducing clinician burden across diverse healthcare settings.”
