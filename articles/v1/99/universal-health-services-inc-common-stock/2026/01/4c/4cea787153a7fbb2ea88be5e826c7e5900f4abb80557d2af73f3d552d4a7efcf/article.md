# Becker’s Interview: UHS’ Roselle Charlier on Patient Engagement


Medium-GettyImages-1133527295


**January 2026 –** In 2025, King of Prussia, Pa.-based Universal Health Services sent more than 1 million text messages to patients to solicit Google reviews, which remain vital for health systems even in the era of[AI search](https://www.beckershospitalreview.com/digital-marketing/ai-is-the-lever-26-healthcare-marketing-priorities/) , a leader told *Becker’s* .


Not only that, but UHS, a publicly traded system with 29 acute care and 345 behavioral health hospitals, responded to those reviews in an average of 12 hours, aiming to make current – and future – patients feel like they’re being heard.


Press Ganey recently bestowed UHS with its[2025 Guardian of Excellence Award](https://uhs.com/news/uhs-receives-2025-press-ganey-human-experience-guardian-of-excellence-award/) for ranking in the top 1% of providers for consumer experience.


*Becker’s* recently caught up with[Roselle Charlier](https://www.beckershospitalreview.com/digital-marketing/what-11-health-system-marketing-chiefs-are-watching-in-25/) , UHS Vice President and Chief Marketing and Communications Officer, to learn more.
