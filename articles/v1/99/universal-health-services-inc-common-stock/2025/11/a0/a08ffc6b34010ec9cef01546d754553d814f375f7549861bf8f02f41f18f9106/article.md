# UHS Leaders Engage with The Forum of Executive Women to Strengthen Leadership and Collaboration Across the Region


6514654723891891701_(002)


*UHS Leaders attend The Forum of Executive Women Annual Leadership Event 2025. (L to R) Lauren Pezor, Michelle Bryk, Roselle Charlier, Gerry Geckle, Diane Hill Lieb, Susan Baranowski, Jennifer Pasquale and Maria English*


**King of Prussia, PA (November 3, 2025)** — Universal Health Services (NYSE: UHS) proudly continues its longstanding support of The Forum of Executive Women, the Greater Philadelphia region’s premier professional women’s organization for advancing leadership excellence and community engagement.


UHS leaders attended The Forum’s Annual Leadership Breakfast, a flagship event that convenes more than 500 executives from across the region to exchange ideas, explore leadership trends and discuss opportunities shaping the future of business in Greater Philadelphia. This year’s program featured a keynote by macroeconomist Arunima Sinha, PhD, Vice President of Global/US Economics Research at Morgan Stanley, and a dynamic panel discussion with regional leaders in finance, philanthropy, civic engagement and entrepreneurship.


“The Forum of Executive Women provides an important platform for learning, collaboration and connection among leaders who are driving impact across industries,” said Roselle Charlier, VP, Chief Marketing and Communications Officer at UHS. “UHS is proud to support The Forum’s mission and to participate in programs that inspire professional growth and strengthen our region’s business and philanthropic communities.”


As part of this year’s event, UHS was again recognized among the organizations honored in The Forum’s annual Women in Leadership Report for their representation of women on the company’s Board of Directors. UHS’ engagement with The Forum of Executive Women reflects the company’s ongoing investment in leadership development and its commitment to fostering a culture where all employees can thrive and contribute to the company’s success.


## About The Forum of Executive Women


Founded in 1977, The Forum of Executive Women is the Greater Philadelphia Region’s premier women’s organization, actively working to bring together, and leverage the influence of, professional women to expand the impact and power of women in the workplace and beyond. Forum membership has grown over four decades to comprise more than 600 of the most influential leaders throughout the region. Visit[foew.com](https://www.foew.com/) for more information.
