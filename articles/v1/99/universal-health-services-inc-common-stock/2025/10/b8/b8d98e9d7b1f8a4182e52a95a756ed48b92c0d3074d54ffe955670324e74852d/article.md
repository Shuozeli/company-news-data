# Universal Health Services Announces Marc D. Miller Is Featured in LEADERS Magazine – October 2025


Marc_Miller


**King of Prussia, PA (October 8, 2025)** – Universal Health Services (NYSE: UHS) today announced that Marc D. Miller, President and CEO, has been featured in the October 2025 edition of *LEADERS Magazine* .


In this feature article about creating the future of healthcare, Miller discusses UHS’ mission, its keys to success and the critical role innovation plays in UHS’ industry-leading position.


“Innovation is a key aspect of everything we do. We innovate in a variety of ways which enables us to better compete in the market and, oftentimes, lead the market as a top healthcare provider,” said Miller. “Our dedication to innovation is key to ensuring our facilities thrive.”

## About Universal Health Services


One of the nation’s largest and most respected providers of hospital and healthcare services, Universal Health Services, Inc. (NYSE: UHS) has built an impressive record of achievement and performance, growing since its inception into a Fortune 300 corporation. Headquartered in King of Prussia, PA, UHS has 99,000 employees. Through its subsidiaries, UHS operates 29 acute care hospitals, 331 behavioral health facilities, 39 outpatient facilities and ambulatory care access points in 39 U.S. states, Washington, D.C., Puerto Rico and the United Kingdom.
