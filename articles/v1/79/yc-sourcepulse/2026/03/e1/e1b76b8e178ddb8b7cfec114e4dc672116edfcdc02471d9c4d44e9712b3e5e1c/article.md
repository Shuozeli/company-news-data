The $Q airdrop is now live.


If you have been active in the Quack AI ecosystem by minting Passports, voting with AI agents, referring others, participating in governance or holding an AI DAO SBT, your rewards may already be waiting.


This guide explains how to check your eligibility, how to claim your tokens, how vesting works, and what to do if you want to transfer your allocation.


## Step 1: Check Your Eligibility


Visit the official airdrop portal:
[https://claim.quackai.ai/season-1](https://claim.quackai.ai/season-1)


Connect your wallet to instantly see one of the following statuses.


- **Eligible** — You are on the airdrop list and can claim.
- **Transferred** — You were on the list but already sent your allocation to another address.
- **Ineligible** — You were not on the airdrop list and did not receive tokens.
- **Received Allocation** — Someone transferred their allocation to your wallet.
- **Unknown** — Your wallet has not interacted with the portal yet.


## Step 2: Know the Claim Window


The claim period starts on **September 2, 2025 at 8:00 AM UTC** and ends on March **28, 2026 at 8:00 AM UTC** .


After this period, all unclaimed tokens will expire. There will be no way to recover them.


## Step 3: Use the Correct Network


Claims are only supported on **BNB Chain** .


Make sure your wallet is connected to **BNB Chain** before proceeding. Claims on other networks will not work and will fail.


You will need to pay a small gas fee (~0.00002 BNB) to claim your airdrop. If a claim fails, your airdrop allocation will be locked for 4 minutes as a security measure.


During this time:


- Your **“Currently Available”** status will change to **“Temporarily Unavailable”**
- The claim card will shift from **dark yellow to light yellow**


Once the 4-minute window ends, you’ll be able to try again.


## Step 4: Understand the Vesting Schedule


The airdrop follows a structured vesting plan.


- You receive 10% of your tokens at the Token Generation Event.
- There is a 1-month cliff where nothing unlocks.
- The remaining 90% unlocks over the following 4 months at 22.5% per month.


You can track your vesting status directly in the claim portal and see whether your tokens are Locked, Available Now, or Claimed.


## Step 5: Claiming Your Tokens


To claim:


1. Go to[https://claim.quackai.ai/season-1](https://claim.quackai.ai/season-1)
2. Connect your wallet
3. Review your total allocation and available balance
4. Click Claim and confirm the transaction in your wallet


Tokens will be sent to your address. If you miss the deadline, the unclaimed balance is permanently lost.


## Step 6: Transfer Rules


You can transfer your airdrop, but only under specific conditions.


Transfers are allowed if:


- You are marked as Eligible
- You have not claimed any portion of your tokens
- The recipient is marked as Ineligible


To transfer:


- Open the portal and click “Want to transfer your airdrop?” to begin the transfer process.
- Enter the recipient’s EVM wallet address.
- Proceed through the confirmation modal.


If you change your mind before confirming, you can switch back to Claim Mode.


Once confirmed, transfers cannot be reversed. Partial transfers are not supported. The entire remaining allocation is moved. The recipient will follow the same vesting schedule.
