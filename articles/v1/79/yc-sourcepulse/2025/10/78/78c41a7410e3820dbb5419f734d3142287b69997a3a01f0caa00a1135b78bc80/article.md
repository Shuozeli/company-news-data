The **first unified sign-to-pay and governance layer** for the Agent Economy on BNB Chain.

## Why x402 BNB Exists


Today, making a simple payment on-chain feels like setting up a new account every time.


You approve, confirm, pay, and repeat.


Experienced users may tolerate it, but most people never will.


**x402 BNB** introduces a new way.
Users sign once, and everything else including transfer, gas payment, and policy enforcement happens automatically.


BNB still settles gas, but users never touch it directly. They simply sign, and the transaction completes under a verifiable set of rules.


Think of it as **“Pay with Signature”** built directly into the BNB ecosystem.


## How It Works


Under the hood, x402 BNB relies on two standards that bring structure and safety to the process.


**EIP-7702 Delegated Execution** lets a user give one-time permission for a transaction to execute on their behalf.


**EIP-712 Witness Signatures** define exactly what is being paid — token, amount, receiver, nonce, and expiry — so that intent and execution are cryptographically linked.


A **facilitator** combines both signatures into a single transaction and sponsors the gas with BNB. This creates a type 0x04 transaction that runs directly in the user’s account without extra approvals or pop-ups.


Each transaction emits standardized receipts that can be indexed, audited, and connected to Quack AI’s policy engine for governance or compliance.


The result is simple to use but fully transparent.

## Why the Technology Is a Step Forward


Web3 has often traded convenience for security.
x402 BNB removes that trade-off.


It introduces structural safeguards such as:


- Whitelisted contracts for safe delegation
- Dual nonces to prevent replay
- Automatic expiry rejection
- Metadata binding to ensure each signature can be used only once


Every transaction can be verified. Every rule is enforced on-chain.
No centralized permissions, no hidden custody, no manual oversight.
Just trust that is earned through code.


## Built for Developers, Agents, and Everyone in Between


Because x402 BNB is signature-based, it can handle far more than single transfers. Developers can batch multiple payments, send to many recipients, or settle with several tokens at once — all from one signed payload.


This enables real use cases such as:


- Automated agent payouts
- Multi-recipient reward programs
- Subscription renewals or service payments
- Treasury or DAO distributions


It is the infrastructure that lets agents, users, and businesses operate with the efficiency of any modern digital system.
