## What the Passport Represents


For the community, minting a Passport is the first step toward:


- **Identity in governance** : a portable proof that you are part of Quack AI’s ecosystem.
- **Delegation to AI Agents** : so your voice is always active even when you are not watching proposals around the clock.
- **Quackipedia access** : your onchain companion that answers governance questions, explains proposals, and helps you navigate the ecosystem in real time.
- **Access to rewards** : from earning AIQ points to participating in governance campaigns.


The Passport turns passive observers into active participants. Whether you hold one wallet or ten, whether you are deep in DeFi or just discovering Web3, the Passport makes it simple to show up, get counted, and have your say.

Numbers in Web3 come and go. TVL rises, token prices fluctuate, campaigns peak and fade. Adoption tells a different story.

It means governance is no longer reserved for insiders or full-time DAO voters. It proves that decentralized decision-making can work at scale.


For us, this milestone is a reminder of why we started: to make governance more accessible, more intelligent, and more effective for everyone.
