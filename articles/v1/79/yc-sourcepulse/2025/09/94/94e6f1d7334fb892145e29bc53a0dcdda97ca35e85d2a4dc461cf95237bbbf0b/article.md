## What is Quack AI?


Quack AI is the AI-native governance layer for Web3, designed to help decentralized projects govern better, faster, and more efficiently. In a world where millions engage with DAOs, dApps, and tokenized assets, Quack AI introduces intelligence, automation, and clarity to decision-making.


We solve what most governance systems can’t:


- Idle token holders
- Complex, inaccessible proposals
- Infrastructure that doesn’t scale


With AI Agents that vote for you, Passport-based identity that works across chains, and a modular engine that plugs into any protocol, Quack AI makes governance smart and inclusive.


Soon, users will be able to create personal AI twins with customizable preferences for voting and engagement. We are not just fixing governance. We are making participation simple, scalable, and actually useful.

## $Q Token & Upcoming TGE: Powering Community-Led AI Governance


Now let’s talk about the powerhouse running the entire ecosystem: the $Q token.


$Q is the governance token of the Quack AI ecosystem, powering everything from AI-led voting to incentives and access to advanced tooling. It plays a critical role in making governance faster, fairer, and truly scalable for protocols and users across Web3.


We’ve allocated **79% of the token supply** to the community and ecosystem because we believe ownership should stay with the people who participate. Governance only works when it’s in the hands of the users.


And now, after months of development, testing, and feedback, we’re proud to share that $Q will officially list across top-tier exchanges including Binance, KuCoin, and others starting at 7:00 AM UTC on **September 2.** More exchange listings are on the way.


Trading on Binance begins at 7:00 AM UTC, followed by other exchanges going live at 8:00 AM UTC. Airdrop claim will also open on the same day. Stay tuned to our official channels for details.
