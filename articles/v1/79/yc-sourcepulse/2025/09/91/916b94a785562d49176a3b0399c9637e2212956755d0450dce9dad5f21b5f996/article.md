## Token Allocation

### Community & Ecosystem (79%)


**79% of the total $Q supply** is dedicated to the community, ecosystem development, and protocol sustainability, distributed across several key areas to ensure long-term impact and alignment:


- **Community (30%)** : Reserved for rewarding users who engage with governance, vote, delegate to AI Agents, and participate in Passport-based activities. This allocation supports a user-led growth flywheel driven by points, incentives, and reputation.
- **Ecosystem & Partnerships (15%)** : Supports dApps, protocols, and tools integrating with Quack AI. This includes grants, DAO tooling builders, and long-term collaborators advancing governance adoption across chains.
- **Airdrop (12%)** : Targets early Passport holders, active community contributors, AI DAO SBT holders, governance voters across partner chains, and select Web3 communities participating through ecosystem campaigns. Designed to reward loyalty and give users meaningful skin in the game.
- **Treasury (11%)** : A portion of the Treasury (4%) is unlocked at TGE. This is intentional. These funds are being reserved to address future needs such as additional exchange listings, liquidity requirements, compliance obligations, market development demands, and risk management, with a clear focus on long-term preparedness. None of it goes to the team or internal stakeholders.
- **Marketing & Growth (6%)** : Fuels awareness, user acquisition, KOL activations, and campaign-based growth across key Web3 markets and regions.
- **Liquidity (5%)** : Provisioned to ensure smooth listing, trading, and on-chain utility across DEXs and CEXs during the early phase of token rollout.


### **Core Team (10%)**


Reserved for long-term contributors and builders of Quack AI. Tokens are subject to a 12-month cliff followed by gradual vesting to ensure deep alignment with the protocol’s future.


### **Investors & Advisors (11%)**


Allocated to strategic backers who supported Quack AI’s early development, infrastructure scaling, and go-to-market growth. These allocations are also subject to long lockups and vesting schedules to preserve integrity.
