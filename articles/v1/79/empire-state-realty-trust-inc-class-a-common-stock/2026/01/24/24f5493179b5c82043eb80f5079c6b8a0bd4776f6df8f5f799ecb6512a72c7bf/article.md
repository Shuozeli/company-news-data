# The Empire State Building: The Ultimate Workplace in Midtown Manhattan

Tenants of the Empire State Building benefit from its more than 65,000 square feet of


[exclusive amenities](https://www.esrtreit.com/blog/empire-state-building/top-five-features-of-the-empire-state-building-club-level-esrt/) , which include its state-of-the-art fitness center, tenant-only conference center, and the newly added Empire Lounge, which features a 400+ person all hands space, lounge with comfortable seating and private phone booths, a basketball/pickleball court, and “ESRTees” golf simulators.


The building offers an array of in-building dining options like the


[Starbucks Reserve® Empire State Building Store](https://www.esrtreit.com/blog/sustainability/starbucks-reserve-and-esrt-a-beacon-of-sustainability/) , Ghirardelli Chocolate, Chipotle, Alidoro, Tacombi, Chop’t, Sushi Beauu, and the building’s signature restaurant, STATE Grill and Bar.
