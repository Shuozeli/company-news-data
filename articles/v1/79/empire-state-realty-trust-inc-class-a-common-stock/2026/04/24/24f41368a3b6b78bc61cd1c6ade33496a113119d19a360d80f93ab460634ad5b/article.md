# Office Space in Midtown Manhattan: Inside 111 W. 33rd Street with Nespresso

## Built for Sustainable, High-Performance Workplaces


ESRT is recognized for leadership in[energy efficiency and indoor environmental quality](https://www.esrtreit.com/sustainability/) .


- 100% of ESRT’s portfolio is WELL Health-Safety Rated


- 100% of its New York City commercial office portfolio is ENERGY STAR certified


For tenants, this translates into healthier indoor environments and measurable energy performance, both of which are increasingly important in corporate real estate decisions.


For Nespresso, a B Corporation, these factors were essential.


“We need to make sure that our brand and everything we do is purposeful and brings benefit to the community and the planet,” said Bai. “ESRT has helped us in our B Corp journey by providing us with key insights into our data and understanding our environmental impact, including our waste footprint and energy consumption.”


*Nespresso offices at 111 W. 33rd Street*


## A Partnership That Supports Tenant Goals


Beyond building performance, ESRT works directly with tenants to support sustainability and workplace outcomes.


During Nespresso’s office buildout, ESRT’s in-house sustainability expertise and network of vetted vendors helped source responsible material and partners.


“Building out an office with sustainability in mind brings a lot of challenges,” said Korolenko. “The partnership with ESRT was important to us is because we were really able to tap into the resources and abundance of different vendors, contractors, and suppliers to really deliver on our sustainability goals.”


This hands-on approach helps tenants align their physical space with broader ESG commitments and reduces friction during design and construction.
