# Empire State Realty Trust Releases 2025 Sustainability Report

## Continued Global Leadership in Sustainability


ESRT ranked first in management among all companies in the Americas by[GRESB](https://www.gresb.com/) and earned a 5-Star rating for the sixth consecutive year. This distinction reflects sustained excellence in ESG performance and positions ESRT among the top-performing real estate companies globally.


GRESB’s annual assessment evaluates environmental, social, and governance practices across the real estate sector, and ESRT’s continued top ranking underscores its commitment to transparency, measurable results, and industry leadership.


## Measured Energy Reduction Across the Portfolio


Energy efficiency remains central to ESRT’s sustainability strategy and long-term asset performance.


The[Empire State Building](https://www.esrtreit.com/properties/office/empire-state-building1/) achieved a 49% reduction in energy use since 2007, while the company’s full office portfolio achieved a 39% reduction since 2009. These results reflect ESRT’s long-term approach to energy management.


## Healthy Buildings Across 100% of the Portfolio


ESRT maintained its leadership in healthy buildings, with a continued focus on indoor environmental quality and occupant well-being.


- 100% of the portfolio achieved the WELL Health-Safety Rating


- Recipient of the WELL Health-Safety Leadership Award for the third consecutive year


- Achieved the Healthy Building Catalyst Award


These achievements highlight ESRT’s consistent approach to health and safety standards, as well as its ability to implement best practices across the entire portfolio.
