# The Rooftop at 1333 Broadway: A Tour of ESRT’s New Outdoor Amenity in NYC

When the workday ends, the Rooftop transforms into one of the most distinctive private event venues in Midtown.


Tenants can book the space through the ESRT Events & Hospitality Team for corporate gatherings, client receptions, product launches, and team celebrations — with capacity for 200+ guests, full AV, dedicated catering partners like STATE Grill & Bar, and a weather policy that guarantees an alternative date within 72 hours.


Few venues in the city offer this combination: a turnkey rooftop, professional event support, and unmatched views of the Empire State Building.
