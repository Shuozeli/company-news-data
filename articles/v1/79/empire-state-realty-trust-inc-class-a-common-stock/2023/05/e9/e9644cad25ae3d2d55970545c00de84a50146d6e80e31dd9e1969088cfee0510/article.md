# Starbucks Reserve® and ESRT: Sustainability That Leases

## Greener Store Design


From the earliest design conversations through construction, Starbucks and ESRT worked together to integrate sustainable strategies that support both operational efficiency and environmental performance.


Key features of the Starbucks Reserve Empire State Building store include:


- 100% LED lighting, which significantly reduces energy consumption compared to traditional lighting
- Low-flow water fixtures that conserve water without performance sacrifice
- Mercury-free environments that support safer material choices
- Energy-conscious systems designed to align with ESRT’s building performance standards


These strategies help Starbucks reduce its environmental footprint as it operates within the “World’s Most Famous Building.”


## A Partnership Built on Performance


The collaboration between Starbucks and ESRT reflects a shared commitment to sustainability that begins long before doors open to customers. That collaborative process allowed both teams to align on building systems, materials, and operational strategies that support long-term environmental performance.


## Sustainability Leadership at the Empire State Building


ESRT is widely recognized as a global leader in sustainable building operations. The Empire State Building is consistently ranked among the most energy-efficient in the United States, supported by industry-leading certifications and performance standards.


Through initiatives like the Empire State Building’s innovative deep energy retrofit and ESRT’s portfolio-wide efficiency programs, the company continues to demonstrate how historic buildings can deliver modern sustainability performance.


For tenants, these investments translate into tangible benefits like lower operating costs, efficient building systems, and healthy workplace environments in the heart of New York City.


## Location, Experience, and Efficiency


Located at the base of the Empire State Building in Midtown Manhattan, the Starbucks Reserve® Empire State Building store benefits from the building’s unmatched accessibility and amenities.


The property offers convenient access to major transit hubs, world-class retail, and the iconic Empire State Building Observation Deck, voted the #1 Top Attraction in New York City by Tripadvisor travelers for four consecutive years.


For tenants like Starbucks, the location supports both customer experience and operational efficiency.


## Sustainability That Leases


With high-performance, healthy buildings, premier locations, and collaborative tenant partnerships, ESRT continues to attract leading global brands that value efficient workplaces and retail environments.


Together, Starbucks and ESRT demonstrate how thoughtful design and shared environmental goals can create spaces that work well for businesses and customers.
