# Unlock the Power of Automation with Arketa's New Pre-Built Playbooks

## **Unlock the Power of Automation with Arketa's New Pre-Built Playbooks**


At Arketa, we understand the challenges wellness business owners face in maintaining consistent, personalized communication with clients. That’s why we’ve made using automations easier than ever with our new pre-built automation playbooks. Designed specifically for wellness business, these playbooks take the hassle out of managing client interactions, allowing you to focus on what you do best – running your studio.


### **Effortless Automation for Your Studio**


Our pre-built automation playbooks cover a range of essential touchpoints, from welcome emails and milestone celebrations to win-back campaigns for lost clients. Implementing these playbooks is straightforward: simply select a playbook and set it live. In minutes, you'll be creating high-converting, personalized, and engaging automated touchpoints for your customers.


### **Playbooks Designed for You**


**Create Automations in Minutes** : We've identified the most critical automations for your business and built them for you. Just choose a pre-built automation, and it will generate end-to-end copy, emails, and texts, all branded with your personalization tokens. You can set it live as is or customize the copy and add or delete touchpoints to fit your studio’s unique needs.


### **Powerful Triggers for Seamless Engagement**


**Instant Communication** : Turn on automations and let them do the work for you. Our powerful triggers send communication touchpoints to clients instantly based on actions such as booking their first class or reaching their 100th class milestone. These automations ensure you never miss an important customer milestone, keeping clients engaged at the right time with the right message.


### **Boost Client Retention**


**Personalized Touchpoints** : Keep your clients engaged with personalized communications. Automate welcome emails and texts to new clients, educating them on your studio's offerings. Celebrate loyal clients' achievements with milestone emails that recognize their accomplishments and encourage their ongoing commitment to your studio.


### **Win Back Lost Clients**


**Re-engagement Campaigns** : Don’t let a client go cold. Our custom triggers allow you to automatically re-engage clients who haven’t booked a class in a while or who have canceled their memberships. This eliminates the need for time-consuming tracking and manual messaging, ensuring you never miss an opportunity to re-engage and retain your clients.


### **Goal Oriented Sequences**


**Track Conversions** : Arketa's pre-built playbook are designed to help you reach conversion goals, from clients booking a specific class or purchasing an intro offer. A conversion goal is the best way to measure campaign success - it provides a clear objective, allowing you to assess effectiveness. By defining specific actions or outcomes you want clients to take, you can gain valuable insights, and optimize campaigns for better results.
