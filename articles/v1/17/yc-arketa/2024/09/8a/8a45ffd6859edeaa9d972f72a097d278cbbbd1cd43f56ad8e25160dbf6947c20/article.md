# Arketa Reports 2.0: The Ultimate Tool for Data-Driven Success

## **Arketa Reports 2.0: The Ultimate Tool for Data-Driven Success**


In the fast-paced world of wellness, having the right data at your fingertips can make all the difference. Whether you're tracking sales, analyzing client retention, or assessing the performance of your classes and instructors, accurate and timely insights are crucial. But let’s face it—sifting through spreadsheets, exporting data, and manually manipulating information in Excel or Google Sheets can be time-consuming and overwhelming.


Introducing **Arketa Reports 2.0** , our most powerful and advanced reporting feature yet. Designed specifically for wellness businesses, Arketa Reports 2.0 takes the guesswork out of data analysis, giving you the exact insights you need to run and grow your business—all in just a few clicks.


## **Why Arketa Reports 2.0?**


Arketa Reports 2.0 isn’t just another reporting tool; it’s a comprehensive solution built to meet the unique needs of wellness studio owners and operators. Here’s how it can transform your business:


### **Run Reports in Minutes**


Time is precious, and Arketa Reports 2.0 ensures you don’t waste a minute. With over 45 pre-built reports directly available in your dashboard, you can instantly run the most critical reports with a single click. No more searching for data or spending hours calculating results—our reports automatically pull in the relevant data, providing you with instant calculations and the insights you need to make informed decisions on the fly.


### **Custom Data Visualization**


Every business has unique needs, and Arketa Reports 2.0 gives you full control over how you view your data. With easy drag-and-drop reordering of columns, checkboxes to include or exclude specific data points, and powerful filter controls, you can tailor your reports to show exactly what you need. Whether you’re sorting data by customer, class name, instructor, or date range, Arketa makes it easy to find the precise insights you’re looking for.


### **Gain Powerful Insights**


Data is only as good as the insights it provides. Arketa’s pre-built reports are not just about numbers—they’re designed to help you understand your business better and grow it. Dive deep into reports like ‘Attendance Analysis’ and ‘Retention’ to learn which classes are the most popular, which times work best, and which instructors are driving the most revenue. With these insights, you can make strategic decisions that boost your bottom line.


### **Favorite Reports for Quick Access**


At Arketa, we understand that every wellness business is different. That’s why we’ve made it easy to save and favorite your customized reports for quick access. Whether it’s a daily sales report or a weekly retention analysis, you can store your most commonly used reports and access them with just a click, eliminating the need to recreate them every time.


### **Why Choose Arketa Reports 2.0?**


We built Arketa Reports 2.0 with one goal in mind: to simplify your life as a wellness studio owner. By automating data pulling, providing intuitive data views, and offering custom filtering, we’ve eliminated the need for manual data manipulation and complex spreadsheet work. Now, you can focus on what you do best—running your studio and serving your clients—while Arketa takes care of the reporting.


### **Get Back to What You Love**


Running a wellness studio is more than just a job; it’s a passion. And with Arketa Reports 2.0, you can spend more time doing what you love and less time managing data. Let us handle the numbers while you focus on delivering the best experience for your clients.


Ready to elevate your business? Explore Arketa Reports 2.0 today and discover how easy it is to make data-informed decisions that drive growth.
