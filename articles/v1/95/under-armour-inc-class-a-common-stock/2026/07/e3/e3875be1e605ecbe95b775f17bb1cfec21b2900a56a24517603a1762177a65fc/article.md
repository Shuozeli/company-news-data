# Under Armour and Bobby Witt Jr. Bring the Thrill of the “Perfect Pull” to the Diamond with the Dream Pull PE

Built to translate the mindset of a collector into performance footwear, the footwear brings the feeling of a perfect pull to the diamond. On the side of the cleat and turf, the bubble features a lockup of Bobby Witt Jr.’s “JR” logo and the Under Armour logo printed inside. On the heel, the back bubble reads “GM,” referencing Gem Mint condition, while chromed, iridescent detailing creates a dynamic, light-shifting effect similar to a high-end parallel card.


For the first time, fans can see, touch and wear Bobby’s “JR” logo and signature, embossed on the tongue of the Dream Pull cleat — a detail previously reserved for Bobby’s personal pairs.
