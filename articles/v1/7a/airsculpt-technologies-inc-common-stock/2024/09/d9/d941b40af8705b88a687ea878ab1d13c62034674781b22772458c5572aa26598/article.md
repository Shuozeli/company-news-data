# Just Jared: Brittany Cartwright Reveals Secret Cosmetic Procedure She Had Before Filing for Divorce from Jax Taylor


Brittany Cartwright is looking and feeling her best.


In a new interview, the 35-year-old reality star, best known for starring on Vanderpump Rules and The Valley, revealed the secret cosmetic surgery procedure she had done before filing for divorce from Jax Taylor.


Back in May, Brittany underwent an AirSculpt fat removal and skin tightening session to prepare for the summer.


“I wanted to look good after everything I’ve been through this year,” Brittany told The Daily Mail on Wednesday (Sept. 18). “I want to look my very best. I wanted to get my sparkle back.”


Brittany admitted that she “wasn’t really nervous” when she underwent the two-hour treatment over Memorial Day weekend.


“The work was done on my stomach,” Brittany shared. “I wanted some help with contouring on my lower abdomen because after I had, my son Cruz \[in 2021\], my stomach was just never the same. Now I am smaller and I feel great about it.”


“It was one session I call it ‘one and done’ because not only did it take out fat, but it also tightened my skin at the same time,” Brittany continued. “It is not like traditional liposuction it’s different — it’s body sculpting.”


Brittany went on to say that the recovery time for the session is about six months, but she went to dinner after the session and “didn’t feel bad.”


“It just felt like I had a really hard workout the day before because I was sore here and there,” she explained.


Now that she’s had some cosmetic work done, Brittany said that she’s focusing on working on herself to internally “heal” from her split from Jax, 45, after 5 years of marriage.


“To be honest with you I thought I would be with Jax forever so this is so new for me,” Brittany said. “It is taking some time to heal and move on.”
