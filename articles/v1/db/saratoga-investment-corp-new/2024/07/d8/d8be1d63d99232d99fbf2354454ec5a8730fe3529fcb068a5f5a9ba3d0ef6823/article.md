# The Five Best BDC ETFs To Consider In 2024

[Business development company exchange-traded funds](https://www.dividend.com/bdc-dividend-stocks-etfs-and-funds/) (BDC ETFs) provide investors a way to gain exposure to a diversified portfolio of BDCs. A BDC ETF invests in multiple business development companies, which provide capital to small and mid-sized businesses, often in the form of loans or equity investments. By investing in a BDC ETF, individuals can benefit from the growth and income potential of these companies while mitigating the risks associated with investing in a single BDC.


Here, we will take a closer look at five of the


[best BDC ETFs](https://www.dividend.com/bdc-dividend-stocks-etfs-and-funds/) for investors to consider in 2024.


## 1. VanEck Vectors BDC Income ETF (BIZD)


[The VanEck Vectors BDC Income ETF](https://www.vaneck.com/us/en/bizd/fact-sheet/) (BIZD) provides investors with targeted exposure to business development companies. By tracking the MVIS US Business Development Companies Index, BIZD ensures the inclusion of BDCs that offer high dividend yields. This ETF primarily focuses on companies that invest in middle-market businesses, offering both debt and equity financing. BIZD’s portfolio comprises industry leaders like Ares Capital Corporation, ensuring stability. Although the concentrated nature of BIZD can lead to heightened volatility, its potential for substantial income makes it appealing for yield-focused investors.


## 2. WisdomTree U.S. High Dividend Fund (DHS)


[WisdomTree U.S. High Dividend Fund](https://www.wisdomtree.com/investments/index/wthye) (DHS) is an ETF that aims to provide broad exposure to high-yield dividend stocks in the U.S., incorporating a diverse array of sectors, including business development companies. DHS follows the WisdomTree U.S. High Dividend Index, which selects companies based on their dividend yields. While not exclusively focused on BDCs, this ETF’s strategy includes BDCs to enhance overall yield. The diversified approach of DHS mitigates sector-specific risks, making it a balanced option for investors seeking income.


## 3. Invesco KBW High Dividend Yield Financial ETF (KBWD)


[Invesco KBW High Dividend Yield Financial ETF](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/KBWD/?guccounter=1&guce_referrer=aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cuZ29vZ2xlLmNvbS8&guce_referrer_sig=AQAAAItG5JvaxDoPC_rAQ6J6wyNA3wB7JBC_LVuR1oapGvlLTPLxrsC8KNu1L0bwf7oYPnUtpFlqyVdWw2j_3ABE_bJ6qWA-_uehYU45yN1-q_v6zaXyF4Py1Af3UdAqJpT4FmTGs4eYl6GEEZ5yu3Y8xKeCMPuYPHu4ulTQxSsA0TqZ) (KBWD) targets high-yield financial stocks, prominently featuring business development companies. The ETF follows the KBW Nasdaq Financial Sector Dividend Yield Index, which includes financial entities that provide high dividend yields. KBWD’s portfolio includes a mix of banks, insurance firms, and BDCs, creating a diverse financial sector representation. This blend offers the dual benefits of high income from BDCs and the stability of other financial stocks.


## 4. Etracs Monthly Pay 2xLeveraged Closed-End Fund ETN (CEFL)


[Etracs Monthly Pay 2xLeveraged Closed-End Fund ETN](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/CEFL/history/) (CEFL) provides a leveraged income opportunity by investing in closed-end funds that include BDCs. This ETN tracks the ISE High Income Index and offers monthly compounded 2x leverage on the index’s performance. BDCs boost a fund’s yield, capitalizing on their high dividend payouts. CEFL’s leveraged nature increases both potential income and risk, making it suitable for income-seeking investors who are comfortable with higher volatility.


## 5. iShares U.S. Financials ETF (IYF)


[The iShares U.S. Financials ETF](https://www.ishares.com/us/products/239508/ishares-us-financials-etf) (IYF) aims to offer widespread exposure to the financial sector in the U.S., incorporating a variety of financial institutions and business development companies. By tracking the Dow Jones U.S. Financials Index, IYF includes banks, insurers, investment firms, and BDCs. This diversified mix allows investors to gain from the high dividend yields of BDCs while spreading risk across other stable financial sectors. IYF is suitable for investors seeking income and growth from a broad financial sector ETF, with the added benefit of BDC exposure.


## Is A BDC ETF Better Than Buying A Single Stock?


​​The best BDC ETFs often offer greater diversification compared to buying a single stock. By investing in a BDC ETF, you spread your risk across multiple companies, reducing the impact of a single company’s poor performance and leading to more stable returns over time. Additionally, BDC ETFs provide professional management and easier access to investment opportunities; however, they might also come with higher fees and less control over individual stock choices.


## Conclusion


BDC ETFs present a compelling investment opportunity for those seeking diversified exposure. As the economy evolves in 2024, these ETFs can offer both growth and income potential while spreading risk across various holdings. If you are interested in balancing your portfolio with unique assets, considering BDC ETFs might be a crucial step toward lasting success.
