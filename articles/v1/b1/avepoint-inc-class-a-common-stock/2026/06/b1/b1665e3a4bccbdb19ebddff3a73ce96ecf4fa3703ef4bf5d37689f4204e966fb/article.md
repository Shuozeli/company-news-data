# AvePoint Confidence Platform June Updates: Accelerating AI Lifecycle Automation and Multicloud Governance

The June 2026 updates for the AvePoint Confidence Platform introduce powerful capabilities to **automate AI agent lifecycles, eliminate SaaS waste, and accelerate data recovery workflows** . As organizations deploy AI agents, expand multicloud ecosystems, and navigate complex compliance landscapes, maintaining absolute visibility is paramount. From **native integrations with Microsoft 365 E7 and Salesforce Agentforce** as well as continued integrations into Google Vertex, to **automated multicloud backup and dynamic governance policies** , this release is designed to minimize administrative overhead while strengthening enterprise resilience.

## What’s New in Command Centers


[AgentPulse](https://www.avepoint.com/solutions/agentic-ai-governance) Command Center continues to evolve as your central control tower to monitor, manage, and govern AI agents across your organization. This latest release bridges the gap between fragmented data environments and actionable compliance, delivering unprecedented visibility and control to enterprise IT leaders.


### Unified Multicloud Agent Visibility


Eliminate the friction of jumping between disconnected environments just to track your AI workforce. Command Center now **brings Salesforce Agentforce inventory and activity into the same unified dashboard** as your Microsoft and Google agents. This single control point allows you to seamlessly track adoption, assess security risks, and maintain consistent governance across multiple clouds without losing a unified line of sight.


### Smarter License Tracking and Cost Optimization


Accurately measuring your true ROI on AI requires precise seat utilization data. With **native support for**


[Agent 365](https://www.avepoint.com/webinars/agent-365-vs-avepoint-agentpulse) **and Microsoft 365 E7 licenses** , Command Center makes it effortless to spot adoption gaps by showing exactly who is licensed versus who is licensed


*and* active. New dedicated reporting **highlights inactive licensed users** to help you pinpoint specific training needs, while refined dashboards let you exclude licensed users from pay-as-you-go views for highly accurate cost estimates and forecasting.


### Actionable Operational Insights and AgentPulse Discovery


Upgraded discovery and compliance workflows permanently remove the manual digging from your day-to-day operations. The updated **Operational Efficiency dashboard automatically tracks how automated policy enforcement resolves violations** over time, proving compliance at a glance. Furthermore, enhanced AgentPulse discovery **adds native support for scanning modern Microsoft Foundry agents** , delivers improved connection management for[Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform (formerly Vertex AI) agents](https://www.avepoint.com/blog/strategy-blog/vertex-ai-agents-explained-how-google-vertex-ai-works) , and introduces flexible container routing to optimize your underlying infrastructure.

## Backup and Recovery


### What’s New in[Cloud Backup for Microsoft 365](https://www.avepoint.com/products/cloud-backup/microsoft-365-backup)


Enhancements to Cloud Backup for Microsoft 365 accelerate data recovery and provide IT teams with greater control through advanced automation and item-level precision.


#### High-Speed Express Restorations and Automated APIs


Administrators can now **browse and recover specific files or folders** across SharePoint, OneDrive, Teams, and Groups in seconds using **high-speed Microsoft APIs** , eliminating the restrictive, all-or-nothing approach of restoring entire site collections. This item-level precision minimizes the impact on tenant environments and drastically improves recovery time objectives (RTOs). Furthermore, IT teams can **automate these large-scale restorations without logging into the product interface** ; new programmable endpoints allow you to trigger file and folder recoveries directly through custom code, integrating automated recovery workflows into existing IT pipelines for maximum scalability.


### What’s new in[Cloud Backup for Salesforce](https://www.avepoint.com/products/cloud-backup/salesforce)


This release introduces advanced recovery orchestration and relational data management for Salesforce to minimize downtime and eliminate manual database reconstruction.


#### Orchestrated Salesforce and Docusign Recovery


Administrators can now **automatically reconcile Docusign file links within Salesforce** during a restoration. Because restored Docusign objects receive new unique identifiers, standard recoveries often orphan related records; this new orchestration **ensures all Salesforce references instantly point to the newly recovered versions** while automatically labeling old entries as "obsolete" to keep business operations and audit trails fully functional.


#### Multi-Level Archive Restoration


IT teams can **recover archived Salesforce records along with up to 10 levels of related parent and child records** in a single operation. Through a visual selection matrix, the platform **automatically maps out the entire dependency tree** , such as Accounts, Contacts, Opportunities, and Quotes, preserving full data integrity and complex lookups without requiring manual database reconstruction.


### What’s New in[Cloud Backup for Google Workspace](https://www.avepoint.com/products/cloud-backup/google-workspace-backup)


This release deepens application-layer protection for Google Workspace by expanding coverage to critical collaboration tools without requiring additional IT configuration.


#### Automated Google Forms Backup and Recovery


Administrators can now **back up and restore Google Forms, quizzes, and surveys** across both My Drives and Shared Drives. This ensures you can granularly restore data collection structures alongside standard Drive data to minimize operational downtime. Because Google classifies Forms data as a subset of Google Drive, **these items are automatically included in protected drives by default** , keeping your data collection safe without any extra administrative overhead.


### What’s New in[Cloud Backup for IaaS + PaaS](https://www.avepoint.com/products/cloud-backup/azure-backup)


This release introduces high-speed identity restoration and multicloud infrastructure support, giving IT teams deeper granular control over their hybrid cloud environments.


#### Entra ID Express Recovery and External Identity Protection


Administrators can now use **Microsoft's Express API for high-speed restoration** , significantly reducing downtime during identity-related disasters. The update surfaces these native "Express" recovery points alongside standard backups directly inside the calendar restore wizard. Furthermore, IT teams can **safeguard External Users, User Flows, App Registrations, and Custom User Attributes** with policy-based backup and granular recovery for Microsoft Entra External ID. A dedicated dashboard card streamlines management for licensed users, ensuring seamless access for external stakeholders while retaining data protection support for legacy Azure AD B2C setups.


#### Multicloud S3 Resiliency and Azure Disk Exclusions


Enterprises can achieve true multicloud resilience by **backing up virtual machines and storage across Amazon Web Services (AWS), Azure, and Google Cloud** from a single portal. This update allows teams to secure Amazon S3 buckets with automated discovery and immutable backups using either SaaS mode or the CAP (Common Alerting Protocol) Gateway, supporting flexible in-place or out-of-place restorations at the bucket and file levels. To lower cloud storage costs elsewhere, administrators can **isolate and exclude specific virtual disks from Azure VMware Solution backup jobs** . Critical Operating System (OS) disks are protected by default and cannot be excluded, allowing teams to safely bypass transient data while keeping vital system drives secure.


#### Real-Time Infrastructure Backup Tracking


Teams can now **monitor active jobs across VMs, databases, and cloud storage workloads using real-time backup progress reports** directly on the dashboard. This interface displays the specific objects and individual items currently being processed, allowing administrators to accurately track performance, predict completion timelines, and maintain complete status awareness without manual refreshes.


### What’s New in Cloud Backup for SaaS Applications


This release introduces deeper application integrations and multicloud storage choices to eliminate manual recovery workflows and protect complex operational logic.


#### Resilient Jira Service Management Protection


Data protection now extends to Jira Service Management to **safeguard complex configurations like SLAs and Request Types** , preventing tedious manual rebuilds after a misconfiguration. The platform restores service desk tickets alongside their associated customer organizations and asset links to maintain an accurate audit trail and keep service portals fully functional.


#### Integrated CRM and Docusign Restoration


For revenue and legal operations, **automated link reconciliation ensures seamless continuity** during data recovery. When Docusign files are restored, the integration automatically identifies and updates the related Salesforce record links, preventing orphaned data and keeping business workflows completely uninterrupted.


#### Expanded Okta Identity Coverage


Enterprises can now **back up and restore critical Okta sign-on policies, discovery rules, workflow tables, and console settings** , alongside newly supported objects like email/SMS templates and certificates. This allows administrators to quickly roll back a corrupted sign-on policy without rebuilding their entire identity and access management workflow from scratch.


#### Multicloud Storage Flexibility


Hosted storage options now **support AWS and Google Cloud Platform (GCP)** alongside Microsoft Azure. This multicloud expansion allows organizations to consolidate backup data within their preferred cloud ecosystem, helping to maintain consistent data residency and simplify storage management across the application stack.


### What’s New in ReCenter


ReCenter reduces help desk ticket volume by giving users self-service recovery control. The latest updates introduce streamlined manager data recovery to simplify employee offboarding and protect data continuity.


#### Manager Data Recovery for Exchange and OneDrive


Bridging the data gap when an employee leaves the company is now a seamless process. Administrators can **restore emails and files from departed or inactive users directly into a manager's account** by enabling the recovery function for orphaned accounts. This update eliminates manual data exports and streamlines access to critical exchange and OneDrive workloads, ensuring vital business intelligence is never lost.

This release introduces automated AI agent guardrails and expanded self-service provisioning to eliminate administrative bottlenecks and streamline lifecycle management across hybrid environments.


#### AI Agent and Automation Lifecycle Management


The Renewal and Metadata Enrichment process now extends past Copilot Studio to **support Microsoft 365, Foundry, and SharePoint agents** . Hybrid organizations can eliminate manual, PowerShell-driven distribution group management. Furthermore, a **new bulk guest user renewal feature** allows administrators to scale operations faster by consolidating multiple guest access reviews into a single actionable task when guests share the same renewal profile and primary contacts.


#### Expanded Modern API Support


Organizations can scale governance operations with less labor using **expanded Modern API support** . The API framework now includes dedicated endpoints for Microsoft 365 Group settings, Viva Engage communities, site collection profiles, and Power Platform contact management, allowing teams to programmatically manage their broader collaboration ecosystems.

New EnPower capabilities maximize operational efficiency, scale automated governance, and drive direct cost savings across enterprise cloud ecosystems.


#### Comprehensive AI Agent Governance and Lifecycle Management


Administrators can now **automate the management of decentralized AI infrastructure** across Microsoft 365, Copilot Studio, SharePoint, and Azure AI Foundry. ## Policies and Insights


### What’s New in[AvePoint Policies for Microsoft 365](https://www.avepoint.com/products/policy-enforcement) ?


This release delivers new advancements designed to minimize administrative overhead, strengthen collaboration safeguards, and inject smarter automation into your tenant governance strategy.


#### Human-Verified Site Ownership


Establishing true accountability is simplified with an interactive feedback loop that **replaces silent, automatic user promotions with human-verified ownership** . When a new owner candidate is flagged, the user receives an interactive Adaptive Card via email or MyHub to accept or decline with a single click. This explicit sign-off eliminates administrative rework while the captured responses continuously train predictive assignment models to improve future accuracy.


#### Automated Demotions for Restricted **Administrators**


Enforcing the principle of least privilege no longer requires manual IT cleanup or disruptive access cuts. The platform's auto-fix capabilities now extend to Site Owner and Site Collection Administrator Restriction rules, **automatically demoting non-compliant or restricted users to standard members** instead of blocking them entirely. This automates cleanup for common violations, ensuring consistent tenant security while preserving access continuity for active collaborators.


#### Behavior-Based Link Security and Targeted Guest Detection


Security controls now align with real exposure risk rather than arbitrary timelines. The Sharing Link Expiration Enforcement rule now **supports expiration based on the last access date** , shifting mitigation to behavior-based risk reduction so administrators can automatically expire stale and inactive sharing links without disrupting active collaboration. Additionally, the Ghost Guest User Detection rule now **supports Defined Groups** , allowing IT teams to isolate high-risk zones or exclude long-term external collaborators and service accounts to drastically reduce review fatigue.


#### Group-Based Policy Scans


The latest scan engine update closes a critical visibility gap by **including site domain groups during evaluation** . The system automatically assesses these groups when running Site Collection Administrator and User/Group Restriction rules. Because modern Group- and Team-based sites frequently rely on domain groups to manage permissions, **this update aligns policy enforcement with actual user architecture** to eliminate blind spots without requiring new configuration.


### What’s New in[AvePoint Insights for Microsoft 365](https://www.avepoint.com/products/security-insights) ?


AvePoint Insights continues to sharpen its data protection capabilities with updates that drive operational efficiency, improve administrative trust, and provide deeper visibility into your security environment.


#### Centralized Data Management and Redesigned Scan Visibility


Administrators can now discover, evaluate, and validate data types from a single workspace that **combines Data Type Management and the System Integration Testing (SIT) Availability Test** into a single view, allowing teams to run on-demand availability tests instantly and eliminate trial-and-error configuration. Complementing this workspace is a **redesigned Scan Details experience** that provides absolute clarity regarding scan freshness, progress, and status. By introducing **richer metadata, including active risk definition profiles and comprehensive object scan histories** , this update replaces guesswork with transparent, explainable data, empowering teams to verify environmental health directly in-product while reducing escalation overhead.
