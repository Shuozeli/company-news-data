Co. is a global provider of seed and crop protection solutions focused on the agriculture industry and contributing to a healthier food supply. The segment is engaged in the seed markets, such as North America corn and soybeans, Europe corn and sunflower, as well as Brazil, India, South Africa, and Argentina corn.
