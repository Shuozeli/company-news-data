# Under Armour and 424 Unveil Collaborative Capsule at Paris Fashion Week

Under Armour and Los Angeles-based luxury menswear label 424 have unveiled their first collaborative capsule collection as part of 424’s Spring/Summer 2027 runway presentation at Paris Fashion Week, held on June 23 at the Archives Nationales. The partnership brings together Under Armour’s performance heritage with 424’s distinct fashion language.


The two brands align on a shared aspirational ethos, each expressing a steadfast belief in the American Dream. Reflecting on the relentless climb that has led him to his fourth runway in Paris, 424 Founder and Creative Director Guillermo Andrade draws inspiration from his favorite park in Los Angeles, where he often escapes to think or simply kick a ball around. This sentiment is echoed in the front row, with guests including Under Armour Global Ambassador Gunna, artists Rilès and Tony Effe, and Under Armour athletes Sacha Boey, Laura Freigang, and Souleymane Cissokho.
