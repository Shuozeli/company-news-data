# Under Armour Names François Arnaud as Global Brand Ambassador

Known for his commitment to training, wellness and performance both on and off screen, Arnaud reflects the modern ‘forever athlete’ mindset - someone who pursues constant improvement because it’s part of who they are.


The partnership builds on Under Armour’s growing momentum across entertainment and culture, following recent ambassador announcements with multi-platinum country music star Parker McCollum and Grammy-nominated rapper Gunna. Together, these partnerships reflect the brand’s expanding view of performance and its belief that athletic mindset extends far beyond the field of play.


Arnaud’s first campaign for Under Armour launches globally this week, historically the hottest week of the year in North America.
