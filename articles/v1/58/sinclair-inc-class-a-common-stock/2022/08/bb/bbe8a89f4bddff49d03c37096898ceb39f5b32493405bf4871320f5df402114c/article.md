# Connect to Congress - Legislators Speak Directly to Constituents in Local Markets - Sinclair Broadcast Group

Connect to Congress is a multimedia initiative that enables members of Congress in Sinclair’s news markets to speak directly to their constituents on a regular basis through their local TV stations. Combining broadcast, digital and social media technologies, Connect to Congress offer’s Sinclair’s local market viewers new ways to get answers to questions about what matters most at home.
