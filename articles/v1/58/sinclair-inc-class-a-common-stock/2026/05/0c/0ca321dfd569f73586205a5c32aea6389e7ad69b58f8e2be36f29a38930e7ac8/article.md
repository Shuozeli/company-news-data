## **Sinclair Wins 12 Telly Awards – CBS Austin, Full Measure with Sharyl Attkisson, Criminally Obsessed, Amazing America, COMET TV, and WKRC Cincinnati Honored**


05.26.2026


**BALTIMORE, MD – May 26, 2026** – Sinclair is pleased to announce it has been honored with 12 Telly Awards. The Telly Awards honor excellence in video and television content across all screens, recognizing the industry’s best work from creators, brands, and media companies around the world.


“These awards reflect the full scope of Sinclair, where local news excellence, investigative journalism, streaming and podcasting all thrive under one company. Seeing recognition across so many formats in a single year is a testament to the talent and creativity of our teams who are creating award-winning content that connects with audiences across every platform,” said Rob Weisbord, COO and President, Local Media.


The following are the awards won by Sinclair:
