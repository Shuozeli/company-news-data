## **Sinclair Wins 23 RTDNA Regional Edward R. Murrow Awards**


05.28.2026


*12 of the Company’s Content Centers Honored for Outstanding Journalism*


*WJAR/ Providence, RI Honored for Overall Excellence*


*KATV/Little Rock, AR* *and* *WRGB/Albany, NY* *Named Best Newscasts*


*Additional Honors include Breaking News, Hard News, News Series, Feature Reporting, Continuing Coverage Investigative Reporting, Sports Reporting, Digital and Podcast*


**Baltimore, MD – May 28, 2026** –Sinclair is proud to announce 12 of its owned and/or operated content centers across the country have been honored with a total of 21 RTDNA Regional Edward R. Murrow Awards for Outstanding Journalism, a testament to the company’s commitment to providing the best in local journalism.


The Murrow Awards are sponsored by the Radio Television Digital News Association (


[RTDNA](https://www.rtdna.org/edward-r-murrow-awards) ) and honor outstanding achievements in broadcast and digital journalism.


“Winning 21 Edward R. Murrow Awards across 12 content centers is a testament to the journalists who show up every day committed to telling the stories that matter most to their communities. Our teams continue to raise the bar for local journalism, and I could not be prouder of what they have accomplished,” said Scott Livinsgton, Senior Vice President of News.
