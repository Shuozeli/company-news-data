## **Sinclair and AMP Sports Announce Style of Play, Women's Soccer Podcast Hosted by Julie Ertz and Kealia Watt**


05.27.2026


**Weekly series launches June 2, featuring insight, storytelling, and analysis from two of the game’s most accomplished players**


**HUNT VALLEY, MD.- May 27, 2026 –** Sinclair’s AMP Sports today announced the launch of *Style of Play* , a new weekly women’s soccer podcast hosted by Julie Ertz and Kealia Watt. The series will kick off on June 2, across all major podcast platforms, with full video episodes available on[YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@styleofplayshow) .


Ertz, a two-time Women’s World Cup champion and one of the most decorated players in U.S. Soccer history, and Watt, a NCAA Division 1 National Champion, will bring expert analysis, personal storytelling, and behind-the-scenes insight. Each episode will explore the latest headlines, key moments shaping the sport, and feature conversations with athletes, coaches, and influential voices from across the global soccer landscape.


“The growth of women’s soccer is undeniable, and *Style of Play* gives us an opportunity to connect fans with the voices and perspectives of athletes who have competed at the very highest level,” said Rob Weisbord, COO and President of Local Media at Sinclair. “We’re especially pleased to launch the show during the World Cup, at a time when global interest in the sport is growing. Fans will have the opportunity to hear unique insights and commentary from two of the top former U.S. Women’s National Team players, Julie and Kealia, whose credibility, authenticity, and deep understanding of the game make this podcast a natural extension of how we’re continuing to expand our sports content and engage audiences across platforms.”


*Style of Play* is supported by a strong roster of brand partners, including Coors Light, Airbnb and ZipRecruiter.


*“Style of Play* is where the game gets personal. This is a fresh, unfiltered look at life on and off the pitch through the eyes of the women changing the game. From elite performance and locker room mentality to motherhood, identity, pressure, style, growth, and everything in between. No two journeys are the same, but every story shares one thing in common: greatness,” said Ertz. “As we move closer to the Women’s World Cup in 2027, there’s never been a more exciting time to be part of the conversation and celebrate the continued rise of women’s soccer and women’s sports.”


“We want to create a space where athletes can speak authentically, from the biggest moments on the pitch to the stories, challenges, and perspectives that happen behind the scenes, and we’re excited to help shape and elevate that conversation with *Style of Play* ,” added Watt. “Women are creating new opportunities, changing perceptions, and driving incredible growth across all sports, and we can’t wait to spotlight the voices and stories leading that movement.”

**Host Bios**


**Julie Ertz**
Julie Ertz is a two-time Women’s World Cup champion and one of the most decorated players in U.S. Soccer history. The only player to be named both U.S. Soccer Young Female Player of the Year and U.S. Soccer Female Player of the Year twice (2017, 2019). She played every minute of the 2015 World Cup and was a leader of the 2019 World Cup winning team. Ertz was named to the FIFA FIFPRO Best XI in 2015 and 2019. A two-time Olympian, Ertz helped the U.S. win bronze in Tokyo in 2021 after returning from injury to play in all six matches. She reached her 100th cap on March 5, 2020 after a 611-day absence, following the birth of her oldest son in 2022. Ertz also played in the NWSL for the Chicago Red Stars, earning Rookie of the Year honors in 2014, and later joined Angel City FC in 2023. Off the field, Ertz co-founded the Ertz Family Foundation with husband and NFL tight end Zach Ertz, focusing on faith, sports, and education to uplift communities. A Santa Clara alumna, she majored in Communications and was a Hermann Trophy semifinalist during her collegiate career.


Instagram & X: @julieertz


**Kealia Watt**
Kealia Ohai Watt is a former professional soccer player and a NCAA Division 1 National Champion. As a freshman at UNC, Watt led the team with 14 goals and was named to both the First Team All-ACC, and ACC All-Freshman Team. In 2012 Watt helped lead the Tar Heels to a National Championship and was named the tournament’s Most Outstanding Offensive Player, earning her a spot on the All-Tournament Team alongside four of her teammates. Selected with the second overall pick in the 2014 NWSL College Draft to the Houston Dash, she dazzled in her debut season and was a Rookie of the Year Finalist. In 2016 she donned the captain’s armband midway through a stellar season finishing tied for the Golden Boot and earning NWSL Best XI honors. She would serve as captain of the Dash until her departure 2019, joining the Chicago Red Stars where she would play the final two seasons of her career in the NWSL. She has three caps as a member of the U.S. Women’s National Team on her resume, as well. Off the field, Kealia and her husband—former NFL All-Pro JJ Watt—are active philanthropists, raising money for hurricane relief funds in Texas, and providing after-school athletics support for middle school children through her collaboration with the JJ Watt Foundation. Watt also made her broadcasting debut in 2024, serving as a contributor and sideline analyst for the NWSL on Amazon Prime.


Instagram: @kealiamae X: @kealiaohai
