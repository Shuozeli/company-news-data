# Marsh appoints Teresa Palandra as Canada CEO

**TORONTO** , June 29, 2026 ­─­[Marsh](http://corporate.marsh.com/) (NYSE: MRSH), a global leader in risk, reinsurance and capital, people and investments, and management consulting, today announced the appointment of Teresa Palandra as Canada CEO, in addition to her current role as President of Mercer Canada, effective August 1, 2026. Ms. Palandra will continue to be based in Toronto and will report to Pat Tomlinson, President and Chief Executive Officer of Mercer and CEO of Marsh US and Canada.


Ms. Palandra brings extensive leadership experience within Marsh and a deep understanding of the Canadian market to the role. Since assuming the leadership of Mercer Canada in 2023, she has driven market expansion with a clear focus on strategy, client excellence and operational performance.


“A seasoned and innovative leader, Teresa will lead the strategic direction for Marsh Canada, ensuring we continue to strengthen our risk, people and investments capabilities and solutions for clients,” said Mr. Tomlinson. “We are confident that her leadership will further our ability to serve clients’ evolving needs in the market.”


“This is a pivotal moment for Canadian clients as they seek to accelerate growth amid global economic and trade uncertainty, while navigating an era of rapid technological change and embracing the opportunities of AI,” said Ms. Palandra. “I look forward to partnering with our colleagues to deliver client-focused, data-driven solutions that help organizations manage these risks, invest with confidence and build resilient workforces across Canada.”


Ms. Palandra succeeds Sarah Robson, who will retire on July 31 after more than 33 years of distinguished service at Marsh in senior leadership roles in both Canada and the United States. She has served as CEO of Marsh Canada since 2023 and was President of Marsh Risk Canada from 2018 until earlier this year.


“We are grateful to Sarah for her leadership, many contributions and unwavering commitment to clients over the years. We wish her the best in this next chapter,” said Mr. Tomlinson.
