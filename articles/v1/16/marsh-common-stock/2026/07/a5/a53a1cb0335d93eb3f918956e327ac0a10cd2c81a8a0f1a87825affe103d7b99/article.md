# Marsh increases quarterly cash dividend

**NEW YORK** , July 8, 2026 ­─­ The Board of Directors of Marsh (NYSE: MRSH) today declared a 10% increase in the quarterly dividend from $0.900 to $0.990 per share on outstanding common stock, payable on August 14, 2026, to stockholders of record on July 23, 2026.
