### Global Medicine Use Trends 2026


Global medicine use and spending are entering a new phase shaped by health policy shifts, trade dynamics, and the continued adoption of innovation. By 2030, usage is projected to approach four trillion defined daily doses, with China driving much of the growth while mature markets stabilize. This video explores the outlook to 2030, highlighting the role of innovative therapies, evolving pricing dynamics, and the implications for patients and health systems worldwide.
