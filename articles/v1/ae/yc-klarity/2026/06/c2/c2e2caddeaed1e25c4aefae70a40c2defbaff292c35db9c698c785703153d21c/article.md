# AI Agents Are Ineffective Without Context: Klarity & Cognida.ai Join Forces to Change That

San Francisco, CA — \[June 15, 2026\] The Klarity Team is thrilled to announce a strategic partnership with[Cognida](https://www.cognida.ai/) .ai, an AI-native engineering and solutions company that helps enterprises operationalize AI — building and scaling production-grade systems that deliver measurable business outcomes.


Together, Klarity and Cognida are giving enterprise clients a powerful new foundation for AI transformation.


Cognida.ai's clients come to them with complex transformation challenges: how to leverage AI and modern data architectures to move faster, reduce costs, and build better products. But even the most sophisticated AI solution can underdeliver when built on an incomplete understanding of the business processes it's meant to serve. Klarity solves this by observing how work actually flows across teams, capturing the tacit knowledge and process detail that interviews and workshops routinely miss and thereby producing a living, comprehensive Company Brain of how the organization truly operates.


As part of the partnership, Cognida.ai will integrate Klarity’s platform into its AI transformation methodology and jointly bring the platform to enterprise customers as part of a comprehensive AI modernization offering.


For Cognida.ai's clients, this means:


1. **Faster, higher-fidelity discovery** — Klarity's platform replaces weeks of manual stakeholder interviews with a rapid, comprehensive picture of current-state processes across functions and teams.
2. **AI transformation built on real process data** — Rather than designing AI solutions around assumed workflows, Cognida.ai's engineers and data scientists can anchor their work in the actual processes that need to change, making solutions more targeted and more likely to stick.
3. **Accelerated time-to-value** — With a clearer, more complete process foundation from day one, Cognida.ai can move faster from design to deployment—and deliver measurable outcomes more quickly for large enterprise clients.


" *We’re thrilled to partner with someone deeply technical, customer outcome-obsessed, and trusted by some of the leading enterprise organizations in the world to help with their AI Transformation. Together, we're making sure that AI transformation starts from a place of real understanding, not guesswork and ultimately delivers real business value.* "


— Andrew Antos, Co-Founder & CEO, Klarity


“ *The first generation of enterprise AI was about access to powerful models. The next generation will be defined by access to proprietary business context. Every enterprise has unique knowledge embedded in its processes, decisions, and ways of working—that is the true moat. By combining Klarity’s ability to capture that context with Cognida’s expertise in building enterprise-grade AI systems, we’re enabling organizations to turn their own operational knowledge into lasting competitive advantage* .”


- Feroze Mohammed, CEO, Cognida.ai
