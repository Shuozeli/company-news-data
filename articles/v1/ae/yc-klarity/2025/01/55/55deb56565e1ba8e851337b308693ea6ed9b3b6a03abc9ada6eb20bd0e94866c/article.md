# What’s New in Klarity – January 2025

## Simplify How You Start Current State Capture


### AI Interviewer


Klarity’s AI Interviewer is the first-ever voice-based enterprise interview bot, designed to simplify how you capture workflows. Forget the frustration of coordinating meetings across time zones or sitting through lengthy calls. The AI guides you through structured questions while you share your screen, capturing detailed inputs and screenshots along the way. Once your interview is completed, Klarity processes your responses in just 5–8 minutes, transforming them into polished, professional documents—no meetings, no delays, just fast, efficient current state capture.


### Run Klarity with Existing Files


Not every process has a video walkthrough ready to go, and creating one takes time you don’t always have. With Klarity, you can skip the hassle entirely by uploading existing files—PDFs, Word docs, or Excel sheets—and jumpstart your current state capture in minutes.


## Collaborate and Refine in One Place


### In-App Editor


Say goodbye to version control chaos with Klarity’s In-App Editor. Collaborate on processes in real time with tracked changes that show exactly who made updates and when. No more stale files, endless downloads, or searching for the latest version—everything stays up-to-date in a central repository that’s always accessible.


### AI Edits


Klarity’s AI Edits introduces a built-in chat sidebar that simplifies revisions. Whether you need to adjust tone, clarify sections, or add missing details, the AI generates tailored suggestions to help you update your documents quickly and effectively. All edits happen directly in the In-App Editor, keeping your workflow smooth and your content polished.


## Tailored Templates for Every Workflow


### Pre-Made Templates


Klarity’s pre-built templates make creating key artifacts like SOX Narratives, BRDs, SOPs, and PDDs faster and more consistent. Curated with best practices in mind, these templates provide a structured foundation that aligns with enterprise standards. Your team can start quickly and focus on delivering results instead of building from scratch.


### Template Editor


Rebuilding templates from scratch wastes valuable time and effort. With a new template editor, you can upload an existing document, and it will preserve your headers, footers, and branding while removing old content. From SOX Narratives to internal workflows, this ensures your templates stay consistent, polished, and ready to adapt for any project.
