# OSI Systems Receives ECAC/EU G1 Certification of Trace Detection Solutions for Airport Security

OSI Systems, Inc. announced today that its Security division’s Itemiser® 4DX and new Itemiser 5X trace detection systems have achieved ECAC/EU G1 approval, meeting the highest European standards for aviation security. This approval reflects compliance with the European Civil Aviation Conference (ECAC) and European Commission requirements for trace detection systems, supporting enhanced security for airline passengers.


“Achieving ECAC approval and EU G1 certification demonstrates our commitment to delivering innovative solutions that meet rigorous global security standards and help our customers protect people and assets worldwide,” commented Ajay Mehra, President and Chief Executive Officer of OSI Systems.


The Itemiser 4DX and the Itemiser 5X systems are widely used by aviation security authorities around the world with thousands of systems deployed.


In addition to airport passenger and cargo screening, the Company’s trace detection solutions can be utilized for prisons, customs and border operations, law enforcement, event security, and critical infrastructure applications.


**About OSI Systems**


OSI Systems designs and manufactures specialized electronic systems and components for critical applications. The Company operates through three key segments: Security, Optoelectronics and Manufacturing, and Healthcare. Its Security division delivers advanced inspection systems, turnkey screening solutions, and comprehensive support services to protect people and infrastructure. The Optoelectronics and Manufacturing segment serves as a global supplier of high-performance optoelectronic solutions and precision manufacturing services for leading OEMs. The Healthcare segment focuses on patient monitoring, diagnostic cardiology, and related services to enhance clinical care and patient outcomes. Serving over 170 countries, OSI Systems strategically positions its sales, service, R&D, and manufacturing capabilities worldwide to provide fast and efficient delivery and support.
