## Sensibull Overview


Sensibull is an options trading platform focused on strategy analysis, payoff visualization, and options execution. It is designed for traders who primarily trade options manually and want tools to analyze positions before placing trades.


### Features of Sensibull


**Strategy Builder**


Sensibull offers a drag-and-drop Strategy Builder that helps traders create and visualize options strategies without writing code.


**Options Analysis**


The platform provides payoff charts, strategy visualization, and option chain analysis to help traders evaluate different option positions.


**Live Market Data**


Access live market data and option chain information to monitor positions and make informed trading decisions.


**Paper Trading**


Sensibull allows traders to practice options strategies in a simulated environment before trading with real capital.


**Alerts and Notifications**


Create price and strategy alerts to stay updated on important market movements.


**Execution**


Sensibull integrates with supported brokers, allowing users to execute options strategies directly from the platform.


**Pricing**


Sensibull offers free and paid plans. Advanced features and premium tools are available through subscription plans.
