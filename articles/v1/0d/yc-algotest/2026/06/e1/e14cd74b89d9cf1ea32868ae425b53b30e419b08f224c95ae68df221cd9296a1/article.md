### Strategy Analysis That Goes Deeper


**Scenario Analysis** lets you stress-test three variables at once: underlying price, implied volatility, and time to expiry. You can see how your strategy behaves if the market drops 200 points and IV rises by 5 simultaneously, before you put any capital at risk.


**Monte Carlo Analysis** runs your strategy across 10,000 different price paths the underlying could take until expiry. Instead of one payoff line, you get a distribution of outcomes: probability of profit, expected drawdown range, and where tail risk actually sits.


**IV Skew** gives you two live views inside the builder:


-


Fixed Strike Skew: plots IV against absolute strike prices so you can spot cheap or expensive strikes before entering spreads


-


Floating Strike Skew: plots IV against delta-based moneyness so you can compare maturities without being misled by strike shifts


No other free tool in India currently shows both views in real time across all optionable stocks and indices.


**Event Volatility Analysis** shows how IV has historically behaved around high-impact events: RBI policy, Union Budget, Fed meetings, and earnings. If you are building a strategy around an upcoming event, this tells you what the market has typically priced in.


### Multi-Broker Execution

## Dhan Option Strategy Builder: Overview


Dhan's Option Strategy Builder is built into the Options Trader Web platform. If you already have a Dhan account and trade options manually, it is a capable tool for planning trades before executing them.


### What Works Well


-


**Real-time payoff graph** : updates live as the market moves so you can watch expected outcomes shift throughout the session


-


**Live Greeks** : Delta, Theta, Gamma, Vega for the full position; toggle between decimals and per-lot view


-


**Pre-built templates** : fast-create Iron Condor, Iron Fly, Long Straddle, Short Straddle, Bull Call Spread, Bear Put Spread, Long Strangle, and more; execute as basket orders in one click


-


**Multi-Strike OI** : plots underlying price against open interest for calls, puts, and the underlying. Useful for reading market positioning at a glance. Not available in AlgoTest.


-


**Hedge Benefit view** : shows your margin reduction upfront for every strategy before you place the trade


-


**Pledge Benefit** : use your equity holdings as instant margin for options positions


-


**All segments** : covers equity, index, commodity, and currency F&O


### Where It Stops


Dhan's builder is a planning and execution tool. There is no backtesting, no Monte Carlo, no forward testing, and no automation.


You can build an Iron Condor and see its payoff. But you cannot ask how it performed over the last 3 years on BANKNIFTY, run it on paper before risking capital, or deploy it automatically every Thursday. When you want any of those, Dhan cannot deliver.


The builder also only works within the Dhan ecosystem. If your broker account is elsewhere, this tool is not accessible to you.
