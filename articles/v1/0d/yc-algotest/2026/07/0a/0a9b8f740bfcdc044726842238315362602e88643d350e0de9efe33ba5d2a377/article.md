## Quantsapp Overview


Image via Quantsapp


Quantsapp is an options analytics platform used by retail traders, brokers, and institutions across India. Founded in 2017, it focuses on real-time market analysis and options research rather than on end-to-end strategy testing and automation workflow.


### Key Features of Quantsapp


-


**Live Option Chain Analytics:** Access real-time option chain data, Open Interest (OI), Greeks, and Max Pain analysis.


-


**Strategy Builder & Optimizer:** Create payoff diagrams and use the Optimizer to scan thousands of possible strategy combinations based on your market outlook.


-


**Manual Execution Tools:** Place trades through connected brokers using bracket orders, stop-loss, target orders, and Alert Trigger Orders.


-


**Options Simulator:** Practice and evaluate strategies using the built-in options trading simulator.


-


**Analyst-Led Signals:** Access structured trade recommendations and entry/exit signals from **SEBI-registered Research Analysts** .


-


**Educational Resources:** Learn options trading through webinars, courses, and certification programs covering topics like Greeks, Open Interest, and strategy analysis.
