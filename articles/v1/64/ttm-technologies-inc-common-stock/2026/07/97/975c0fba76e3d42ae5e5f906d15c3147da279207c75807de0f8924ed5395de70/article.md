# Intern Spotlight:Fahim

We are excited to highlight one of our talented 2025 Information Technology Interns from our Malaysia, Penang facility, Fahim, who made remarkable strides during his time


at TTM.


**1. What school do you attend, and what is your major?**


I graduated from Universiti Teknologi MARA Kuala Terengganu with a Bachelor's degree in Mobile Computing.


**2. Why did you choose TTM Technologies for your internship?**


I chose TTM because it is a leading company, and I wanted to be in an environment where I could grow and learn new things. Being a part of a strong team allows me to contribute while gaining hands-on experience.


**3. What did you enjoy most about your internship journey?**


What I enjoyed the most was working with my IT team. Their unwavering support and guidance have greatly enriched my knowledge. They have taught me the function of the Manufacturing Execution System ("MES") and how it improves the efficiency of the operational processes.


**4. What project are you most proud of?**


One of the projects I am most proud of was collaborating with the development team on the design of the User Interface and User Experience for the new Automated Guided Vehicle ("AGV") Navigator system. Additionally, I enjoyed creating 3D designs for the production line.


**5. Would you recommend the TTM internship program to fellow classmates? Why?**


I would highly recommend the TTM Internship program to my classmates. This internship has offered me a valuable learning opportunity, and the culture of TTM is always willing to assist and guide interns. It's an excellent place for anyone looking to grow professionally.


**6. Any interesting facts about you or any hobbies?**


Here's a fun twist: I'm an introvert, but I love hanging out with friends! I also love playing games, which helps me relax, especially on tiring days.


Fahim's journey at TTM exemplifies the dynamic and enriching opportunities our internship program offers. We are proud to have him on our team and look forward to seeing his continued growth and success.
