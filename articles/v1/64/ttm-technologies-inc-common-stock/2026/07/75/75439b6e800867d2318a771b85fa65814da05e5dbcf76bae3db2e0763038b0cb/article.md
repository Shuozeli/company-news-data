# Pioneering Innovation in the Commercial Space Revolution

The


[commercial space](https://www.ttm.com/markets-we-serve/commercial-space) industry is experiencing unprecedented growth, and TTM Technologies stands at the forefront of this transformation. Goldman Sachs forecasts the satellite market to grow from $15 billion today to $108 billion by 2035 in its base-case scenario, with the most optimistic projections reaching $457 billion. As the sector expands rapidly, the demand for advanced, reliable printed circuit board solutions has never been greater.


**Comprehensive Space Technology Solutions**


TTM Technologies delivers a complete portfolio of diversified technology products and solution offerings specifically engineered for space applications. Our capabilities span


[Printed Circuit Boards](https://www.ttm.com/solutions/printed-circuit-boards) (“PCB”s),


[RF and Specialty Components](https://www.ttm.com/solutions/rfs-components) (“RF&S”), RF and Microwave systems,


[Microelectronics](https://www.ttm.com/solutions/microelectronics) , and Engineered Systems—providing end-to-end solutions for the most demanding space missions.


**Advanced PCB Capabilities**


Our PCB capabilities set industry benchmarks. With layer count capability exceeding 72 layers, we tackle the most complex design challenges. We utilize extreme low-loss materials essential for signal integrity in space communications, and our thermal management embedded coins ensure optimal performance in the harsh thermal environments of space.


**Engineered RF and Specialty Components**


TTM's RF&S components are chosen not as commodities, but for their ability to meet stringent size, performance, and cost targets. Customers deploy our couplers, baluns, power dividers, and terminations throughout their intermediate-frequency sections, where RF&S solutions help meet critical BOM and form-factor constraints. This precision-engineered approach ensures optimal system integration while maintaining the reliability standards demanded by space applications.


**Beamforming Technology Expertise**


TTM's expertise in space beamforming technology distinguishes us in the market. We develop RF Beam Steering Networks for transmit and receive satellite antenna payloads, delivering both passive and active beamforming components for flexible spot beam AESA (Active Electronically Scanned Array) payloads. Our solutions power platforms across GEO MIL-SATCOM, MEO Commercial & MIL-SATCOM, and LEO Commercial SATCOM applications.


**Proven Heritage and Certification Excellence**


With a long-standing history in the space industry, TTM Technologies maintains high-quality standards. Our AS9100 and Nadcap certifications demonstrate our commitment to aerospace excellence. Recently, we achieved JOSCAR certification, expanding our proven delivery capabilities throughout the European region. All TTM space products meet IPC Class 3 compliance—the rigorous standard required by NASA for mission-critical applications.


As commercial space continues its explosive growth trajectory, TTM Technologies remains committed to delivering the innovative, reliable solutions that power humanity's reach beyond Earth.
