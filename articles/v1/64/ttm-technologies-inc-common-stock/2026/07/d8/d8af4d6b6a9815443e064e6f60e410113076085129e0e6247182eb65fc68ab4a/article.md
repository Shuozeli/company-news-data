# TTM Technologies: Agility Isn’t a Strategy. It’s a Competitive Advantage.

At TTM, we identify the demand signals early and invest in infrastructure and technology before the market forces the issue. The result is a company that doesn’t react to change but anticipates it.


**Expanding America’s Advanced Manufacturing Footprint**


When domestic manufacturing became a national priority, TTM was already building. Recognizing the growing demand for American-made PCBs for critical technologies across U.S. Defense and Space programs, we made significant investments in our infrastructure and manufacturing processes.


We expanded our Syracuse campus by adding a new 215,000-square-foot advanced manufacturing facility, further strengthening our industry-leading U.S. footprint.


TTM's New Syracuse Facility


Purpose-built for next-generation defense technologies, the facility supports increasingly complex and miniaturized PCB requirements while helping to:


- Strengthen supply chain resilience through domestic production


- Deliver mission-critical reliability for sensitive defense applications


- Accelerate production timelines through streamlined U.S.-based manufacturing


- Support national security priorities with a dedicated, cleared manufacturing base


The facility primarily serves U.S. Government agencies, National Security programs, and leading defense prime contractors — reinforcing TTM's critical role across the defense ecosystem.


**Expanding Our Reach. Deepening Our Impact.**


TTM’s strategy has never been about waiting for opportunity — it is about anticipating where the market is heading and moving decisively.


Driven by growing demand for advanced, U.S.-manufactured PCB technologies supporting American data center infrastructure, TTM acquired a 750,000-square-foot facility in Eau Claire, Wisconsin, to expand its advanced PCB manufacturing capabilities. This acquisition is more than a capacity expansion. It is a deliberate investment in the future of domestic technology manufacturing, positioning TTM at the center of one of the most consequential technology waves of our time: the rise of generative AI.


Concept Image of TTM's Eau Claire Facility


This facility will be aligned to serve the customers and industries driving the next generation of computing. Its primary customer base will be the data center computing and networking companies powering generative AI applications, a rapidly expanding market placing unprecedented demands on the supply chains that support it. As AI workloads continue to scale, the need for high-performance, high-reliability PCBs — produced domestically, with speed and precision — has never been more critical. We are ready to meet this demand.


**Building What’s Next**


The investments we have made in our facilities, technologies, and partnerships are not the finish line — they are the foundation.


Through our expansion projects in Syracuse, NY and Eau Claire, WI, TTM is doing more than growing our manufacturing footprint. We are deepening our roots in the communities that have supported us, creating long-term economic opportunity, through lasting careers and building the kind of domestic industrial capacity that America's most critical industries depend on.
