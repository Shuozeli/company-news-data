# TTM Technologies: Solving Customer Challenges Through Advanced Interconnect Solutions

At TTM Technologies, we're proud to highlight a milestone that reflects our commitment to pushing the boundaries of advanced interconnect solutions: the development and delivery of Asymmetric Printed Circuit Board (“PCB”) N+M Technology. Achievements like this are what cement our position as the leader in AI interconnect solutions.


**What is Asymmetric PCB N+M Technology?**


Modern electronics are pushing the limits of what printed circuit boards can do. As devices become faster and more powerful, managing how signals travel and how power is distributed across a board becomes increasingly complex – and increasingly critical.


N+M technology is TTM's answer to that challenge. Built on a hybrid asymmetric architecture — a design approach that separates the board into distinct sections, N+M dedicates separate PCB sub-sections to high-speed data transmission and power management in different thickness, respectively. By keeping these functions separate, N+M minimizes interference and allows each to perform at its greatest potential.


*Drilling Room in TTM Facility*


The result is a cleaner, more reliable signal and more efficient power performance — two things that matter enormously in today's high-demand electronics applications.


**The Challenge We Set Out to Solve**


The rapid growth of AI infrastructure, data centers, and advanced networking has created new and complex demands on printed circuit board technology. Customers in these spaces require solutions that deliver exceptional signal integrity and power performance catering different cable sizes – without compromising on quality, reliability, or manufacturability.


TTM recognized this need early and took early development with the customers and decisive action.


**How TTM Delivered**


Bringing N+M technology to market required more than technical expertise; it demanded a coordinated, strategic effort across our organization. Our dedicated N+M Task Force was assembled to do exactly that.


*Asymmetric PCB*


The team focused on four key areas:


- Clear Timelines: Establishing and meeting defined timelines to ensure customers could plan and execute with confidence
- Drive Development Builds: Rigorously proving out the technology's capabilities before bringing it to market
- Capital Investment: Making the right infrastructure and capacity investments to support long-term customer needs
- Design Guidance: Delivering fully documented customer design guidelines to improve product quality, reliability, and performance — and to ensure seamless customer adoption


The result was a technology solution that was not only technically groundbreaking but also ready for real-world, large-scale deployment.

This milestone also reflects our broader strategy of aligning with the megatrends shaping the electronics industry — from AI, hyperscale data centers, and networking to aerospace, defense, and beyond. By investing in technologies like N+M, we are ensuring that TTM remains at the forefront of innovation for years to come.
