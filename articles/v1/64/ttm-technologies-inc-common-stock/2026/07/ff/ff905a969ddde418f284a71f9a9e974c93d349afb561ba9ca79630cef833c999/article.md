# Built to Endure: Mission-Critical Electronics for Aerospace & Defense

In aerospace and defense, there is no margin for error. No second chances. No acceptable failure rate. The electronics that protect nations, enable warfighters, guide aircraft, support space systems, and power mission-critical platforms have to work the first time, every time, under conditions that would defeat lesser technology.


TTM Technologies ("TTM") supports aerospace and defense programs with mission-critical electronics across advanced printed circuit boards ("PCB"), RF and microwave systems, microelectronics, radar and IFF solutions, radiation-hardened products, integrated electronics, line replaceable units ("LRU"s), and U.S.-based manufacturing.


**From the Circuit Board to the Mission System**


TTM is embedded across long-cycle programs tied to national defense,


[space exploration](https://www.aia-aerospace.org/wp-content/uploads/Artemis-II-AIA-Members-Graphic.pdf) , and next-generation military modernization - programs measured not in quarters, but in decades. Advanced radar and electronic warfare systems.


[Missile defense architectures](https://investors.ttm.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/398/rtxs-raytheon-awards-ttm-technologies-a-multi-year) . Secure communications platforms. Space systems operating in the harshest environment known to engineering. These are enduring partnerships built on proven performance, manufacturing depth, and uncompromising reliability.


That role is not accidental. It is the result of deliberate investment in the capabilities, certifications, engineering knowledge, and production scale that the world’s most demanding aerospace and defense programs require.


*TTM’s microelectronics solutions support satellite communications and space missions.*


**Engineered for Where Others Cannot Go**


The environments TTM products support are unforgiving. Extreme temperatures. Radiation. Vibration. High-frequency signal demands. SWaP-C constraints. Supply chain scrutiny. Mission timelines where failure carries operational consequence. TTM’s capability set reflects that reality:


- [Advanced PCB manufacturing](https://www.ttm.com/en/markets-we-serve/aerospace-and-defense/interconnect-solutions) and circuit board production for conventional, HDI, Ultra-HDI, flex, rigid-flex, RF/microwave, backplane, and substrate-like applications.
- Vertically integrated solutions from Tier 4 to Tier 1 - one partner across the program lifecycle, from bare board to complete mission system.
- [Radiation-hardened](https://investors.ttm.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/381/ttm-technologies-launches-msk5065rh-compact) electronics designed for space applications, helping protect against cosmic ray events and high-energy particles that would compromise standard electronics.
- [IFF technologies](https://www.ttm.com/index.php/en/solutions/mission-systems/surveillance) and system support for ground, airborne, shipboard, and related mission environments, including Mode 5 considerations where applicable.
- Advanced radar sensor solutions trusted across airborne, shipboard, and ground-based applications.
- [Advanced microelectronics](https://www.ttm.com/en/solutions/microelectronics) and packaging capabilities for the density, performance, and reliability demands of next-generation defense electronics.
- Active beamforming capabilities for phased array radar and electronic warfare applications.
- Specialty RF components and modules engineered for the frequencies and tolerances that defense radar and mission systems demand.
- [Integrated electronics](https://www.ttm.com/en/markets-we-serve/aerospace-and-defense/integrated-electronics) and LRUs built for rugged aerospace and defense platforms where reliability, maintainability, and lifecycle support matter.


**Built for SWaP-C-Constrained Platforms**


Modern aerospace and defense platforms are increasingly defined by size, weight, power, and cost constraints. Capability has to fit the platform, survive the environment, and support the mission without adding unnecessary integration burden.


TTM supports integrated electronics and line replaceable units that help program teams manage performance, ruggedization, reliability, manufacturability, and lifecycle support across SWaP-C-constrained missions. From radar and communications electronics to surveillance and mission-system hardware, TTM helps customers reduce integration risk while supporting the demands of fielded platforms.


*TTM’s Mode 5 Operational Autonomous Surveillance ("M5OAS") System*


**One Partner. Every Tier. Every Mission.**


In defense procurement, single-source accountability is increasingly rare. TTM provides it across the hardware stack. From the printed circuit board that forms the electrical foundation of a system, to the RF module that processes the signal, to the integrated mission hardware delivered for operational use, TTM can serve as a trusted partner across the electronics lifecycle.


That vertical integration is increasingly valuable in a procurement environment where supply chain exposure, domestic production, traceability, and quality systems are under greater scrutiny. TTM’s U.S.-based manufacturing footprint, facility security clearances, and AS9100-certified quality systems support the trust and accountability aerospace and defense programs require.


**Built for Programs Where Failure Is Not an Option**


Behind every program in TTM’s aerospace and defense portfolio is a commitment not only to the prime contractor or program office, but to the people who depend on the system when it matters most. That commitment shapes how TTM designs, manufactures, qualifies, and supports every platform and program.


TTM does not just build components. It builds the electronic foundation that mission-critical systems run on. Whether a program is in early development, qualification, sustainment, or production, TTM is built to support the hardware stack that aerospace and defense missions depend on.


From the circuit board to the mission system, from the launch pad to the battlefield, TTM builds what cannot fail.
