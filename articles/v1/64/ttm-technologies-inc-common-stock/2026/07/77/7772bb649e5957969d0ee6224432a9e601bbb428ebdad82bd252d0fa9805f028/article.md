# Advancing Medical Device Manufacturing with TTM Technologies

The medical device industry is experiencing rapid technological advancements and an increasing demand for innovative solutions. As healthcare systems evolve and medical equipment becomes more sophisticated, manufacturers are under pressure to deliver high-quality, reliable products at competitive prices. Among these innovations, flexible printed circuit boards (“PCB”s) have emerged as critical components, especially in devices that require compact and lightweight designs.


Rigid-Flex products provide reliable integration of multiple rigid PCB assemblies into a single PCB module capable of supporting assembly, space/weight savings, and mechanical stability, making them popular in applications such as surgical robotics and diagnostic equipment. TTM Technologies (“TTM”) plays a crucial role in this innovation by offering expertise in the development and manufacturing of these components. With a focus on miniaturization and integration of electronic components within medical devices, TTM provides our customers with high-quality products that ensure reliable sourcing and cost-effectiveness, enabling them to meet the increasing demands of the industry.


**Challenges**


With the growing demand for Rigid-Flex and Flex products, one of our customers faced significant challenges. They needed a reliable source for rigid-flex and plate capacitor flex components, as their existing supplier couldn't meet their evolving demands and cost expectations. The primary concerns were the escalating costs and the need for consistent quality across their supply chain.


In response to these challenges, our customer sought a partnership that would not only provide a solution to their supply issues but also support their goal of sustainable and cost-effective production processes. They needed a partner who could bring expertise in fabrication and testing while ensuring flexibility and responsiveness.


**The TTM Advantage**


TTM successfully addressed the challenge by qualifying our Toronto facility to produce rigid-flex and plate capacitor flex components. With the support of our Logan facility's engineering team, we developed a robust fabrication and testing methodology tailored specifically for Rigid-Flex and plate capacitors, aiming to alleviate the cost pressures faced by our client.


One of the standout advantages of TTM’s solution lies in our commitment to teamwork and collaboration. The Logan engineering team worked closely with the customer to enhance our capabilities, enabling us to meet their stringent requirements. Our support went beyond just delivering a product; it involved bringing innovative solutions to life through tailored approaches and a dedication to quality.


TTM's focus on advanced technologies, combined with the collaboration of our teams, sets us apart. Our ability to develop custom solutions and rapidly adapt to changing demands positions us as a preferred partner in the medical sector.


As the industry evolves, TTM remains dedicated to providing solutions that empower our customers to navigate their challenges successfully.
