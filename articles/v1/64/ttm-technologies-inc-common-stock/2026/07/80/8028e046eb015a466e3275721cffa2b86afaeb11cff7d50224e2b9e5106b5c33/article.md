# Intern Spotlight: Ryan Santo

We are excited to highlight our talented 2025 Marketing Intern from Chippewa Falls, WI, Ryan Santo. Ryan is making remarkable strides this summer at TTM Technologies.


**1. What school do you attend, and what is your major?**


I attend the University of Wisconsin-Stout. I am majoring in Digital Marketing Technology.


**2. Why did you choose TTM for your internship?**


I chose TTM for its reputable internship program and the opportunity to work on real-world projects that matter.


**3. What are you enjoying most about your internship journey so far?**


I really enjoy being a part of the marketing team. Everyone is dedicated to achieving our goals and are supportive when I have questions. I also enjoy learning more about how this company operates.


**4. What project are you most proud of?**


So far, I am most proud of being able to assist with the internship program by collaborating with the team, creating graphics, and planning the logistics of events.


**5. Would you recommend the TTM internship program to fellow classmates? Why?**


I would highly recommend TTM to anyone looking for a hands-on summer experience. The internship program immerses you into an industry that you might have not even considered working in. This company’s structure and team leadership provide interns with the skills and tools to have a bright future.


**6. Any interesting facts about you or any hobbies?**


I enjoy listening to music and experiencing it live in concert. I am traveling to Minnesota for my third concert this year, with at least one more planned for later this summer.


Ryan’s journey at TTM Technologies exemplifies the dynamic and enriching opportunities our internship program offers. We are proud to have him on our team and look forward to seeing his continued growth and success.
