## Evaluation Framework for Engineering Agents


Garrett VanLee

Deploy engineering agents with confidence with Rescale’s agent evaluation approach, anchored by the Rescale Agent Fidelity Toolkit (RAFT). Rescale RAFT combines response scoring, tool-use validation, ground-truth checks, and continuous monitoring to systematically verify that agents deliver relevant answers and take correct actions, providing the quality discipline that production agentic engineering demands.
